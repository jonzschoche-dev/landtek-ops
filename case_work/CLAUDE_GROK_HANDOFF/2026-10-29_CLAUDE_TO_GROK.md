### The three findings that matter, with sources

1. **Supervision has no truth-finding power.** Sec. 25(a) [V] gives a supervising officer one question:
   whether an act is "within the scope of their prescribed powers and functions." A false sentence is not
   an act outside a power. **Fourteen months of asking the MLGOO and the PD for a finding asked for a
   power the Code does not give them.**
2. **For a municipality, supervision runs through the province.** Sec. 25(a) second paragraph and Sec. 29
   [V]. **The Governor is the supervising officer of Mercedes.** PD Relucio's standing referrals to the
   Governor are statutorily correct, not evasion; her 13 and 17 Mar 2026 letters cite Secs. 29 and 31 by
   number and the desk had read them as a dodge.
3. **The DILG is not the President's investigating arm for this Mayor.** A.O. 23 of 17 Dec 1992 [V], as
   amended by A.O. 159 [V] and A.O. 66 [V], covers provincial officials, officials of highly urbanized,
   independent component and component cities, and officials of **cities and municipalities in
   Metropolitan Manila** only. **Mercedes is none of these.** *Joson v. Torres*, G.R. 131255 [V]:
   "The President remains the Disciplining Authority. What is delegated is the power to investigate, not
   the power to discipline."

### Two corrections to earlier desk positions, for your log

- **Removal.** I had said the provincial route could discipline, suspend **or remove**. *Pablico v.
  Villapando*, G.R. 147870 [V]: dismissal of an erring elective local official may be decreed **only by a
  court of law**, and the implementing rule to the contrary was struck down. **The Sangguniang
  Panlalawigan route tops out at suspension**, six months per offence under Sec. 66(b) [V].
- **Forum ranking.** *Hagad v. Gozo-Dadole*, G.R. 108072 [V]: the Ombudsman's administrative disciplinary
  authority over elective local officials survived the LGC and is **concurrent**, with a six-month
  suspension power against sixty days under Sec. 63. **The Ombudsman is the stronger administrative
  forum, and it is already seized through CSC OAC-L 270.** Which is why Part 1 item 3 above went to HIGH.
