## 1. HIGH — Draft motion and Note 2 quote the superseded 1997 text of Rule 35 §§3–4
- **Claim:** `DRAFT_MOTION_TO_RESOLVE_SJ_2026-10-18.html` ¶¶4–5 (header: "Quoted texts are from lawphil (… 1997 Rules, Rule 35)") quotes §3 "[a]fter the hearing, the judgment sought shall be rendered forthwith …" and §4 "the court at the hearing of the motion … shall ascertain … It shall thereupon make an order specifying the facts … The facts so specified shall be deemed established." `02_SUMMARY_JUDGMENT_2026-10-18.html` relies on the same words ("it 'shall thereupon make an order specifying …'").
- **Source checked:** A.M. No. 19-10-20-SC (2019 Amendments to the 1997 Rules of Civil Procedure), effective 1 May 2020, read on lawphil today: <https://lawphil.net/courts/rules/am_19-10-20-sc_2019.html>. The case was filed in 2026, so the amended text governs.
  - §3 now reads: "The motion shall cite the supporting affidavits, depositions or admissions, and the specific law relied upon. The adverse party may file a comment and serve opposing affidavits, depositions, or admissions within a non-extendible period of five (5) calendar days from receipt of the motion. Unless the court orders the conduct of a hearing, judgment sought shall be rendered forthwith if the pleadings, supporting affidavits, depositions and admissions on file, show that, except as to the amount of damages, there is no genuine issue as to any material fact and that the moving party is entitled to judgment as a matter of law. Any action of the court on a motion for summary judgment shall not be subject of an appeal or petition for certiorari, prohibition or mandamus."
  - §4 now reads: "If on motion under this Rule, judgment is not rendered upon the whole case or for all the reliefs sought and a trial is necessary, the court may, by examining the pleadings and the evidence before it and by interrogating counsel, ascertain what material facts exist without substantial controversy, including the extent to which the amount of damages or other relief is not in controversy, and directing such further proceedings in the action as are just. The facts so ascertained shall be deemed established, and the trial shall be conducted on the controverted facts accordingly."
- **Result:** Contradicted. The amended §4 is discretionary ("may … ascertain"); "at the hearing of the motion," "shall thereupon make an order specifying" and "facts so specified" are gone. The amended §3 adds that any action on the motion is not reviewable by appeal, certiorari, prohibition or mandamus.
- **Fix:** (a) Replace ¶¶4–5 of the draft motion with the amended text above and change the header source line to A.M. No. 19-10-20-SC. (b) In Note 2, drop "shall thereupon make an order specifying" and say the court *may* ascertain the uncontroverted facts, and that facts so ascertained are deemed established. (c) Reword the alternative prayer to ask the Court to "ascertain" (not "specify") the Annex A facts as established. (d) Note 2's line that an early reading of ¶7 "protects the judgment on appeal" should be checked against the new last sentence of §3 (the ruling on the motion itself is not reviewable). (e) ¶1 "No reply was required" fits the amended §3 (comment within five calendar days, no reply step); keep, with counsel to confirm the comment was timely.
