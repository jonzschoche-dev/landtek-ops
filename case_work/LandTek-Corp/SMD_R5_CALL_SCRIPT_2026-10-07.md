# Call script: DENR Region V, Surveys and Mapping Division (SMD)

Prepared 7 Oct 2026. Office: DENR V Technical Services, SMD, Old Albay, Sagpon, Legazpi City. Email smd.r5@denr.gov.ph.
**Hours: Monday to Thursday, 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM** (closed Fridays, per SMD's email of 24 Sep 2026).

**Two separate clients, two separate calls.** Do Call A and Call B as two different calls, or at least say clearly that you are moving to an unrelated matter. Don't mention one client's papers in the other's call, and don't ask SMD to combine them in one transaction or one receipt.

**Ground rules for the caller**
- You are calling to **confirm receipt, get reference numbers, fees and release arrangements**. Don't argue the substance of either request on the phone.
- Don't agree to withdraw, change or narrow any request. If they ask, say: "I'll pass that to Mr. Zschoche and he will reply in writing."
- Get the **name and position** of everyone you speak to.
- Ask them to **confirm by reply email**. A verbal "we received it" helps, but the email is the proof.
- Write down the exact time of the call.

**Reference: SMD-SCS-INC-26-172** · Survey Plan Psu-143364, Lot 4 (OCT P-1616) and BLBM No. 1

**Opening**
> "Good morning/afternoon po. My name is ____, calling for Mr. **Allan V. Inocalla**, the registered owner of OCT P-1616. This is about your Reference Code **SMD-SCS-INC-26-172**, the request for the survey plan **Psu-143364, Lot 4**, and the GPPC for **BLBM No. 1**. May I please speak to the Land Records Section, Window 3?"

**Point 1: the records that are ready for release**
> "Your office emailed on 22 September that the survey records and GPPC Certification are ready at Window 3, with a fee of **₱125.00**. We'd like to arrange pickup."

Ask:
1. Are the documents still held at Window 3 for pickup?
2. Does the ₱125.00 cover all three items: the **GPPC Certification**, the **certified survey plan**, and the **lot data computation**? If not, what is the total?
3. Mr. Inocalla's representative, **Mr. Marlon Malaluan**, will pick up. He'll bring Mr. Inocalla's written authorization, a copy of Mr. Inocalla's ID and his own valid ID. **Is that enough, or does the authorization need to be notarized?** (It is not notarized now. If they require notarization, write it down and don't argue.)
4. Is there anything else he should bring, such as a request slip or the reference letter?

**Point 2: the follow-up request of 24 September (sister plans)**
> "On 24 September we also emailed a supplementary request in the same thread for two related plans: **Psu-143364 Amd.** (covering Lots 1, 7 and 8, OCT P-1615, P-1516 and P-1617), and **Psu-143363 and/or Psu-143363 Amd.** (Lot 5, TCT T-20757), with their lot data computations."

Ask:
1. Was that supplement received, and does it have its own reference code?
2. Has it been researched or assessed? What are the fees?
3. Can it be **released together** with the Psu-143364 records so Mr. Malaluan makes one trip? If not, when will it be ready?

**Close**
> "Could you please send a short email reply to jonathan@hayuma.org confirming the fee and that Mr. Malaluan may pick up on Mr. Inocalla's behalf? Thank you very much po. Your office has been very helpful."
