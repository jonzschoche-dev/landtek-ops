## 4. Citizenship-transfer bridge — HONEST design (the trap + the clean way)
**The trap:** a pre-baked option letting Jonathan (a foreigner) acquire the opco shares later reads as "the
Filipinos are only holding it for him until he can formalize" — which is the **Anti-Dummy Law** violation and
would void the whole structure. Do **not** paper a nominee-style call option.
**The clean way:**
1. **Anchor the value in the IP, not the opco equity.** The platform/IP is Jonathan's throughout — so his
   value is never in the third parties' hands; only the opco *operating* equity is, and it's a lean services
   shell. The Filipino principals genuinely own and run that shell.
2. **On naturalization, Jonathan/Patricia acquire opco equity through a *legitimate future transaction*** —
   a new share issuance or a purchase **at fair value** once they are qualified — like any eligible investor,
   not the exercise of a pre-agreed hold-for-me option.
3. **Do not memorialize present beneficial ownership.** Any "path to ownership" language must be counsel-drafted
   to avoid implying the Filipinos hold in trust for a foreigner. **This is the single most counsel-sensitive
   point in the whole build — flag it to the corporate lawyer explicitly.**
