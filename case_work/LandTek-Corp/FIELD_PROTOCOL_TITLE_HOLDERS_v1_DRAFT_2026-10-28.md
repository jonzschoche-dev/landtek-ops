## 1. Why title holders are different

1. **They hold registry paper.** The fight is over the validity of their title or deed (nullity /
   reconveyance), not possession by tolerance. Pay-to-stay, leases and ejectment-by-tolerance do not fit and
   could concede their possession. [R: scan S1, S3]
2. **Ratification risk.** In CV26360 the defense argues tacit ratification (Arts. 1910, 1403). Our answer is
   that no heir ever signed or received anything: "No heir signs anything" and the payments "never reached any
   owner" (WAR_OF_THE_STATUTES §13 row 6; §4). Any LandTek act toward a title holder that accepts money,
   gets something signed, "confirms" a sale, or bargains over their purchase could be argued as ratification,
   and not only on their lot but across the whole de la Fuente cluster. [R + P for the application]
3. **What they knew, and when, is the case.** Good faith is presumed (Art. 527); it is lost "from the moment
   facts exist" showing awareness (Art. 528); registration is the operative act (PD 1529 §§51, 53). The facts
   that decide each holder's good faith (price, date, what was annotated, who sold) are in records, and must be
   gathered without tipping anyone off. [R: WAR_OF_THE_STATUTES §6]
4. **CV26360 is live.** Gloria Balane is the lead defendant and represented. The defense already runs a
   harassment narrative (Baliza appears in the Torralba JA). Anything that looks like pressure on the
   transferees can be used in court. [R: BARANGAY_UNO brief cautions]
5. **Velvet posture applies doubly.** Jonathan's fact-finding-first directive (28 Oct 2026). Title holders
   are often the more established families; chismis from them travels further. [R]

## 3. Hard rules (no exceptions)

1. **No field contact during fact-finding.** Kristyle's house-to-house visits cover occupants only, "no
   title holders" (Jonathan, 28 Oct 2026). Title holders are on the canvass skip list. No door visits, no
   interviews, no letters. [R: Jonathan 28 Oct; P for "no letters"]
2. **Nobody but counsel ever discusses their purchase, price, papers or options with them**, and only on
   Jonathan's go. [P]
3. **Never accept** money, documents, signatures, "confirmations", tax payments or offers from a title
   holder, and never sign anything they present. Route anything offered to counsel unopened. (Ratification,
   §1.2.) [P][C]
4. **Never say a title is fake or void** to them, to neighbours, or to the barangay. Our position goes out
   only in counsel's papers. (Chismis, harassment narrative, defamation exposure.) [P]
5. **Never offer** pay-to-stay, lease, sale or "regularization". [R: scan S1, S3]
6. **Balane: counsel to counsel only.** [R]
7. **No talk about one holder to another.** [P]
8. **Barandon is told before any Track A step leaves the desk**, because it touches CV26360. [R: BARANGAY_UNO
   brief; P for the standing rule]
