Civil Case No. 26-360 · 18 October 2026 · internal; nothing goes out without Jonathan's go through the Chief of Staff.

The narrative is ours. Each note states the case the way the record proves it, so that by the time counsel acts, the storyline is already written: the class limit, the revocation, the deed paid for after it was signed, the partition before its own survey, registration after the agent died. The summary judgment motion puts that narrative on paper in the court's own order.

The rules of the feed.

  1. One page, one topic, one decision, one date. Plain language. No statute spine, no case law, no strategy maps.
  2. Nothing is sent until the last spoonful is answered. A reply, a signature or a meeting counts as answered. Silence for five working days means a call, not a second note.
  3. Our errors are "corrections," never "his errors." The record is corrected by us; the note says what we will do, not what went wrong.
  4. Strategy stays off email. The trap map, the trajectory, the audit and the held records are shown at a meeting, from a printed copy that comes home with us. Email forwards.
  5. Every note ends with the ask, and the ask is one thing he can do in under an hour.
  6. The heavy documents exist and are offered once, as "available if you want the full working file." They are never attached unasked.
  7. Through the Chief of Staff, from the jonzschoche@gmail.com account used for counsel, saved as a draft first, sent only on Jonathan's word.
