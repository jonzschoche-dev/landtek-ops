Civil Case No. 26-360, Zschoche v. Balane: counsel focus sheet

MTC Mercedes, Judge Cu · plaintiff's dates 14 Oct, 4 Nov, 11 Nov, 2 Dec, 9 Dec 2026 · 14 October 2026, updated 15 October · internal, not a pleading. Everything here is backed by the Master Trial Playbook, the War of the Statutes and the bound dossier (exhibit pages); this sheet is the short version.

The case in one line

Cesar sold to a buyer outside the class his written authority allowed, without the consultation it required; the buyer paid the owners rent for decades, never asked them, and finished the paperwork years after the agent was dead and his authority had been published as revoked. Lead with the class. Revocation is the backup. Never argue Article 1922 both ways.

The order of argument, every time

Class (Exh. E ¶7; Exh. B p. 3) → void (Arts. 1874, 1898, 1409) → not an innocent purchaser (Arts. 526, 528; PD 1529 §51) → no partition (Art. 496) → consequences (Arts. 449, 452, 546 ¶1, 1678, 549, 2208, 2232, 2234) → counterclaim falls.

The three words that decide it on this court's method

void (Art. 1874) · aware (Art. 526) · parties (Art. 496). A fourth sits in the SPA itself: therefor (a down payment is paid for a sale, and a sale needs an object and a price: Arts. 1318, 1458, 1460, 1475, 1482).

3. What Ramos will say, and the one-line answer

RamosAnswer
The SPA was annotated since 1996 as power "to sign, negotiate for the sale"; no revocation was ever annotated.The annotation sent every buyer to the instrument (§52). The instrument names the class and the consultation. Her own processor pulled the full text in August 2021 before registering it.
Gloria was in the class: rent to Llamanzares, a ₱3,000 down payment in the 1980s.No Llamanzares contract or receipt exists. The only "Down payment" is 6 Jan 2005, to Salvador. Rent is not a payment "therefor." A down payment needs an object and a price, and theirs were fixed in 2006 and 2016 by Cesar's camp.
"Prior consultation" was a private instruction (Art. 1902).It is in the registered instrument, so it is a term "as written" (Art. 1900), not a secret order. Non-consultation is ours to prove, never through Jonathan: the notarial registers and Registry files in chief, Salvador S31 on cross, Patricia on rebuttal. Class leads; consultation corroborates.
At most unenforceable (Art. 1403) and ratified by the receipts and Patricia's 2023 affidavit of loss.Art. 1874 says void; Art. 1409 says void cannot be ratified. Receipts went to a revoked agent's camp. An affidavit of loss is not consent to a partition; Patricia explains it herself.
Survey done in 2006 under Cesar; 2020 only formalized it.Consent from an agent revoked in 2005 is consent from nobody; the plan says surveyed 8 Sep 2020; the partition cites a plan number that did not exist before 17 Sep 2020; the certification is false as to the owners on any version.
Builders in good faith; reimbursement and retention.Renters until 2016 (Art. 1678: half value or removal, no retention); buyers in bad faith after (Art. 449); only preservation expenses refunded (Art. 452). Macasaet and Casibang needed the owner's permission; none here.
Harassment counterclaim; the ARTA complaints.The counterclaim stands or falls with the title. The referrals are the agencies' own acts; used, if at all, on cross for bias only.
