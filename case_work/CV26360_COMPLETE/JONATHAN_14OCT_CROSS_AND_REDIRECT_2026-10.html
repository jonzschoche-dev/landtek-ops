C. The qualifier: re-direct, keyed to each cross point

Each line below is one question counsel can ask and the one-sentence answer, usable only if the matching cross point was opened. D. The ten-second reset, for the stand

Wide question, small answer. Which exhibit says this. Concede the small truth. Never a mind. Never a date you have not seen on paper. If you do not know, "I do not know." If you cannot say, "I can't say." Then stop talking.
