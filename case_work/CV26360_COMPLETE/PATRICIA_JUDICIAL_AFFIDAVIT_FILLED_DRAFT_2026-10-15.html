A3. I inherited it. My sisters and I inherited the property from our mother. On December 7, 1988 we adjudicated her property among ourselves by an extrajudicial settlement, in equal undivided shares. It included the land under Transfer Certificate of Title No. T-4497, with an area of 139,132 square meters. [her affidavits, ¶¶2–3; Exh. E ¶4]

Q4. Where have you lived from 1988 to the present?

A4. In California, in the United States, throughout that time. In 1992 I lived in Huntington Beach. I now live at 8216 Pennington Drive, Huntington Beach, California. [CONFIRM] [Exh. E (1992 address, Huntington Beach); Exh. A (current address); she adds any other places and years]

A6. I knew that he had negotiated sales of portions of the land to people who signed contracts to sell with him or paid him down payments or partial payments. I did not deal with those buyers myself. [CONFIRM]
