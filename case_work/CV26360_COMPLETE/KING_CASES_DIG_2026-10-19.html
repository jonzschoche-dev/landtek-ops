What is new, in order of value.

  1. Princess was recorded in the barangay blotter on 19 May 2020 saying "May usapin pa yan." Entry 2763, page 43 of the San Roque blotter book, certified by the Punong Barangay on 20 May 2020. In this case she swears: "Sa buong panahon ng aking paninirahan doon, wala akong naranasang pagtutol mula sa sinuman" (her affidavit, S11). ✓
  2. Jomil swore in 2020 that he was acting "on instructions of the owner of the property in the person of Gloria Balane thru Nelly Juntilla." His counter-affidavit before the prosecutor, as recorded in the joint resolution of 28 July 2020. In this case the Answer lists him to testify to "the absence of any notice of dispute prior to the filing of the case." ✓
  3. The prosecutor's ruling that ownership was undecided was mailed to the respondents. The 16 September 2020 resolution is copy-furnished to "Complainant and Respondents" by registry receipts 1666 to 1670 and 1694, all stamped 23 September 2020. Jomil and Juntilla were respondents. ✓
  4. Gloria's side claimed the land as owner in writing from 11 February 2020. Juntilla attached a letter "from their counsel, dated February 11, 2020," telling the Kings not to build "as the owner now needs the lot for her own use"; a confrontation before the Punong Barangay followed on 11 May 2020. ✓ The letter itself and its author are not in any file we hold.
  5. In 2020 the Balane side dated its possession from the 2016 sale. The prosecutor recorded that Gloria bought by "a Deed of sale executed by Cesar M. DeLa Fuente as the attorney in fact of the owner Mary W. Keesey on September 2016 and since then they allegedly were in possession of the property as they were paying taxes thereon." Their Answer here puts the family on the land as renters "since 1975." ✓
  6. Another family's house stood on the parcel when Gloria bought. The prosecutor describes the parcel as the land "in which the house purchased by Spouses Donata & Pepe King was built." Ruby Ferrer sold that house, "Kasama na rin … ang karapatan o Rights Ng Lupa," to the Kings on 18 May 2015, sixteen months before the deed. ✓ A buyer in good faith must look into the rights of others in possession. [COUNSEL]
  7. The Ferrer house stood "sa pagitan nina Gng. Nieves de Leon at Gng. Jocelyn Estayo," facing the Mercedes to Daet road. A De Leon household is next to the compound's road frontage. Whether Nieves de Leon is Shirley's family is for Shirley to say. ✓ [ASK HER]
  8. None of this appears anywhere in the defendants' papers here. A keyword search of their Answer and the judicial affidavits of Princess, Erwin, Salvador and Gloria finds no mention of King, Donata, Juntilla's 2020 complaints, Aquino, the prosecutor, the guards, the 2020 demolition, Crim. 20-6030, the perjury case, Civil Case 8563 or the Court of Appeals. Juntilla appears once, in the Answer's verification.

4. Left alone, and why

  • Their filing quirks in the Court of Appeals: Jomil's verification signed "in Cozumel, Mexico" with a blank Makati jurat; a certification of no related case while Civil Case 8563 was pending; a 2021 jurat recorded as "Series of 2020." These belong to other courts and invite a sideshow. Not used.
  • The type-weight difference on the prosecutor's 16 Sep 2020 resolution. Seen on a photocopy of a certified machine copy only. No claim is made about it.
  • Gloria's citizenship in 2016 to 2021. Her own affidavit attaches a Canadian passport and a Philippine identification certificate under R.A. 9225 dated 12 March 2025; her title says "FILIPINO CITIZEN." The Constitution allows a former natural-born citizen to take private land "subject to limitations provided by law" (Art. XII §8), and R.A. 8179 allows up to "five thousand (5,000) square meters in the case of urban land … for business or other purposes." Her 2,587 square meters falls within that, so this does not void the sale. Noted only as a fact about her papers. Not pursued. [COUNSEL]
  • King's own interest in the land (the Ferrer house) stays in the witness pack as a cross risk to manage, not a point to press.
