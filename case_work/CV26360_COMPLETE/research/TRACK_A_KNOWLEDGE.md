### A. Judicial admissions of tenancy (in the record now, independent of Gloria's excluded JA)

**A1. The Answer admits the family paid monthly rent to the owners' administrator.**
- Source: doc 405 · `B2` · p5 (¶40); p6 (¶44(b))
- Verbatim: "they paid monthly rent to the previous administrator Ben Llamanzares through Manuel Violeta" (¶40); "they regularly paid rent to the previous administrator, Ben Llamanzares, through Manuel Violeta" (¶44(b)).
- In evidence: yes. A verified pleading; a judicial admission (Rule 129 §4) that needs no proof.
- Strength: 5
- Ramos: admitted on purpose. Rent to the owners' administrator is what makes Gloria a "qualified buyer" under the SPA (¶40), so he will call it proof of a long relationship, not of bad faith. Our reply: a lessee holds for the owner (Art. 525); a lessee cannot deny the lessor's title (Art. 1436); and a tenant who paid the owners' administrator for decades knew whose land it was. **NEEDS-COUNSEL-VERIFICATION** on how Art. 1436 applies when the tenant later buys from the agent.

**A2. The Answer admits the family occupied "while they were renting" until the land "was offered for them to purchase."**
- Source: doc 405 · `B2` · p6 (¶43)
- Verbatim: "From 1975 while they were renting the property, to the time that it was offered for them to purchase, and even years after the title has already been transferred to the defendants, no one from among the plaintiff and her siblings bothered to question defendants' possession and eventual ownership."
- In evidence: yes (verified Answer).
- Strength: 5
- Ramos: laches. The owners never objected for decades. Reply: the owners did not object because the occupant was their paying tenant. Silence toward a tenant is not acquiescence in a sale.

**A3. The defense offers Exhibit 5 "to prove rents paid," conceding that the receipts include rent.**
- Source: doc 405 · `B2` · p10 (¶61(e))
- Verbatim: "Exhibit '5' series Receipts titled 'Heirs of Mary W. Keesey Estate' and/or issued by Cesar M. de la Fuente or Salvador Osum dela Fuente, to prove rents paid and the installment or partial payments made by Gloria Balane, Efren Balane, and/or their family for the subject property"
