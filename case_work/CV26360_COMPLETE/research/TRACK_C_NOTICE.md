## Position (read this first)

1. **The agency link is now proved from their own pleading.** The defendants' verified Answer says Gloria is "herein represented by NELLY H. JUNTILLA … by virtue of a Special Power of Attorney dated May 28, 2020" (C1). The same agent and the same SPA ran the 2020–2021 King litigation over this parcel. That closes the "is Juntilla really Gloria's agent?" gap in the bad-faith map (C1/C2 there).
2. **Notice chain, all before 23 Nov 2021:** Aug 2019, written revocation notice copied to Atty. Belen, who later handled Gloria's transfer (C12–C14). May 2020, Gloria's own SPA identifies the land by the heirs' tax declaration, owners "HRS. OF MARY WORRICK KEESEY", California address (C2). Jun–Sep 2020, prosecutor's record of a live ownership dispute run through Juntilla (C4–C5). Sep–Oct 2020, publication (C11). **9 Jul 2021, King's Verified Answer, stamped received by the Aquino Law Office (counsel for Juntilla and Jomil), states Cesar "was stripped of" authority "way back in 2005" and attaches the Bicol Post pages (C7–C8).** That is 137 days before registration.
3. **The hinge is law, not fact:** whether notice to Juntilla (agent under a litigation-and-administration SPA) or to her lawyer is notice to Gloria. NEEDS-COUNSEL-VERIFICATION. Without it, C7 is still strong impeachment of "no notice of dispute" (C18, C19).
4. **Two hurts to manage:** the prosecutor's 2020 resolutions call Gloria "the landowner" and the restraint "a form of an exercise of a lawful right" (C4, C5). And the Bicol Post notice names only T-4497, not T-52540 or Gloria (C11).
5. **No document found from any Balane to Jonathan, Patricia, Barandon or the heirs before suit,** other than Erwin's 5 Feb 2026 letter (C16). No reply to the 18 Aug 2025 demand found (C15).
