## Position (read first)

1. **The "second version" of the deed does not exist.** Defense Exhibit 4 (trial-brief D2) is **Rosalina Hansol's 1999 deed** under the 1991 SPA: ₱74,100 for 494 sqm. It is not a re-execution of the Balane sale. Every copy of the Balane deed in hand carries the same notarial numbers (Doc 304 / Page 61 / Book CXVI / s. 2016, Asis), the same signatures and the same text (finding 3.1–3.3). **Drop "serial re-execution" from every product.** The D2 file label is wrong.
2. **The strongest new indicators are on the registration side:**
   - The LRA subdivision plan was **surveyed 8 Sep 2020 and approved 6 Apr 2021**, "made for GERALDINE K. HOPPE, ET AL.", and certifies the survey was done "with the full knowledge and consent of the owner(s)". Yet the Partition-Subdivision Agreement that uses this plan is dated **15 Mar 2017** (finding 6.1–6.3).
   - The same notary's register numbers run backwards: the partition is Doc **402** (15 Mar) and the confirmation is Doc **115** (28 Mar), both in **Book CXXII** (finding 5.3).
   - Elevado pulled the RD copy of the 1992 SPA on **12–16 Aug 2021**, a month **before** Gloria appointed her on 15 Sep 2021 (finding 8.3).
3. **Already in evidence:** Exhibit B and the defense's own Exhibits 1, 5 and 6 carry most of the chain:
   - the loss affidavit 2 days before the deed
   - the court duplicate
   - payments continuing after the deed
   - June 2020 taxes paid **with surcharge and interest**, by the Province's own computation, which proves the deed sat unpresented for about 3.7 years (finding 7.3)
   - the 2020 eCAR under cancelled T-38838
   - registration 5 years 1 month after the deed, and 4 years 5 months after Cesar's death
4. **What hurts us** is collected at the end (H1–H8). The worst items:
   - the incomplete CTCs we filed as Exhibits H and E
   - Patricia's own 2023 affidavit of loss entered on cancelled T-38838 **and** on T-2021002127, the title created by the partition we attack
   - the finality of the Spec. Proc. 2156 order
