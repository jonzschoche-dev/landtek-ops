## 0. Bottom line

1. **We hold six signed writings by her.** Four are in 26-360 (6 Feb, 11 Mar, 12 Mar and 4 Sep 2026) and two are in Crim. Case 20-6030 (27 Nov 2020 and 6 Jan 2021). We also hold one notice the Clerk of Court issued in her name (24 Apr 2026). **We do not hold the rulings that matter most:** the 13 May 2026 pre-trial order that excluded Gloria Balane's JA, any ruling on the JA-admission motion (doc 1088), any ruling on our Summary Judgment motion (doc 393), the 28 Aug 2026 order, and the mediation or JDR referral.
2. **What her writing shows:** she names the governing rules at the outset, sets hard deadlines, gives a one-time indulgence with an express warning, and writes short paragraphs in English that end with concrete operative directives. In 7 writings she cites no jurisprudence. In her only reasoned order (6 Jan 2021) she cites the statute and rule by section and works rule → penalty arithmetic → disposition.
3. **She has never written "Summary Procedure" in this case.** Her 6 Feb order says the case is governed by the Amended Rules of Civil Procedure, the Revised Rules on Evidence and the Rules on Electronic Filing (doc 423 p.1). The spine and the defense (doc 1088 ¶2) both say Summary Procedure. Do not use that label in any filing until counsel confirms it.
4. **Record intersection.** Defendant Jomil Torralba was an accused before her in Crim. 20-6030, and our 11 Nov witness Donata M. King was the private complainant there (doc 1099; doc 581 pp.80–81). This is a fact about the witness and the parties, not about the judge. Counsel should expect the defense to use the 20-6030 history to attack King's credibility (§5).

6. **Disposition:** "WHEREFORE, premises considered, for having been prematurely filed, this case is REMANDED to the Camarines Norte Provincial Prosecution Office for appropriate action and the warrants issued for the arrest of herein accused are hereby RECALLED."

**Notes:** She paraphrases the rule rather than block-quoting it. She cites no jurisprudence. She reverses her own 27 Nov 2020 warrants on a rule-based ground (40 days later). ## 3. Profile, strictly from her text

**(a) Procedural strictness**
- She announces the governing rules on her own initiative: "let it be known to the parties that this case is governed by the Amended Rules of Civil Procedure, the Revised Rules on Evidence, and the Rules on Electronic Filing" (423 p.1).
- She grants indulgence once, says so, and ends it: "Liberally applying the rules of procedure … No further liberality shall be accorded." (417)
- She warns about sanctions for form defects: "further filing of incorrect pleadings which in effect disrupt court processes shall warrant appropriate sanction" (417).
- She requires strict compliance and names the e-filing rules: "strictly compliant with the Rules of Civil Procedure, specifically the amendments thereto, as well as the Rules on Electronic Filing" (418).
- Her calendars are hard: "final and intransferable unless the ground for resetting is allowed under the rules" (4 Sep order). She records compliance: "Noting both counsels timely compliance" (4 Sep).
- The notice issued in her name states waiver consequences: "The failure without just cause of a party and counsel to bring the evidence required at the pre-trial shall be deemed a waiver of the presentation of such evidence" (392 p.2). This tracks Rule 18 and is clerk-issued.
- She will raise a procedural defect of her own case and undo her own act: "for having been prematurely filed, this case is REMANDED … the warrants … are hereby RECALLED" (581 p.80).

**(b) Documentary vs. testimonial evidence.** Her own writings say almost nothing on this. The only line is "After personally examining the complainant and her witnesses, the Court finds probable cause" (1099). **NOT FOUND:** any writing by her on JA form, originals vs. copies, or exhibit admission. The JA exclusion is known only from the parties (see (f)).

**(c) Jurisprudence.** None cited in any of the 7 writings. She cites statutes and rules by section, paragraph and promulgation date ("Section 72 of Republic Act 10951 which was promulgated on August 29, 2017"; "Paragraph 2, Section 1, Rule 112 of the 2019 Amended Rules of Court", 581 p.80).

**(d) Register and style**
- **Language:** English only; no Filipino in any writing.
- **Paragraphs and sentences:** paragraphs are mostly one sentence. Sentences are short, except one long "Considering that … and considering further that …" chain in her reasoned order.
- **Fixed formulas:** "Before this court is …", "In today's hearing, …", "WHEREFORE, …", "SO ORDERED.", "Given this __ day of __ in Mercedes, Camarines Norte."
- **Numbers:** written in words and figures ("THIRTY (30) CALENDAR DAYS"; "One Hundred Eighty Thousand Pesos (P180,000.00)").
- **Operative detail:** exact names, receipt numbers, dates and payee (581 p.80).
- **Tone:** impersonal; no adjectives about the parties.

**(e) Foreign or absent parties and representatives**
- She adopts the plaintiff's representation caption as filed: "PATRICIA KEESEY ZSCHOCHE herein represented by her Attorney-in-fact JONATHAN PAUL ZSCHOCHE" (423, 417, 418, 4 Sep).
- She checks who has authority to act: "Atty. Karlo M. Dialogo, albeit his presence is noted, has no authority to prosecute this case" (581 p.80).
- The notice in her name requires written, full authority for representatives: "A representative, through a special power of attorney, may appear on behalf of a party, but shall be fully authorized in writing to enter into an amicable settlement, to submit to alternative modes of dispute resolution, and to enter into stipulations or admission of facts and documents." It also says non-appearance is excused "only for acts of God, force majeure, or duly substantiated physical inability" (392 pp.1–2).

## 4. Write-for-this-court rules
Each rule is tied to her own words. The rules are [INFERENCE] as to effect, but every anchor is quoted.

1. **Lead with the rule by section, then apply it, then pray.** Anchor: 581 p.80 (statute → Rule 112 §1 → "Considering that …" → WHEREFORE). For the memorandum, each section should run: rule (Art. 1878 Civil Code / Rule 35 / JA Rule §10, pinpointed), then exhibit-tagged facts, then "considering that", then relief.
2. **Make the statute or rule the backbone, not case law.** Anchor: zero cases in 7 writings; she cites by "Section 72 of Republic Act 10951 which was promulgated on August 29, 2017". Use Art. 1878 and Rule 35 (the provisions our SJ motion already invokes) as the spine. Cite only the few SC cases that decide the point, each with G.R. number, date and the one holding sentence. *Bautista-Spille* (G.R. 214057) is the anchor. *Yoshizaki* is not in our library (spine §6 item 13h), so do not cite it unverified.
3. **One point per paragraph, in short declarative English.** Anchor: 417 and 418 are three one-sentence paragraphs each. Do not use Filipino in filings except quoted exhibit text, with a translation.
4. **Name the governing rules the way she did, and do not say "Summary Procedure" unless counsel confirms it.** Anchor: "governed by the Amended Rules of Civil Procedure, the Revised Rules on Evidence, and the Rules on Electronic Filing" (423). Calling a document a "summary procedure" filing invites a mislabeling correction.
5. **Make e-filing form perfect: caption, transmittal, service, explanation.** Anchor: "strictly compliant with the Rules of Civil Procedure … as well as the Rules on Electronic Filing" (418) and "incorrect pleadings which in effect disrupt court processes shall warrant appropriate sanction" (417). Copy her caption exactly, including "079-2021002126" and "ARP NO. GR-2023-II-07-001-00256".
6. **Treat every date as final, and file compliance on time with proof.** Anchors: "final and intransferable unless the ground for resetting is allowed under the rules" and "Noting both counsels timely compliance" (4 Sep). Any resetting request must name the rule ground in its first sentence.
7. **Expect one indulgence at most, and ask for it plainly.** Anchor: "Liberally applying the rules of procedure … No further liberality shall be accorded" (417). If a supplemental JA or errata (Jonathan's) needs leave, ask once, in one paragraph. State the valid reason, the absence of prejudice and readiness to pay the JA Rule §10 fine. Do not argue "substantial justice" at length.
8. **Prove authority on the face of every appearance.** Anchors: "has no authority to prosecute this case" (581 p.80) and the SPA must be "fully authorized in writing" (392 p.2). Every plaintiff filing and the offer of evidence should identify the SPA by which Jonathan acts and its exhibit mark. Use the apostilled broad SPA if counsel uses it, and flag the guardianship status if it bears on authority. Do not leave authority to inference.
9. **Write the prayer as an operative order she can adopt word for word.** Anchor: her WHEREFORE paragraphs carry exact receipt numbers, amounts, bank branch and payee (581 p.80). Pray with exact particulars:
   - declare the 29 Sep 2016 Deed of Absolute Sale (Exh. H) void;
   - direct the Registry of Deeds of Camarines Norte to cancel TCT No. 079-2021002126;
   - direct the Assessor to cancel ARP No. GR-2023-II-07-001-00256;
   - order the defendants to vacate the 2,587 sqm lot, identified by technical description or exhibit.
10. **Write numbers in words and figures; write dates in full.** Anchor: "THIRTY (30) CALENDAR DAYS"; "One Hundred Eighty Thousand Pesos (P180,000.00)".
11. **Keep the fact section to one-sentence facts, each tagged to an exhibit and page.** Anchor: her recitals carry only the facts the disposition needs: who appeared, the penalty and the receipts (581 p.80).
12. **Offer of evidence:** for each exhibit, state the purpose in one line and what it proves, in the form the notice demanded ("The documents or other object evidence to be marked, stating the purpose thereof", 392 p.1). Never cite a draft (doc 396 / B4 is a draft). Correct our own misstatements before she finds them: Complaint ¶50 (SPA annotation date), Complaint ¶25 ("September 24, 2016"), and 1087 ¶16 (Answer "filed April 1").
