The witnesses, in the court's own form

Civil Case No. 26-360, Zschoche v. Balane, MTC Mercedes · 19 October 2026 · internal; for Jonathan and counsel; nothing filed or sent.
The court's writings in hand cite statutes by article and section and run rule → "Considering that" → WHEREFORE, with exact particulars (playbook §5.1). The ten questions and their articles are those already set out in the War of the Statutes (§0 and §12). This document asks one thing of each witness: which question does the testimony answer, under which article's words, and what line does it earn in an order written in that form? The italic "Considering that" lines are our drafting in that form, for counsel's memorandum and decision table. They are not a prediction of any ruling. Tags: [VERIFY] not yet on a certified copy · [COUNSEL] counsel's call.

Read this first.

  1. Five of the ten questions are answered by documents alone. Jurisdiction (Q1), the written authority (Q2), void or unenforceable (Q4), revocation (Q5) and the partition (Q7) turn on Exhibits B, E, F, G and H, the Registry entries, and the articles. No witness on either side can move them. Keep every witness off them.
  2. The class question (Q3) is answered by their own witnesses. Their Answer (¶40) says the family "paid monthly rent." Salvador says he was sent to collect "mga bayad sa upa" (S19). Princess says "noong una ay nirerentahan lang ito ng aking magulang" (S7). While Gloria's affidavit stays excluded, their only proof of a Llamanzares purchase is what Salvador says Llamanzares told him and Cesar around 1992 (S14 to S15), with no contract to sell and no Llamanzares receipt. If her affidavit is admitted, she gives a first-hand account of three ₱1,000 payments to Violeta, without receipt (S21 to S22); it is answered by her own Answer (¶40, "monthly rent") and her own 2005 "Down payment" receipt.
  3. Awareness under Article 1898 is in their verified Answer. "Gloria checked the SPA" before and during the transfer (¶44[h]). Paragraph 7 is in that SPA. Whoever read the SPA read the limits. This holds even if Gloria's own affidavit stays out.
  4. Good faith until 23 November 2021 (Q6) is answered by dated facts, not opinions. They come from Princess's and Jomil's own record in the King cases and from Mrs. King's 2021 pleading. Article 528 asks only whether "facts exist"; the dates do the work.
  5. Improvements, fruits and damages (Q8, Q9) need dates and amounts. Who built what and when; which rooms were rented; when the demand was made. Exemplary damages need a compensatory or temperate award first (Art. 2234).
