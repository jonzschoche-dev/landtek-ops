# Research brief: a balanced, adversarial review of Zschoche v. Balane (Civil Case No. 26-360, MTC Mercedes, Camarines Norte, Philippines)

You are asked for an independent, balanced opinion from a Philippine civil-litigation standpoint. Treat the attached pack (01_CASE_PACK) as the plaintiff side's internal working file: it contains the strategy, the statute map, the evidence audit, the witness plans, the trap map, the defense's modelled path to victory, and drafts that have not been filed. Assume the defense is competent and will read the same record.

## The case in five lines

1. Three absent co-owners (heirs of Mary Worrick Keesey, in California) gave an agent, Cesar de la Fuente, a Special Power of Attorney on 16 March 1992. Paragraph 7 allowed him to sell the land only to "third persons in possession of CONTRACTS TO SELL executed by the previous administrator, MR. BEN LLAMANZARES and/or to those who have paid their down-payments or have made partial payments therefor to the latter ... after prior consultation with us." The SPA was annotated on the title in June 1996.
2. The owners revoked the agency on 15 August 2005 (consular instrument); the revocation was never annotated; it was published in a newspaper in September–October 2020.
3. On 29 September 2016 the agent sold a 2,587 sqm portion to Gloria Balane for ₱250,000 "in hand paid"; the buyers' own receipts show ₱602,750 paid to the agent's side from 2003 to February 2017, ₱219,000 of it after the deed. The buyers' own verified Answer says the family had occupied the portion as renters since 1975, paying rent to Llamanzares through his man Violeta, and pleads a ₱3,000 down payment to Llamanzares "in the 1980s" for which no paper exists.
4. The agent died in June 2017. A Partition-Subdivision Agreement dated 15 March 2017 (citing a survey plan made in September 2020) and a Deed of Confirmation signed by the agent alone were registered with the sale on 23 November 2021, and a title issued to Gloria Balane.
5. The heirs sued in February 2026 for reivindicacion, nullity of the deed, cancellation of the title, accounting and damages. Trial dates: plaintiff 14 Oct, 4 Nov, 11 Nov, 2 Dec, 9 Dec 2026; defense 13 Jan to 10 Mar 2027. A summary judgment motion filed in April 2026 has no ruling in the orders we hold.
