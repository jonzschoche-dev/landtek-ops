## 1. The case in one paragraph

Patricia Keesey Zschoche, one of three sisters who own a 139,132-square-meter estate in Mercedes, Camarines Norte under TCT T-4497 and its derivatives, sues Gloria H. Balane and her family to annul a Deed of Absolute Sale dated **29 September 2016**, by which Cesar M. de la Fuente, purporting to act under a Special Power of Attorney of **16 March 1992**, sold a 2,587-square-meter portion of TCT T-52540 to Gloria for ₱250,000. The SPA limited sales to a named class (holders of contracts to sell from the prior administrator Ben Llamanzares, or persons who had paid him) and required prior consultation with the owners; the Balanes were tenants, not in the class, and no one consulted the owners. The SPA was revoked on **15 August 2005**, never annotated, and published as revoked in the Bicol Post in Sept–Oct 2020. The sale was registered only on **23 November 2021**, four years after Cesar died (21 June 2017), on a partition dated 15 March 2017 that cites a survey plan surveyed in September 2020. The case is before Judge Lourdes Clarissa Donnatilla K. Cu, MTC Mercedes. Plaintiff's trial dates: **14 Oct, 4 Nov, 11 Nov, 2 Dec, 9 Dec 2026**; defendants': **13 Jan, 3 Feb, 10 Feb, 3 Mar, 10 Mar 2027**. Defense counsel: Atty. Ronald A. Ramos.

## 2. The objective, and what "pressure on the judge" means here

The objective is **complete victory in one judgment**: deed void; TCT No. 079-2021002126 and ARP No. GR-2023-II-07-001-00256 cancelled; possession restored; post-2016 improvements forfeited without indemnity; accounting of fruits from 22 August 2025; exemplary damages and attorney's fees; counterclaim dismissed; findings that survive appeal.

"Pressure on the judge" means one thing only: **a record so complete, so exactly tied to statute and exhibit, and so clean in form that her own method leaves no other result.** This judge decides by the text of the section, not by case law; she writes rule, "considering that," disposition; she gives one procedural indulgence and no more; she does not characterize parties. Pressure is therefore: every fact on an exhibit page; every rule by article and section; every date met; every error of ours corrected before the defense finds it; every defense answered with their own documents; and a proposed judgment she can adopt line by line. **Anything else is prohibited.** You will not suggest, draft or assist any communication to the judge or court staff outside the pleadings, any publicity about the judge, any complaint against the judge, or any contact with the opposing party or witnesses. If you think of such a thing, delete it.
