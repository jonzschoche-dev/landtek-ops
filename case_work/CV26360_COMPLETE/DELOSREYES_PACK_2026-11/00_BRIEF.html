The decision first: call her, or not. [Counsel's call]

  • What she adds: that Efren and Gloria "dati rati na nagtutuyo ng mga isda" on the land (Q4), and that she has lived in San Roque since her birth on 5 June 1979 (Q5). Use of the land, not a purchase; it fits their own Answer (¶40: renters since 1975), which already says as much.
  • What her affidavit risks: Q2 to Q3 ("maimpluwensiyang mga tao"), Q4 and Q8 (what "kasama na ang mga defendants" knew), Q7 second sentence (she learned of the sale "kay Jonathan at sa mga dokumentong ipinakita niya"), and Q9 ("malamang na sinamantala … ang koneksyon at impluwensiya nila sa gobyerno"). Each invites a motion to strike, and Q9 would be read back to the court on cross.
  • Her history with the defendants: she was named "Francia de Villa" as a respondent in Nelly Juntilla's 2020 robbery and grave threats complaint over the tires (dismissed); she was Mrs. King's co-defendant in Civil Case 8563 (captioned once as "Francia Delos Santos"); she is the friend who called Mrs. King on 27 May 2020.
  • If she is not called, her affidavit is simply not considered (JA Rule §10[b]), so the risky lines never come before the court. Nothing in our case depends on her.
  • If she is called, keep direct to Q1, Q4 (fish-drying, without the "kasama na ang mga defendants" clause), Q5, Q6 and the first sentence of Q7 ("Hindi ko po alam kung papaano nakapagtayo ng bahay doon ang mga defendants"); withdraw Q2 to Q3, the rest of Q7, Q8 and Q9 at the start of her direct.

2. Questions for counsel to ask her in the briefing

Counsel asks; she answers in her own words; nothing is suggested to her.

A. So that cross holds no surprises

  1. Her full name and civil status: "de los Reyes," "de Villa," "Delos Santos"; Pancho De Villa. Which is right, and why do her 2020 and 2021 papers differ?
  2. Juntilla's 2020 robbery and grave threats complaint naming "Francia de Villa": was it her, what was it about, how did it end?
  3. Civil Case 8563: is it pending, decided or settled?
  4. The Bicol Post: where and when did she get it, and to whom did she give it?
  5. Has Jonathan or anyone for the plaintiff shown her documents or talked with her about her testimony? (Her Q7 says she learned of the sale from Jonathan.)

B. Facts we do not yet have

  1. The fish-drying: which years, how often, on which part of the land, and whether it was before the present house.
  2. When the Balane house was built or rebuilt, if she saw it.
  3. Did she ever see or hear, first-hand, the Balanes pay rent or anything else for the lot, and to whom?
  4. The survey of the Balane lot that went to the barangay: did she see it, when, who directed it, and is there a blotter certification?
  5. What she saw herself on 27 and 30 May 2020 (for counsel's knowledge; it is not in her affidavit).
  6. Have the room rentals been visible to her: since when, to whom?

C. Papers to bring

  1. Her PhilID (Exh. P).
  2. Any barangay certification, photograph or letter about the land or the 2020 incidents.

3. Don't-says

  • "Malamang," "sinamantala," "impluwensiya," "koneksyon"; nothing about the defendants' positions in government.
  • Nothing learned from Jonathan; nothing about the Registry, the SPA or Cesar.
