**J2. ¶(7): exemplary damages of ₱200,000.**
- Art. 2232 (VERIFIED) requires that "the defendant acted in a wanton, fraudulent, reckless, oppressive, or malevolent manner." Art. 2234 (VERIFIED) requires that "the plaintiff must show that he is entitled to moral, temperate or compensatory damages before the court may consider the question of whether or not exemplary damages should be awarded."
- The draft awards no moral, temperate or compensatory damages, and its "considering" paragraphs contain no Art. 2232 finding. A judge who "does not characterize parties" will not write "fraudulent" without a dated basis.
- Fix:
  - (a) Add a compensatory or temperate head that is proved: the accounting of fruits from August 22, 2025 is compensatory in kind; or the actual expenses of the demand and registry certifications, with receipts.
  - (b) Add a "considering" paragraph that reaches Art. 2232's word "reckless" or "oppressive" from dated documents only: deed reciting "in hand paid" vs Receipt No. 0228 "Full payment" of February 2017; partition of March 15, 2017 citing a plan surveyed September 8, 2020.
  - (c) Confirm the amount is pleaded and within jurisdiction. The playbook note "counsel to confirm" stands.
