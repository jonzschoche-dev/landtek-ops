The Balanes' path to victory, and how we close it

Civil Case No. 26-360 · 16 October 2026 · standalone study document, for Jonathan and counsel · written as Atty. Ramos would plan it · read with the trap map in the memorandum pack (Annex A) · Part 6 is reserved for the transcript of Jonathan's testimony of 14 October.

Their best path in one paragraph. They win if the court believes Gloria paid Ben Llamanzares a down payment in the 1980s, which would put her inside the paragraph 7 class, and if our proof that no owner was consulted never gets in. Gloria's affidavit could carry the first, if the court admits it. The second comes if Patricia's rebuttal is excluded or her standing is attacked. Then the sale looks like one the SPA allowed, made under an annotated power with no revocation on the title. Everything else they have either buys time, saves money, or waits on our mistakes.

The two cards that matter: the 1980s down payment, and Patricia. We close both before trial without revealing anything we hold.

Assume they will manufacture what they lack. Their own papers already point to the vehicles. Gloria's excluded affidavit (S20 to S24) says the 1980s payment was written in Manuel Violeta's "brown columnar book," which went "to Cesar and then the Agcaoili Law Office." That is the plaintiff's own lawyers. The Answer's side also claims rights "from a certain Kenneth Asistin Jr." Part 3 maps the cast they could produce, the gates they must pass, and the fixed facts that break an invented story.

3. The manufactured case: how invented witnesses get in, and how they break

3.1 The gates they must pass

Their witnesses and exhibits were fixed at pre-trial. Anything new must get through a gate, and each gate is a ruling this judge makes strictly.

  • A witness not in their pre-trial brief. Parties are bound by their pre-trial briefs. Evidence-in-chief not identified at pre-trial is barred "except if allowed by the court for good cause shown" (A.M. No. 03-1-09-SC; counsel to confirm the official text). Objection: the facts were known to them since 1975, so there is no good cause; and the court wrote "No further liberality shall be accorded."
  • A late or supplemental judicial affidavit. Allowed only once, for a valid reason, without undue prejudice, and with a fine (JA Rule §10[a]). Oppose every request: the story was available in April 2026.
  • Gloria's excluded affidavit. Its admission motion has been pending since 19 May 2026, and it carries the Violeta-ledger story. Our Comment-Opposition stands.
  • A "newly discovered" paper (a Llamanzares contract, the ledger, a letter "from the owners"). It was not pre-marked, so it needs leave. Object, and demand the original and its custody history: who held it, where, since when, and why it is surfacing now.
  • Sur-rebuttal after Patricia. They may ask to answer her with a new "consultation" witness. Sur-rebuttal is the court's grace, not a right. Object to anything beyond what Patricia actually said.
  • Stretching an existing witness on re-direct. Re-direct is limited to matters raised on cross. Object to new matter.

3.3 The anchors to fix now, quietly

An invented story fails on dates it cannot know in advance. Each anchor below is fixed now, before any new witness appears, and none needs a court filing.

  • Benjamin Llamanzares: date of death (PSA), and the dates of his administration.
  • Salvador dela Fuente: PSA birth certificate (his affidavit gives no age); the earliest genuine "Von" signature is 25 October 2003; the comparison set of his signatures is Exh. 5 itself.
  • Manuel Violeta: whether he is alive, his date of death if not, and his role. Ask the family and its Makati counsel whether the "brown columnar book" exists, and if it does, what it actually shows. It may list the Balanes as renters.
  • Kenneth Asistin Jr.: who he is, his age, where he lived, and any declaration or instrument in his name.
  • The genuine Llamanzares paper: the 1977 Deeds of Final Sale annotated on T-4497, as the benchmark of form, notary and wording.
  • The receipt books: the printer's imprint and any authority to print, which fixes the earliest possible date of a genuine receipt.
  • Cesar in 2016 and 2017: where he was and his condition, from the Spec. Proc. 2156 record and his death certificate.
  • Patricia's whereabouts: her passport entries for any date a witness says she approved something in person.
  • Gloria's whereabouts: in Mercedes through her council terms to 2017; in Toronto by May 2020 (the consular SPA).
  • The closed list: the defense witnesses named in the pre-trial order and in Answer ¶59. Anyone else needs leave.

3.4 Breaking an invented witness who gets in

  1. Gate first. Object on the record before any testimony: not in the pre-trial brief, no good cause, prejudice.
  2. Lock checkable details. Ask for the date, the amount, the place, who was present, what paper changed hands, who kept it, and when the witness last saw it. Short questions, one fact each.
  3. Prior silence. Ask why the witness is absent from the verified Answer, the pre-trial brief, and the three defense affidavits of April 2026.
  4. Interest. Ask about kinship, tenancy under the Balanes, and employment by a municipality where Princess is a councilor and Erwin the Municipal Engineer.
  5. Do not confront on cross with the anchors. Let the details stand; the anchors land on rebuttal.
  6. Never call it fabrication in court. The dates do that. Remedies for false testimony are counsel's decision after judgment, and are never used as a threat.

3.5 The pattern already on their own papers

The court can see this without our saying a word:

  • receipts whose serial numbers run out of order;
  • a deed reciting "in hand paid" against receipts that run to February 2017;
  • a partition dated before its own plan existed;
  • a 2020 tax clearance (eCAR) naming a title cancelled in 1999;
  • the agent's loss affidavit signed two days before the deed.

An invented witness who appears late fits that pattern. We present the pattern as dates, never as accusation.

4. Where they can genuinely win, ranked

  1. The 1980s down payment is believed and our consultation proof is shut out. This is the only path that defeats the class leg. It needs Gloria admitted, credible, and unrebutted. Close it with hearsay objections to Salvador and Erwin, a document-by-document cross of Gloria, and Patricia keyed to whatever Gloria says.
  2. Patricia is cast as incapable through the guardianship papers. That would weaken Exh. A and her rebuttal at once. The petition cannot be changed now, so the answer must be ready: the petition alleges, it does not find; its ground is absence and inability to attend in person; her sworn acts of 2023 and 2025, and her own voice on rebuttal, show a competent owner.
  3. Jurisdiction. It is not likely to succeed, but it is fatal if it does, and it can be raised on appeal. Keep the authority verified.
  4. Money and time. Retention and a long appeal keep them in possession and collecting rent. Answer it with the fruits and a damages figure on the record.
  5. Joinder. This only becomes a risk if the court orders it and we are not ready.

5. Quiet moves now that close or blunt their paths

  1. Guardianship: know the exact words, prepare the answer. Obtain the petition as filed and any order issued in it, and read their exact wording. Prepare the competence answer above. Keep Patricia's affidavit consistent with the petition: she lives abroad and acts through her son, which is what the petition says. Nothing is said about it in this case unless Ramos raises it.
  2. Hearsay objections, scripted. For Salvador and Erwin on anything before they were personally involved: "Objection, no personal knowledge; the witness was not present." This reveals nothing.
  3. Cesar's heirs identified, with an address for each, in case the court orders joinder.
  4. Jurisdiction authority verified and kept in counsel's file.
  5. Gloria's cross finished before the court rules on her affidavit, so her admission is never a surprise.
  6. The anchors in Part 3.3 fixed, starting with Llamanzares, Violeta and the "brown columnar book."
  7. No contact between Jonathan and any occupant or defense witness from now on; any approach to our witnesses written down and reported to counsel.
