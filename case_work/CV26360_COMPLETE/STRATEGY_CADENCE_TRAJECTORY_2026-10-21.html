3. The one cadence decision that matters now

When do the Registry and notarial records come in? Counsel's plan (21 Oct): subpoenas "once we have completed the presentation of all our available witnesses and exhibits, or during rebuttal." The second chair's flag (deadlines C2): records that prove our own case belong in the case-in-chief, because rebuttal is for "rebutting evidence only" (Rule 30 §5[f]) and unmarked documents need the court's leave (A.M. No. 03-1-09-SC).

  • Why it matters less than it looks. The documents-only case for Art. 1898 needs none of them; everything it uses is already marked or is in their Answer and exhibits.
  • Where it does matter. The partition ground (the agreement itself, who signed, the true entry date in the notary's register). If it is to be a ground in our own case, the records should be subpoenaed returnable before 9 December, with leave to mark them. If they are used only to answer the Answer's ¶46 ("the partition is regular") after the defense presents it, rebuttal is the place.
  • Ask counsel which (in Note 2, with the Pre-Trial Order question).
