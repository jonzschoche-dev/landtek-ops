MOTION FOR PRODUCTION AND INSPECTION OF DOCUMENTS
(Rule 27, Rules of Court)

PLAINTIFF, through counsel, respectfully states:

  1. Section 1 of Rule 27 provides that "[u]pon motion of any party showing good cause therefor, the court in which an action is pending may (a) order any party to produce and permit the inspection and copying or photographing, by or on behalf of the moving party, of any designated documents, papers, books, accounts, letters, photographs, objects or tangible things, not privileged, which constitute or contain evidence material to any matter involved in the action and which are in his possession, custody or control."
  2. Plaintiff moves for the production, inspection and copying of the following documents, each of which is in the possession, custody or control of the defendants and is material to an issue the defendants themselves raised:
    1. Any contract to sell, receipt, ledger entry or other writing issued by Benjamin Llamanzares or Manuel Violeta to defendants Efren Balane or Gloria H. Balane concerning the subject lot. The Answer alleges a down payment to the previous administrator "recognized and later credited" (¶40), and the judicial affidavit of defendant Engr. Erwin H. Balane states that the purchase was made "first with Ben Llamanzares." These writings go to whether the defendants belong to the persons in whose favor the Special Power of Attorney of March 16, 1992 (Exhibit "E") authorized a sale.
    2. The originals of the receipts marked as Defendants' Exhibit "5", and the receipt books, including stubs or duplicates, from which they were issued. The order, dates and amounts of these receipts go to the defendants' allegation of payment before the deed of September 29, 2016.
    3. The records of rentals received from rooms or structures on the subject lot from August 22, 2025 to date. The Answer admits that rooms are rented (¶27). These records go to the accounting and to the fruits and damages prayed for.
    4. The building permits, plans, contracts, receipts and other writings showing the date and cost of construction of the improvements for which the defendants claim reimbursement and retention (Answer ¶49).
    5. The owner's duplicate certificate of Transfer Certificate of Title No. 079-2021002127, issued in the names of Geraldine K. Hoppe, Patricia K. Zschoche and Marcia Ellen Keesey upon the registration of November 23, 2021, if it is in the possession, custody or control of any of the defendants.
  3. Good cause exists. Each item is identified by the defendants' own pleading or affidavit. None is privileged. Each bears on an issue the defendants placed before the Court: membership in the authorized class, payment, the improvements, and the fruits. Production now, before the defendants present their evidence, avoids delay at trial and allows the documents to be examined before cross-examination.
  4. Plaintiff proposes that production be made at the office of the Clerk of Court, or at the office of undersigned counsel, within fifteen (15) days from receipt of the Court's order, at a time agreed by counsel, with leave to copy or photograph each document at plaintiff's expense.

WHEREFORE, plaintiff respectfully prays that the Court ORDER the defendants to produce and permit the inspection and copying or photographing of the documents described in paragraph 2(a) to (e), at the time, place and manner stated in paragraph 4 or as the Court may fix, and, as to any item not in their possession, custody or control, to so state under oath.

Other just and equitable reliefs are likewise prayed for.
