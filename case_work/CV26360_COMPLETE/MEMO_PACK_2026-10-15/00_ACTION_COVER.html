To Atty. Bonifacio T. Barandon, Jr. · from Jonathan Paul Zschoche, attorney-in-fact · 15 October 2026

Actions for counsel, in the order they unfold

The strategy (Annex A). The defense story is frozen in affidavits sworn on 1 April 2026, and most of the records that contradict it, they do not know we hold. We prove our case with the exhibits already marked, gather the rest quietly, let their witnesses commit on cross, and spring the records with my mother on rebuttal, when no new affidavit can repair their story.

No discovery motions, no custodians in our case-in-chief, nothing revealed before it can no longer be answered. A separate study document maps the Balanes' own path to victory, which runs through a claimed 1980s down payment to Llamanzares and an attack on my mother, and the moves that close it.
