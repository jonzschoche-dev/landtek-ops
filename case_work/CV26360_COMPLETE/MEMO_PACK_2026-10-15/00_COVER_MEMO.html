Consultation must be proved, and not by hearsay

Non-consultation is part of our claim, so it is ours to prove (Rule 131 §1). The buyer's burden on her own good faith (Bucton) is a backstop only. In our case-in-chief it rests on the notarial registers and Registry files (no owner appeared or signed); in the defense stage, on their own witnesses under cross. First-hand proof comes from my mother on rebuttal. The class limit does not depend on consultation, which is why it leads.

Damages need a peso figure

Exemplary damages require a temperate or compensatory award first (Art. 2234). No witness in our case gives a monthly rental value yet. On cross, Princess and Erwin are asked how many rooms are rented and for how much (Answer ¶27); their answer supplies it.

PRIORITY 4

What we do not argue, and why

  • Not that the SPA is void on its face. It is in writing, names the property, and gives a power to sell; my own affidavit (Q23) concedes Cesar could sign. The Supreme Court upheld a power built the same way in Tumibay v. Lopez (G.R. No. 171692, 3 June 2013) and voided the sale made outside its condition under Article 1898. That is our case.
  • Not that "the SPA never covered T-52540" as a stand-alone ground. Ramos answers it with the 1996 annotation and PD 1529 §59. Use it with the class: the deed names no title, and only the agent's own confirmation supplies the link (Art. 1910; Bautista-Spille).
  • Not that the subdivisions creating T-52540 were unauthorized. T-52540 is my family's own title and our Exhibit B.
  • Not that substitution was forbidden. Article 1892 permits it unless prohibited; the answer to the 2012 "delegation" is that nothing was left to delegate after 2005.
  • Not the Agcaoili letter as proof of 2016. It shows the family's position in 2019.
  • Nothing in the case-in-chief about the ARTA, Civil Service and Ombudsman referrals, the Garrido estate, the NBI, the Office of the President, "syndicate," or any adjective about a defendant or the court. The referrals are held for cross on bias only if a witness claims neutrality.
  • The 1991 Agcaoili SPA is no source of authority to sell. the 1991 SPA (Doc. No. 260, Page No. 53, Book No. I, Series of 1991, Makati, acknowledged before the Agcaoili notary) is in our own certified Registry file Ref. 2025001098, pp. 2 to 4, and in the Spec. Proc. 2156 record. It grants general control and supervision of the properties, payment of taxes, collection of money with power to sign receipts, and representation before the BIR, the Registry and DAR. It grants no power to sell. A power in general terms "comprises only acts of administration" (Art. 1877), and a sale needs a special power (Art. 1878[5]). If Ramos offers it, it explains only why Cesar collected rent and issued receipts: administration.

The court's own form. The orders in this case and the two in Crim. 20-6030 cite statutes and rules by section, cite no case law, proceed rule, then "considering that," then disposition, and state exact particulars (receipt numbers, amounts in words and figures). The 11 March 2026 order states: "No further liberality shall be accorded." The 6 February 2026 order calls the dates "final and intransferable." An earlier order found that a lawyer "has no authority to prosecute this case" where the authority was not on the record. Our filings follow that form: errors corrected by us before they are raised, complete exhibits, authority identified on every appearance, and every filing on time.
