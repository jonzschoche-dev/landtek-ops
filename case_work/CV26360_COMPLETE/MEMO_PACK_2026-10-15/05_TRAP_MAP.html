How this unfolds: the record, the lock-in, the trap

Civil Case No. 26-360 · 15 October 2026 · for counsel · replaces the plan to call records custodians in our case-in-chief on 2 December.

The essence. The defense story is already frozen. Salvador, Princess and Erwin swore their judicial affidavits on 1 April 2026, and changing them now needs the court's leave. Nearly every key claim in those affidavits is contradicted by a certified record, and most of those records the defense does not know we hold.

If we show the records now, we give them five months to explain, drop a witness, or ask leave to amend. If we hold them, their witnesses affirm their affidavits in January, commit to them on cross, and the records land on rebuttal, when no new affidavit can repair the story.

So: prove our case with what is already marked, gather the rest quietly now, lock them in on cross, and spring the records on rebuttal.

1. Why the rules allow it

  • The pre-marking bar covers only evidence-in-chief. "No evidence shall be allowed to be presented and offered during the trial in support of a party's evidence-in-chief other than those that had been earlier identified and pre-marked during the pre-trial, except if allowed by the court for good cause shown" (Guidelines on pre-trial, A.M. No. 03-1-09-SC). [Quoted from secondary reproductions; the official text was not reached. Counsel confirms.] Rebuttal and impeachment evidence fall outside it.
  • The bar also means the custodians do not belong in our case-in-chief. The Registry file, the notarial registers and the DENR file were never pre-marked. Calling them on 2 December would need "good cause" leave. That needs leave the court has said it will not readily give, and it shows our hand.
  • Rebuttal is "rebutting evidence only" (Rule 30 §5[f]). That is exactly what these records are, once a defense witness has sworn the claim they contradict.
  • Gloria's own papers are admissions of a party. They are evidence in their own right and need no foundation. A non-party witness's own prior statement, such as Salvador's 2025 letter, needs the Rule 132 §13 foundation on cross: show it, fix the date and place, and let him explain.

The class limit stands on pre-marked exhibits and the defendants' Answer:
