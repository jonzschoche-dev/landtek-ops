**Do not argue "no power to delegate".** Art. 1892 lets an agent appoint a substitute unless the principal prohibits it. The SPA is read as not prohibiting it [VERIFY the Exh. E text for any prohibition clause]. The point is framed in three parts below.

**Text** (Civil Code, LAW cc.txt, exact):

Such benefit to the creditor need not be proved in the following cases: (1) If after the payment, the third person acquires the creditor's rights; (2) If the creditor ratifies the payment to the third person; (3) If by the creditor's conduct, the debtor has been led to believe that the third person had authority to receive the payment."
