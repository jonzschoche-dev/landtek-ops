# REQUEST FOR ISSUANCE OF SUBPOENA DUCES TECUM (with proposed subpoenas)

Plaintiff, through counsel, respectfully requests issuance of the subpoenas duces tecum below, and states:

## I. Rule

1. **Rule 21, Section 1** [VERIFY 2019 text]: "Subpoena is a process directed to a person requiring him to attend and to testify at the hearing or the trial of an action … It may also require him to bring with him any books, documents, or other things under his control, in which case it is called a subpoena duces tecum."
2. **Rule 21, Section 3** [VERIFY 2019 text]: "A subpoena shall state the name of the court and the title of the action or investigation, shall be directed to the person whose attendance is required, and in the case of a subpoena duces tecum, it shall also contain a reasonable description of the books, documents or things demanded which must appear to the court prima facie relevant."
3. **Rule 21, Section 4** [VERIFY 2019 text]: the court may quash a subpoena duces tecum "if it is unreasonable and oppressive, or the relevancy of the books, documents or things does not appear, or if the person in whose behalf the subpoena is issued fails to advance the reasonable cost of the production thereof."
4. **Rule 21, Section 6** [VERIFY 2019 text]: service is to be made by tendering "the fees for one day's attendance and the kilometrage allowed by these Rules", and "[i]f the subpoena is duces tecum, the reasonable cost of producing the books, documents or things demanded shall also be tendered."

## II. Prima facie relevance

5. Defendants' Exhibit "5" consists of receipts from printed booklets headed "HEIRS OF MARY W. KEESEY ESTATE", numbered 0019 to 0228 within a printed series "0001-1250", and from generic receipt pads (Def. Exh. 5 pp.2–33).
6. Salvador Osum dela Fuente states that he collected the payments and issued the receipts in Exhibit "5" (Judicial Affidavit, Q&A 18–22). He also states that he turned the payments over to Cesar M. de la Fuente (Q&A 23).
7. The exhibit contains only some numbers of the series. For example, Nos. 0137 to 0216 do not appear, between No. 0136 (May 2016) and No. 0217 (August 2016) (p.30). The serial numbers and the dates do not run in the same order in several places (pp.26–33).
8. The complete booklets, their stubs or duplicates, their covers (which ordinarily show the printer and the printing authority), and any collection ledger bear directly on:
   - (a) when, and in what order, the receipts were issued;
   - (b) whether the series was used for other payors;
   - (c) the amounts and the character of the payments (rent or price);
   - (d) the turnover of the money.

   These are matters in issue on the payment defense and on the accounting claim.
