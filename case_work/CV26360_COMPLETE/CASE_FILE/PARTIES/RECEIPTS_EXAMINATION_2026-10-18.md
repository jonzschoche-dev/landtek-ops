## 1. Bottom line

- Def. Exh. 5 holds **111 receipts**: 110 on collection forms, plus 1 surveyor's receipt (p.1).
- The 110 collection receipts total **₱602,950** [VERIFY: sum of scan readings]. The deed price is ₱250,000 (DX6 p.1).
- **Nine receipts, totalling ₱219,000, are dated after 29 Sep 2016.** That is the date of the deed that recites ₱250,000 "in hand paid" (DX6 p.1). The last is No. 0228, Feb 2017, "Full payment for lot in San Roque, Mercedes C.N." (p.33).
- Most receipts carry no name of a collector. The estate forms (2007–2017) print the label **"Authorized Collector"** with no name (pp.9–11, 20, 23–33). No receipt names Cesar.
- Two signature forms appear. "Von dela Fuente" is written in cursive on 4 receipts, Oct 2003 to Apr 2005 (pp.2–3). A stylized "[?]dela Fuente" appears on all the others from Nov 2004 to Feb 2017.
- In visible form, **neither** signature resembles Cesar's 1991–2007 full-name signature or his 2016–2017 short mark (GROK_REVIEW/CESAR_SIGNATURE_COMPARISON pp.1–4). This is a scan observation only.
- Salvador's judicial affidavit says he collected the payments and issued these receipts (Def. JA Salvador, S18–S22, pp.4–5). It does not say who signed "Von dela Fuente" or the stylized form [VERIFY on cross].
- Serial numbers on the estate forms run out of date order at least 6 times (§4.3).
