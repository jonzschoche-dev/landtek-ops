## 2. Case-in-chief theory

The 1992 SPA is genuine, but it could not authorize this sale. Paragraph 7 allows a sale only to holders of Llamanzares contracts to sell, or to those who paid Llamanzares, and only "after prior consultation" (TL 16 Mar 1992; Exh. E; Def. Exh. 7). Gloria Balane was outside that class: the Answer pleads entry as renters in 1975 (CM row 3, Answer ¶¶40, 43, 44). No owner was consulted [Patricia, on rebuttal]. The owners revoked the SPA on 15 Aug 2005 (Exh. F), and the revocation was published on 25 Sep, 2 Oct and 9 Oct 2020 (Exh. G, G-1; Answer ¶18 admits publication) (TL). The revocation itself was never annotated on T-52540 (TL 15 Aug 2005; Exh. B). The lead argument does not depend on it.

**Rule.** Art. 1898: "If the agent contracts in the name of the principal, exceeding the scope of his authority, and the principal does not ratify the contract, it shall be void if the party with whom the agent contracted is aware of the limits of the powers granted by the principal…" (VA).

**Application.** The SPA was annotated on T-52540 on 21 Jun 1996 (Exh. B). Under PD 1529 §52, registration is "…constructive notice to all persons from the time of such registering, filing or entering" (VA; TL 21 Jun 1996). In August 2021, the buyer's processor, Karen Gay Elevado, pulled a certified copy of the full SPA, with ¶7 in it, before registration (Def. Exh. 7, p. 1 footer; TL 12–16 Aug 2021). The LRA reference is given as 2021004150 in TL and 2021004160 in the consultation memo [VERIFY on the exhibit].

**Disposition.** The deed of 29 Sep 2016 is void, and **TCT 079-2021002126** should be cancelled (case caption; SUB).
