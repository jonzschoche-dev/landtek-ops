## Verdict

**The argument is real, but as Jonathan framed it, it is mostly a premise problem.** The SPA (Exh. E) describes one property: the undivided 139,132 sq m parcel under T-4497. It names no other title and no lot. On its face it does not name T-52540 or Lot 2-X-6-I-4-C. Under Arts. 1874, 1878(5) and 1881, read strictly, that is a legitimate ground. **Three record facts blunt it as a stand-alone nullity ground:**

1. **T-52540 descends from T-4497.** Its p. 1 cites the same LRC Record No. 10784 the SPA cites (Exh. B p. 1; Exh. E CTC p. 2).
2. **The Registry carried the SPA onto T-52540.**
   - First annotation: 21 Jun 1996, "to sign, negotiate for the sale of the parcel of land in this title" (Exh. B p. 3).
   - Second annotation: Entry 2021003235, "with power to sign and negotiate for the sale of the land in this title" (Exh. B p. 5).
   - PD 1529 §59 *requires* carry-over of subsisting annotations to derivative titles.
3. **The owners' side has never attacked other sales of portions of the same lot made under the same SPA.** The 2005 Era sale of Lot 2-X-6-I-4-C-2 was registered under it (Exh. B p. 3).

**What survives, and is strong:**

- **The 2016 deed names no title at all.** It describes "Lot 2-X-6-I-4-C (portion), Psd-05-026197," 2,587 sq m. It invokes the SPA but does not state the ¶7 class or ¶7 consultation (Annex K p. 3).
- **The link to T-52540 was supplied only by Cesar's own *Deed of Confirmation* of 28 Mar 2017.** It was registered on 23 Nov 2021, after his death on 21 Jun 2017 (Annex K p. 7; Exh. B pp. 5–6).
- **The sale was of an unsegregated portion of co-owned land.** No approved plan existed until 6 Apr 2021. That brings in Art. 493 and PD 1529 §58.

**Recommendation:**

- Run the description point as a **strict-construction amplifier**. Read with the ¶7 class limit, the consultation condition and the 2005 revocation, it supports this line: the SPA authorized sale of portions of the T-4497 estate only to the ¶7 class after consultation.
- Do **not** plead "the SPA never covered T-52540" as a separate nullity ground. Ramos defeats it in one sentence with §59 and the 1996 annotation.
- "A new SPA was needed" is not a rule found in the Code text. It is our inference from Arts. 1878 and 1881 [INFERRED].

## 6. Bottom line for counsel

1. Lead with **revocation (Art. 1919–1920)** and the **¶7 class (Arts. 1881, 1898, 1900 "as written")**.
2. Use the **T-4497 description** under *Bautista-Spille* to show the SPA is construed strictly. Do not use it to argue that T-52540 is outside it.
3. Add the new, record-based points:
   - The deed names no title (Annex K p. 3).
   - The T-52540 link was supplied by the agent alone in 2017 and registered after his death (Annex K p. 7; Exh. B pp. 5–6). Arts. 1317 and 1910 let only the principal ratify.
   - §58: no approved plan existed until 2021.
   - Art. 493: an agent's specific-portion sale for all co-owners before partition.
4. Obtain the **1991 Agcaoili SPA** (Entry 2016002312) before trial. Ramos may use it as an alternate source of authority.
