- **Purposes (p. 2):** generic.

**(a) The Balanes' status**

**(c) The owners' knowledge**

**(d) Arrival**

**(e) The 2021 title or the sale**
- Q7 (p. 4): "ngayong huli na ay nalaman ko **kay Jonathan at sa mga dokumentong ipinakita niya sa akin** na diumano nabili ng mga defendants ang lupang … sa … Cesar De la Fuente na wala naman palang kapangyarihang magpabili" [only lately did I learn, **from Jonathan and the documents he showed me**, that the defendants supposedly bought the land from Cesar de la Fuente, who it turns out had no power to sell].
- That is **hearsay** plus a legal conclusion.

**(f) Her own basis for possession:** not stated. She was born and lives in Purok 3. The JA does not say whether her lot is on the Keesey land.

**(g) Harmful content**
- **Q9 (p. 4), the most damaging passage in the three JAs:** "**Malamang na sinamantala** ng mga defendants at iba pang sangkot na mga tao na nasa Amerika ang mga tunay na may-ari at **malamang din pong sinamantala nila ang koneksyon at impluwensiya nila sa gobyerno** …" [**probably** the defendants and others involved **took advantage** of the true owners being in America, and **probably also took advantage of their connections and influence in government**].

  - **Recommend withdrawing Q9 on direct**, or not presenting her at all.
- **Q2–Q3 (pp. 2–3):** the defendants are "talagang mga kilala at **maimpluwensiyang** mga tao" [truly well-known and **influential** people]. Characterization; withdraw it.
- **Q4 and Q8:** testify to the defendants' knowledge. Opinion; expect a motion to strike.
- **Q7:** she learned of the sale from Jonathan. Hearsay; it also shows coordination with the plaintiff.
- **Bias, not in the JA:** she was King's co-defendant in Civil Case 8563 (Annex N pp. 1, 6), and King's complaint-affidavit names her as the "concerned neighbor and friend" who called on 27 May 2020 (Annex N p. 11).

- **Q11:** Gloria said she bought "mula sa mga may-ari" [from the owners]. Gloria's own claim of an owner-sourced purchase is double-edged: it is an admission that she knew the land had owners. Use it carefully.
- **Purpose (b):** "primitive owner since time immemorial" is an overstatement; the title chain shows OCT T-106 of 1934.
- The retired "negotiate only" line: not present.
