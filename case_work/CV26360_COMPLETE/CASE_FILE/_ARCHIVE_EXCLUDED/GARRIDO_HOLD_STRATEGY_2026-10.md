## 5. Why holding wins

1. **Information is worth most while undisclosed.** Each early reveal gives them time to adapt. The mayor and governor were already told (Jonathan's May 2025 letter, doc 365, names Ferdinand), so surprise is partly spent. Disclose nothing further.
2. **Sequence favors us.** Their witnesses commit to good faith first; cross-examination and rebuttal come after.
3. **Their witnesses can open the door.** Testimony about the family's honest dealings lets contrary evidence in as rebuttal or impeachment.
4. **Ramos's exposure operates silently.** If confirmed, it already gives him a reason to want the case resolved. Do not mention it. Barandon alone decides whether to raise a documented conflict.
