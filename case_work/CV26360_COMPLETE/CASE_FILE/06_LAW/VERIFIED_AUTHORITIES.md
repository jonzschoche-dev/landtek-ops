## Statutes (exact text, T1 fixes applied)
- **Art. 528:** "Possession acquired in good faith does not lose this character except in the case and from the moment facts exist **which show** that the possessor is not unaware that he possesses the thing improperly or wrongfully."
- **Art. 450:** "The owner of the land on which anything has been built, planted or sown in bad faith may demand the demolition of the work, or that the planting or sowing be removed, in order to replace things in their former condition at the expense of **the person who built, planted or sowed**; or he may compel the builder or planter to pay the price of the land, and the sower the proper rent."
- **Art. 449:** "He who builds, plants or sows in bad faith on the land of another, loses what is built, planted or sown without right to indemnity."
- **Art. 1874:** "When a sale of a piece of land or any interest therein is through an agent, the authority of the latter shall be in writing; otherwise, the sale shall be void."
- **Art. 1898:** "If the agent contracts in the name of the principal, exceeding the scope of his authority, and the principal does not ratify the contract, it shall be void if the party with whom the agent contracted is aware of the limits of the powers granted by the principal…"
- **Art. 1902:** "…Private or secret orders and instructions of the principal do not prejudice third persons who have relied upon the power of attorney or instructions shown them."
- **Art. 1910 ¶2:** "As for any obligation wherein the agent has exceeded his power, the principal is not bound except when he ratifies it expressly or tacitly."
- **Art. 1911:** "Even when the agent has exceeded his authority, the principal is solidarily liable with the agent if the former allowed the latter to act as though he had full powers."
- **Art. 1317 ¶2** and **Art. 1403(1):** unenforceable unless ratified (the defense's articles).
- **Art. 487:** "Any one of the co-owners may bring an action in ejectment." (Its reach in reivindicatoria is unverified.)
- **Arts. 2232 and 2234** (exemplary damages need a basis award first: T6 J2).
- **Art. 1678** (lessee's useful improvements, one-half value).
- **PD 1529:**
  - **§48:** "A certificate of title shall not be subject to collateral attack. It cannot be altered, modified, or canceled except in a direct proceeding in accordance with law."
  - **§51:** "The act of registration shall be the operative act to convey or affect the land insofar as third persons are concerned."
  - **§52:** "…be constructive notice to all persons from the time of such registering, filing or entering."
  - **§53:** both sentences as in WAR.
  - **§64:** "…Any instrument revoking such power of attorney shall be registered in like manner."
  - The "innocent holder" in §53 is to be read **through Art. 526**; the words "not aware … of any flaw" are Art. 526's (T1 S5).
- **BP 129 §33(3), as amended by RA 11576 (July 30, 2021):** MTC jurisdiction where the assessed value "does not exceed Four hundred thousand pesos (P400,000.00) exclusive on [sic] interest, damages of whatever kind, attorney's fees, litigation expenses and costs."

## Rules
- **Rule 30 §5(f)** (not §5(c)): "The parties may then respectively adduce rebutting evidence only, unless the court, for good reasons and in the furtherance of justice, permits them to adduce evidence upon their original case."
- **Rule 18 §7:** the pre-trial order controls "unless modified before trial to prevent manifest injustice."
- **Rule 132 §13:** foundation for prior inconsistent statements.
- **Rule 132 §§34–35:** formal offer; "Documentary and object evidence shall be offered after the presentation of a party's testimonial evidence. Such offer shall be done orally unless allowed by the court to be done in writing"; testimony is offered "at the time the witness is called to testify."
- **Rule 40 §2:** appeal within 15 days of notice.
- **Judicial Affidavit Rule (A.M. 12-8-8-SC):**
  - **§2(a):** file and serve "not later than five days before pre-trial or preliminary conference or the scheduled hearing with respect to motions and incidents."
  - **§10(a):** late submission is waived, but the court "may, however, allow only once the late submission … provided, the delay is for a valid reason, would not unduly prejudice the opposing party, and the defaulting party pays a fine of not less than P1,000.00 nor more than P5,000.00."
  - **§5:** subpoena for a records holder who declines.
