## Verdict
The fixed dates are right. They come from the 4 Sep 2026 order (Annex AB p. 4, VERIFIED): plaintiff 14 Oct, 4 Nov, 11 Nov, 2 Dec, 9 Dec 2026; defendants 13 Jan, 3 Feb, 10 Feb, 3 Mar, 10 Mar 2027; all 1:30 PM; "final and intransferable unless the ground for resetting is allowed under the rules." Three things in the plan are wrong or risky:
1. The "move to modify the pre-trial order before trial" step is **already out of time** as framed: trial began today.
2. The rebuttal plan puts **case-in-chief evidence** (records custodians R2, R4, R5, part of R7) into rebuttal. Under Rule 30 §5(f) that evidence is excludable unless it goes in by 9 Dec.
3. The "supplemental affidavit for Jonathan by 21 Oct" comes after he has testified. It needs recall with leave, not just a new affidavit.

## Findings by severity

### HIGH
**C1. "NOW — move to modify the pre-trial order before trial (Rule 18 §7, 'to prevent manifest injustice')."**
- Source: Rule 18 §7: the order "shall control the subsequent course of the action, unless modified before trial to prevent manifest injustice."
- Result: **DISCREPANCY (timing).** Trial opened at 1:30 PM today with Jonathan. If the motion was not filed before today, "before trial" has passed. The remaining routes are:
  - Rule 30 §5's "unless the court for special reasons otherwise directs" (issues beyond the pre-trial order);
  - trial by implied consent, on evidence admitted without objection [VERIFY rule in the 2019 text: Rule 10 §5].
- Fix: counsel reads the 13 May pre-trial order today. If the partition's nullity, the improvements regime, or records custodians are missing, counsel decides whether to (a) move at once citing Rule 30 §5's opening clause, or (b) offer the evidence and rely on no objection. Both are counsel's call; the plan should stop saying "before trial."

**C2. Rebuttal sequence R2, R4, R5 (Registry Primary Entry Book and presenter; DENR plan file and Engr. Mangiga; notarial registers for Docs. 402 and 115) and R7 (Building Official and Assessor returns).**
- Rule: Rule 30 §5(f): "The parties may then respectively adduce rebutting evidence only, unless the court, for good reasons and in the furtherance of justice, permits them to adduce evidence upon their original case." The playbook cites "§5(c)"; see T1 S8.
- Result: **DISCREPANCY (classification).** Each of these proves an element of **our** claim:
  - who presented the void deed;
  - that the partition could not have existed in 2017;
  - that no owner consented to the survey;
  - when structures went up.
- That is case-in-chief. Holding it back invites "evidence upon their original case," which needs leave, and this judge gives one indulgence.
- Fix: every custodian and return **goes in on 2 Dec**, with the formal offer on 9 Dec. The playbook §6.1 row for 2 Dec already lists most of them, but §5.3 still schedules them as rebuttal. Delete them from rebuttal, or limit rebuttal use to answering a specific sworn defense statement with an exhibit **already admitted**.
- The same applies to R1 (Salvador's letter) **as proof of the class**. As impeachment it can come in on cross; as substantive proof, its proponent is us. Authentication through Jonathan (recipient) is weak, so it needs Salvador's admission on cross (13 Jan–10 Mar). Note that use in rebuttal must then be impeachment only.

**C3. "By 21 Oct: supplemental affidavit for Jonathan (Q60–66), if sought, with motion for leave."**
- Result: **DISCREPANCY (procedure).** After today, Jonathan will have testified and probably been cross-examined. Adding testimony means **recalling** the witness, which requires leave (Rule 132 §9 [VERIFY 2019 numbering]). The JA Rule requires affidavits 5 days before the hearing (JA Rule §2 [not fetched; VERIFY]).
- Fix: if more from Jonathan is needed, file one motion to recall with the supplemental JA attached, set for 4 Nov or 11 Nov. That spends the single indulgence. Better: put the same facts through Patricia (Ex. O), which is a new witness, not a recall.

### MEDIUM
**C4. "By 6 Nov: King's supplemental affidavit" for 11 Nov.** The JA must be filed at least 5 days before the hearing [JA Rule §2, VERIFY]. 11 Nov minus 5 days is 6 Nov, so this is the **last** day. With the court's e-filing and service rules, aim for 30 Oct.

**C5. "By 30 Oct: De Leon confirmed for 4 Nov; any supplement to Exh. N."** The supplement deadline is the same as C4: 30 Oct is fine (5 days is 30 Oct at the latest). Recommend 28 Oct.

**C6. Patricia's affidavit (Ex. O, reserved).** Not on the §6.1 clock at all. If she is to testify (by videoconference or deposition: counsel to choose the rule [VERIFY: Rule 23; the court's videoconferencing guidelines]), her JA must be in 5 days before her date (2 Dec latest → 27 Nov). A deposition abroad needs leave and time.
- Fix: decide by 21 Oct.

**C7. "By 27 Nov: all subpoena returns in hand."** Sound. Subpoena requests must be filed by about 21 Oct for the court to issue them and for service and compliance (Rule 21 §10, reasonable time [VERIFY]).

**C8. 9 Dec formal offer.**
- Rule 132 §35 [VERIFIED]: "Documentary and object evidence shall be offered after the presentation of a party's testimonial evidence. Such offer shall be done orally unless allowed by the court to be done in writing."
- Fix: a one-line purpose per exhibit (§34: "The purpose for which the evidence is offered must be specified"). Prepare a written list to read from.
- Also note: §35 says "As regards the testimony of a witness, the offer must be made at the time the witness is called to testify." **Barandon must state the purpose of Jonathan's testimony today when calling him.**

**C9. "Rest subject to rebuttal; ask for rebuttal dates after 10 Mar 2027."** This is consistent with Rule 30 §5(f) and Rule 30 §5(g): once evidence is admitted, the case is "deemed submitted for decision, unless the court directs the parties to argue or to submit their respective memoranda." Fix: ask for memoranda on the record when resting, or the case may be submitted without them.
