## Verdict
The case law is in good order. All 23 decisions exist at the G.R. number, date and parties the playbook gives, and every quoted phrase except one string-cite attribution appears verbatim in the decision named. No citation is fabricated. The defects are smaller but this judge will notice them:
- Two Civil Code articles are misquoted by a word or phrase: Arts. 528 and 450.
- One case-string attributes a *Bucton* quotation to *Duenas* and *Vallido* as well.
- Two plan and Registry quotations are worded differently from the exhibit.
- *Tolentino*'s date is incomplete.
- The rebuttal rule is cited as Rule 30 §5(c); the text is §5(f).
- Two cases are double-edged and should be used knowingly: *Republic v. Ciruelas* and *Central Surety v. Hodges*.

## C. Characterizations to watch (not citation errors)
- Playbook Pillar B: "*Duenas* reads §51 to require good faith to last until [registration]". **VERIFIED.** The en banc Court speaks of "broadening the standard of good faith and diligence reposed upon the purchaser until registration of the sale on the title," and of buyers who "should be continuing purchasers for value and in good faith up to the time that they register the sale on the title which is devoid of any annotation." **Caution:** the second passage is framed around a title "devoid of any annotation." Ours carried the 1996 SPA annotation, which cuts in our favour (notice), but quote the first passage, not the second. Do not cite *Vallido* for "good faith must concur with the registration": that line is the Court of Appeals' reasoning, recited in *Vallido*.
- The playbook's closing note says every case was "fetched from lawphil.net and checked … 13 October 2026." That is substantially true. The three findings in rows 8, 15 and S1/S2 show it was not complete.
