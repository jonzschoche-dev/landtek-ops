THE MANDATE FINDING (sec. 16, and it reframes the campaign)

- Sec. 25(a): supervision exists "to ensure that their acts are within the scope
  of their prescribed powers and functions." That is the ceiling. An untrue
  sentence is not an act outside a power, so NO supervising officer is empowered
  to find it false. Fourteen months of asking the MLGOO and the PD for a finding
  asked for a power the Code does not give them.
- Sec. 25(a) 2nd par. + Sec. 29: for a MUNICIPALITY, supervision runs THROUGH THE
  PROVINCE, and the duty is on "the province, through the governor." Relucio's
  referrals to the Governor are statutorily correct, not evasive. Her 13 and
  17 Mar 2026 letters cite Secs. 29 and 31 by number. We read that as a dodge.
- The forums that DO reach a signed untruth: (a) Sangguniang Panlalawigan, the
  only body with original administrative jurisdiction over an elective municipal
  official, Sec. 61(b), on the Sec. 60(c) ground of "dishonesty, oppression,
  misconduct in office, gross negligence, or dereliction of duty", decision
  appealable to the OP, preventive suspension by the GOVERNOR under Sec. 63(a)(2),
  max 60 days, and a real clock under Sec. 62(a) of 7 + 15 + 10 days;
  (b) the Ombudsman, already seized via CSC OAC-L 270; (c) the courts under RPC
  Art. 171(4), whose "legal obligation to disclose the truth" element is supplied
  by the MLGOO's own 25/26 Aug Indorsements.
- The Schedule of Default is already the draft of a Sec. 61(b) complaint. What it
  lacks is a verification, a named respondent, and the Sec. 60 ground pleaded as
  a ground.
- The Sec. 60(c) candidate is NOT the deed sentence. "A valid Deed of Donation"
  with no instrument produced is UNSUPPORTED. "Actively devoted to their intended
  public road purpose", said of a road lot covered with houses reported in writing
  twice, is the one that can be shown FALSE.
