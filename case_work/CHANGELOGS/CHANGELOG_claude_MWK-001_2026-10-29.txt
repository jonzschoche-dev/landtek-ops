THE ANSWER

- NO, not for this Mayor. A.O. 23 (as amended by A.O. 159 and A.O. 66) covers
  only (i) provincial governors and provincial board members, (ii) mayors, vice
  mayors and council members of highly urbanized, independent component and
  component cities, and (iii) mayors, vice mayors and council members of cities
  and municipalities IN METROPOLITAN MANILA. Mercedes is a municipality of
  Camarines Norte. Its Mayor is outside A.O. 23 entirely.
- A.O. 159 amended only Rule 1 secs. 2 and 3, not the coverage. A.O. 66 restated
  the Disciplining Authority and allowed a Special Investigating Committee in lieu
  of the DILG Secretary. Neither expanded coverage.
- Why the line sits there: Metro Manila has no province, so sec. 61(b)'s forum
  (the sangguniang panlalawigan) does not exist for its municipalities. A.O. 23
  reaches them because nothing else can. No gap, no overlap.
- Joson v. Torres confirms each step and supplies the governing sentence: "The
  President remains the Disciplining Authority. What is delegated is the power to
  investigate, not the power to discipline." The DILG's investigative power rests
  on the alter-ego principle, corollary to the President's control power.
- So sec. 16.1 is confirmed from two independent directions: by sec. 25(a)
  (supervision cannot find facts false) and by coverage (the DILG has no
  investigative mandate over this Mayor at all).

THE LEVER THE SEARCH FOUND (sec. 16.6) - HELD, NOT PULLED

- A.O. 23's FIRST covered group is provincial governors and provincial board
  members. And sec. 29 puts the duty to supervise Mercedes on "the province,
  through the governor." Those two facts meet.
- So against the GOVERNOR, for failure to supervise under sec. 29: the Office of
  the President is the Disciplining Authority and THE DILG SECRETARY IS THE
  INVESTIGATING AUTHORITY. And Rule 3 sec. 3 allows filing for LGUs outside Metro
  Manila THROUGH THE CONCERNED DILG REGIONAL DIRECTOR, who must transmit to the
  DILG Secretary within 48 hours and authenticate the documents, the Secretary
  forwarding to the OP within 48 hours. That is RD Escober, the office we have
  been writing to since July. Such a complaint travels UPWARD on a 48-hour clock,
  twice. It cannot be referred downward.
- HELD for four reasons recorded in the file: (1) the Province has not refused us,
  PPDC Licarte wrote to the Mayor on 23 Jun and the Governor noted it; (2) the
  Escober letter is still unread, so provincial and regional conduct must not be
  characterised; (3) no schedule of provincial default exists, no directive to the
  Governor, no dated non-compliance by him; (4) it is the least reversible move
  available in this matter.
- What it changes TODAY is tone, not action: the DILG lane is the correct lane,
  aimed one level up, and the provincial record we are building is its predicate.

CEILINGS, VERIFIED (sec. 16.7)

- Sec. 66(a): investigation terminated within 90 days of its start; decision in
  writing within 30 days after the end of the investigation.
- Sec. 66(b): suspension shall not exceed the unexpired term or SIX MONTHS FOR
  EVERY ADMINISTRATIVE OFFENSE. Sec. 66(c): removal bars candidacy.
- Sec. 67(b): SP decisions appealable to the OP within 30 days; OP decisions final
  and executory.
- Pablico v. Villapando: the last paragraph of sec. 60 means dismissal of an
  erring elective local official may be decreed ONLY BY A COURT OF LAW; removal is
  "lodged exclusively with the courts"; Art. 124(b) Rule XIX of the IRR is void
  insofar as it vests removal in the disciplining authority. Followed in G.R. No.
  170626 (2008). SO THE SANGGUNIANG PANLALAWIGAN ROUTE TOPS OUT AT SUSPENSION, up
  to six months per offence. It cannot remove him.
- Hagad v. Gozo-Dadole: the Ombudsman RETAINS administrative disciplinary
  authority over elective local officials after the LGC, CONCURRENT with the OP;
  no implied repeal; Ombudsman preventive suspension up to SIX MONTHS against
  sixty days under LGC sec. 63.
- Reading across: the Ombudsman is the stronger administrative forum, it is
  already seized of this matter via CSC OAC-L 270 of 23 Jun 2026, and its
  suspension power is six times the Governor's. That is the forum to feed, and it
  is already fed.
