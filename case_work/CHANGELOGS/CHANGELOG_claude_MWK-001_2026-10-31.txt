DECIDED

- A single evidence pack to every officer is REJECTED, and Jonathan agreed. Reasons
  recorded: a mandate is narrow and personal, so one pack hands each recipient a
  document mostly outside his own authority, which is the condition under which an
  officer refers instead of acting; volume supplies a lawful non-answer
  ("voluminous, under evaluation") that cannot be defaulted; a pack addressed to
  everybody has no owner, so nobody is in default; and shipping is not service,
  because a clock runs from proof of receipt.
- RULE ADOPTED: breadth is for proof of notice, depth is for the forum that must
  rule, and the two are never mixed.
- RULE ADOPTED: for each hand-delivered item the deliverable is TWO copies, the one
  left with the office and ours for the receiving stamp. The stamp is the product.
- The 102-page pack's home is a forum that MUST rule: annexes to a verified
  Sec. 61(b) complaint before the Sangguniang Panlalawigan, or the Ombudsman, which
  is already seized via CSC OAC-L 270. Nothing on the officer map requires the pack.
