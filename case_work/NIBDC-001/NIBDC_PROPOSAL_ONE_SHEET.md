# NIBDC × ALLAN INOCALLA — PROPOSAL ON ONE PAGE
## EXPA-000250-V Dual-Track Development · Draft for discussion · Non-binding · Blanks to be negotiated · Date: ______

**The deal in one sentence:** NIBDC does not oppose the Capacuan Minahang Bayan — and in return receives a **share of its production from day one**, plus a **committed joint venture on large-scale development** that activates **if and when Allan brings the funding**. **No cash is asked of NIBDC.** Allan contributes work; economics are set on performance after the Exploration Permit issues.

**The structure — Big Brother / Little Brother:** **NIBDC is the Big Brother** — tenement holder, technical and safety supervisor, primary owner of the geological data. The **Capacuan miners are the Little Brother** — a registered association operating under a Small-Scale Mining Contract inside the declared Minahang Bayan, selling ore through the mercury-free MFPS route. Established Philippine coexistence practice: formalized, planetGOLD-aligned, community-backed — the model regulators want to see.

**NIBDC gets**
- **A share of Capacuan Minahang Bayan production** — terms set in the coexistence MOA; the statutory 1.5% floor attaches once the EP issues
- A **joint-venture position in large-scale development of its own tenement** — **Allan agrees to fund the large-scale development**
- The **exploration work program performed as Allan's contribution** — shaft inventory, mapping, sampling (incl. the 250-ha Inocalla historical area) — data that updates NIBDC's MGB file
- A **dedicated Operator** (Allan) collecting all royalties and fees due NIBDC from shafts on the tenement — documented and receipted

**NIBDC is asked — now (Stage 1, stands on its own)**
1. Board resolution **adopting the concept in principle**; authorize the President to negotiate
2. **Non-objection letter + Board concurrence** with (or replacement of) the 2009 MOA — the exact cure MGB named in 2019; the application has waited since 2017 for it
3. Authorize the **amended Exploration Work Program**; joint follow-up with **MGB Central Office** on EP issuance

**After the EXPA clears (Stage 2, on performance — no percentages discussed at this stage)**
4. **Allan's participation in NIBDC** and his **Operating Agreement as Operator** — terms set then, on performance
5. **Further powers for Allan to develop the tenement outside Capacuan** — targeted operations, shaft formalization and coexistence agreements, and processing routes across the wider 2,360 hectares

**Why now:** MGB Region V endorsed the EP for issuance on **10 July 2025** (MTES-2025-411). The Capacuan agreement comes **first**, stands alone, and stays stable regardless of everything else.

*Compliance: extraction stays 100% Filipino; foreign participation only as financing, equipment, or processing services; nothing filed in NIBDC's name without Board authority; Philippine mining counsel reviews every instrument before signing.*
