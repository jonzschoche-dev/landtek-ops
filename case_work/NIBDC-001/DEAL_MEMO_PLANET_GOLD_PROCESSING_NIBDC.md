## 7. Joint Venture with NIBDC

- A joint venture is to be formed between the **pending Minahang Bayan** and **NIBDC**.
- The Minahang Bayan must **coordinate with NIBDC on any shafts it operates**.
- The joint venture's purpose is set out in Section 10.
- **NIBDC tenement status (MGB record):** EXPA-000250-V covers **2,360.04 hectares** in Paracale, Jose Panganiban and Labo. By Memorandum **MTES-2025-411 dated 10 July 2025**, MGB Region V endorsed the application to the MGB Central Office *"for further evaluation and issuance of Exploration Permit,"* recording that financial capability (paid-up capital ₱28,368,500; Amanah Islamic Bank credit guarantee of 23 April 2025), prior-rights clearance, NCIP compliance and all LGU endorsements are complete. **Central Office action after July 2025 is to be confirmed.**
- **Legal relationship between the Minahang Bayan and the EXPA:** a Minahang Bayan declared while the EXPA is pending **closes that area to the Exploration Permit** unless the small-scale miners consent, any royalty forming a community trust fund (RA 7942 §19(e)). NIBDC, as an applicant, holds no existing mining right and its consent is not required (RA 7076 §3; DAO 2015-03 §8), though it is entitled to notice and comment (DAO 2015-03 §9(d)). **If the Exploration Permit issues first, the position reverses:** NIBDC becomes the holder of an existing mining right and a carve-out then requires its consent, capped at 25% of the area above 500 hectares, with royalty to NIBDC (DAO 2015-03 §8(c); RA 7076 §14). **Sequencing the Minahang Bayan declaration against Exploration Permit issuance is therefore a priority item.**
- Stephen Lloyd's *"Big Brother / Little Brother"* pre-feasibility study (22 August 2026) is noted as **his proposal**; it is not an NIBDC document and the structure above does not depend on it.

## 10. Purpose

The joint venture with NIBDC (Section 7) serves three purposes:

1. **Secure the Minahang Bayan declaration** over the candidate area of EXPA-000250-V. While the EXPA is pending, no NIBDC consent is legally required (RA 7076 §3; DAO 2015-03 §8); once declared, the area is closed to the Exploration Permit absent the small-scale miners' consent (RA 7942 §19(e)). A letter of non-objection from NIBDC nonetheless shortens the notice-and-comment step (DAO 2015-03 §9(d)), and the Investors can procure it because they act for Maribel Villarreal;
2. **Place control of both the tenement and the Minahang Bayan with Allan** — as NIBDC shareholder (Section 8) and as principal of the small-scale mining cooperative holding the contract in the declared area — so that consent, royalty and coordination are internal matters; and
3. **Open exploration across the full 2,360.04 hectares under EXPA-000250-V**, with the Exploration Permit endorsed for issuance (Section 7).

## 15. Compliance Notes

1. **Filipino ownership.** Small-scale mining contracts require **one hundred percent (100%) Filipino ownership**.
2. **No transfer of contracts.** Small-scale mining contracts **cannot be subcontracted, assigned, or transferred**.
3. **Foreign money — permitted channels only.** Foreign money may come in only as **financing, equipment, or processing services** — **not** as ownership or management control of extraction.
4. **Paper trail.** Maintain a clear paper trail showing that the five million pesos (₱5,000,000) funds **the plant and operations**, not a disguised stake in the extraction contracts.
5. **JV boundary.** The NIBDC joint venture must **not pull the processing side into prohibited territory**.
6. **Counsel gate.** **Philippine mining counsel must review** (a) the financing papers, (b) Maribel Villarreal's NIBDC sign-off, (c) any CPC filing, (d) the security documents over purchased equipment and the assignment of Stephen Lloyd's net-profit share (Section 4), (e) the incorporation papers and ownership structure of CoOp-Test Lab Mining Corp (Section 12), and (f) the **executed** Operational Partnership Agreement between SMBC and Stephen Lloyd, (g) MGB Region V area-status certification of the candidate Minahang Bayan area against EXPA-000250-V, (h) the MGB Central Office status of EXPA-000250-V after Memorandum MTES-2025-411, and (i) evidence that the candidate area was occupied and actively mined by small-scale miners before 1 August 1987 (DAO 2015-03 §8(a)) — **before any money is wired or any rights are transferred**.
7. **Equipment procurement discipline.** Equipment purchased with loan proceeds shall be paid for **only against internally consistent, VAT-compliant quotations or invoices**, and **only into the supplier's independently verified corporate bank account — never a personal account**. Supplier identity (SEC registration, BIR TIN) and bank details must be verified through channels independent of the quotation itself before any payment.
8. **Sequencing.** The Minahang Bayan petition and the Exploration Permit are on converging tracks; which issues first determines whether NIBDC's consent is required (Section 7). The Investors control both sides only once Section 8 is executed; until then, treat the two tracks as potentially adverse and do not let either be filed without the other in view.
