# Scenario Note — Large-Scale Action Over the Inocalla 250 Hectares: Legal Gates and Profit Waterfall
*Working note 2026-08-25. Companion to the Deal Memorandum, BBSB Concept Paper, and Allan Authority Checklist. Not legal advice; for counsel review.*

## The frame
Regalian doctrine (Const. Art. XII §2): minerals belong to the State; the Inocalla family owns the SURFACE of the ~250 ha (unsettled estate of Vicente Inocalla Sr.), not a mineral tenement. Mining rights over the ground: NIBDC's EXPA-000250-V (endorsed for EP issuance, MTES-2025-411) + CSSMA's pending Minahang Bayan petition. The family's legal assets in a large-scale scenario: surface ownership, Allan's control positions (NIBDC shares + Operating Agreement + CSSMA), and the pending MB.

## Who can act large-scale there
Effectively only NIBDC — the EXPA closes the ground to competing applications (Yinlu's 2009 protest failed). "Large-scale action imminent" therefore means NIBDC itself (Allan-controlled once Section 8 + the Operating Agreement are executed) converting EP → MPSA.

## Gates any large-scale development must pass on this ground
1. EP → MPSA (60% Filipino NIBDC; DMPF; ECC).
2. Surface access: surface-use agreement with the landowners, or the RA 7942 §§75–76 easement route (bond + compensation). **The estate is unsettled with no administrator — nobody can validly sign for the 250 ha today. This is the critical path.**
3. Small-scale layer: declared MB carve-out closed to large-scale absent small-scale miners' consent + community-trust-fund royalty (RA 7942 §19(e)); CSSMA (Allan) holds the lever. Same provision if registered small-scale miners occupy the ground without a declared MB.
4. NCIP: FPIC Compliance Certificate exists for the EXPA (CCRV-08-08-157); ICC royalty per FPIC MOA if ancestral-domain-affected.
5. LGU endorsements (favorable on record); agrarian-segregated portion excluded.

## The two family streams — keep them separate
The family collects twice through different doors: (1) the estate's surface royalty — ALL heirs, through the estate; (2) Allan's personal positions (NIBDC shares, Operator compensation, CSSMA standing). Clean separation prevents an heirs' dispute from infecting the venture: estate money is attackable by heirs; Allan's earned compensation and shareholding are not, if papered distinctly.

## The one advance action this scenario demands
Settle or administer the estate (administrator petition or extrajudicial settlement among all heirs) BEFORE large-scale action is imminent. Every other gate is already in Allan's hands or on the record; the surface-use agreement is the one signature line nobody can currently fill — and doing this before the ground is visibly valuable is cheaper and less contentious than after.
