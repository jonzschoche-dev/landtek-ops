## 3. Payment schedule

**Agreed so far:** principal ₱5,000,000 · interest-only at 30% · full repayment within 6 months of funding · capital returns through plant operations · plant operational by month 3.

**The one number we need to agree:** whether "30%" is **flat for the six months** or **30% per annum**. Both are common; they differ by ₱750,000. Tick one:

| Basis | Interest on ₱5M | Total to repay |
|---|---|---|
| ☐ **A — 30% flat for the term** | ₱1,500,000 | **₱6,500,000** |
| ☐ **B — 30% per annum (6 months = 15%)** | ₱750,000 | **₱5,750,000** |

**Timing — three ways to lay it out** (tick one; "T" = funding date):

| Month | Option 1 — Even monthly | Option 2 — Operations-aligned | Option 3 — Bullet |
|---|---|---|---|
| T + 1 | interest ÷ 6 | — (plant in rehab) | — |
| T + 2 | interest ÷ 6 | — (plant in rehab) | — |
| T + 3 | interest ÷ 6 | — (plant commissioning) | — |
| T + 4 | interest ÷ 6 | interest ÷ 3 | — |
| T + 5 | interest ÷ 6 | interest ÷ 3 | — |
| T + 6 | interest ÷ 6 **+ ₱5,000,000 principal** | interest ÷ 3 **+ ₱5,000,000 principal** | **all interest + ₱5,000,000 principal** |

Worked example — Option 2 with Basis A: nothing due months 1–3 while the plant is being brought up; ₱500,000 at each of months 4, 5, and 6; plus ₱5,000,000 principal at month 6. Total ₱6,500,000.
Worked example — Option 2 with Basis B: ₱250,000 at each of months 4, 5, and 6; plus ₱5,000,000 principal at month 6. Total ₱5,750,000.

**Our view:** Option 2 fits how this deal actually works — the money comes back from plant operations, and the plant isn't earning until month 3 or 4. We'd rather the schedule match reality than pretend the plant pays from day one.

**If the plant runs behind:** we talk before month 4, not after month 6. Tell us early and we work the schedule together; that's the partnership we want.

**Release of the ₱5M:** ☐ in one sum at T, or ☐ in stages tied to the work (equipment purchase → installation → commissioning). Your call on what the rehab actually needs — tell us.
