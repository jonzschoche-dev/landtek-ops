## Substance (transcribed)
1. **APSA-000322-V was CONVERTED into an Exploration Permit application, now denominated EXPA-000250-V**, pursuant to Section 9 of DAO 2021-25 ("All pending Mineral Agreement applications intended for exploration shall be converted into Exploration Permit applications").
2. Section 7 of **DAO 2022-03**: the Provincial Mining Regulatory Board (PMRB) is authorized to declare mineralized onshore areas suitable for small-scale mining as **Minahang Bayan** — and Section 7(b)(1) **includes areas covered by pending Exploration Permit applications** among those that may be declared.
3. **Neither Section 7(b)(1) nor any other provision of DAO 2022-03 requires the CONSENT of the Exploration Permit applicant** before an area covered by its application may be declared a Minahang Bayan. Section 9(d) merely requires that mining applicants be **NOTIFIED** of the filing of a petition affecting their applied area.
4. The Office's notice of the pending Petition for Declaration of Minahang Bayan was made solely to comply with DAO 2022-03 notice requirements. "**It should not be construed as requiring your consent or as recognizing any right to withhold or prevent the declaration of the area as a Minahang Bayan on that basis.**"
