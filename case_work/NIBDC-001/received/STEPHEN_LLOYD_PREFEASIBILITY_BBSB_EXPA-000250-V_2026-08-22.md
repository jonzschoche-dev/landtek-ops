NIBDC – EXPA-000250-V | Pre-Feasibility Study — Camarines Norte, Philippines
PRE-FEASIBILITY STUDY — Dual-Track Development of Exploration Permit Application EXPA-000250-V
Big Brother / Little Brother Coexistence Model — Integration of Formal Large-Scale Exploration with a Designated Minahang Bayan and Historical Shaft Mapping
Proponent: Northern Islands Builders and Development Corporation (NIBDC) · Tenement: EXPA-000250-V (and related controlled claims) · Location: Paracale, Jose Panganiban & Labo, Camarines Norte, Region V · Commodity: Gold and associated minerals · Document Date: 22 August 2026 · Status: Pre-Feasibility Concept Study – Internal Planning Document

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
NIBDC holds EXPA-000250-V covering multiple parcels in the Paracale–Jose Panganiban–Labo gold district. The study evaluates a dual-track strategy combining conventional large-scale exploration with a formalized "Big Brother / Little Brother" (BBSB) coexistence model. A selected portion of the tenement is allocated for declaration as a Minahang Bayan under RA 7076; an organized small-scale mining cooperative ("Little Brother") operates within it under a Small-Scale Mining Contract, while NIBDC ("Big Brother") provides technical supervision, safety training, preferential ore offtake, and access to historical underground workings, which are rehabilitated and mapped to generate a 3-D ore-body model at lower cost than surface drilling. Advantages claimed: formalization of artisanal activity; early employment and cash flow; geological data from historical workings; social license; policy alignment. Financial base cited: share capital ~Php 28.4M, property and equipment ~Php 27.2–27.6M, Php 5M credit guarantee/line from Amanah Islamic Bank.

2. INTRODUCTION AND PROJECT BACKGROUND
NIBDC incorporated 1989 (SEC Reg. No. 164872); primary purpose includes general construction, land development, and activities connected with mines and shafts. Revised Exploration and Environmental Work Programs and updated financial capability documentation submitted to MGB-V. Precedents cited: Itogon Suyoc Resources / Apex Mining arrangements; Samahan ng mga Minero sa Barangay Casalugan.

3. LOCATION, TENURE AND ACCESS
Barangays referenced: Pinagbirayan Malaki and Munti, Talusan, Dagang, Batobalani, Capacuan (Paracale); Santa Rosa Norte and Sur (Jose Panganiban); Cabatuhan (Labo). Tenure at EXPA stage; progression to EP then MPSA is the long-term objective. Minahang Bayan allocation = a discrete surveyed portion declared by the PMRB upon petition, subject to NIBDC consent and coexistence agreements.

4. GEOLOGY, MINERALIZATION AND HISTORICAL WORKINGS
Gold-bearing quartz veins in metamorphic and intrusive rocks; network of historical shafts, winzes, drifts, stopes, many rehabilitable at modest cost. No code-compliant resource estimate yet. Systematic mapping/sampling of historical workings + surface mapping, geochemistry, targeted diamond drilling → 3-D model and resource estimate. Priority to workings within/adjacent to the candidate Minahang Bayan area.

5. BBSB MODEL — key elements: area allocation (typically tens of hectares); coexistence/commercial agreement (MOA or contract-mining: boundaries, safety/environmental standards, production reporting, preferential ore purchase by NIBDC at transparent prices, training, dispute resolution); technical and safety support; data sharing (NIBDC retains primary rights for the larger resource estimate); SDMP integration.

6. EXPLORATION AND RESOURCE DEFINITION PLAN — Phase 1 Baseline & Access (months 1–6): shaft inventory, safety assessment, selective rehab, survey control, cooperative engagement, MOA drafting, PMRB petition. Phase 2 UG Mapping & Sampling (months 7–18). Phase 3 Drilling & Resource (months 19–36): diamond drilling, QA/QC, MRE, updated EWP/DMPF.

7. PRELIMINARY MINING AND PROCESSING CONCEPT — selective narrow-vein underground methods within the Minahang Bayan; ore preferentially sold to NIBDC (or designated buyer) at market-linked prices less treatment/refining charges; toll-processing options if a central facility is developed; large-scale concepts (selective UG or limited open pit; gravity/flotation/cyanidation) deferred to later studies.

8. ESG — formalization and mercury reduction (planetGOLD linkage); community engagement; environmental protection; governance under RA 7076, Mining Act, MGB regulations; SDMP integration.

9. PRELIMINARY ECONOMIC CONSIDERATIONS — balance sheet (share capital ~28.4M; PP&E ~27.2–27.6M) and Php 5M Amanah credit guarantee/line "provide a foundation for Phase 1 and 2"; UG mapping cost-effective vs drilling; early offtake cash flow; formalization reduces disruption risk. Full economic model after Phase 2. Phases 1–2 expenditure "expected to be manageable within existing credit facilities and balance-sheet capacity, subject to detailed budgeting." Option value: accelerates EP/MPSA conversion.

10. RISKS — boundary disputes; safety incidents; environmental non-compliance; commercial disagreement on offtake; regulatory delay; data-quality and social-license opportunities — each with generic mitigations.

11. CONCLUSIONS AND RECOMMENDATIONS — (1) complete outstanding EXPA compliance (latest AFS, original credit guarantee documentation); (2) identify/engage a small-scale cooperative, commence coexistence MOA; (3) rapid inventory/safety assessment of historical shafts in the candidate area; (4) revised EWP including shaft rehab/UG mapping; (5) formal consultation with Camarines Norte PMRB and LGUs on the Minahang Bayan petition; (6) Phase 1 budget and schedule.
