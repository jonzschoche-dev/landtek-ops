## 1. Why we are writing

NIBDC stands at the best moment in the twenty-year life of its tenement. By Memorandum **MTES-2025-411 dated 10 July 2025**, MGB Region V endorsed Exploration Permit Application **EXPA-000250-V** to the MGB Central Office *"for further evaluation and issuance of Exploration Permit"* — with financial capability accepted, the sole docketed protest dismissed in 2010, NCIP compliance certified, and favorable endorsements from every concerned barangay and municipality on record. The application your officers carried through two decades of moratoria and conversions is, at last, at the door of issuance.

The Proponent proposes a partnership that converts that position into a producing, funded, publicly-financeable project — while keeping NIBDC fully compliant with Philippine mining and ownership law. This memorandum sets out what the Proponent brings, the structure proposed, and the specific decisions the Board is respectfully asked to make.

## 2. What NIBDC holds

- **EXPA-000250-V:** 2,360.04 hectares across Paracale, Jose Panganiban and Labo — the heart of the country's oldest continuously mined gold district — descended from APSA-000322-V (2004) and converted under EO 130 in 2022.
- **A clean file:** prior-rights clearance (Lot 8 excluded 2008), NCIP FPIC Compliance Certificate, area status and clearance, full publication and posting, favorable endorsements from all concerned LGUs, and the Region V endorsement of July 2025.
- **A candid observation, offered respectfully:** the exploration program ahead — shaft rehabilitation, underground mapping, and diamond drilling — requires capital beyond NIBDC's present liquid resources. The Amanah Islamic Bank credit guarantee satisfies MGB's financial-capability requirement; the work itself needs people, equipment, and organization on the ground. This proposal closes that gap **through a work exchange** — organization, data, operatorship, and processing access contributed as work, with economics established on performance — **not through any cash call on NIBDC**.

## 3. What the Proponent brings

1. **A work exchange, not a cash ask.** The Proponent commits **work**: the staged program in Section 5 — shaft inventory, rehabilitation, mapping and sampling — together with the operatorship function (Section 4), coordination of the small-scale side, and the processing route. **No investment amount is committed and no cash is sought from NIBDC.** The parties' economics will be established in **performance-based participation agreements, set up after the EXPA is cleared** (issuance of the Exploration Permit). Documented work performed on the tenement further strengthens NIBDC's file. Any third-party financing that later enters does so only as financing, equipment, or processing services (Section 8).
2. **A working processing route.** The Proponent's group is financing the activation of the ₱29-million **Mercury-Free Processing System (MFPS)** plant at Barangay Casalugan — built under the UN-backed planetGOLD Philippines Project and managed by the Samahan ng mga Minero sa Barangay Casalugan (SMBC) — together with a secondary toll-processing station for smaller ore batches. An experienced operating team is engaged at the plant.
3. **Access to international capital.** Allan Villafría Inocalla's Canadian investor network, and a prepared path to a **TSX Venture Exchange listing via the Capital Pool Company program** — an independent NI 43-101 technical report, a qualifying work program, and a public vehicle into which NIBDC-related interests may be brought **as permitted by Philippine law and MGB rules, and only with NIBDC's written consent**.
4. **Local organization.** Coordination of the small-scale mining side — the registered cooperative, the Minahang Bayan petition, and community engagement — through Allan's standing in the area.
5. **A geological data commitment.** The Proponent commits **sampling and tunnel data from the 250-hectare Inocalla historical mineral area**, and from other areas selected for targeted operations, delivered to NIBDC to **update its Exploration Work Program, MGB filings, and resource documentation** — continuous underground grade and structural data over ground mined since 1987, at a fraction of the cost of equivalent surface drilling.

## 4. The proposed structure — "Big Brother / Little Brother"

**The core exchange, in one sentence:** NIBDC's non-opposition to — and Board concurrence in — the Capacuan Minahang Bayan earns NIBDC **a share of that Minahang Bayan's production**, agreed contractually in the coexistence MOA from day one (the statutory 1.5% floor attaches automatically once the Exploration Permit issues); and NIBDC commits in principle to a **joint venture for large-scale development of the tenement, activated if and when the Proponent secures large-scale funding**, on terms negotiated at activation.

A coexistence model on established Philippine practice: **NIBDC as Big Brother** (tenement holder, technical and safety supervisor, primary holder of geological data) and an **organized small-scale mining cooperative as Little Brother**, operating under a Small-Scale Mining Contract inside a declared **Minahang Bayan** carved from a surveyed portion of the tenement area.

- **Coexistence MOA** between NIBDC and the cooperative: boundaries, safety and environmental standards, production reporting, dispute resolution — at arm's-length, market-linked terms.
- **Ore offtake** through the MFPS plant: mercury-free, transparent pricing, treatment charges agreed in advance.
- **Historical workings as an exploration asset:** the district's old shafts and levels, rehabilitated and mapped under supervision, yield continuous grade and structural data at a fraction of drilling cost — data that flows to **NIBDC's** resource model and accelerates the path from Exploration Permit to MPSA.
- **Community standing:** formalized, mercury-free small-scale livelihood strengthens the social license the larger project will need, and aligns with the planetGOLD agenda already active in the municipality.
- **Operatorship of the NIBDC side.** The Proponent will serve as **NIBDC's Operator** under a board-approved Operating Agreement: **controlling and actively conducting** all exploration operations on the tenement and, upon MPSA conversion, extraction operations; arranging and supervising all **processing**, including the MFPS route; and conducting the **purchase and marketing of ore on NIBDC's behalf**, including preferential ore buying from the Minahang Bayan under the coexistence MOA; and **collecting for NIBDC's account all royalties, fees, and shares due from any shafts operating within the tenement area** — under coexistence and formalization agreements, and under the statutory royalty (RA 7076 §14) once NIBDC's rights attach — with every collection documented and receipted, and unpermitted operations brought into the formalization pathway. (Under an Exploration Permit the large-scale side explores only; commercial extraction on the tenement follows MPSA conversion — in the interim, production and ore trading run through the small-scale side and the buying program.) The Operator will be supported by a dedicated **technical and environmental adviser** responsible for keeping mine-safety, processing, and environmental standards at par with national requirements and planetGOLD benchmarks — particularly for operations beyond the Capacuan area.
- **Extraction rights remain with the miners.** All extraction within the declared Minahang Bayan belongs to the contract-holding association and its registered members under the Small-Scale Mining Contract. **Nothing in this proposal or in the coexistence MOA limits, licenses, or conditions the association's right to mine within the declared area.** NIBDC's role inside the carve-out is technical and safety support and, once the Exploration Permit issues, the statutory consent-and-royalty relationship — not an operational veto. Ore offtake to the MFPS plant is a **preferred route on terms to be agreed, not an exclusivity** or a condition of extraction.

**Why acting together, now, protects NIBDC.** Under RA 7942 §19(e), an area covered by small-scale miners is closed to mining applications absent the small-scale miners' consent; while the Exploration Permit remains pending, a Minahang Bayan petitioned by others could remove part of the tenement area from NIBDC's reach with no compensation. Within this partnership, the Minahang Bayan is petitioned **in coordination with NIBDC**: NIBDC issues a letter of non-objection, the coexistence MOA is in place from day one, and once the Exploration Permit issues, the carve-out proceeds under DAO 2015-03 §8(c) with **NIBDC's consent and a royalty to NIBDC** of not less than 1.5% of gross gold value (RA 7076 §14). The partnership converts a statutory risk into a royalty stream, a data pipeline, and a funded community ally. This is not hypothetical: the Minahang Bayan application of the Capacuan Small-Scale Miners Association has been pending **since 2017** precisely for want of a consent that MGB will accept — the Bureau ruled in 2019 that the consent given through the 2009 MOA signed by then-President Artemio Villareal *"shall first be concurred by the current BOD before this Office consider it a valid consent"* (Annex A). The Board's concurrence is the named cure, on MGB's own instruction.

**Sequence of commitments.** First, and immediately: the **NIBDC–Capacuan agreement** — the Board's concurrence with (or replacement of) the 2009 MOA and the letter of non-objection (Annex A). **That agreement precedes everything else and remains stable on its own terms; it is not conditioned on any of the arrangements below.** Second, **after the EXPA is cleared**: the performance-based structure — the Operating Agreement, the Proponent's participation in NIBDC, and the listing-vehicle terms — negotiated and executed with each party's economics based on demonstrated performance.

## 5. Staged program (indicative — to be filed only as an amendment to NIBDC's Exploration Work Program)

| Phase | Window | Work |
|---|---|---|
| 1 — Baseline & access | Months 1–6 | Shaft inventory and safety assessment; survey control; cooperative engagement; coexistence MOA; Minahang Bayan petition; NIBDC non-objection letter |
| 2 — Underground mapping | Months 7–18 | Staged rehabilitation; geological and structural mapping; channel sampling; integration with surface geochemistry; preliminary 3-D vein model |
| 3 — Drilling & resource | Months 19–36 | Targeted diamond drilling; QA/QC; mineral resource estimate; updated EWP / DMPF toward MPSA conversion |

## 6. What NIBDC receives

- A **delivered work program** — shaft inventory, rehabilitation, mapping and sampling performed as the Proponent's contribution under the work exchange, at **no cash cost to NIBDC** and without dilution of its tenement position;
- **A share of Capacuan Minahang Bayan production** — contractual under the coexistence MOA **from day one** (terms set in the MOA), with the statutory minimum of 1.5% of gross gold value attaching automatically once the Exploration Permit issues;
- A **committed joint-venture position in large-scale development of its own tenement** — **the Proponent agrees to fund the large-scale development**, the joint venture activating when that funding is secured;
- **Participation in the public listing vehicle** for NIBDC-related interests — terms to be agreed at Stage 2, subject to MGB rules;
- **All geological data** generated over the tenement, held primarily by NIBDC;
- A **dedicated Operator** (Section 7, Item 7) aligning the management of exploration, processing, and ore marketing under Board oversight — capability NIBDC does not currently carry in-house;
- Strengthened **financial-capability file** and social license, and joint follow-through with MGB Central Office on Exploration Permit issuance.

*(All blanks are open commercial terms for negotiation between NIBDC and the Proponent.)*

## 7. What we respectfully ask the Board to approve

**Now (Stage 1 — the Capacuan agreement, standing on its own):**

1. A **Board resolution adopting the dual-track development concept in principle**, including the two-stage sequence in Section 4, and authorizing the President to negotiate definitive agreements;
2. A **letter of non-objection / coexistence** addressed to the Provincial Mining Regulatory Board regarding the Minahang Bayan petition over the agreed candidate area — together with the current Board's **concurrence with, or replacement of, the 2009 MOA**, as MGB required in Annex A. **In exchange, NIBDC receives its contractual share of Capacuan Minahang Bayan production under the coexistence MOA.** This agreement precedes the arrangements below and remains stable regardless of them;
3. A **commitment in principle to a joint venture** between NIBDC and the Proponent for **large-scale development of the tenement**, taking effect **if and when the Proponent secures large-scale funding** — terms negotiated at activation on performance-based principles;
4. **Authorization to prepare an amended Exploration Work Program** incorporating shaft rehabilitation and underground mapping, for filing by NIBDC;
5. A **joint approach to MGB Central Office** to establish the current status of EXPA-000250-V following MTES-2025-411.

**Upon clearance of the EXPA (Stage 2 — the performance-based structure; no percentages are proposed at this stage):**

6. The **President's written sign-off on the Proponent's participation in NIBDC** (and that of his nominees) — stating the exact nature of the stake (equity, contractual interest, or operating rights), her authority to bind NIBDC on the joint undertaking, and consent in principle to include NIBDC-related interests in the listing vehicle — **terms set on performance at that stage**, structured to preserve the 60% Filipino ownership required for MPSA conversion;
7. An **Operating Agreement appointing the Proponent as NIBDC's Operator**, with authority to control and actively conduct exploration and (upon MPSA conversion) extraction operations, processing arrangements, the purchase and marketing of ore for NIBDC's account, and the **collection of all royalties, fees, and shares due NIBDC from shafts operating within the tenement area**, under Board oversight — **compensation performance-based**, terms to be agreed;
8. **Further powers for the Proponent to develop the tenement outside Capacuan** — targeted operations, shaft formalization and coexistence agreements, and processing routes across the wider tenement area, under the Operating Agreement and Board oversight.

## 8. Compliance commitments

The Proponent commits, and the definitive agreements will provide, that: small-scale mining contracts remain **100% Filipino-owned** and are never subcontracted, assigned, or transferred; foreign participation enters only as **financing, equipment, or processing services** — never ownership or management control of extraction; a clear paper trail is kept for every peso; nothing is filed or represented in NIBDC's name without Board authority; and **Philippine mining counsel reviews every instrument before execution**.
