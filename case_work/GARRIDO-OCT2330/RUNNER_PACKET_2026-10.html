Records Retrieval Checklist

TCT No. 4512 and TCT No. T-4538 · Daet and Mercedes, Camarines Norte

Top priority: the complete registration file for each of the two transfers, TCT 4512 and TCT T-4538: the instrument, every supporting document, and the Primary Entry Book entry. Do not leave the Registry of Deeds without both.

Bring

  • The five request letters in this packet, filled in and signed by you
  • Your own valid government ID, plus two photocopies
  • Cash for fees. Keep every official receipt.

Rules

  • You are the requester. These are public records, so you may request them in your own name.
  • Ask for certified true copies, not plain photocopies.
  • If asked why, say “for verification of title records.” Do not discuss any case or any person named in the records.
  • If refused or delayed, do not argue. Write down the office, staff name, reason, and date.
  • Photograph every document before leaving the counter.
