## 2. Disposition (doc 14205, pp. 3–9)

1. SPA objection **rejected**: Zschoche's standing "proceeds from his own participation in the transaction", so no SPA is needed to file.
2. Forum-shopping objection **rejected**: disclosing other complaints is not forum shopping.
3. **No prima facie §21(e)** (and, in the conclusion, §21(b)): the request is a records/FOI request, not a §4(f) "government service".
   Not listed in the MEO Citizen's Charter (Annex G, pp. 242–250).
4. Express reservation (p. 7): this is **not** a finding that the refusal or the SPA demand was proper. Denial of access must be tested "under the appropriate laws … governing access to public information".
5. **Referral to CSC Regional Office V** of the 5 Feb 2026 threat language, "without making any finding" on liability.
6. Recommendation: CLOSURE + CSC R5 REFERRAL.
