# The Certificate of No Record: deep dive (ARTA-1378, with 0690/0792 and the OP petition)

> Companion to [RESOLUTION_REVIEW_2026-09-15.md](RESOLUTION_REVIEW_2026-09-15.md). LandTek work product, not a legal opinion; 1378 has no counsel.
> Tags: `[doc N]` documents.id · `[gm N]` gmail_messages.id · `[VERIFY]` = not confirmable from the corpus or law library.

> **UPDATE 16 Sep 2026 — this memo's odds assessment is now too pessimistic.** The Citizen's Charter *is* in the corpus, annexed inside the 4 Jun 2026 OP Second Manifestation (doc 13978), covering both the Municipal Assessor's and the Municipal Engineering Office's services. Two findings follow:
> 1. **The Assessor's chartered services include "Issuance of Certification of No Improvement, No Property Holdings and Aggregate Landholdings"** and "Issuance of certified true/machine copy of assessment of real property" — simple G2C transactions with published checklists and periods. A no-record certification is, by the Municipality's own Charter, an ordinary chartered service.
> 2. **ARTA itself held on 25 Aug 2026 (1321, doc 6908) that a request for certified copies of existing records IS a government service** — "ARTA finds that the government service sought by Zschoche may reasonably fall within the service denominated as 'Research/Verify History of Records of Tax Declaration' under the Citizen's Charter" — and applied the §21(e) elements against the Charter's 2–3 day period. Fifteen days later, in 1378, it held that requests for copies of or certifications from existing records are not government services at all.
>
> That inconsistency, between two resolutions of the same Authority, in the same Municipality, on the same complainant, is a far stronger appeal ground than anything in §3 below. It is now Ground B.1–B.3 of the appeal draft. What remains wrong is the claim our 8 Jun Manifestation made — that the **Municipal Engineering Office's** section lists these services. It does not; the analogue sits in the **Assessor's** section.

## Bottom line

1. **ARTA's factual statement is wrong, but the legal point behind it is weak for us.** ARTA wrote that Zschoche "was not applying for
   the issuance of a … certification". Item A.1 asked for exactly that. But §4(f) doesn't list "certification", and the test our own
   Manifestation invoked (grant of a right or authorization) doesn't include it either. A no-record certificate is an attestation after
   searching the office's files, which is the records-retrieval activity ARTA said is not a government service.
2. **The Manifestation never argued the certificate on its own.** It folded it into eight categories and claimed all were "expressly
   listed" in the Citizen's Charter. The Charter ARTA attached shows that claim is wrong for the certificate and for most of the other seven.
3. **This question is already before the Office of the President.** The 5 May 2026 petition on 0690/0792 (same respondent,
   050526-MRO-234187) argues that a request for a "certification of no record" is a §4(f) government service [doc 703 p. 6].
   A separate 1378 appeal would re-argue a pending issue and risks the A.O. 22 §9 stay freezing the CSC referral.
4. **The better use of the 1378 Resolution is as support in the pending petition.** In 1378, ARTA dropped the citizenship ground it
   used in 0690/0792 and held Zschoche has standing without an SPA. Both help the pending petition. Filing that in the 0690/0792
   proceeding is not an appeal of 1378, so there is no stay.
5. **What the certificate would prove is largely already on paper:** Balane's 21 Oct 2025 letter says no building permit was issued
   "recently" for the Teope lot. The duty Building Officials clearly have is **enforcement** (PD 1096 §205, inspection and stop-work),
   not certification.

## 3. Does the certificate argument work?

**For us**
- **Other sections treat certifications as outputs agencies issue.**
  - §4(j): processing time runs "to the issuance of certification or such similar documents".
  - §9(b)(1) ¶4: "license, clearance permit, certification or authorization".
  - §10: automatic approval of an "application or request for issuance of license, clearance, permit, certification or authorization".
- **ARTA's own concessions (1378 Resolution p. 6):** the office "is mandated to issue permits, clearances, and certifications", and
  Charter omission "does not, by itself" remove a transaction from §4(f).
- **Record error:** ARTA's statement about item A.1 is simply wrong.

**Against us (stronger)**
- **The definition itself:** §4(f)'s list omits "certification". §10's automatic-approval mechanism assumes an *original application*
  with "all required documents … and all required fees", the permit model. It doesn't fit a records search.
- **The office's own answer shows it's a records search:** the 21 Oct letter says confirming no permit means checking "the previous
  building official's" records. That is the "retrieval of historical … records" ARTA says is outside §4(f).
- **How we asked:** item A.1 was conditional ("if applicable"), bundled with sweeping "entire history" requests, and labelled FOI.
- **PD 1096 (library, §§201–302):** the Building Official issues permits, inspects, and may stop work or discontinue occupancy (§205
  and enforcement powers). The library text has **no express duty to issue no-record certifications**.
- **The Charter:** it has no certification line for the MEO at all.

**Net:** it's arguable, but reversal odds are low. The same question is already before the Office of the President in 0690/0792.

## 4. An inconsistency to fix before the Office of the President sees it

We describe the 21 Oct 2025 letter three ways:
- **In the OP petition:** a "phantom response", undelivered and "substantively non-responsive" [doc 703 p. 4–5].
- **In the 0690/0792 Reply:** an **admission** that no building permit exists [doc 706, reply ¶3.12/3.16].
- **In the 26 Jan 2026 request e-mail:** the letter was attached and followed up on, so we had it by then.

These can be reconciled: not received when sent, learned of later, and substantively a partial admission. But any future filing must
say that explicitly, or it reads as having it both ways.
