## I. THE POINT PRESENTED

This Petition presents one documentary point. The Authority closed the complaint on the premise that Petitioner "was not applying for the issuance of a building permit, occupancy permit, clearance, **certification**, or any other permit or authorization ordinarily processed by the Municipal Engineering Office" (Annex "A", pp. 6–7). That premise is contradicted by the request the Resolution itself attaches. Item A.1 asked the Municipal Engineer, as Building Official, to issue a **"Certificate of No Record: A certification stating that NO building permit exists for the subject structure, if applicable"** (Annex "B").

The Resolution also accepts that the office "is mandated to issue permits, clearances, and certifications as part of its standard functions" (Annex "A", p. 6). Its conclusion therefore rests on a misreading of what was asked.

Petitioner does **not** press the broader parts of his request (the historical permit files for the 2021 subdivision and the sports complex). Those are the parts the Resolution describes as the "retrieval, compilation, and reproduction of archival records." This Petition concerns only the application for a certification that the Building Official issues from his own permit register.

## III. ERRORS ASSIGNED

### A. The Resolution misapprehended the application: item A.1 was an application for issuance of a certification.

The Resolution's reasoning turns on a distinction between **access to existing records**, which it holds is not a government service, and an **application for the issuance** of a permit, clearance or certification, which it holds is one (Annex "A", pp. 6–7). Item A.1 falls on the side the Resolution itself treats as covered. It did not ask for a copy of any existing document. It asked the Building Official to **issue a certification**, under his signature, as to whether a building permit exists for an identified structure.

The words "if applicable" do not change the nature of the application. They acknowledge only that the certificate issues if the office's register shows no permit; if a permit exists, the office says so. Either way the office acts on an application in the ordinary course. A finding resting on a misreading of the application cannot sustain the closure.

### B. The Resolution excluded from R.A. No. 11032 a class of transaction the Authority itself treats as covered, and left the Section 21(b) charge unadjudicated.

**B.1. The Resolution contradicts the Authority's own ruling of two weeks earlier, on the same complainant, in the same Municipality.**

In its Resolution dated **25 August 2026** in CTN SL-2026-0209-1321, the Authority examined Petitioner's request to the Municipal Assessor of Mercedes "for certified true copies of various real property assessment records," and held: *"ARTA finds that the government service sought by Zschoche may reasonably fall within the service denominated as 'Research/Verify History of Records of Tax Declaration' under the Citizen's Charter of the Municipality of Mercedes, Camarines Norte"* (Annex "H", p. 5). It then applied the elements of Section 21(e) — the first being that *"the applicant transacted a government service"* — and measured the office's performance against the Charter's *"two (2) to three (3)-day processing period"* (Annex "H", pp. 6–7).

A request for certified copies of existing records was thus, on 25 August 2026, a government service within Section 4(f). On 09 September 2026 the same Authority held that requests for copies of, or certifications from, existing records are not government services at all. A municipality is one corporate entity, and Section 4(f) is one definition. The two constructions cannot both stand, and the Resolution does not acknowledge the departure, much less explain it.

**B.2. The Charter of the same Municipality recognizes the very act sought here; an office's omission is an agency failure, not the applicant's penalty.**

The Citizen's Charter of Mercedes, in its **Municipal Assessor's Office** section — the pages the Authority itself annexed in CTN SL-2026-0209-1321 — lists and regulates *"Issuance of certified true/machine copy of assessment of real property (tax declaration)"* and *"Issuance of Certification of **No Improvement, No Property Holdings and Aggregate Landholdings**"*: a **simple**, government-to-citizen transaction, open to a declared owner or an authorized representative, with a published checklist, fee and processing period (Annex "H-1"). A certification that an office's records show **no** entry for a given property is, by the Municipality's own Charter, an ordinary chartered service. Petitioner asked the Building Official for that same species of act, applied to the permit register he keeps.

That the Municipal Engineering Office's own section omits the counterpart is a compliance deficiency under Sections 5 and 6 of R.A. No. 11032, not a grant of immunity. The Authority's own practice confirms it: in CTN SL-2026-0209-1319, finding no chartered service for the records there requested, it endorsed the matter to its Operations Group *"for the reengineering of [the agency's] Citizen's Charter."* To hold otherwise is to give every office an incentive to leave its most contested record categories off its Charter and so place them beyond review.

**B.3. Section 21(b) is triggered by the imposition itself, independently of Section 21(e) — and the Resolution never adjudicated it.**

Section 21(e) is worded in terms of government services: *"[f]ailure to render **government services** within the prescribed processing time on any application or request without due cause."* Section 21(b) is not. It reads, in full: *"[i]mposition of additional requirements other than those listed in the Citizen's Charter."* The Section 4(f) threshold the Resolution applied to Section 21(e) therefore does not, by its terms, dispose of Section 21(b). Section 9(a)(2) makes the same point from the other direction: any deficiency notice to a requesting party *"shall be limited to those enumerated in the Citizen's Charter."*

By demanding, on 05 February 2026, a *"duly issued, authenticated or apostilled Special Power of Attorney"* as an absolute condition of release, Respondent exercised official authority over the transaction. He cannot invent an unpublished requirement to block a request and simultaneously claim the request lies outside the Act. The Authority itself has held that Petitioner's standing *"proceeds from his own participation in the transaction"* and that an SPA *"is not indispensable"* (Annex "A", p. 4), and has expressly declined to find *"that the SPA requirement was legally justified"* (Annex "A", p. 7).

The Resolution nonetheless disposes of Section 21(b) without reasons. Its caption reads *"For: Violation of Sections 21(b) and (e)"* and its concluding paragraph finds *"no prima facie evidence … for violations of Sections 21(b) and 21(e)"* (Annex "A", pp. 1, 8); yet its ISSUE is framed on Section 21(e) alone, its FINDINGS address Section 21(e) alone, and Recommendation No. 1 cites Section 21(e) alone (Annex "A", pp. 3, 9). Rule IV, Section 7 of the 2023 ARTA Revised Rules of Procedure requires a Resolution to contain the *"issues to be resolved, findings and recommendations."* A charge carried in the dispositive portion but absent from the issues and the findings has not been adjudicated.

**B.4. The statute itself treats a certification as an output of processing, and the act sought lies in the Building Official's ordinary course.**

"Processing time" runs *"from the receipt of an application or request … to the **issuance of certification** or such similar documents approving or disapproving an application or request"* (Sec. 4(j)). The law repeatedly groups *"license, clearance, permit, **certification** or authorization"* as the outputs of covered applications (Secs. 9(b)(1), 9(e), 10). Section 4(f) must be read with those provisions; it cannot be read to exclude the certifications the same statute elsewhere treats as issuances.

The act sought is, moreover, ordinary course for this office. The Building Official *"is the official charged with the duties of issuing building permits"* and is *"primarily responsible for the enforcement"* of the National Building Code in his jurisdiction (P.D. No. 1096, Sec. 205). Whether a permit exists for an identified structure is answered from the register the office keeps in issuing permits — a **simple transaction** (Sec. 4(m)), not the archival compilation the Resolution describes. The Resolution's own premises point the same way: the office *"is mandated to issue permits, clearances, and certifications as part of its standard functions,"* and a service's absence from the Charter *"does not, by itself, remove it from the ambit of a 'government service'"* (Annex "A", p. 6).

Finally, the service was never rendered. Respondent did not issue the certificate, did not state that a permit exists, and did not issue a written disapproval on any ground recognized by law; he conditioned all action on an unpublished requirement. His earlier letter of 21 October 2025, in a separate transaction, said only that *"there is no building permit issued recently pertaining to that particular area"* and pointed to records of *"the previous building official"* (Annex "D"). That is not a certification, and it is why item A.1 was requested.

### C. The Resolution's account of the record is materially incomplete.

These points are not individually decisive, but they show the record was not fully evaluated:

- The Resolution states that the Authority referred the complaint to the Committee on Anti-Red Tape on "13 February 2026," before the complaint was filed. Its own Annex A-1 is dated **23 February 2026**.
- It states that "ARTA SLRFO received no response." In fact the Committee adopted CART Resolution No. 05 on 6 April 2026 and transmitted a Referral Report on 16 April 2026. That Resolution adopted a standing policy of non-release to Petitioner (Annex "E"). None of this is mentioned.
- It records the Supplemental Affidavit as received on 17 June 2026. It was transmitted to the Litigation Division by e-mail on **8 June 2026** (Annex "A", Annex "F" thereof; transmittal e-mail on file).
