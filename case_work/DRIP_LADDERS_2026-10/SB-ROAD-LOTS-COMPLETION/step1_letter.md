**Re: Completing the 1980 donation of the eight road lots to the Municipality: registration and naming of the streets**

Dear Honorable Vice Mayor and Members of the Sangguniang Bayan:

Magandang araw po. I write as attorney-in-fact of Patricia Keesey Zschoche, a co-owner of the lands of the Heirs of Mary Worrick Keesey, under the Special Power of Attorney attached as Annex "A".

By a Deed of Absolute Donation dated 14 April 1980, Mary Worrick Keesey, through her attorney-in-fact, donated to the Municipality eight road lots, 10,208 square meters in all, for public streets, each to be named as stated in the Deed. The Municipality accepted them by Resolution No. 75-79, as amended by Resolution No. 103-86 of 16 July 1986. The eight lots and their stipulated names are listed in Annex "B". (Our earlier letters gave the year of the Deed as 1979; the Deed is dated 14 April 1980.)

The donation is complete as between the donor and the Municipality, and we honor it. But it was never completed on the register. Only Road Lot 2 (Marcia Keesey Street, 1,726 sq m) was annotated, on TCT No. T-32917 in 1995. The other seven lots appear on no title in the Municipality's name, and the streets have never been named as the Deed provides. Our letter of 3 October 2025 on the naming was referred to the En Banc Committee during the 17th Regular Session, as the Secretariat kindly informed us on 22 October 2025.

So that the public streets stand clearly in the Municipality's name, and the remaining land of the Heirs is clearly separate from them, may I respectfully ask the Sangguniang Bayan to:

1. act on the 3 October 2025 referral by naming the eight streets as stated in the Deed and in Resolution No. 103-86;

2. authorize the Municipal Mayor to complete the registration of the seven road lots not yet annotated, with the segregation plan and technical descriptions the Registry of Deeds will require; and

3. let me know the number and date of any action taken, so that I can obtain certified copies.

On the Heirs' side, I will gladly cooperate, as attorney-in-fact of a co-owner, in any step of the registration that requires the registered owners, and I will help the Municipality's offices locate the plans and title references.

A reply within fifteen (15) working days, or a note of when the matter will be taken up, would be much appreciated. A reply by e-mail to jonzschoche@gmail.com is perfectly fine.

Maraming salamat po.

Respectfully yours,



**JONATHAN PAUL ZSCHOCHE**  
Attorney-in-Fact for Patricia Keesey Zschoche, a co-owner  
Dasmariñas Street, Barangay VIII, Daet, Camarines Norte
