**Subject:** Re: NOTICE OF REFERRAL - CTN SL-2026-0423-1891 (DILG): outcome of the referral, for the Authority's record

Dear Dr. Sanmocte and the Complaints Handling team,

Magandang araw po! I am the Attorney-in-Fact for Patricia Keesey Zschoche, the complainant in CTN SL-2026-0423-1891. I write only to report to the Authority what became of its referral, so that the record on your side is complete.

On 4 May 2026 the Authority referred the complaint to the Department of the Interior and Local Government, under Section 17(d) of Republic Act No. 11032, for appropriate action. The complaint concerns the composition and the conduct of the Committee on Anti-Red Tape of the Municipality of Mercedes, Camarines Norte.

From the documents I hold, the referral then travelled as follows:

1. On 6 May 2026 the DILG CART Secretariat endorsed it to DILG Region V, per a memorandum signed by Assistant Secretary Ester A. Aldana, CESO II, Vice-Chairperson of the DILG Committee on Anti-Red Tape.
2. On 21 May 2026 I asked Region V for a case tracking number. On 25 May 2026 Region V replied that the matter had been referred to the concerned division for appropriate action. No tracking number has been given to me, then or since.
3. On 2 June 2026 the Region V Records Section forwarded the file onward to Camarines Norte, and on 4 June 2026 the DILG Provincial Director forwarded it to the Office of the Governor.
4. On 23 June 2026 the Provincial Planning and Development Coordinator of Camarines Norte, who is also the Province's ARTA Focal Person, noted by the Governor, transmitted the complaint to the Office of the Municipal Mayor of Mercedes, for the attention of the Municipality's own ARTA Focal Person. That letter states that the Province acted after consultation with the ARTA Southern Luzon Regional Field Office and with the Provincial Director of DILG Camarines Norte, who advised that the matter falls under the jurisdiction of the Local Government Unit of Mercedes. I attach that letter.

I respectfully bring this to the Authority's attention for one reason only. The subject of the referral is the Committee on Anti-Red Tape of Mercedes, and the Municipal Mayor chairs that Committee. The referral has therefore been placed with the office that is its subject, and I have received no determination from any level since.

For completeness, the Authority referred the same complaint to the Civil Service Commission on the same date. On 23 June 2026, through OAC-L Letter No. 270, s. 2026, the Commission forwarded it to the Office of the Ombudsman, noting that the official involved is an elective official.

May I respectfully ask three things:

1. That this outcome be noted in the Authority's record for CTN SL-2026-0423-1891;
2. Whether the Authority considers the referral closed on its side, or still pending with the Department; and
3. Whether the Authority wishes to exercise its monitoring function in relation to the referral, which I leave entirely to its judgment.

I am not asking the Authority to decide any property question. That forms no part of this report.

Maraming salamat po sa inyong tulong!
