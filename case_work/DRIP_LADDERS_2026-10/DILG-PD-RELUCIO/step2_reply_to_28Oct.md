## The rule this revision establishes

**An item leaves the Schedule only two ways: marked PERFORMED, or marked WITHDRAWN, in writing.**
It never leaves by being left out of the next letter. A running schedule whose item count falls is an
invitation to argue that the missing items were abandoned, and the two items with the longest counts
(7 and 8, at 496 and 373 days) are exactly the ones a shorter letter would have dropped.

The Sec. 252 item is the only one ever withdrawn, on 20 October 2026, which is why the corrected
edition has nine items where the 19 October edition had ten. That withdrawal is on the record and was
our own doing, so it costs nothing.

## What we deliberately do NOT do

- **No fourth demand for a date certain from the LGU.** Three have gone out. A fourth converts a cooperating recorder into a defensive officer and produces a local-autonomy reply.
- **No ask that she write to the Assessor or the Building Official directly.** Her 30 March doctrine says she will not, and asking produces that doctrine in reply. Items 7 and 8 go through the Mayor, which is her own rule.
- **We do not press item 9 on her.** It is the Department's own file. Pressing it here invites another referral to the Governor and pulls her into the national lane we are keeping separate.
- **No mention of the 29 October letters** to the DILG CART Secretariat and ARTA, and no cc to anyone.
- **No statute cited in the body.** Not one section number. The Schedule already carries all fourteen provisions; the letter does not need to lecture a Provincial Director on the Code.
- **No reference to the Hall parcel as a compensation claim, and none to Civil Case 26-360.** Those belong to other desks and other forums.
- **No claim of default on the 20 October e-mail.** She answered inside the period. Saying otherwise would be false and would cost us the one official who writes inconvenient things down.
