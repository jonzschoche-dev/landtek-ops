Dear Director Relucio,

Magandang araw po! Thank you for your letter of 28 October 2026, and for answering the very next day. I appreciate it.

Thank you also for telling me that the referral from your Regional Director reached your Office on 27 October 2026, and that another follow-up letter has gone to the Provincial Government.

My note of 27 October asked two things. Your letter kindly answered the first. On the second, may I still ask for the reference or control number assigned to the matter, so that I can follow its progress properly and stop troubling your staff with questions about where it sits. This is the third time I have asked for a number, and I am sure it is only an oversight.

On the conference you will be holding with your MLGOO, I would be grateful if your Office would let me know when it has taken place. So that it is useful, these are the four items still outstanding from what the MLGOO directed on 25 and 26 August 2026:

1. The session records I requested, and the fee for the copies. The LGU answered on 1 September that these were "being verified and consolidated." No fee has been assessed and no date was given.
2. The record book of resolutions and ordinances with their dates of passage. The LGU answered that these are recorded. The book itself has not been produced.
3. The street matter pending since May 2025. The LGU answered that it was "already acted upon," without giving the number or the date of the resolution or ordinance that acted upon it.
4. The specific public instrument behind the claim of legal title, which the MLGOO asked for by name on 26 August. The LGU answered "a valid Deed of Donation," without its date, its donor, its notarial entry, or a copy.

Each of these is a record to be produced or a fee to be assessed. They are not matters that call for the exercise of discretion, and any one of them can be closed in an afternoon with a photocopy.

I understand the matter stays open pending your conference and your apprisal, and I am content to wait for it. So that your Office is not troubled with repeated follow-ups, I will simply write once more if I have not heard anything by 20 November 2026.

May I also ask for a copy of the follow-up letter your Office sent to the Provincial Government, for my own records.

Maraming salamat po sa inyong tulong!

Respectfully yours,
