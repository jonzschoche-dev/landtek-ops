Dear Provincial Assessor Magana:

**1. First, the record of what your Office has done right — and I say this on the record.** Your Office compiled and released to the estate the historical declarations, the Barangay 5 and Barangay 1 transaction-history ledgers, and the computerized Declarations of Real Property for Barangays 1, 3 and 5; confirmed in writing on **26 January 2026** that it had *"[d]one the research of all transactions"*; and on **1 July 2026** committed that *"all your requested relief will be replied with"*, requesting the certified titles and survey plan for verification against the tax-mapping records — which I transmitted the following day. That cooperation is the reason the estate's assessment record is as far along as it is, and I have said so in every forum where these matters are heard.

**2. What remains is precisely the part only supervision can finish.** Two months have passed since the 1 July commitment, and the matters placed before your Office on **29 June and 13 July 2026** remain open:

  (a) the **improvements / structural assessment records** and the **tax maps** for the estate parcels — the two record classes still unproduced by any office (your own Memorandum No. 2024-01 excepts tax maps even from its restriction);
  (b) the **reconciliation** of the municipal and provincial records for Barangay 5 — the discrepancy your Office's own verification was to resolve; and
  (c) the matter of the **Municipal Assessor of Mercedes**, who — fifteen months after a chartered request and an official fee (O.R. No. 7383466) — has rendered nothing, and who continues to invoke **your Memorandum No. 2024-01** as her ground for withholding, notwithstanding that **Section 5 of that same Memorandum exempts a party to a pending case**, which the requester at all relevant times was.

**3. Why the ask matters — stated plainly.** Absent your Office's supervision, its silence will be read as **effectively endorsing** what the record shows: a chartered 2–3 day service left unrendered for fifteen months after the fee was officially received; a sworn declaration that the work "cannot possibly be done" while **your own Office had done and released it**; and the invocation of **your own Memorandum No. 2024-01** for a withholding its Section 5 exemption does not permit. I do not believe your Office endorses any of that — its own conduct proves the opposite — which is exactly why its supervision, and not merely its example, is now needed.

**4. The ask.** Under **Section 472 of the Local Government Code**, your Office exercises technical supervision over the municipal assessors of the province. I respectfully request that your Office:

  (a) **Direct or technically advise the Municipal Assessor of Mercedes** to complete the chartered service paid for in June 2025 — the certification of history and certified records — or to state in writing the specific legal ground for each item withheld;
  (b) **Clarify in writing the proper application of Memorandum No. 2024-01**, including its Section 5 exemption, so that your issuance is no longer invoked for a withholding it does not authorize;
  (c) **Complete the verification and reconciliation** your Office undertook on 1 July 2026, and furnish the improvements records and tax-map extracts requested on 29 June and 13 July 2026, with any lawful fees assessed for payment; and
  (d) Favor me with a written response within **fifteen (15) days** — and, should your Office decline any item, **say so in writing**, so that the estate may govern its remaining remedies accordingly.

**5.** I make this request in the same spirit as the cooperation your Office has shown: the estate does not ask anyone to decide ownership or any contested question — only that the assessment records of registered land be complete, consistent between the two offices that share them, and available as the Code provides. Your Office has done the larger half of that work already. This letter asks it to finish — and to ensure its own issuance is not used to undo it.

Thank you for your service and your Office's continued assistance.
