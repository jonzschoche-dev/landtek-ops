## Why the small players, in one page

The Mayor is an elective official: his lane is the Ombudsman, his exposure is political, and the
clock runs on someone else's calendar. **Every other name below is an appointive career officer.**
That changes four things at once:

1. **Forum.** Appointive career officers fall under the **Civil Service Commission (RO V, Legazpi)**
   under the 2017 RACCS. No need to queue behind the Ombudsman's elective-official track. The CSC
   is already seized of a parallel Mercedes matter — ARTA referred Engr. Balane there on
   30 April 2026 — so the route is proven, not theoretical.
2. **Cost asymmetry.** For the Mayor an administrative charge is politics. For a career employee it
   is the pension, the eligibility and the next promotion. The same filing that a politician absorbs,
   a career officer cannot.
3. **Simplicity.** Each one has a **single failure an investigator can grasp in ten minutes** —
   a fee never assessed, a service paid for and never rendered, a protest never decided, a permit
   confirmed absent and never enforced. No land-title theory, no donation history, no two-year
   narrative. That is the opposite of the ARTA-1319 mistake.
4. **Defection.** They are also the custodians of what we actually want. The moment one of them
   performs — one fee assessment, one certified copy, one permit record — the Mayor's "no statutory
   ground exists" collapses, because his own subordinate will have found the ground. Pressure on the
   small players converts either into production or into evidence. Both are wins.

**Discipline carried over:** the charge is the documented non-performance, never the land dispute.
Ownership stays reserved to the courts. Nothing below asks any forum to decide title.

## Sequencing recommendation

**File Olaguera first, alone.** It is the ripest, the simplest, the least entangled with any other
forum, and it carries the comparator that kills the standard defence. One clean win — or one clean
preventive measure — against the Secretary changes how every other office in that building reads the
next letter.

**Then Abla**, once the forum-shopping certification is squared against the pending OP petition.

**Then Macale**, which needs only the protest document pulled and the §252 lapse stated.

**Hold Balane** for Barandon. **Teope** stays on the demand track, not a new complaint.

**What not to do:** do not file all four on the same day. Four simultaneous complaints read as a
campaign against a municipality; one complaint with a 250-day counter and a December comparator
reads as a citizen who was refused a record and is right. The second is what wins, and each filing
that lands makes the next one easier.
