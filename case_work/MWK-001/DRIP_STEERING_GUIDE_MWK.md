## 1. The doctrine in one page
1. **Anchor.** Every communication serves a matter goal: CV 6839 money (₱5,124,922.47 principal, about ₱21.6M with interest per doc 377), T-4497 chain recovery (CV 26-360, SP 2680), LGU compound pay-or-buy (₱59,521.00/mo on the LGU's own valuations, docs 528/152/1070), and the accountability record on officials. No goal, no letter.
2. **Sleep.** The drip is not combative; it puts the office to sleep while the record builds. Routine letters (first ask, editions, R1):
   - make one small, ordinary ask, with neutral identification and the previous letter cited by date;
   - give the easy yes (we pay, collect, or accept e-mail) and a polite 15-working-day reply period;
   - stay at ≤250 words, with the SPA as the only annex.
   They never contain amounts, posture words, statutes, forums, cc's (unless Jonathan directs it per item), adverse annexes or internal labels. The live lint enforces this.
3. **History before every ask.** Read the prior asks, answers and refusals for that office first. Never repeat a refused or answered ask. Instead, *narrow* it (e.g. particulars), *route* it to another custodian, or *build on their own words*. Never reveal what we already hold.
4. **Baby steps.** Each letter earns one record element (receipt, an answer or recorded silence, an admission, a receipt, a demand). Strategic instruments (Schedule of compensation, damages demand, comparators, supervisor packs) are the **last** step of a ladder, and only on Jonathan's go.
5. **Clock and ladder.** No receipt, no clock. Wait 15 working days from proven receipt before the next step to the same office, and send at most one new step per office cluster per week. Escalation goes **upward** (supervisor → notarized R2 affidavit → forum), never back at the same officer, and never a second letter that argues.
6. **Account and thread.** Send from the account where the office's thread lives (jonzschoche@gmail.com by default; jonathan@hayuma.org where that thread started; paper where the history is paper). Always reply in the same thread. Jonathan may override per item (CV6839-LBP-BALANCE: jonzschoche@gmail.com, 20 Oct 2026).
7. **Review queue.** At least 4 drafts always wait for Jonathan's comment, shown one at a time. A draft is staged only when he says "make the draft", and is never sent by the system.
8. **Money stays internal.** Each official's dereliction is priced in `loss_line` and logged every cycle. Costs are stated outward only as sworn neutral fact at R2 (Jonathan's affidavit, remote e-notarized under A.M. No. 24-10-14-SC), and only on go.
9. **Counsel lanes.** CV 26-360 defendants (Engr. Balane, Torralba, the Balanes) go through Atty. Barandon only. Teope is held for a posture check. SP 2680 goes through Atty. Botor in court. Records letters to courts stay strictly clerical.
10. **Truth.** Every fact in a letter must trace to a document (doc or gmail id). Inferences are never stated as fact. When a figure is cited, cite the right source (₱5,124,922.47 = doc 377, not doc 376).
11. **Feedback ledger first.** Before drafting or considering any drip, read `case_work/MWK-001/DRIP_FEEDBACK_LEDGER.md` (live in the corpus): every past critique from Jonathan, for that office and ladder and in general.
