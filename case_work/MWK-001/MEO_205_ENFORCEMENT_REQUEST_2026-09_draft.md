Sir:

**1.** I write as attorney-in-fact of **Patricia Keesey Zschoche**, registered co-owner and heir of the late **Mary Worrick Keesey** (apostilled Special Power of Attorney enclosed; titles enclosed). This is **not a request for records**. It is a request that your Office perform the enforcement duties that **Section 207** of the National Building Code places upon the Building Official.

**2.** By your letter of **21 October 2025** you advised me, as to the construction in Barangay 1 on the lot occupied by Mr. Antonio Teope, that *"[a]s far as this office is concerned there is no building permit issued recently pertaining to that particular area,"* and that your Office was *"requesting the concerned person to coordinate with this office as to the compliance in securing permits."* I am not aware that any notice of violation, order to stop work, order of discontinuance of occupancy, or inspection has followed.

**3.** The land on which that structure and others stand is registered to the heirs and is declared for taxation by the Municipality in the heirs' names; the taxes on those declarations have been paid `[FILL years; enclose receipts]`. **No lease, deed, written consent, or other instrument has ever been executed by the heirs in favour of any occupant.** No previous administrator of the estate held authority to consent to construction: the agency instruments of record confer a power *"to negotiate for the sale"* only, and under Civil Code Articles 1877–1878 a power touching an immovable is confined to its special object. The authority of the former attorney-in-fact was revoked in 2005. Where an occupant asserts an "agreement" with a previous administrator, **no such instrument has been produced** — and in any case **Section 301** admits no exception for private consent.

**4.** Accordingly, and with respect, I request that your Office:

  (a) **inspect** the structures standing on the parcels listed in the enclosed schedule, under the power **Section 207** gives you to *"enter any building or its premises at all reasonable times to inspect and determine compliance,"* beginning with the Barangay 1 structure that is the subject of your own letter;

  (b) **state in writing, for each structure, from your own register**, whether a building permit was issued (number and date), whether a **Certificate of Occupancy** was issued under **Section 309**, and what document was submitted in satisfaction of **Section 302(2)** — which requires a *"[c]ertified true copy of the TCT covering the lot"* and, where the applicant is not the registered owner, *"a copy of the contract of lease"*;

  (c) where no permit exists, **issue the notice of violation and order the work stopped**; and where a structure is **used or occupied** without a Certificate of Occupancy, **order the discontinuance of that occupancy**, as Section 207 authorises; and

  (d) proceed under **Section 215** as to any structure found dangerous or ruinous.

**5.** I ask for no demolition by any private hand and propose none. I ask only that the officer the law charges with enforcement exercise that charge, after his own inspection and with due process to every occupant.

**6.** May I respectfully ask to be advised in writing of the action taken. Should no action be taken, I intend to bring the matter to the **Secretary of Public Works and Highways**, in whom **Sections 201 and 203** of the Code vest the administration of the Code and *"general powers of supervision and control."*
