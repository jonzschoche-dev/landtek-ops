### 0.1 All velvet glove

Every later authorized communication should be calm, respectful, factual and readily answerable.
Acknowledge assistance, identify the relevant record, and make one precise request appropriate to the
recipient's own responsibility. Ask for a record, confirmation, explanation or date. Leave room for
the office to supply information or correct the record.

Keep routine correspondence brief and free of accusations, threats, pressure language or announcements
of prospective escalation. Legal grounds can be cited plainly where needed. Keep candid procedural
analysis and possible next steps in the internal record. Formal filings retain their required facts,
legal grounds, disclosures and relief, expressed neutrally. Courtesy does not dilute deadlines,
evidence qualifications or the specific requested outcome.

For later drafting only: "We would appreciate a written update on the action taken under the
6 May memorandum, with a copy of any report or disposition. If the matter remains under review,
kindly identify the office handling it and the anticipated completion date."

The existing Romero draft's paragraph 9 announces escalation and treats absence of a reply as
non-action. Preserve the draft, but mark that wording for a later authorized velvet-glove revision.
No draft is released or rewritten by this addition.

### 0.2 OP admissibility, fees, service and consolidation audit

LandTek assembles the records; the designated legal reviewer determines their procedural effect.
"Pending appeals" remains a working description, not proof of admission, perfection or jurisdiction.
Review each challenged disposition separately, including 0690 and 0792 where their requirements differ.

For every filing record: docket/parties and requested relief; appellate or discretionary supervisory
route and its legal basis; actual decision receipt and any valid reconsideration; applicable deadline
and filing-mode rule; notice and memorandum filing evidence; required signatures/verification;
service on the agency and affected parties; fee receipt or exemption motion AND disposition;
OP case number/status/orders; consolidation request, covered dockets and ruling. Record the source
page, reviewer/date, gap owner and next step. Classify each requirement as VERIFIED COMPLIANCE,
VERIFIED DEFECT or UNRESOLVED. A stamp alone does not complete this audit.

AO22 baseline, subject to the particular route, special law and applicable later rules: ordinarily
15 days from decision notice or valid reconsideration denial; notice, required service and the
PHP 1,500 fee within the applicable notice period; memorandum within 30 days after the notice,
with required content/service. Perfection includes notice, fee and memorandum. Verify any exemption.
Presidential review is not automatically available without a statutory appeal; a supervisory label,
promise to pay later or consolidation request is not a demonstrated cure [L1].
