**1. Basis of the rate.** The compensation rate is anchored on the **Municipality of
Mercedes' own Assessor's valuation**: the Municipality's own Declaration of Real Property
(ARP No. GR-2014-HH-07-005-00236) declares land within this same cadastral cluster at a
market value of **₱943,810 for 973 sq m — ₱970.00 per square meter**. Applying the
conservative convention of **six percent (6%) of land market value per annum** yields:

> **Reasonable compensation: ₱58.20 per sq m per year — ₱4.85 per sq m per month.**

This rate is a floor, not a ceiling: it is **subject to upward adjustment upon independent
appraisal**, and is without prejudice to claiming the higher of the appraised rental value
or this schedule for any period.

**3. Accrual.** Compensation accrues **from the date of service of the demand** on each
occupant and monthly thereafter for so long as occupation continues, with **legal interest
at 6% per annum** on amounts unpaid from each monthly due date. Prior-period claims
(from the inception of bad-faith occupation) are **expressly reserved** and are not waived
by this Schedule.

**4. Terms.**
  (a) **Without prejudice / non-waiver.** Nothing in this Schedule, nor the acceptance of
  any payment under it, (i) recognizes any lawful basis for the occupation, (ii) waives
  any action for recovery of possession, reconveyance, cancellation, demolition (Arts.
  449–450, Civil Code), or damages, or (iii) constitutes a lease or any consent to occupy.
  It is a demand for **reasonable compensation for use and occupation of registered land
  occupied without instrument.**
  (b) **Regularization invited.** Any occupant wishing to regularize by written lease at
  market terms may propose it in writing; until an instrument is executed, the meter runs.
  (c) **Payment.** To the estate's designated account, monthly within ten (10) days of
  each statement; receipts issued without prejudice per (a).
  (d) **Compliance note.** Structures without building permits or occupancy certificates
  remain unlawful under P.D. No. 1096 irrespective of any payment hereunder.
  (e) **Statements.** A monthly Statement of Account will be served on each occupant
  (Part III); non-payment accrues and bears interest — non-response does not suspend accrual.

The Municipality occupies **8,706 sq m of registered land of the Estate of Mary Worrick

Enclosed is the estate's **Schedule of Reasonable Compensation**, rated on **the
Municipality's own Assessor's declared market value** for this same ground. From the date
of service hereof, reasonable compensation accrues at **₱42,224.10 per month**, with legal

period of occupation and to recovery of the land itself.

The Municipality has three lawful paths, and I invite it in good faith to choose one in
writing within fifteen (15) days: (1) **produce the instrument** on which its occupation
rests; (2) **regularize** — a lease at market terms, or acquisition at fair market value
under the modes the Local Government Code provides; or (3) **pay the scheduled
compensation** monthly while the parties address the foregoing. Silence leaves only the
meter and the remedies.

This demand adjudicates nothing and asks the Municipality to adjudicate nothing — it asks
the Municipality to do what every occupant of another's registered land must do: show its
paper, regularize, or pay.

Respectfully, **JONATHAN PAUL ZSCHOCHE**, Attorney-in-Fact for Patricia Keesey Zschoche
