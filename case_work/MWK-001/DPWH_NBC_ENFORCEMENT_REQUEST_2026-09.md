This letter asks this Department for **no record, no document and no information**. It asks for **enforcement of the National Building Code**, which is a duty your Department administers and which is owed to the public at large. I state this at the outset because requests arising from this same estate have repeatedly been re-characterized by other offices as requests for information and disposed of on that footing. **2. What this letter is, and what it is not.** It is not an appeal under **Section 307** of the Code. Section 307 lies to an "applicant/permittee" from the "non-issuance, suspension or revocation" of *his own* permit, and no such advice has been issued to me. What I respectfully invoke instead is the Secretary's own authority over the administration of the Code:

> **Section 201.** *"The administration and enforcement of the provisions of this Code including the imposition of penalties for administrative violations thereof is hereby vested in the Secretary of Public Works, Transportation and Communications, hereinafter referred to as the 'Secretary.'"*
>
> **Section 203.** *"For purposes of carrying out the provisions of this Code, the Secretary shall exercise general powers of supervision and control, including the formulation, promulgation and enforcement of the necessary rules and regulations."*

The Building Official who has not acted holds his office at your Department's designation. Section 205 provides that *"the Secretary may designate incumbent Public Works District Engineers, City Engineers and Municipal Engineers to act as Building Officials in their respective areas of jurisdiction,"* and that the designation *"shall continue until regular positions of Building Official are provided or unless sooner terminated for causes provided by law or decree."* The officer concerned signs as **Municipal Engineer** of Mercedes. `[VERIFY: obtain or request confirmation of the instrument of designation under which he acts as Building Official — this letter should not assume one exists in a particular form.]`

The relevance is narrow and it is this: the tax declarations the Municipality issued, and the taxes the heirs paid on them, identify the land as the heirs' and identify the heirs as the parties whose registered property is being built upon. **Section 302(2)** of the Code requires that every application for a building permit be accompanied by a *"Certified true copy of the TCT covering the lot on which the proposed work is to be done"* — and, *"[i]f the applicant is not the registered owner, in addition to the TCT, a copy of the contract of lease."* The titles are in the heirs' hands. **No lease, and no instrument of any kind, has ever been executed by the heirs in favour of any person now building or residing on this land.**

**4. No one holds the owners' consent.** I state the following of my own knowledge and on behalf of my principal, and I am prepared to attest to it under oath (proposed affidavit at Annex "E"):

  (a) Neither **Patricia Keesey Zschoche** nor, to our knowledge, any co-owner of the estate has given consent — written or otherwise — to the construction, erection or maintenance of any structure now standing on the estate's registered parcels.
  (b) **No previous administrator of the estate held authority to give that consent.** The only agency instruments of record confer powers of a different and narrower object: the Special Power of Attorney of **16 March 1992** authorised its holder *"to negotiate for the sale"* of the property, and only in favour of a defined class of persons holding Contracts to Sell from the previous administrator, after prior consultation with the principals. Under **Articles 1877 and 1878** of the Civil Code, authority touching an immovable is express and confined to its special object. **A power to negotiate a sale is not a power to consent to construction.**
  (c) The authority of the estate's former attorney-in-fact, **Cesar M. de la Fuente**, was in any event **revoked in 2005**.
  (d) No occupant has ever produced to the heirs — nor, so far as the heirs have been able to learn, to the Office of the Municipal Engineer — a lease, a deed, a written consent, or any instrument evidencing a right to build.

I put paragraph (b) squarely because the Building Official's own letter records the contrary assertion of the occupants. It is dealt with in paragraph 5 below.

**5. The Building Official's own admission, and what has not followed it.** By letter dated **21 October 2025** (Annex "F"), the Office of the Municipal Engineer of Mercedes, over the signature of **ERWIN H. BALANE, Municipal Engineer**, wrote to me concerning construction in Barangay 1 on a lot occupied by Mr. Antonio Teope. The letter states, in its own words:

> *"As far as this office is concerned there is no building permit issued recently pertaining to that particular area."*

and continues:

> *"In restrospect, the building was there for a while and since you mentioned that they had agreements of some sort with the previous administrator of the property, I would strongly encourage to check their records so that we can ascertain that no permits were issued by the previous building official,"*

and further:

> *"For the meantime, we are requesting the concerned person to coordinate with this office as to the compliance in securing permits."*

Three things appear on the face of that letter, and I invite the Department's attention to each.

  **(i) The office knows of the construction.** It is not a matter reported to it and unverified; the officer writes that *"the building was there for a while."*

  **(ii) The office knows there is a compliance gap.** It does not say the structure is permitted. It says it is *"requesting the concerned person to coordinate with this office as to the compliance in securing permits."* That sentence is the Building Official's own characterisation of the structure as one that has yet to comply.

  **(iii) As to the "agreements of some sort with the previous administrator"** — the letter records an assertion, not a document. **No such agreement has ever been produced**, by the occupant, by the Municipality, or from the estate's own records; and for the reasons in paragraph 4(b), no administrator of this estate held the power to authorise construction upon it. Respectfully, an unproduced private arrangement is not a substitute for a building permit in any event: **Section 301** admits of no exception for private consent, and by its terms binds *"any agency or instrumentality of the government"* equally with private persons.

**Ten months and twenty-six days have passed since that letter** `[VERIFY — recompute at signing: 21 October 2025 to the date of service]`. In that interval, and so far as the heirs have been informed, **no notice of violation has issued, no work has been ordered stopped, no order of discontinuance of occupancy has been made, no inspection report has been furnished, and no certification has been issued** as to the permit status of that structure or of any other structure on the estate's land.

**6. What the Code required in that interval.** I set the duties out in the Code's own words, because the case here is simply that they were not performed.

> **Section 301. Building Permits.** *"No person, firm or corporation, including any agency or instrumentality of the government shall erect, construct, alter, repair, move, convert or demolish any building or structure or cause the same to be done without first obtaining a building permit therefor from the Building Official assigned in the place where the subject building is located…"*
>
> **Section 309. Certificate of Occupancy.** *"No building or structure shall be used or occupied and no change in the existing use or occupancy classification of a building or structure or portion thereof shall be made until the Building Official has issued a Certificate of Occupancy therefor as provided in this Code."*
>
> **Section 207. Duties of a Building Official.** *"In his respective territorial jurisdiction, the Building Official shall be primarily responsible for the enforcement of the provisions of this Code… In the performance of his duties, a Building Official may enter any building or its premises at all reasonable times to inspect and determine compliance with the requirements of this Code… When any building work is found to be contrary to the provisions of this Code, the Building Official may order the work stopped… Likewise, the Building Official is authorized to order the discontinuance of the occupancy or use of any building or structure or portion thereof found to be occupied or used contrary to the provisions of this Code."*
>
> **Section 215. Abatement of Dangerous Buildings.** *"When any building or structure is found or declared to be dangerous or ruinous, the Building Official shall order its repair, vacation or demolition depending upon the degree of danger to life, health, or safety…"*
>
> **Section 213. Penal Provisions.** *"It shall be unlawful for any person, firm or corporation, to erect, construct, enlarge, alter, repair, move, improve, remove, convert, demolish, equip, use, occupy, or maintain any building or structure or cause the same to be done contrary to or in violation of any provision of this Code."*

Section 301 governs the **building**. Section 309 governs the **occupancy** — and it is an independent requirement: a structure that was never permitted cannot have passed the final inspection and Certificate of Completion that Sections 308 and 309 make the condition of a Certificate of Occupancy. Each day such a structure is *used or occupied* is a continuing state of non-compliance that Section 207 empowers the Building Official to bring to an end.

**7. The scope of what I am asserting — stated precisely, and no further.** I ask the Department to note the limits of my own claim, so that its inquiry is not misdirected:

  (a) As to the **structure in Barangay 1 occupied by Mr. Antonio Teope**, the absence of a recent building permit is **admitted in writing by the Building Official himself** (paragraph 5). No Certificate of Occupancy has ever been shown to exist for it.
  (b) As to the **other private and informal structures — residential and commercial — standing on the estate's registered parcels**, I do not assert from my own knowledge that each is unpermitted. I assert that **no permit, no Certificate of Occupancy, and no instrument of right has ever been produced to the heirs for any of them**, and that the office that holds the register has not answered on any of them. Which of them are permitted is a fact that only the Building Official's own register can establish, and that is a principal object of this request.
  (c) As to structures associated with **titled adverse claimants** on estate land, I am informed that permits may have issued. If they did, the question this Department is in a position to reach is a different one: whether **Section 302(2)** could lawfully have been satisfied, given that the certified true copy of the title covering that ground stands in the heirs' names and no lease from the heirs exists.
  (d) I make **no request for demolition by any private hand, and none for self-help of any kind.** Every remedy sought here is an order of the proper officer, made after that officer's own inspection and due process.

**8. What is respectfully requested.** That the Secretary, in the exercise of the powers of supervision and control conferred by Sections 201 and 203, and over the officer designated under Section 205, be pleased to direct the Building Official of Mercedes, Camarines Norte:

  **(1)** To **conduct an ocular inspection** under Section 207 of the structures standing on the parcels scheduled at Annex "C", beginning with the structure in Barangay 1 that is the subject of his own letter of 21 October 2025, and to render a written inspection report;

  **(2)** To **determine and state in writing, from his own register, for each such structure**: whether a building permit was issued and under what number and date; whether a Certificate of Occupancy was issued under Section 309; and, where a permit issued, what document was submitted in satisfaction of **Section 302(2)**;

  **(3)** Where no building permit exists, to take the enforcement action Sections 207 and 301 commit to him — **notice of violation, order to stop work, and where a structure is used or occupied without a Certificate of Occupancy, an order of discontinuance of occupancy** — and to proceed under **Section 215** as to any structure found dangerous or ruinous;

  **(4)** To **report to this Department in writing** what action has been taken, and why none was taken in the ten months following his letter of 21 October 2025;

and further, that this Department:

  **(5)** **Exercise its own supervisory inquiry** into the non-enforcement described above, through the NBCDO and/or the Regional Office and District Engineering Office, including such **administrative action under Section 212** as the facts may warrant; and

  **(6)** **Advise the undersigned in writing** of the action taken, at the address below.

I do not ask this Department to adjudicate ownership, and nothing here requires it to. The heirs' registered title is not in issue before you. What is in issue is that construction and occupation are proceeding on registered private land, that the officer charged with the Code's enforcement has admitted in writing that no permit was issued for at least one such structure, and that for ten months nothing has followed.
