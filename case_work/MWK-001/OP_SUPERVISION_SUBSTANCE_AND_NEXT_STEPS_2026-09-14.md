## 2. Where the procedure is exposed (this is why there is no case number)

1. **ARTA's own notices route the remedy through A.O. 22, not "supervision".** Every Litigation Division transmittal (gmail 42, 38220, 102526) says: no motion for reconsideration; "file a Notice of Appeal with the Office of the President within fifteen (15) days … thereafter the Rules of Procedure of the said office will govern."
2. **A.O. 22 s. 2011 has never been complied with on any of the five.** §2 (notice + proof of service on agency and affected parties + fee), §3 (₱1,500 within the appeal period), §5 (perfection = notice + fee + memorandum), §6 (memorandum within 30 days), §7 (memorandum contents incl. certified copy of the resolution), §8 (non-payment / defective memorandum = ground for dismissal). No fee was ever tendered; each filing says the fee "will be paid upon notice". Proof of service on the individual respondents is present on the manifestations but the 1321 petition shows only "copy furnished: DG, ARTA".
3. **No evidence ARTA transmitted records** (§4, within 10 days of the notice of appeal). Nothing in the corpus shows ARTA acknowledging any OP filing.
4. **Routing.** The 5 May, 4 Jun and 23 Jul filings were hand-filed at the **Malacañang Records Office** (stamped transmittals). MRO's only reply (gmail 53, 6 May) says the matter was "forwarded to the concerned OP delivery unit / action office" — unnamed. The 1321 packet was shipped by LBC with **PACE (Presidential Action Center)** as consignee. PACE is the public-assistance/complaints desk (E.O. 15 s. 2023); its charter process is to refer to the agency concerned, i.e. back to ARTA. Appeals are decided by the Executive Secretary through the **Deputy Executive Secretary for Legal Affairs** (A.O. 22 §17). Four months without a docket is consistent with the filings sitting on the PACE/referral track, or being held as unperfected appeals.
5. **Representation.** Every filing is signed by Jonathan as attorney-in-fact (US citizen, non-lawyer). `matters.lead_counsel` for MWK-OP-PETITION = Jonathan; no counsel exists on any ARTA docket.
