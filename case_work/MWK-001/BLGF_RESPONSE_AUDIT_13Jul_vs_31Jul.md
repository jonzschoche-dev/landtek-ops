## 2. The §VII trap they walked into

§VII said, in advance and in writing: an acknowledgment, a referral back to the
Municipal Treasurer, or an instruction to get records from the supervised office
**"will not answer this request."** What happened:

- Manlapaz (17 Jul) **referred it to Macale** for written explanation — the exact
  disqualified move — and issued **no independent determination** of his own
  (Question §I.1–4 to him: unanswered).
- BLGF **adopted the referred-back explanation wholesale**, annexes unexamined by
  the taxpayer, and issued no independent technical position on any §V question.
- No docket/control number in the body, no action officer identified, no statement
  of corrective action — the six §VII items, none supplied.
- §VII closed: failure to give a substantive supervisory disposition "will be
  documented as part of the record concerning whether the available supervisory
  mechanisms acted after receiving actual notice." **That record is now made.**

## 3. Misstatements / strawmen in the BLGF reply

1. **Strawman on Annex A**: proves 7 Mar receipt — a fact the request conceded at
   §III.7. Used to imply the withholding claim was refuted; it wasn't addressed.
2. **"Updated statements were furnished during previous transactions"** — answers
   2023–2025 history; the complaint was the **10 Dec 2025 → 7 Mar 2026** withholding
   of the CY 2026 assessment after two written requests. Not engaged.
3. **"Addressed by ARTA"** — ARTA's own 21 May 2026 letter (Attachment 1 to the
   request) **expressly preserved** RPT liability, payment validity, and the
   propriety of the Treasurer's actions, and said its closure must not be read as
   permitting withholding of records. BLGF cites the closure while ignoring the
   preservation — of a document attached to the very request it was answering.
4. **Ordinance switch**: reply rests on Ord. 22-2024 without acknowledging that the
   Treasurer's own cited authority was Ord. 2-2004 §208 — the exact discrepancy
   Question A2–A3 flagged.
5. **"Payment credited only on 5 and 7 Apr"** — treats crediting as the whole story;
   never mentions the LGU could not identify its own incoming credit and issued ORs
   only 7 Jul (E13–E15), nor the taxpayer's reliance on the assessment's stated
   balance.

## 4. Strongest unanswered points, ranked for escalation

1. **C8/C9 — installment interest.** On 7 Apr 2026 only the Q1 installment (due
   31 Mar) was delinquent; Sec. 250 gives a right to quarterly payment; interest on
   the entire annual tax, from January, has no visible LGC basis. The reconstructed
   overcharge: interest component ₱4,111.86 vs. ~₱257 (2% × Q1 ≈ ₱12,849.55 × 2% ×
   1 mo). BLGF's silence here is total.
2. **A1–A4 — nobody has identified the governing ordinance consistently.** Three
   citations in the record. A charge whose legal basis changes per letter is not yet
   a lawful charge.
3. **D11–D12 — Sec. 249 publication/posting proof.** If absent, the
   discount-forfeiture and delinquency framework for 2026 wobbles at the root.
4. **F17–F19 — the 13-ARP aggregate on one ARP** — no rule cited, schedule never
   required.
5. **B5–B7 — RA 6713 causation** — the Dec 10/Dec 30 letters give it teeth: the LGU
   was warned in writing, mid-discount-period, that its silence was consuming the
   discount.
