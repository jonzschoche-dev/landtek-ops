## I. Referral down is not oversight

The Province supervises its component municipalities. Under Section 25(a) of R.A. No. 7160,
general supervision over municipalities is exercised "through the province". Section 29
requires the province, through the governor, to "ensure that every component city and
municipality within its territorial jurisdiction acts within the scope of its prescribed
powers and functions" (see also Section 465(b)(2)(i)). The Provincial Assessor exercises
"technical supervision and visitorial functions over all component city and municipal
assessor[s]" (Section 472(b)(12)). The Provincial Treasurer exercises "technical supervision
over all treasury offices of component cities and municipalities" (Section 470(d)(6)).

On the record, the Province has not used that mandate. It has sent each matter back down to
the Municipality whose conduct is the subject of the complaint. The DILG–Camarines Norte
packet of letters dated 2 September 2026, released and emailed to me on 7 September 2026
(Annex "A"), shows this:

1. DILG forwarded my complaints to the Provincial Government in March 2026 (referrals of 16
   and 17 March) and again on 4 June 2026. The Province reviewed them and **referred them to
   LGU Mercedes on jurisdictional grounds**.
2. On 23 June 2026 the Provincial ARTA Focal, Mr. Teodoro L. Licarte, sent ARTA Referral
   **CTN SL-2026-0423-1891** to the Mayor for "appropriate actions", describing it as within
   **LGU Mercedes jurisdiction**. The transmittal is **Noted by Governor Padilla**. That
   referral concerns the Mercedes Committee on Anti-Red Tape, which the Mayor chairs.
3. On 2 September 2026 PD Relucio asked the Provincial Legal Officer to update me
   **directly** on the action taken on my March 2026 letter-complaints. I have received no
   such update.

The Provincial Assessor wrote to me on 1 July 2026: "Rest assured that all your requested
relief will be replied with upon my return on Monday, July 6, 2026." I sent the certified
titles he asked for on 2 July. I have received nothing further. The Provincial Treasurer
received my Formal Protest and Supervisory Request of 13 July 2026 and asked the Municipal
Treasurer to explain. The Municipal Treasurer replied to the Provincial Treasurer, not to me,
and I have received no written answer from that Office.

MLGOO Guerrero is not a subject of this letter. His Indorsements of 25 and 26 August 2026
drew written replies from the Mayor and the Sangguniang Bayan Secretary on 1 September 2026.
Those replies left the particulars below open. Completing them is the Province's role.

## II. Demands, within ten (10) working days of receipt

Please issue written directives to LGU Mercedes, under your respective mandates and with a
definite compliance period that you set, requiring the following in writing.

**1. Session records and fee assessment.** The Sangguniang Bayan Secretary should issue a
written fee assessment, the adoption status of each session, and a release or inspection date
for the session records of 21/22 January, 4 February and 11 February 2026. Her 1 September 2026
reply (Annex "C") says the records "are being verified and consolidated" and will be released
upon payment of prescribed fees. This confirms that the records exist, but she has assessed no
fee. I will pay any lawful fee the day it is assessed. (Sections 469(c)(5) and (7), R.A. 7160.)

**2. Deed identification.** The Mayor should identify the specific instrument on which the
Municipality's claim to the Municipal Hall compound rests: date, donor, notarial details,
acceptance resolution, and registration or annotation status. He should also produce the
original, the notarial protocol copy, or the registered original for inspection. DILG's
Supplemental Indorsement of 26 August 2026 (Annex "B") required this. On 1 September 2026
(Annex "D") the Mayor answered only "a valid Deed of Donation". The memorandum of encumbrances
on TCT No. T-32911 (Annex "E") shows no donation or municipal transfer. This is identification
only. Ownership and validity remain with the courts.

**3. Street-naming enactment.** The Sangguniang Bayan Secretary should state the number and
date of the enactment by which the street-naming matter pending since May 2025 "has already
been acted upon", and furnish its minutes. If no such enactment exists, she should state that
in writing.

**4. Fisheries-construction fact-finding on the T-32911 compound.** The Municipality should
state whether works are proceeding on the compound parcel covered by TCT No. T-32911. It should
also state which office or entity is building and funding them, under what building permit and
appropriation, and under what claimed authority as to the land, including how the use fits the
purpose of whatever instrument is named under item 2. This is a fact-finding request only. It
is not a demand for compensation or for any determination of a taking date.

**5. Donated road lots.** On 1 September 2026 the Mayor wrote that the roads are "actively
devoted to their intended public road purpose" and that there is "no statutory ground" for
administrative action. A road covered by a house is not devoted to road purpose. **Direct the
Mayor (and the Building Official as appropriate) to state in writing, lot by lot among the
donated road lots:**
  (a) which portions remain open for public road use;
  (b) which portions are occupied by structures, by whom, since when;
  (c) which of those structures have building permits; and
  (d) what administrative action under §§17 and 21 (and PD 1096) has been taken or will be
      taken, with dates — or a written justification for taking none.

**6. ARTA CTN SL-2026-0423-1891 / CART.** The Province referred 1891 to the Mayor on 23 June
2026 as a matter of LGU jurisdiction. On 1 September 2026 the Mayor wrote that "there are no
further administrative actions required or appropriate on the part of the LGU at this stage."
That is a refusal of the duty under Section 444(b)(1)(x) of R.A. 7160 to "cause to be
instituted administrative or judicial proceedings against any official or employee of the
municipality who may have committed an offense in the performance of his official duties".
Referral to outside fora does not suspend that duty. Having sent 1891 to the Mayor, the
Province should require him to act on it. **Direct the Mayor to state in writing what
proceedings he has instituted, or will institute within a definite period the Province sets,
against the municipal officials concerned — or the specific legal justification for
instituting none, provision by provision.**

**7. Provincial Assessor.** Under Section 472(b)(12), please issue a written directive to the
Municipal Assessor of Mercedes. It should require her to render the records service paid for on
20 June 2025 (O.R. No. 7383466; Annex "F"), or to state in writing the specific legal ground
for each record withheld. Please also complete the verification your Office undertook on
1 July 2026 (Annex "G"), and assess any lawful fee for payment.

**8. Provincial Treasurer.** Please answer in writing the Formal Protest and Supervisory
Request of 13 July 2026 (Annex "H") on ARP No. 001-00249, item by item. Under Section 470(d)(6),
please also direct the Municipal Treasurer to issue her written decision on that protest under
Section 252, or to certify that none has issued.

**Copies to me.** For every item, please furnish me with each directive on the day it issues
and each municipal reply on the day it is received.

A further endorsement that returns the file to Mercedes without a written directive and a
compliance period does not satisfy this demand. Section 5(a) of R.A. No. 6713 separately
requires a reply within fifteen (15) working days stating the action taken.

## III. What this letter does not ask

It does not ask any Office to decide ownership of any parcel or the validity of any
instrument, and it asserts no taking date. It makes no accusation against MLGOO Guerrero.
