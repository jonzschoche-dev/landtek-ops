## IV. GROUNDS — THE STATUTORY DUTIES INVOKED

I quote the statute rather than paraphrase it, so that no one can later say the duty was
unclear.

**Section 25(a), R.A. No. 7160.** The President's general supervision over local governments
is exercised "*through the province with respect to component cities and municipalities*".

**Section 29.** "*The province, through the governor, shall ensure that every component city
and municipality within its territorial jurisdiction acts within the scope of its prescribed
powers and functions.*"

**Section 465(b)(2)(i).** The governor shall "*Ensure that the acts of the component cities and
municipalities of the province and of its officials and employees are within the scope of
their prescribed powers, duties and functions*"; and, (iii), "*Issue such executive orders for
the faithful and appropriate enforcement and execution of laws and ordinances.*"

**Section 472(b).** The provincial assessor shall "*(1) Ensure that all laws and policies
governing the appraisal and assessment of real properties for taxation purposes are properly
executed*"; "*(5) Prepare, install and maintain a system of tax mapping …*"; "*(6) Conduct
frequent physical surveys to verify and determine whether all real properties within the
province are properly listed in the assessment rolls*"; "*(9) Issue, upon request of any
interested party, certified copies of assessment records of real property and all other
records relative to its assessment, upon payment of a service charge or fee to the
treasurer*"; and "*(12) In the case of the provincial assessor, exercise technical supervision
and visitorial functions over all component city and municipal assessor, coordinate with
component city or municipal assessors in the conduct of tax mapping operations and all other
assessment activities, and provide all forms of assistance therefor.*"

**Section 470(d)(6).** "*In the case of the provincial treasurer, exercise technical supervision
over all treasury offices of component cities and municipalities.*"

**Section 200.** The province is "*primarily responsible for the proper, efficient and
effective administration of the real property tax.*"

**Section 61(b).** "*A complaint against any elective official of a municipality shall be
filed before the sangguniang panlalawigan whose decision may be appealed to the Office of the
President.*" The Province is not merely a supervisor of
Mercedes; it is the forum that disciplines its Mayor.

**R.A. No. 6713, Section 5(a).** "*All public officials and employees shall, within fifteen
(15) working days from receipt thereof, respond to letters, telegrams or other means of
communications sent by the public. The reply must contain the action taken on the request.*"

**R.A. No. 3019, Section 3(f).** It is a corrupt practice for a public officer to be
"*Neglecting or refusing, after due demand or request, without sufficient justification, to
act within a reasonable time on any matter pending before him … for the purpose of favoring
his own interest or giving undue advantage in favor of or discriminating against any other
interested party.*"

**Civil Code, Article 27.** "*Any person suffering material or moral loss because a public
servant or employee refuses or neglects, without just cause, to perform his official duty
may file an action for damages and other relief against the latter, without prejudice to any
disciplinary administrative action that may be taken.*"

"Local autonomy" is not an answer to any of these. Autonomy is the Municipality's freedom to
act **within** its powers. Sections 29 and 465 exist precisely for the case where it does not.
A supervisor who invokes the supervised unit's autonomy to excuse the supervised unit's
breach has not deferred; he has abdicated.

## V. GROUNDS — REFERRAL IS NOT SUPERVISION

The record now documents a closed circle in writing. ARTA referred to the DILG and the CSC.
DILG referred to the Province. The Province referred to the Municipality. The Municipality
routed the matter to its Committee on Anti-Red Tape — chaired by the Mayor, with the
Municipal Assessor sitting on it — which resolved (CART Resolution No. 6, s. 2026) that the LGU "shall not release, provide, or
act upon the requested documents … unless and until he sufficiently establishes and submits
proof of his legal personality or authority."" The complaint about the committee was then referred, by the Province, to the
committee's chairman.

No one in that circle has to coordinate for it to work. Each office need only decline, and
the file returns to the officials complained of. That is why this petition is written the way
it is: **a further endorsement that returns the file to Mercedes without a written directive,
a period for compliance, and a stated consequence for default will be treated as a refusal to
supervise after due demand, and will be bound as an exhibit with that title.**
