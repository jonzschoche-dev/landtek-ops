   or documents establishing the legal bases for those government occupancies" (PNP Station, Municipality,

   notices (T4497_TAKING_RECOVERY_SPINE): the land-records custodian disclaims any instrument for the
   occupancies.

   decides nothing about T-32911, T-4497, Psd-221861 or OCT 2018000090.
