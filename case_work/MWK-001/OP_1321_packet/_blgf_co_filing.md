Dear Executive Director Agcaoili:

I write as Attorney-in-Fact for Patricia Keesey Zschoche, an heir of the assessed owner of the properties covered by ARP No. 001-00249, Municipality of Mercedes, Camarines Norte. The Bureau supervises both the **local treasury** and the **local assessment** operations of that Municipality (Section 470, R.A. No. 7160; Section 43, Chapter 10, Title II, Book IV, Administrative Code, and E.O. No. 127, §4). This letter respectfully asks the Central Office to look at both — because at every level below it, supervision has so far consisted of declining to supervise.

**1. The computation no office has yet examined — the sharpest, smallest question in this file.** The 2026 interest charge of the Municipal Treasurer was computed at 2% per month on the **entire annual tax, from January 2026** — four months, ₱4,111.86 — although Section 250 of the Code gives the taxpayer the right to pay in quarterly installments, and on the dates payment was credited (5 and 7 April 2026) only the **first-quarter installment** (due 31 March) was past due. Interest lawfully computed on that installment for that month is approximately **₱257**. The charge imposed is roughly **sixteen times** that figure, and no office — municipal, provincial, or regional — has cited any provision of the Code authorizing annual-tax-from-January interest. The governing ordinance itself has been cited **three different ways in three letters** (Ordinance No. 2-2004; No. 31-2015; No. 22-2024, §208): a charge whose legal basis changes with each letter is not yet a lawful charge. Nor has any office produced the **Section 249** publication and posting proof for the 2026 schedule on which the discount forfeiture and delinquency framework depend.

**2. What Regional Office No. V actually disposed of.** My Formal Protest and Supervisory Request of 13 July 2026 put **nineteen numbered questions and record requests** to the Regional Office and the Provincial Treasurer. The Regional Director's letter of 31 July 2026 (released 14 August) answered **none of them**. It did not reach the computation in paragraph 1. It did not examine the documented withholding of the CY 2026 assessment itself — requested in writing on **10 December 2025**, with written notice on **30 December 2025** that the silence was consuming the discount period, released only on **7 March 2026** after an ARTA complaint (CTN SL-2026-0128-1210) had been filed against the Mayor and the Treasurer — a six-week, docketed withholding the letter set aside as "administrative," "previously acted upon," and "distinct." And it accepted the Treasurer's assertion that statements of account were "always" furnished without examining the dated record that contradicts it. I resubmitted the complete 37-page request to the Regional Office on **15 August 2026**; it was acknowledged on 17 August as "referred to the concerned personnel," and nothing has issued since.

**3. The Section 252 period has now lapsed.** The 2026 principal of ₱41,118.56 was paid **under protest** on 7 July 2026 (O.R. Nos. 0434682–84, so annotated), with a written protest and demand for reconsideration of the interest and forfeited-discount components filed the same day. The Municipal Treasurer's sixty-day period to decide under **Section 252(a)** expired on or about **5 September 2026 without any decision**. The taxpayer is proceeding to the statutory remedies; but the lapse itself is a supervision fact: a treasurer under the Bureau's supervision received a lawful protest, official payment under protest, and sixty days — and issued nothing.

**4. The assessment chain shows the same pattern.** The Municipal Assessor of Mercedes has left a **Citizen's-Charter records service (2–3 days published time) unrendered for fifteen months**, the ₱300.00 research fee having been officially received on 20 June 2025 (O.R. No. 7383466) — while the **Provincial Assessor's own office** compiled and released to the taxpayer the very record classes involved and confirmed in writing (26 January 2026) that the research was "done." The Provincial Assessor, who exercises technical supervision over the municipal assessor under **Section 472**, committed in writing on 1 July 2026 to act on the taxpayer's escalation — and has issued nothing in the two months since. The result: at the municipal level the service is refused; at the provincial level the supervisor is silent; and the two offices' records for the same barangay remain unreconciled.

**5. What I respectfully request.** Not a re-litigation of any office's conclusion — an exercise of the supervision only the Central Office can now supply:

  (a) **Rule on, or refer for authoritative ruling, the interest computation** in paragraph 1 (annual-tax-from-January versus the Section 250/255 installment basis), and require production of the certified ordinance provision and the Section 249 proof on which the 2026 charges rest;
  (b) **Review Regional Office No. V's disposition** of the 13 July request — specifically its non-response to the nineteen questions of record — and direct that the pending 15 August resubmission be acted upon with written, itemized answers;
  (c) **Note the Section 252 lapse** and issue appropriate guidance to the Municipal Treasurer of Mercedes on the timely decision of protests, the timely release of assessments and statements of account, and responses to written taxpayer inquiries;
  (d) **On the assessment side**, issue appropriate guidance or a directive through the proper channel so that the fifteen-month-old chartered records request is at last acted upon and the municipal-provincial records for Barangay 5 reconciled; and
  (e) Favor me with a written response, and with the case or reference number under which this request is recorded.

This request is made without prejudice to, and with express reservation of, all rights and remedies under R.A. No. 7160 and other laws — including the appeal now proceeding from the Section 252 lapse. Nothing herein concedes the validity of any charge or of any factual assertion in the documents referred to. I can furnish the complete documentary set — the 13 July request and its ten attachments, the Regional Office's letter, the Treasurer's 21 July 2026 explanation, the protest and official receipts, and the assessor-chain correspondence — upon request, and much of it is already in the Regional Office's record.

Supervision that can never issue guidance is not supervision. I ask the Central Office to issue some.
