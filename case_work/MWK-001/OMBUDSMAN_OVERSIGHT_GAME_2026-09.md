> Law quoted verbatim from `legal_chunks`; legal conclusions NEEDS-COUNSEL.

## 1. The move in one line

**The Petition converts the supervisors' silence from "a gap" into "a refusal after due
demand." The Ombudsman is where a refusal after due demand becomes a case against the
person who refused.** Everything below is about making that conversion credible enough
that they act before it is needed — and executable on schedule if they do not.

## 2. Why the Ombudsman, and not another petition

The existing game (GAME_THEORY §1) is buck-passing: each supervisor's dominant move is
Deflect because P(this lands on *me personally*) ≈ 0. The Petition raises that
probability by naming and dating each office's non-action. The Ombudsman is the only
forum in the chain that:

1. **Reaches every player at once.** R.A. 6770 §21: "*disciplinary authority over all
   elective and appointive officials of the Government and its subdivisions … local
   government*" — Governor, PLO, Provincial Assessor, Provincial Treasurer, Mayor, Abla,
   Macale, Olaguera, in one caption. (Impeachable officials, Congress, Judiciary excepted.)
2. **Carries the one sanction untouchable men fear.** §24: the Ombudsman "*may
   preventively suspend any officer or employee under his authority pending an
   investigation, if … the evidence of guilt is strong, and (a) the charge … involves
   dishonesty, oppression or grave misconduct or neglect in the performance of duty*" —
   up to six months, without pay. An elected Governor or Mayor suspended for neglect of
   duty is a headline, not a letter.
3. **Cannot be deflected back down.** There is no "LGU jurisdiction" below the
   Ombudsman to refer to. Deflect leaves the strategy set.
4. **Is already seized of Mercedes.** Docket **IC-OC-JUL-26-1214** (*Zschoche et al. v.
   Torralba et al.*, the ARTA-1212 referral; 1st Indorsement 24 Aug 2026, Deputy
   Ombudsman for Luzon, CREMEB). A supervision complaint files as a **related case**, not
   a fresh void — consistent with GAME_THEORY §2 "STOP adding forums; consolidate."

## 4. What the Ombudsman filing needs per respondent (the elements, honestly)

Two tracks, different burdens:

**Administrative (neglect of duty / conduct prejudicial; R.A. 6713 §5(a)).** No motive
required. Elements: (i) a duty, (ii) a dated request or directive engaging it, (iii)
non-performance beyond the statutory period, (iv) no justification. **This is the strong
track against supervisors.**

**Criminal (R.A. 3019 §3(f)).** "*Neglecting or refusing, after due demand or request,
without sufficient justification, to act within a reasonable time on any matter pending
before him for the purpose of … favoring his own interest or giving undue advantage in
favor of or discriminating against any other interested party.*" The **purpose element**
is the hard one. Against the Mayor it is documented (his 1 Sep motive admission: the SPA
condition exists because the claim touches "the very parcel of land upon which the
Municipal Hall itself is situated"). Against the Governor it rests on the "Mercedes vote"
advice — **hearsay; do not plead it as fact**. Plead §3(f) against the municipal officers;
plead administrative neglect against the provincial ones; reserve §3(f) upward.

**Why officers first, Governor second.** (i) Their cases are stronger (own written
commitments; §5(a) is self-proving). (ii) It gives the Governor the cheapest possible
exit — direct his officers — while the fear is at maximum. (iii) A Governor watching his
own Assessor and Treasurer answer an Ombudsman complaint about a matter he could have
closed with one memo is more embarrassed, and more motivated, than a Governor who is
already a respondent and has nothing left to protect by acting. (iv) It keeps the "velvet
separating device" (GAME_THEORY §3): a controlled litigant who files against the officers
and *invites* the Governor to be the exception is not a crank.

## 6. What filing does to each payoff (the point of the exercise)

- **Deflect stops being free.** Once a related-case complaint exists, every further
  referral-down is an exhibit in a live Ombudsman docket, not a letter in a drawer.
- **Personal cost attaches per person.** Ombudsman respondents answer with their own
  counter-affidavits, on their own time, at their own expense; the Provincial Legal
  Officer cannot defend an Assessor in his personal capacity. Naming beats pesos
  (GAME_THEORY §7).
- **Time reverses.** Under §3(f) and neglect-of-duty, each month after due demand
  re-completes the elements; delay now costs *them*, not us (GAME_THEORY §5).
- **The "Mercedes vote" premise collapses.** Politics protects an official from his
  superior; it does not protect him from the Ombudsman. That is precisely why the
  Governor's cheapest move after this memo becomes "act, and be seen to act."
- **Preventive suspension is the fear, not the plan.** We do not pray for it against the
  provincial officers (evidence-of-guilt-strong + prejudice to the case is a high bar);
  its mere availability under §24 is what a Governor's lawyer will tell him about.

## 7. Their best responses and our pre-built counters

1. **Token directive** ("we have again endorsed to the Municipality for appropriate
   action") → Petition Part V already defines this as refusal; fire the "partial ≠
   compliance" reply same day; it becomes Exhibit 1 in the Ombudsman filing.
2. **Legal-personality exit** (the all-heirs SPA, now at the provincial level) → the
   Province's own Memo 2024-01 §5; Art. 487; the apostilled SPA; the OP is seized of the
   same threshold practice (six dockets consolidated). Expensive for them to invoke twice.
3. **"Sub judice / for the courts"** → Petition VIII: nothing asked touches ownership;
   records, identification and directives are ministerial.
4. **Retaliation** (levy on the ₱14k, permit harassment, a defamation letter over
   "fraud") → document; the cover letter's "fraud" sentence is anchored to four documented
   components; the Petition uses no such word. Keep it that way in every filing.
5. **Delay the Ombudsman itself** (years) → irrelevant to the game: the payoff is at
   filing (personal exposure attaches at filing), not at decision. The OP track is the
   fast one; the Ombudsman track is the heavy one; run both.
6. **The Governor acts** (the desired branch) → credit in writing to him, copied to OP,
   DILG, BLGF; the officers' complaint is *not* withdrawn (threats withdrawn are
   discounted forever) but the Governor's directive is filed with it as the Province's
   answer.

## 9. Do-nots

- Do not name the Governor as Ombudsman respondent in the first filing — it wastes the
  embarrassment and removes his exit.
- Do not plead the "Mercedes vote" as a fact anywhere; it is a reported opinion, used once,
  in the cover letter, framed as something the Governor can disprove.
- Do not open a new forum; file as related to IC-OC-JUL-26-1214 and as a supplement to the
  pending OP proceeding (Ref. 050526-MRO-234187).
- Do not file the CSC-Olaguera / Abla instruments during the same week without checking
  the operator hold (CSC offensive ON HOLD pending guardianship, BLGF spine §5-A) — the
  Ombudsman play above is against *supervisors* and rides the Petition, not the CSC suite.
- Do not assert any taking date or Hall-parcel compensation in the Ombudsman narrative
  (Botor's lane) — the supervision complaint is about records, identification and
  directives, and is stronger for it.

## 10. One sentence

Serve the Petition so the silence becomes a refusal after due demand; give the Province
fifteen working days to be the exception; then file against the officers who refused, in
the Ombudsman docket that already exists, and let the Governor decide whether his name
appears in the narrative or on the caption.
