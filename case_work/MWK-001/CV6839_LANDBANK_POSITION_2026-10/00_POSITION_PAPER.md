## 3. The position
1. **The judgment governs.** Just compensation is a judicial determination; once final it is immutable (F3). Land Bank is a judgment debtor ordered "to immediately pay". It may verify the payee; it may not add conditions the judgment does not contain.
2. **Land Bank's own Charter caps its asks (RA 11032 §21(b)).** Its letter adds, beyond Annex C (F5): a San Vicente tax clearance up to May 1995; registration of the settlement with eCAR and proof of publication; civil-registry certificates; a two-year heir's bond; an "updated" SPA (the Charter says an SPA has no expiry). §21(b): "Imposition of additional requirements other than those listed in the Citizen's Charter" is a violation. (Subject to F6: confirm pages G-11–G-14 before relying on this in any complaint.)
3. **Each co-heir's share is separate.** The award belongs to the co-heirs under the 1993 extrajudicial settlement. Civil Code Art. 1207: concurrence of creditors does not give each a right to the whole, and solidarity is not presumed; Art. 1208: the credit is presumed divided into equal shares. Patricia may demand her one-third without the others. "All heirs" must act together is not the law.
4. **Delay is theirs to pay for.** The Order runs interest "until full payment". Every requirement that is not in the judgment or the Charter adds interest at public expense.
5. **The Cesar payments are Land Bank's to account for.** It paid an agent (SPA of 22 Aug 1991) on T-4501/4502/4503. The heirs are entitled to the record of what was paid, when, in what form, and to whom.

## 4. What this position does NOT claim (guardrails)
- Not that the owner's duplicate or the CISSC is improper: both ARE in the Charter (F5).
- Not that an estate settlement is unnecessary: the Charter lists one. Our point is that the 1993 extrajudicial settlement already exists and should satisfy it.
- No accusation of bad faith; no ARTA / COA / Ombudsman step now. Those wait for Land Bank's answer and the F6 check.
- Interest figures are not computed in the letter until the 2013/2023 point is fixed in court.
