1. **Substitution (Rule 3).**
   - The plaintiff of record acted through Cesar M. dela Fuente (now deceased) and earlier representatives; their authority has ended.
   - Patricia Keesey Zschoche, heir and co-owner under the 1993 extrajudicial settlement, asks to be substituted (or recognized as real party in interest) for her one-third share.
   - Attach: the 1993 settlement; death certificates of Mary Worrick Keesey and of Ernest Francis Keesey (the title reads "MWK m/t Ernest Francis Keesey"); Patricia's birth certificate showing filiation; her apostilled supplemental SPA authorizing this motion and receipt of proceeds.
2. **Clerical correction (nunc pro tunc).**
   - The Order reads 12% "until 30 June 2013" and 6% "from 1 July 2023". Ask the Court to correct "2023" to "2013" so that interest runs without a gap, consistent with the 12% period ending 30 June 2013 and the legal-rate change of 1 July 2013.
   - Ground: a clerical error apparent on the face of the order may be corrected at any time; it does not alter the judgment.
   - *Counsel to confirm the approach and supporting jurisprudence (not in corpus).*
3. **Writ of execution (Rule 39 §1).**
   - The judgment is final and executory; execution is a matter of right.
   - Pray for a writ directing Land Bank to pay Patricia's one-third of:
     - the unpaid principal (total ₱5,124,922.47 → ₱1,708,307.49 per one-third share [compute]);

     - in the cash/bond proportion "as provided by law" for the principal (RA 6657 §18).
   - Ask that Land Bank's compliance be limited to payee verification under its Citizen's Charter Annex C.
   - Alternative prayer: execution for the whole judgment for the benefit of all co-heirs, Patricia's share to be paid to her. *Counsel to weigh against the Art. 1207–1208 one-third route.*
