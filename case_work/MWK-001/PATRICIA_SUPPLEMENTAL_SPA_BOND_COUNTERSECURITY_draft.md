SPECIAL POWER OF ATTORNEY  
(Supplemental — Bond Counter-Security)

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

I, **PATRICIA KEESEY ZSCHOCHE**, of legal age, American citizen, residing at 8216 Pennington Drive, Huntington Beach, California 92646, U.S.A., do hereby name, constitute, and appoint my son, **JONATHAN PAUL ZSCHOCHE**, of legal age, American citizen, residing at Dasmariñas Street, Barangay 8, Daet, Camarines Norte, Philippines, as my true and lawful Attorney-in-Fact, for me and in my name, place, and stead, **solely and limited to** the following:

1. **Subject interest.** Only my **undivided one-third (1/3) ideal share** as co-owner in the Philippine real property interests described in Transfer Certificate of Title No. **T-32911** of the Registry of Deeds of Camarines Norte (and any derivative, successor, or substitute title covering the same parcel), and in any related tax declarations pertaining to that same share — **excluding** the shares of Geraldine Alice Teresita Keesey Hoppe and Marcia Ellen Keesey.

2. **Purpose.** To enable Jonathan Paul Zschoche to post, maintain, renew, or replace a **guardian’s bond under Rule 94** of the Rules of Court in **Special Proceeding No. 2680**, RTC Branch 41, Daet, Camarines Norte (*In the Matter of the Petition for Appointment of a Judicial Guardian of the Properties/Estates of Patricia Keesey Zschoche, Geraldine Alice Teresita Keesey Hoppe, and Marcia Ellen Keesey*), whether as **cash bond**, **surety bond**, or combination, in such amount as that Court may fix.

3. **Express special powers (Art. 1878).** I expressly authorize my Attorney-in-Fact to do any or all of the following with respect to **my undivided 1/3 only**, and only for the Purpose in ¶2:
   - (a) **Pledge, mortgage, hypothecate, assign, or otherwise encumber** my undivided 1/3 interest as **counter-security / collateral** in favor of any Insurance Commission–authorized surety or bonding company issuing the Rule 94 bond;
   - (b) Execute, sign, and deliver **indemnity agreements**, continuing guaranties, applications for surety, deeds of assignment, real-estate mortgages, pledges, undertakings, and all related instruments required by such surety or by the Court;
   - (c) Agree to terms, interest, premiums, and collateral-substitution customary for such bonds;
   - (d) Receive notices, pay premiums and lawful charges from funds I make available for that Purpose, and obtain releases/cancellations when the bond is discharged;
   - (e) Appear before notaries, the surety, the Clerk of Court / RTC Br. 41, and Registry of Deeds as needed to annotate or cancel any encumbrance limited to my 1/3.

4. **Hard limits.** This instrument does **not** authorize:
   - Encumbering or disposing of Geraldine’s or Marcia’s shares;
   - A sale, partition, or conveyance of the whole of T-32911;
   - Use of the Property as collateral for any obligation **other than** the Spec. Proc. 2680 Rule 94 bond (and any court-ordered **additional** bond in that same special proceeding, e.g. under Rule 95, if I later confirm in writing);
   - Waiver of my right to an accounting of the guardianship.

5. **Relation to prior SPA.** This Supplemental SPA is **in addition to**, and does not revoke, the Special Power of Attorney I executed on or about **18 May 2026** (California acknowledgment; Apostille No. **44327**). If any conflict arises on bond/collateral points, **this Supplemental controls** for that limited Purpose.

6. **Ratification.** I give my Attorney-in-Fact full power to do every act requisite for the Purpose as I might do if personally present, with power of substitution only for ministerial acts, and I ratify all that he shall lawfully do by virtue hereof.
