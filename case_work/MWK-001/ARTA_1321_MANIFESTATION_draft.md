## MANIFESTATION
*(Of matters of record contradicting the Resolution's factual premise; of the consequences of the Resolution as it stands; and with respectful invitation to exercise the Authority's Section 17(d) referral power — expressly NOT a motion for reconsideration)*

COMPLAINANT respectfully states:

**1.** Complainant received the Resolution dated 25 August 2026 on 27 August 2026. Complainant is aware that under Section 8, Rule IV of the 2023 ARTA Revised Rules of Procedure, Resolutions of the Authority are not subject to a motion for reconsideration, and **this Manifestation seeks none**. It places matters on the record, states what the Resolution — if left standing unqualified — will be understood to permit, and invites the exercise of powers the Authority retains regardless of the closure.

### I. WHAT THE RESOLUTION, AS IT STANDS, PERMITS

**2.** The Resolution finds that the requested service is chartered at **two (2) to three (3) days**; that Complainant **paid the research fee on 20 June 2025 (O.R. No. 7383466)**; and that the elapsed period *"would substantially exceed"* the charter period. It nonetheless recommends closure, crediting the Respondent's explanation that production was a *"tedious administrative undertaking"* rendering *"immediate delivery impossible."* With respect, if that disposition stands without qualification, the Authority will have accepted — as compatible with R.A. No. 11032 — three propositions no service office should ever hear from its regulator:

  (a) **That an official may misrepresent facts — even under oath — and the misrepresentation itself becomes "due cause."** The impossibility the Respondent swore to on 28 May 2026 was, by then, already disproven in writing: the Office of the **Provincial Assessor** had compiled and **released to this Complainant the very record classes at issue**, and had confirmed **in writing on 26 January 2026** that it had *"[d]one the research of all transactions"* — facts the Respondent had been told directly on 1 October 2025, and whose mechanism her own Response concedes (*"approval of our transaction are being done by the Provincial Assessors Office"*). A due-cause finding built on a disproven impossibility is not a finding; it is the ratification of the misrepresentation.

  (b) **That a hard, published deadline may be expanded indefinitely.** The Citizen's Charter says two to three days. The record now shows **more than fifteen (15) months, continuing as of this Manifestation** — a ratio of roughly **150 times** the published period — with no written extension, no notice of a definite date, and no end. If "complexity," untested against evidence, suffices to suspend a charter period without limit, then no charter period in the Philippines binds anyone: every deadline becomes an opening bid.

  (c) **That an office may accept official payment for a service and simply never render it.** The ₱300.00 research fee was received under **O.R. No. 7383466** on 20 June 2025. Fifteen months later the certified history, the historical declarations, the assessment records, and the tax maps remain unfurnished. The transaction the Authority's enabling law was written to protect — a citizen pays the published fee, the office renders the published service — is left half-executed in the government's favor, indefinitely, with the Authority's closure as its shield.

**3.** Complainant does not believe the Authority intends any of these propositions. But they are what the Resolution's silence will teach — to this Municipality first, and to every office that studies how this case ended.

### II. THE MATTERS OF RECORD (the comparator, in brief)

**4.** The following are documentary and annexed: the Respondent's letter of 16 June 2025 undertaking the full history *"from the oldest book we have (1948)"* while asserting it *"cannot possibly be done in just 15 working days"* (Annex "1"); the Provincial Assessor's Office's actual productions — the 1948-book historical declaration, the **Barangay 5 and Barangay 1 transaction-history ledgers (1950–2014)**, and the computerized Declarations of Real Property for Barangays 1, 3 and 5 bearing that Office's own approval and certification blocks (Annex "2", specimens; Annex "3", the Comparator Exhibit with the full timeline); Complainant's written notice to the Respondent of 1 October 2025 (Annex "4"); and the Respondent's Counter-Affidavit of 28 May 2026 swearing impossibility months after all of the foregoing (Annex "5"). **None of this comparator was addressed in the Resolution.**

### III. WHAT IS RESPECTFULLY ASKED (none of it reconsideration)

**5.** Complainant respectfully prays that the Authority:

  (a) **NOTE and ADMIT** this Manifestation and its annexes as part of the official record of CTN SL-2026-0209-1321;
  (b) **In the exercise of its own sound discretion, and motu proprio if it sees fit, RE-EVALUATE** the Resolution against the comparator — the Authority remaining at all times the master of its own dockets;
  (c) **EXERCISE ITS SECTION 17(d) POWER and REFER the Respondent to the CIVIL SERVICE COMMISSION** — as the Authority did, on this same Municipality, for the Municipal Engineer in CTN SL-2025-1008-0690/-1104-0792 on a record of unanswered letters alone. The present record is stronger by every measure: unanswered letters **plus** an official fee received for a service never rendered, **plus** a sworn justification contradicted by another government office's written confirmation and actual production. If the Balane record warranted referral, this record warrants it *a fortiori*; and
  (d) **STATE IN WRITING**, for Complainant's guidance, whether the Authority's position is that the three propositions in paragraph 2 are compatible with R.A. No. 11032 — so that Complainant may govern his remaining remedies accordingly.
