### 1. Engagement and scope of authority
The Principal appoints the Manager as her **attorney-in-fact and property manager**, with authority, for and
on behalf of the Principal as co-owner and **for the benefit of the co-ownership**, to:
1.1 **Manage, preserve and administer** the Estate Property; pay real-property and other taxes and record the
    same for reimbursement.
1.2 **Identify and engage occupants**; issue **demands** (pay-to-stay / demand for reasonable compensation /
    demand to vacate) and negotiate the terms of continued occupation or departure.
1.3 **Collect** reasonable compensation, rents, and other sums due for the use or occupation of the Estate
    Property; issue receipts; and **hold, allocate, and account** for the same per Section 3.
1.4 **Recover possession** of the Estate Property from occupants without right — the Principal being entitled,
    as one co-owner, to sue for and recover the **whole** for the benefit of all co-owners (Civil Code
    Art. 487) — and to **instruct and coordinate with counsel of record** for that purpose.
1.5 **Represent the Principal** before the LGU, Assessor, Registry of Deeds, BLGF, and other agencies as to
    records, tax declarations, and assessments of the Estate Property.

### 2. Express limits (do not exceed — the guardrails)
2.1 **No practice of law.** The Manager shall **not** file pleadings, appear in court, or render legal advice.
    All court actions are **filed by counsel of record with the Principal (co-owner) as party-plaintiff**;
    the Manager only coordinates, instructs, and funds.
2.2 **Authority ceiling = the Principal's own co-owner authority.** The Manager may do only what the Principal
    herself may do as a **1/3 co-owner**: administer, recover possession for all (Art. 487), and collect. The
    Manager **may NOT** bind the shares of the other co-owners, execute a lease of the whole exceeding one (1)
    year, or **sell, mortgage, or otherwise alienate** the Estate Property or any part — such acts require
    **separate specific written authority and, for the other shares, the guardianship court's authority.**
2.3 **Pay-to-stay is not a lease.** Any occupancy arrangement the Manager grants shall be month-to-month,
    terminable, without prejudice, and expressly **not a lease/tenancy** (to stay within co-owner authority).

### 3. Collection, trust, and accounting
3.1 The Manager collects for the account of the **co-ownership** and holds the sums **in trust**.
3.2 The Manager shall **segregate and account** the shares of the non-participating co-owners (Hoppe, Keesey),
    which may be **credited against their obligations to contribute to the Estate's preservation expenses and
    taxes (Civil Code Art. 488)** rather than remitted, per the Principal's instruction.
3.3 The Manager shall render a written accounting at least [quarterly], supported by receipts, through the
    Estate's reimbursement ledger.

### 4. Manager's compensation
4.1 **Management fee:** ₱[____] per month, OR [__]% of gross sums collected — [choose].
4.2 **Success fee:** [__]% of net proceeds actually recovered/collected for the Estate (income, compensation,
    or, upon separate authority, sale proceeds). *(This is LandTek's economics — the estate-recovery service fee.)*
4.3 Fees are chargeable against Estate proceeds; the Principal's own share bears her proportionate part.

### 5. Expenses and advances
The Manager may advance necessary expenses (records, filing, survey, service, counsel's fees); such advances
are **reimbursable from Estate proceeds as a first charge**, with interest as allowed (Art. 1912), and tracked
in the reimbursement ledger.

### 6. Fiduciary duties
The Manager shall act in good faith and in the Estate's best interest, **avoid self-dealing**, keep Estate
funds separate from its own, and preserve the Principal's provenance/accounting discipline.

### 7. Term and termination
Effective on signing; continues until the mission is complete or terminated by the Principal on [30] days'
written notice; the Manager's accrued fees, advances, and accounting survive termination.

### 8. Representations
The Principal represents she is a co-owner with authority to engage the Manager for her share and for
co-ownership administration/recovery (Art. 487). The Manager represents it is duly organized and is **not a
law firm.**

### 9. Governing law and venue
Republic of the Philippines; venue [Daet / Camarines Norte], without prejudice to the exclusive jurisdiction
of the courts over the underlying actions.
