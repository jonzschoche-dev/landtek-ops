Dear Mr. Guerrero:

Thank you for your letter of 31 July 2026 and for the coordination your Office undertook. I write to accept the express invitation in your letter — that should I obtain additional evidence I believe material, I may submit it — and to respectfully ask that this Office reconsider the premise on which it closed its action.

**1. The premise of the close-out is mistaken: what I have asked for is supervision, and supervision is this Department's own mandate.**

Your letter defers "the determination of the merits" to ARTA, the Office of the Ombudsman, and the Civil Service Commission. But those bodies adjudicate the **individual liability of officials**. What I placed before this Office is different: a pattern of **non-compliance by the Municipality with specific, ministerial duties of the Local Government Code** — and ensuring that local governments discharge the duties the Code imposes is precisely the **general supervision** the President exercises through this Department under **Section 25, R.A. No. 7160**, and through the Provincial Government for component municipalities. No ARTA docket, Ombudsman referral, or CSC case relieves the Department of that distinct function; those proceedings and this supervision run on separate tracks, about separate questions.

This is not my characterization alone — **it is ARTA's own, in writing.** In its Response to Manifestation dated 21 May 2026 (Atty. Rodolfo B. del Rosario, Jr., Chief, Litigation Division), reaffirmed in its Formal Response of 21 July 2026 (Undersecretary Geneses R. Abot, Deputy Director General for Legal), the Authority stated that its resolutions *"should not be read as a determination that the Municipal Treasurer's Office may lawfully refuse, withhold, or deny any document, receipt, or record to which a taxpayer or registered property owner may otherwise be entitled under applicable law,"* and that these matters *"involve substantive questions concerning local taxation, property records, and the administration of real property tax payments, which are **more properly addressed to the concerned local government unit, the appropriate supervisory authority, or other competent forum**."*

The **"appropriate supervisory authority"** over a municipality's compliance with the Local Government Code is this Department. So the present posture is this: your letter of 31 July defers these matters to ARTA — and ARTA has already, in writing, declined them as outside R.A. No. 11032 and pointed them **here**. Each office defers to the other; the ministerial duties go unperformed; and the citizen is left standing in the gap between two deferrals. That gap is precisely what Section 25 supervision exists to close, and I respectfully ask this Office to close it.

To be equally clear about what I am *not* asking: I do not ask this Office to adjudicate the ownership of any parcel, the validity of any donation, or any matter pending before a court. Those are expressly reserved to the courts and the Land Management Bureau. Every item below is a **compliance question under the Code** — answerable by directing an official to perform a statutory duty, or to state in writing the legal basis for not performing it.

**2. A material fact was omitted from the LGU comment your Office relied upon.**

Your close-out rests on the Mayor's written response of 24 July 2026, which represented that five of seven ARTA complaints were "recommended for closure," attaching the recommendations as proof. That is half of each document. **In the same one-page dispositive portions, ARTA referred LGU personnel onward for possible violations of law:**

- **Engr. Erwin H. Balane** (Municipal Engineer) → **Civil Service Commission RO-V**, alleged violation of R.A. 6713 §5(a) (CTN SL-2025-1008-0690 / -1104-0792; endorsement 30 April 2026);
- **Hon. Princess B. Torralba** (Councilor) and **Mr. Tony Teope** (Assistant to the Municipal Mayor) → **Office of the Ombudsman**, alleged violations of R.A. 6713 and R.A. 3019 §3(i) (CTN SL-2026-0128-1212);
- **The entire Committee on Anti-Red Tape of Mercedes — chaired by the Municipal Mayor himself** → referred by ARTA to **this very Department** and to the CSC (CTN SL-2026-0423-1891, Notices of Referral, 4 May 2026), and thereafter forwarded by the **CSC to the Office of the Ombudsman** (OAC-L Letter No. 270, s. 2026, 23 June 2026) precisely because the official involved is elective.

A referral is a transfer of forum for further action — the opposite of an exoneration. Presenting the closure half alone, to a national oversight officer, as proof of vindication omitted a material fact in a submission the Mayor signed. I respectfully ask that your Office read the dispositive portions it already holds **in full**.

**3. The documented breaches of the Local Government Code on which supervision is requested.**

Each item below states the duty, the duty-holder, and the documented conduct. The instruments are enclosed or already in your Office's or the LGU's records.

**(a) Sanggunian records — §469(c)(5), (c)(7), (c)(1) and §52(e).** The Secretary to the Sangguniang Bayan must *furnish certified copies of records of a public character upon request and payment of fees*; keep *the office and all non-confidential records open to the public*; and *keep a journal and record of proceedings*. My requests of 11 February 2026 (sessions of 21/22 January, 4 February, 11 February 2026), reiterated 5 March, traced 8 July, and escalated to your Office 22 July, have produced **zero records, zero fee assessment, and zero record-specific disposition** — with fees tendered and inspection offered. Note the structure of the duty: if the journals and records exist, withholding them breaches §469(c)(5)/(7); if they do not exist, that itself breaches §469(c)(1)/§52(e). There is no compliant answer except production.

**(b) The record book — §469(c)(6).** All ordinances and resolutions, with dates of passage and publication, must be recorded in a book kept for that purpose. The procedural status of the accepted street-naming ordinance has never been stated despite repeated requests. Production of the record book (or the statement that none is maintained) answers it mechanically. I ask your Office to require that verification.

**(c) The Sanggunian's frozen legislative function — §445, §§48–59.** A matter formally placed before the Sanggunian (22 May 2025; followed up 3 October 2025; Formal Motion 26 January 2026; committee referral of record 22 October 2025) has produced **no final action of any kind** — a fact the body itself confirmed in writing on 11 March 2026. A Sanggunian that neither acts nor declines to act, indefinitely, on a matter properly before it is not exercising the legislative function the Code prescribes.

**(d) The standing "all-heirs SPA" condition — §444(b)(1), with R.A. 11032 and Civil Code Art. 487.** The administration conditions estate-related services and records on a Special Power of Attorney "signed by all the heirs" — imposed in writing on 26 January 2026 by Councilor Ong and restated over the Mayor's own signature on 24 July 2026. That requirement appears in **no law, no ordinance, and no Citizen's Charter entry**; it contradicts Art. 487 of the Civil Code (any co-owner may act in behalf of the co-ownership) and is exactly the kind of unpublished, extra-legal requirement R.A. 11032 prohibits. It functions as a standing directive to every municipal office to refuse this estate service.

The condition is also **already answered by a court of law.** Civil Case No. 26-360 (MTC Mercedes) — active litigation over this very estate's registered titles — is prosecuted by **one co-owner heir, Patricia Keesey Zschoche, acting through the undersigned as her Attorney-in-Fact under a single-heir Special Power of Attorney.** The court entertains that authority for the gravest question the estate can present: the recovery of registered title itself. If a single-heir SPA suffices before the judiciary to litigate ownership, it cannot be insufficient before a municipal office to obtain a record or a counter-level service. No LGU may demand a higher grade of authority for a photocopy than the courts require for a judgment. I act, in any event, under a broad, apostilled SPA from the same registered co-owner heir, covering the entire estate and its records.

**(e) The Mayor's duty to correct and to institute proceedings — §444(b)(1)(x).** The Mayor must "ensure that all executive officials and employees … faithfully discharge their duties" and "cause to be instituted administrative or judicial proceedings against any official or employee … who may have committed an offense." The defaults above have been documented to his office since May 2025; his 24 July comment *defends* them. Meanwhile, ARTA referred **his own Assistant** to the Ombudsman and **his Municipal Engineer** to the CSC — and there is no record of any administrative action by the Mayor against either. Acting on such offenses is his affirmative statutory duty, not his option. His 24 July presentation of the "closures" without the same-page referrals — to this Department itself — belongs in the same assessment.

**(f) The donated road lots — §17 and §21.** The road lots donated in 1979 "only for the purpose of constructing roads" (accepted by Res. 75-79, amended by Res. 103-86) carry the LGU's basic-services maintenance duty under §17; encroachments obstructing public use were formally noticed to the LGU on 3 October 2025. §21 permits withdrawal of a road from public use **only by ordinance with the required conditions** — and none is of record. The record book in item (b) either shows such an ordinance or proves there is none.

**(g) The Municipal Hall parcel — a written assertion of "title" contradicted by the record, §19 / §22(a)(4).** The Code provides exactly two lawful paths to land: acquisition by instrument, or expropriation upon a valid offer and just compensation. The Mayor's 24 July comment to this Department asserts the LGU's belief "in the validity of its own legal title" to the compound it occupies. Against that written assertion stand three facts of record:

  1. The ground on which the municipal compound stands is **titled land of the estate — TCT No. T-32911 (Lot 2-A, (LRA) Psd-221861, 8,706 sq m), registered in the name of the Heirs of Mary Worrick Keesey**, a derivative of the senior mother title TCT T-4497 — as verified by the **DENR itself**, whose records projection (letter LMS-25-550, 26 November 2025) determined that Lot 2-A, (LRA) Psd-221861 encompasses cadastral Lots 401, 403, and 405, Cad. 118-D, Mercedes Cadastre — the lots the municipal structures occupy. The title bears **no annotation** of any donation, conveyance, or encumbrance in the Municipality's favor;
  2. The Municipality's **own Sanggunian resolution of 13 March 1996** created an ad-hoc committee "to treat the matter of the ***proposed*** legal acquisition" of the estate parcel — the LGU's own record that, as of 1996, the acquisition had **not** been completed; and
  3. No title, deed, expropriation judgment, or registered instrument in the LGU's favor has been produced at any point in the two years of correspondence, ARTA proceedings, and DILG coordination in which the question has been squarely raised.

Title to this same registered estate is, moreover, presently **sub judice** — Civil Case No. 26-360 (MTC Mercedes) is active litigation over derivative titles of TCT T-4497 — which is precisely why a bare extrajudicial assertion of municipal "title," made in an official submission to a national department, should never have been advanced without the instrument to support it. An assertion of title contradicted by the Torrens register and by the asserter's own records is not a legal position; it is a **misrepresentation to the supervising Department** — the second in the same letter, alongside the half-quoted ARTA closures in item 2 above.

I do not ask this Office to decide ownership — that belongs to the courts, and I reserve it there. I ask this Office to require the Municipality to **identify the instrument** on which its written assertion to the Department rests — or to **correct the assertion in writing**.

**(h) The CART referral addressed to this Department — R.A. 11032 §17(d).** ARTA's 4 May 2026 Notice of Referral placed the composition and conduct of the Mercedes CART — chaired by the Mayor — before the DILG. That referral is addressed to this Department and remains unacted upon. It cannot be deferred to "the agencies handling my complaints"; **this Department is that agency.**
