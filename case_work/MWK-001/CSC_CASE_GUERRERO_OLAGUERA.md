### A.2 Offense selection (2017 RACCS + R.A. 6713 — verified against the embedded law library)

- **R.A. 6713 §5(a)** — *"All public officials and employees shall, within fifteen (15) working days
  from receipt thereof, respond to letters… The reply must contain the action taken on the request."*
  (legal_chunks, `RA 6713 (full)`, chunk 5.) The **8 July 2026 tracer** drew **no reply**; as of
  2026-08-03 that is **18 working days** elapsed [operator-computed; PH-holiday adjustment =
  NEEDS-COUNSEL-VERIFICATION], past the 15-day ceiling. Even the **initial** 11 Feb → 5 Mar reply ran
  **16 working days** [operator-computed] — itself one day late — though she did reply, so lead with the
  tracer, not the first reply.
- **RACCS Refusal to Perform Official Duty** — a **GRAVE** offense, Section 50(B) (legal_chunks,
  `2017 RACCS (CSC Res. 1701077)`, chunk 62, item 8; penalty: suspension 6 mo 1 day–1 yr, first
  offense). The refused duty is **statutory and ministerial**: LGC §469(c)(5) directs the SB Secretary,
  *upon request of any interested party and payment of prescribed fees,* to **furnish certified copies
  of records of a public character** in the Secretary's custody; §469(c)(7) keeps the office and **all
  non-confidential records open to the public** during business hours; §52(e)/§469(c)(1) require the
  journal and record of proceedings to be kept. The "adoption first" rule she invoked reaches only
  **certified minutes** — it is **no lawful basis** to withhold the journal entries, agendas, attendance
  records, motions, referrals, vote results, written manifestations, and copies *marked unadopted*, all
  of which the requester expressly offered to accept and to pay for.
- **Fallback: RACCS Simple Neglect of Duty** — a **LESS GRAVE** offense, Section 50(D) item 1
  (legal_chunks, chunk 65; penalty: suspension 1 mo 1 day–6 mo, first offense) — the safe-harbor charge
  if a hearing officer treats the sustained non-production as negligent inattention rather than a
  deliberate refusal.

**Charge recommendation:** lead **R.A. 6713 §5(a)** (cleanest, self-proving on the unanswered tracer),
plead **Refusal to Perform Official Duty (RACCS grave)** in the alternative on the §469(c) records, with
**Simple Neglect** as the fallback. This is the exact statute family ARTA used for Balane.

  the request to precisely those records and tendered the fees.
