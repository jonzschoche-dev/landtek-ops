Re: **Your Transmittal of 4 September 2026 — demand for completion of your Indorsements of
25 and 26 August 2026; readiness to pick up and pay**

Dear Mr. Guerrero:

Thank you for your Transmittal of 4 September 2026 furnishing the LGU replies of 1 September
2026. Your Office acted when asked. I write now so that action is **completed**.

The 1 September replies do not discharge your Indorsements. They confirm some facts, dodge
others, and substitute categories, deferrals, and “no further action” language for the
specifics you already required. I respectfully **demand** that your Office, within **ten (10)
working days** of receipt of this letter, issue written directives to the responsible LGU
officers requiring them to supply the particulars below within a short definite period you
set, and that you furnish me those LGU submissions the same day they arrive.

A further endorsement that only returns the file to Mercedes, or that grants more
“sufficient time” without a written demand, a timeline, and a stated consequence for default,
is not completion of your Indorsements.

**1. Session records — assessment, availability, pickup.**

Your 25 August Indorsement required status and disposition of the session records of
21/22 January, 4 February, and 11 February 2026, **including official fee assessments**. The
Secretary confirms the records exist and are “being verified.” That is not production.

**Direct the Secretary to issue, in writing:**
  (a) a **fee assessment** I can pay;
  (b) the **adoption status of each session**, stated individually;
  (c) a **date certain** when the records will be available for release or inspection under
      §469(c)(7); and
  (d) release covering the **members’ manifestations** entered in those sessions (part of the
      request since 11 February 2026).

Please provide the written fee assessment. **When the assessment and date are set, I will appear, pick up the
records, and pay the fees in full the same day.** No further chase from your Office should be
needed once those three items issue.

**2. Identification of the deed — which instrument, not a category.**

Your 26 August Supplemental Indorsement required the Local Chief Executive to cite and
**produce** the **specific** instrument behind the LGU’s claim to the Municipal Hall compound.
The 1 September reply says only “a valid Deed of Donation.” That is a genus. It is also a
**retreat** from the LGU’s earlier written assertion of “legal title” (24 July 2026) to a bare
“lawful claim” by donation — still without naming the deed.

Two donation instruments touching this estate are already of record, and they are not the same:

  (a) **12/13 July 1953** — about **8,951.22 sq m**, *"only for the purpose of the site of a
      market and municipal building and its premises"* (compound-site); and
  (b) **14 April 1979** — *"only for the purpose of constructing roads"* (road lots only).

**Direct the Mayor to state in writing which one** — or a third instrument — is the deed
relied upon for the compound, and for that instrument: date; donor; notarial registry details
(document, page, book, series); acceptance resolution/ordinance number and date;
registration and annotation status on TCT No. T-4497 or TCT No. T-32911; and a **copy**.

I do not ask you to decide ownership or validity. I ask that an assertion made to the
Department be matched to **one** specific instrument, as your directive already required.

**3. Street-naming — the enactment said to have closed the matter.**

The Secretary says the street-renaming matter “has already been acted upon” through official
proceedings, while the same reply says the session records are still “being verified” and
claims the §469(c)(6) record book is complete. Those statements cannot all stand without a
cite.

**Direct the Secretary to state the resolution or ordinance number and date**, and to furnish
the **minutes** of the session that adopted it — or to state in writing that **none exists**.

**4. Road lots — “actively devoted” to road purpose.**

Your 25 August Indorsement required compliance status under **§§17 and 21** on donated road
lots. The Mayor answered that the roads are “actively devoted to their intended public road
purpose” and that there is “no statutory ground” for administrative action.

That answer cannot stand where **houses or other structures occupy donated road lots**, or
where the Municipality’s own Building Official has documented an **unpermitted** structure on
estate land used as if it were a road or adjoining a donated road (including the Teope
structure already of record). A road covered by a house is not devoted to road purpose.

**Direct the Mayor (and the Building Official as appropriate) to state in writing, lot by
lot among the donated road lots:**
  (a) which portions remain open for public road use;
  (b) which portions are occupied by structures, by whom, since when;
  (c) which of those structures have building permits; and
  (d) what administrative action under §§17 and 21 (and PD 1096) has been taken or will be
      taken, with dates — or a written justification for taking none.

**5. CART / ARTA 1891 — “no further administrative actions required.”**

Your 25 August Indorsement required action taken on the ARTA / CART referral (1891). The
Mayor admits receipt (24 June) and then writes that **“there are no further administrative
actions required or appropriate on the part of the LGU at this stage.”**

That is a declared refusal of the Local Chief Executive’s duty under **§444(b)(1)(x)** to
ensure that administrative or judicial proceedings are instituted against municipal officials
and employees who violate the Code — including matters already referred onward as to
**Teope** and **Balane**. Outside fora do not suspend that duty.

**Direct the Mayor to state in writing** what proceedings he has instituted, or will
institute within a definite period you set, against the municipal officials concerned — or
the specific legal justification for instituting none, provision by provision.

**6. All-heirs SPA — legal basis, not §16 rhetoric.**

Your 25 August Indorsement required the **legal and statutory basis** for the all-heirs SPA
condition, relative to R.A. 11032 and Civil Code Art. 487. The Mayor answered with **§16**,
“implied powers,” and parens patriae — no ordinance, no Charter provision, no statute that
creates the condition. He also wrote that the condition is “particularly vital” because my
requests concern the parcel on which the Municipal Hall sits. That is motive, not law.

**Direct the Mayor to cite the specific enactment** (statute, ordinance, or Citizen’s Charter
provision) that authorizes withholding records pending an all-heirs SPA — or to **withdraw
the condition in writing**. A general-welfare clause is not a records requirement.

**7. Fisheries construction on the same compound parcel — note and fact-finding.**

As of **[DATE — insert]**, construction is proceeding at the fisheries structure on the
parcel projected by DENR (LMS-25-550, 26 November 2025) as within **Lot 2-A, (LRA)
Psd-221861, TCT No. T-32911** — the same compound as to which you directed the LGU to cite
an instrument. The registered owners have given no consent.

**Note this in the pending administrative record** and **direct the LGU to state in writing:**
whether the works are proceeding; which office or entity is building and funding them; under
what building permit and appropriation; and under what claimed authority as to the land —
including how a fisheries use conforms to the purpose in **whichever** instrument the LGU
names under item 2. The 1953 instrument is limited to a market and municipal building; the
1979 instrument is limited to roads. A fisheries structure is neither.

**What I am not asking.** I am not asking the Department to decide ownership, to fix
compensation for the Hall parcel, or to try any criminal case. Those tracks are elsewhere.
Every item above is **completion of directives your Office already issued**.

I remain grateful for the manner in which your Office opened this supervision. I ask now that
it be finished — in writing, on a clock, with the LGU’s answers furnished to me as they
issue.
