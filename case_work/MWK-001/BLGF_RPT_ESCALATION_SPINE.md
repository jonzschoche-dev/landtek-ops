## 4. Legal grounds inventory

1. **Interest computation (strongest, narrow).** Sec. 255 LGC: interest runs from
   "expiration of the periods as provided in Section 250" — i.e., after the 31 Mar
   deadline. Charging Jan–Apr (8%) instead of Apr (2%) over-collects ~6% of ₱41,118.56
   (~₱2,467). If Ord. 22-2024 §208 mandates accrual-from-January, the ordinance is
   ultra vires vs the LGC. **Need: certified §208 text** (requested in annex letter).
2. **Supervisory dereliction (the escalation ground).** Municipal treasurers are DOF
   appointees under BLGF administrative/technical supervision (LGC §470; EO 127 §4).
   RO V declined to address documented withholding at all — refusal to exercise the
   affirmative half of supervision (guidance/directives), while invoking its limits.
3. **RA 3019 §3(e)/(f), RA 6713 §5(a)** — for the Ombudsman fold-in (1891 track):
   inaction on pending matters; the BLGF letter is the newest deflection node.
4. **Art. 27 Civil Code** — damages vs public servant who "refuses or neglects,
   without just cause, to perform his official duty." Fits documented withholding.
   Judicial; Barandon.
5. **Mandamus** — compel ministerial acts (release of records). Judicial; Barandon.
