**Re: Schedule of Reasonable Compensation for the municipal compound on TCT No. T-32911, and an invitation to regularize**

Dear Mayor Pajarillo:

Warm greetings, and thank you for the attention your office has given the estate's correspondence since our letter of 15 May 2025. We know how much the Municipality carries for its people, and we write in that same spirit of service, hoping to make one long-open matter simple to resolve.

The municipal compound stands on registered land of the Heirs of Mary Worrick Keesey: Lot 2-A, (LRA) Psd-221861, **TCT No. T-32911** (Annex "A-1"). The DENR's own records projection places Cadastral Lots 401, 403 and 405 of Cad. 118-D within that lot, with a total area of **8,706 square meters** (Annex "A-2"). No instrument in the Municipality's favor is annotated on that title.

Enclosed is the estate's **Schedule of Reasonable Compensation**. It does not use an appraisal of ours. It is rated on **the Municipality's own declared market values**: for Lot 401, the Municipality's 2023 Declaration of Real Property for that very lot, **₱1,660.00 per square meter** (Annex "A-5"); for Lots 403 and 405, its declared value for land in the same cadastral cluster, **₱970.00 per square meter** (Annex "A-3"). Each is taken at a conservative six percent a year. That comes to **₱59,521.00 per month** for the compound. For completeness of the record, and so your good office has the full picture, we note respectfully that the estate bears this amount each month the matter remains open.

We would be glad to make the way forward as easy as possible. Any one of these would be welcome:

(1) **Show us the instrument.** If the Municipality holds a deed or other paper on which its occupation rests, a copy is all we ask, and we will study it in good faith.

(2) **Regularize.** We would welcome a written lease at fair terms, or acquisition under the modes the Local Government Code provides. We are happy to meet at your office at your convenience, to prepare the draft lease ourselves for your Legal Officer's review, and to support the Sangguniang Bayan with whatever papers an appropriation needs.

(3) **Pay as scheduled** while the parties work through the above. We would accept a schedule of payments that fits the Municipality's budget calendar.

We would be grateful for your office's reply in writing within fifteen (15) days of receipt. Compensation under the Schedule runs from the date of receipt of this letter, with legal interest on unpaid amounts, and without prejudice to the estate's other rights and reserved claims, all of which remain fully preserved.

This letter asks no one to decide ownership. It asks only what any occupant of registered land is asked: to show its paper, to regularize, or to pay for its use. We are confident it can be settled amicably, and we look forward to working with your office to close it.

With warm regards and sincere thanks,

**JONATHAN PAUL ZSCHOCHE**
Attorney-in-Fact for Patricia Keesey Zschoche
