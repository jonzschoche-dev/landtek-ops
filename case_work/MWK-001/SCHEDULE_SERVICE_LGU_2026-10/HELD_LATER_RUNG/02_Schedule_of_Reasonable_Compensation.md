**SCHEDULE OF REASONABLE COMPENSATION FOR USE AND OCCUPATION**

**Registered land of the Heirs of Mary Worrick Keesey — Poblacion, Mercedes, Camarines Norte**

*Issued by Patricia Keesey Zschoche, registered co-owner heir, through her Attorney-in-Fact Jonathan Paul Zschoche (apostilled Special Power of Attorney, Annex "A-4"), for the benefit of the co-ownership.*

**1. The ground.** Lot 2-A, (LRA) Psd-221861, TCT No. T-32911 (Annex "A-1"), 8,706 square meters by title. Per the DENR records projection (Annex "A-2"), the lot encompasses Cadastral Lots 401 (5,015 sq m), 403 (2,187 sq m) and 405 (1,503 sq m) of the Mercedes Cadastre, 8,705 square meters in all. This Schedule prices each lot on its own cadastral area.

**2. Basis of the rates.** Each rate is the Municipality's own declared market value, at the conservative convention of six percent (6%) of land market value a year:

(a) *Lot 401.* The Municipality's Declaration of Real Property, ARP No. GR-2023-II-07-005-00213 (Annex "A-5"; dated 27 July 2023, effective 2024), declares Lot 401, 5,015 square meters, at a market value of ₱8,324,900.00, which is **₱1,660.00 per square meter**.

> **₱99.60 per square meter per year = ₱8.30 per square meter per month.**

(b) *Lots 403 and 405.* The Municipality's Declaration of Real Property, ARP No. GR-2014-HH-07-005-00236 (Annex "A-3"), declares land in this same cadastral cluster at ₱943,810.00 for 973 square meters, which is **₱970.00 per square meter**.

> **₱58.20 per square meter per year = ₱4.85 per square meter per month.**

These rates are floors. They are subject to upward adjustment on independent appraisal or on a later municipal declaration, and are without prejudice to claiming the higher of the appraised rental value or this Schedule for any period.

**4. Accrual.** Compensation accrues from the date of receipt of the cover letter and monthly after that for as long as the occupation continues. Unpaid amounts bear legal interest at six percent (6%) a year from each monthly due date. Claims for the period before receipt are expressly reserved and are not waived by this Schedule.

**5. Terms.**
(a) *Without prejudice; no waiver.* Neither this Schedule nor the acceptance of any payment under it (i) recognizes any lawful basis for the occupation, (ii) waives any action for recovery of possession, reconveyance, cancellation or damages, or (iii) makes a lease or consent to occupy. It is a statement of reasonable compensation for the use of registered land occupied without an annotated instrument.
(b) *Regularization welcome.* The Municipality may propose a written lease at fair terms, or acquisition under the Local Government Code, at any time. The estate will prepare a draft for the Municipal Legal Officer's review on request.
(c) *Payment.* To the estate's designated account [TO FILL], monthly, within ten (10) days of each statement, or on a payment schedule agreed in writing to fit the Municipality's budget calendar. Receipts are issued without prejudice under (a).
(d) *Statements.* A monthly Statement of Account will be furnished. Accrual continues whether or not a statement is answered.
