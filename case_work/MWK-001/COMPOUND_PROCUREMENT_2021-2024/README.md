Source: the Municipality of Mercedes' own procurement postings on mercedes.gov.ph, as captured by the Internet Archive
(web.archive.org, captures of 18 and 28 Jul 2022; the LGU site now returns 404). The three 2021 awards were checked LIVE

status "Awarded"). The compound sits on Lots 401/403/405, inside Lot 2-A of TCT T-32911 (DENR-PENRO LMS-25-550, doc 1070).
No contact with any office was made. Research only (Jonathan, 24 Oct 2026).
