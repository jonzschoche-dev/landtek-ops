## 4. Recovery Waterfall

1. All recoveries are collected by the **Principal (through the AIF) into the estate's
   account** — never by or through the Partner (Art. 1634 hygiene, §7).
2. Waterfall per recovery: gross → less taxes and mandated withholding → less documented
   third-party pursuit costs (advanced by Partner, reimbursed off the top) → **Net
   Recovery → 50% Principal / 50% Partner.**
3. Monthly statement of account to both parties; annual reconciliation.
4. Partner bears **all** pursuit costs as incurred (filing fees, counsel, sheriff, survey,
   publication); costs are recoverable only from actual recoveries, never from the estate.

## 5. Probe Schedule, Obstruction Events, and the Notice Protocol

- **Annex B — Probe Schedule:** calendarized, genuine performance attempts: certified-copy
  requests (SB records, ordinance record book, tax declarations, permit records), RPT
  tenders, site access + survey requests, permit/clearance applications. Each attempt in
  writing, receipted, copies preserved.
- **Annex C — Obstruction Events (defined):** (a) non-action on a chartered service beyond
  its published processing time; (b) refusal to receive a written request; (c) denial of
  access to public records; (d) denial of site access; (e) imposition of any requirement not
  in law/ordinance/Charter (incl. the "all-heirs SPA" condition); (f) continued occupation
  without instrument after demand.
- **Notice Protocol:** each Obstruction Event triggers a written **NOTICE OF OBSTRUCTION**
  served on the acting office and the Mayor within 10 days, reciting the event, the charter
  entry, and the subsisting commercial contract being injured. Cure period: 15 days.
  *(The notices are the Art. 19/20/21 and §3(e) "undue injury" evidence spine.)*

## 6. Frustration, Termination, and the Deposit/Refund Mechanics

- **Frustration termination:** available to either party upon **three (3) or more uncured
  Obstruction Events**, by written notice **reciting the events verbatim** (dates, offices,
  refusals). The termination instrument is a summary of the evidence file, not an assertion.
- **On frustration:** unreleased tranches (T3/T4 as applicable) are extinguished;
  **converted Tranche 1 and released T2 are retained by the estate**; Partner may elect
  (i) refund of any amount still in escrow, or (ii) leave it in and stand as co-claimant
  for his own damages. Any refund instrument recites the Obstruction Events verbatim.
- **Partner withdrawal without cause:** all paid/escrowed amounts forfeit to the estate.
- **Principal breach/withdrawal:** escrowed amounts return to Partner; released tranches
  refundable only to the extent of undelivered estate deliverables.
- **Damages preservation (both parties):** termination is WITHOUT PREJUDICE to each party's
  claims against any third party whose acts caused the frustration. Estate's frustration
  damages include: the extinguished ₱4M balance (or unreleased remainder), 50% of scheduled
  recoveries as lucrum cessans (priced by this agreement's own economics), and continuing
  unmarketability. Partner's: reliance costs and lost bargain.

## 7. Champerty / Assignment Firewalls

- **Control:** every filing, settlement, compromise, waiver requires the Principal's prior
  written approval. Counsel of record is engaged by and answers to the co-ownership. The
  Partner **funds and assists; it never conducts or controls** any proceeding. [NEEDS-COUNSEL]
- **No assignment:** the Partner acquires a contractual share of proceeds only — never
  ownership of any claim, credit, or cause of action (closes the **Art. 1634** redemption
  trap: no assigned credit-in-litigation for a debtor to extinguish at cost).
- **Non-waiver:** nothing herein recognizes any occupant's lawful possession or waives any
  recovery, reconveyance, or damages action of the estate.

## 10. DAMAGES ARCHITECTURE (Annex D — the maximization layer)

The agreement is engineered so that, on frustration, every damage head is **pre-priced,
pre-noticed, and interest-bearing**. Courts award what is proven with reasonable certainty;
this annex manufactures that certainty in advance.

**D-1. The damage heads (pleaded cumulatively):**

| Head | Amount source | Proof instrument |
|---|---|---|
| Direct loss — extinguished tranches | ₱3,000,000 (T3+T4) + any escrow refunded | The payment schedule itself + escrow records; each blocked milestone = a dated, liquidated figure |
| Lucrum cessans — lost 50% recovery share | 50% × Annex A portfolio value | The **valued Claim Schedule** (D-2) — appraisal-grade methodology, not assertion |
| The rental meter (independent of this agreement) | Schedule of Reasonable Compensation × months | Served tender + monthly statements of account (D-4) |
| Legal interest | 6% p.a. per head | Runs from each **Notice of Obstruction** (Art. 1169 — the notices are the mora trigger; this is why the Notice Protocol is mandatory, not optional) |
| Moral + exemplary vs. officials **personally** | Court's discretion, pleaded high | **Civil Code Art. 27** — a public servant who "refuses or neglects, without just cause, to perform his official duty" answers in damages personally; bad faith strips state-immunity cover. The obstruction record IS the Art. 27 case |
| Attorney's fees + costs | Art. 2208 | Compelled-to-litigate showing — the exhaustion trail (DILG letter, notices, demands) proves it |

**D-2. The Claim Schedule must be VALUED, not just listed.** Each Annex A claim carries:
parcel, occupant, area, the per-sqm rate with its three benchmarks (Assessor's own
valuation · BIR zonal · comparables), accrued compensation to date, and projected 12-month
value. A licensed **appraiser's report commissioned during the term (Partner's cost, per
§4)** converts the schedule from claimant's arithmetic into expert evidence. The portfolio
total × 50% is the lucrum cessans number — grounded, not asserted.

**D-3. Milestone values = liquidated benchmarks.** The tranche amounts are deliberately
matched to the milestones the LGU controls: when the Records milestone is blocked, the
direct loss is not "some damage" — it is **₱1,500,000, dated, tied to a named office's
named refusal.** The payment schedule doubles as the damages table.

**D-4. The running ledger, served monthly.** LandTek maintains a **Damages Ledger** (one
row per Obstruction Event: date, office, act, charter entry violated, peso value, interest
start) and a monthly **Statement of Accruing Damages served on the LGU**. Service does two
things: (i) continuing mora — interest compounds the record; (ii) destroys any future
"we didn't know" defense — the LGU watches the number grow in real time, in writing.

**D-5. Defendant architecture.** Plead the LGU for the institutional heads (frustration,
unjust enrichment, inverse-condemnation interest) and the **officials personally** for
Art. 27 / bad-faith heads (moral, exemplary) — personal liability does not hide behind
municipal funds, and it is the head that changes official behavior. [NEEDS-COUNSEL on
splitting/joinder]

**D-6. Probe density discipline.** More probes = more events = larger aggregate — but every
probe must be a *genuine* performance requirement of the mandate (traceable to a milestone
need). Probes that exist only to farm violations invite an abuse-of-process counter.
Annex B stays tied to what the milestones actually require; the LGU's refusals will supply
the volume on their own.
