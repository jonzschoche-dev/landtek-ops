## 0. The thesis in one line
The senior 1912-surveyed Torrens title **TCT T-4497 never moved.** Three separate instruments were used to take portions of the estate — an unperfected donation, a special patent, and an inconsistent 1997 cadastral survey — **and each one fails on the objective record.** Therefore the heirs never lost ownership; the LGU/PNP occupations rest on paper that could not convey.

## 1. The rock: the senior title
- **TCT T-4497**, Heirs of Mary Worrick Keesey, mother title of the estate; **Lot 2, (LRC) Psd-12802 = 139,132 sq m**, Poblacion, Mercedes; bounded NE by the Pacific Ocean, SE by Rafael Carranceja, SW by the Provincial (Daet–Mercedes) Road, NW by Lot 1. *(deed text; corroborated in the 1319 complaint)*

**Consequence:** a later survey or an unregistered deed cannot defeat a senior registered Torrens title. This is the fixed point everything else is measured against.

## 2. Taking mechanism #1 — the "Deed of Donation" (Municipal-Hall block)
**Instrument:** Deed of Donation, DONOR = "Heirs of Mary Worrick Keesey (Marcia Ellen Keesey, Geraldine Keesey Hoppe, Patricia Keesey Zschoche), represented by Attorney-in-Fact **Cesar M. de la Fuente**"; DONEE = Municipal Government of Mercedes (Mayor **Melchor Ibasco**). Parcel = **6,219 sq m portion of Lot 2, Psd-12802, "with all improvements thereon"** — the Municipal-Hall block between Don Estaneslao Moreno St. (W) and Doña Marciana Moreno St. (E), fronting the Provincial Road (S); Annex "A" plan labels the sea-front north, east, and west all "Heirs of Mary Worrick Keesey." *(photographed deed + Annex "A", ScannerPro folder)*

**Why it conveyed nothing — independent defects:**
1. **Acceptance BLANK** — "Sangguniang Bayan Resolution No. ___ dated ___" left empty. A donation of immovable to an LGU must be accepted by Sanggunian resolution/ordinance and the acceptance perfected (Civil Code Arts. 745, 749). **No acceptance = no perfected donation. Date-independent kill.**
2. **The donee's own 1996 record calls it merely "proposed."** SB Resolution, 10th Regular Session, **March 13, 1996** (Presiding Officer: then-Vice-Mayor **Melchor Ibasco**): *"RESOLVED… to create Ad Hoc Committee to treat the matter of the **proposed** legal acquisition/donating of a parcel of land owned by the Mary Worrick Keesey Estate… finish the transaction ninety (90) days thereafter."* **Admission against interest — the transfer was never completed.** *(VERIFIED — doc 389)*
3. **Not registered / not annotated on TCT T-4497.** *(VERIFIED — doc 348)* Under the Torrens system registration is the operative act; an unregistered deed does not bind the land.
4. **No authority — the de la Fuente SPA was revoked (2005).** He signs as AIF, but that SPA is the one revoked in 2005 (the void-SPA spine); donating land needs a *special* power (Arts. 1919, 1878[5], 1317). The named heirs never personally signed. *(SECONDARY — date-dependent; needs the deed's execution date, see §8)*
5. **Cesar de la Fuente died 21 June 2017** — a completion date after that is impossible. *(banked death date)*
6. **Tax Declaration No. and assessed value: BLANK.**

**Result:** the 6,219 sq m Municipal-Hall block was never conveyed. Still the heirs'.

## 3. Taking mechanism #2 — the PNP Special Patent (eastward)
**Instrument:** Special Patent **OCT No. 2018000090, Lot 402-B (Csd-05-019916-D)** — Mercedes PNP Station. *(doc 1070; doc 739)*
- Your boundary analysis: **TCT T-4497 extends eastward beyond the patent area** → the land was **already titled private property**, which makes it **ineligible for a patent**. A special patent cannot issue over already-registered private land. *(doc 739)*

**Result:** the patent is void at the root → subject to cancellation / reversion of the land to the registered owner.

## 4. Taking mechanism #3 — the inconsistent 1997 cadastral survey
- **March 1996** SB "proposed acquisition" resolution → **1997 cadastral survey** = the survey that redrew the parcels. *(doc 389; doc 739)*
- **PENRO admitted the error.** In **LMS-25-550 (26 Nov 2025)**, Officer Fortuño conceded the cadastral map *"inadvertently indicated"* the lot as **Psd-229480** (a plan belonging to a **different province**) when the correct designation is **Psd-221861** — yet DENR-PENRO and the Municipal Assessor **continue to rely on cadastral records containing admitted technical discrepancies** (Jonathan's sworn attestation). *(doc 1070; sworn — ANNEX C-5 / doc 985)*
- A cadastral survey/map is **administrative — it cannot convey ownership or defeat a Torrens title.** At most it corrupted the *assessment* records and enabled the patent; both are defeasible because the title never moved.

**Result:** the cadastral is erroneous/inconsistent (DENR-admitted), not a source of title. It needs correction, not deference.

## 7. Discipline / calibration (read before any outward use)
- **"Not annotated," "proposed only," "cadastral error admitted"** = VERIFIED against the record (doc 348, 389, 1070/739).
- **Void deed / void patent** = strong legal conclusions from face defects + the record; to be pleaded and adjudicated, not self-declared.
- **Provenance of the deed is UNKNOWN** — who drafted or attached it is not established. Describe it as *"a Deed of Donation appearing in the Municipal Treasurer's records, defective and unperfected on its face"* — **NOT** "fabricated/forged," until authorship + intent are provable.
- Keep the 1997 cadastral **"erroneous/inconsistent/irregular"** (DENR's own word: "inadvertently"), not "fraudulent," for the same reason. You win on the failed transfers either way.
- Client-separation: the road-lot donation (1979, via Llamanzares) and the 1953 T-111 donation are **separate threads** — the road lots are expressly OUT of scope per operator direction.
