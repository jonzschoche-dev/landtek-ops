### 5D. Can this be used as evidence in 26-360 without Concepcion? Recommendation: drop it
- **(a) What public records alone can prove:** that a 2017 self-adjudication recited as notarized by "Ronald A. Ramos" exists (once a certified copy is obtained); that the Addendum was registered on T-4538 with Ferdinand H. Balane as presenter; and the PSA negative certification. They **cannot** prove what Ramos knew. A notary attests to identity, appearance and voluntariness, not to the truth of the contents (NEEDS-COUNSEL-VERIFICATION). They also cannot prove any sale to a Balane, because no deed is in hand.
- **(b) "Concepcion is alive" without her:** only Jonathan's personal knowledge (date and means of last contact) and any later document she signed. Neither exists in the record. If she will not testify she cannot be compelled cheaply, and she could contradict the claim. The PSA certificate alone falls short.
- **(c) Similar acts (Rule 130, §35 of the 2019 amended Rules; NEEDS-COUNSEL-VERIFICATION):** the conduct must be that of the party whose knowledge or intent is in issue. Gloria appears on no Garrido instrument. Ferdinand is not a party and his relationship to her is unproven. The Garrido acts (2017–2025) come after her 2016 purchase, so they say nothing about her state of mind then. **Weak and collateral.**
- **(d) Disqualification:** a lawyer-as-witness motion needs counsel to be a necessary witness on a contested fact in this case. The CPRA (A.M. No. 22-09-01-SC) provisions on lawyer-as-witness and conflict of interest are **not in the law library** (NEEDS-COUNSEL-VERIFICATION). On the facts here (the 1992 SPA's scope, the 2005 revocation, Gloria's 2016 good faith), Ramos has no role. **No basis.**
- **(e) Risks:** it names a non-consenting relative in court. It hands Ramos impeachment material on Jonathan himself: the conflicting family accounts above, plus Jonathan's 9 Feb 2025 email to his aunts, which mentions slowing down "the transaction with Aunt Bingay" and an offer to buy their shares (gmail 4468 / 105207). Jonathan would have to explain that dealing to counsel first. It also feeds the harassment narrative already in their papers: the ₱200,000 moral-damages counterclaim (doc 405), and Erwin's "never accused of any infraction except the complaints by Jonathan Paul Zschoche" (doc 1037). And under Summary Procedure, with plaintiff's settings from 14 Oct 2026, a disqualification fight invites delay. Its prohibited-motion status is NEEDS-COUNSEL-VERIFICATION.

**Straight call:** keep it out of 26-360. Do not plead it, do not cross on it, and do not file a disqualification motion. The lawful lane that already exists is Jonathan's 29 Sep 2025 referral to the NBI (doc 2002), which carried the Concepcion affidavit. Leave it there.
