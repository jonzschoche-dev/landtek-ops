# DRIP CHARTER: MWK-001 (ADOPTED by Jonathan, 20 Oct 2026)

This is the drip's governing document. It was built one question at a time with Jonathan (Q1–Q12) and adopted on 20 Oct 2026.

- It outranks the steering guide, the 005 spec and every template. Where they conflict, this Charter wins.
- Every drip is checked against it: pre-draft gate line 0 CHARTER (`scripts/drip_card.py`).
- It is amended only by Jonathan's comment. Each amendment is logged in `drip_feedback` (scope `charter`) and carries a dated line here.
- Internal: never quoted outward, never an exhibit.

## 1. The client's goal: MWK-001, Keesey estate (Q1, Jonathan, 20 Oct 2026)
> "Article 487 to defend the undivided property and make money, and inspire good governance, prepare the property for investment."

1. **Defend the undivided property.** Patricia, as co-owner, acts for the benefit of the whole co-ownership (Civil Code Art. 487).
2. **Make money** from the estate.
3. **Inspire good governance.** Move the offices to perform their duties. The aim is better government, not punishment.
4. **Prepare the property for investment.** The end state is a clean, documented, investable estate.

## 2. Who is acting (Q2)
Every drip presents: **"Patricia Keesey Zschoche, a co-owner, acting through her attorney-in-fact to protect the property held in common"** (the Art. 487 framing).
- It never claims to speak for Geraldine or Marcia.
- It never says "the heirs" as if all of them authorized it.
- Signed: Jonathan Paul Zschoche, Attorney-in-Fact for Patricia Keesey Zschoche. The apostilled SPA is the only annex.

## 3. Stance toward government offices (Q3)
**Helpful citizen-partner.** We help each office do its job well:
- small, clear, easy requests;
- credit for what an office does right;
- a record of its duties that builds quietly.

Pressure exists only inside the stack (record plan, clocks, meter), never in the tone. This is how the drip "inspires good governance".

## 4. When an office doesn't act (Q4)
**Up the chain, still partner-toned.**
1. Patient nudges to the office.
2. Once the record shows a due request, proof of receipt and silence (at least 15 working days), a calm note goes to the **supervising office**, in the same helpful tone with the record annexed.
3. Only after that, and only on Jonathan's go, a formal step (sworn affidavit, CSC or Ombudsman, court).

Never a harsher letter to the same official.

## 5. Who the drip nudges (Q5)
> "Virtually anyone in the corpus we have a file on or we interact with: office heads, attorneys, etc. The list will continue to grow; that web must be expanding."

The recipient web grows with the corpus. It includes:
- government offices and office heads;
- attorneys;
- occupants of estate land (invite them to regularize);
- the 20 transferees;
- paying parties (Land Bank and any party owing the estate);
- investors and buyers (**later**, only when Jonathan says the property is ready).

Standing guardrails that ride with every recipient (from the ledger):
- **posture:** never nudge a respondent in our live proceedings directly;
- **represented parties:** anyone with counsel in a live case is reached through counsel. CV 26-360 defendants go through Atty. Barandon only;
- **client separation:** MWK recipients never receive another client's matter;
- **no external exposure** of client views until Jonathan says ready.

## 6. What Jonathan approves (Q6)
**Templates + campaigns + exceptions.**
- Each nudge template is approved once, with sample letters.
- Each campaign (template + audience + cadence + goal) is approved before it runs.
- The stack prepares clean letters as drafts in batches, and Jonathan gives **one go per batch**.
- Only letters the gate flags (⛔/⚠) come to him individually.
- Bespoke strategic instruments (Schedule, §252 protest, R2 affidavit) stay one at a time.

## 7. Cadence (Q7)
**Steady trickle.**
- Per recipient: at most one touch every 15 working days.
- Per office cluster: one new step a week.
- Overall: about 10–20 letters a week to start, raised as replies and capacity allow.

## 8. How success is measured (Q8)
> "Trajectory of our goals, compliance, etc., milestones."
- **Trajectory per goal:** each client goal (defend the property, make money, good governance, investment-ready) has a progress line over time. It moves when record-plan lines close.
- **Compliance:** the share of nudges that offices act on: replies within 15 working days, records released, decisions issued, by office and by template.
- **Milestones:** dated, named points per goal (e.g. "CV 6839 Entry of Judgment in hand", "road lots taken off the heirs' declarations", "first occupant lease signed"). They are reported weekly with what moved and what slipped.

## 9. Priority between matters (Q9)
> "All matters are equally important. We should look at each issue on its merits or file and establish the protocol."
- No matter outranks another by default. Each issue is judged **on its own file**: what its record shows, what its law requires, what moves it.
- One **standard protocol** is applied to every matter, so that every matter is handled the same rigorous way (see §10).
- The daily pick still orders individual nudges by their own merits (dates that bind, money at stake, what a reply would unlock). It no longer ranks whole matters above one another.

## 10. The standard protocol for every matter (Q10)
**Step 1 (confirmed): read the file on its merits.** This covers everything on record for the matter (corpus, mail, filings), the client's goal for it, and what has already been asked, answered or refused, before anything is proposed.
**Step 2 (confirmed): a short brief to Jonathan first.** After the reading, a one-page brief per matter:
- where it stands;
- what the goal needs next;
- the drips suggested, each with its "why" (goal → record → who holds it → law).

Jonathan comments. **Step 3:** the record plan, milestones and campaigns are built from his comments, and each comment is routed to the layer it corrects (custodian map, fact sheet, verified law, posture, template, client strategy, record plan). That is how the reasoning equilibrium adapts and grows.

## 11. Never (Q11, all four kept as hard rules)
1. **Never send by itself.** Nothing leaves without Jonathan's go: per batch, or per item for strategic instruments.
2. **Never threaten or argue.** No threats, accusations, adjectives about conduct, or forum names in routine nudges. Strategy stays inside the stack.
3. **Never guess.** No fact without a source; no custodian or legal premise unverified; no re-asking what was refused or answered.
4. **Never cross the lines.** No direct contact with respondents of our live proceedings or with represented parties (reach them through counsel). No mixing clients. Nothing client-facing until Jonathan says ready.
