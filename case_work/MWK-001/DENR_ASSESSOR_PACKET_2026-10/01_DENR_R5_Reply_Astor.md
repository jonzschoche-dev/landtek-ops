Dear ARD Astor:

We acknowledge your letter of 9 September 2026, received by email on 15 September 2026, and the hold you have noted.

**1. The documents you requested.** Enclosed are a certified true copy of TCT No. T-32911 with its complete technical description (Annex "A") and the Land Registration Authority's electronic certified copy of the approved plan (LRC) Psd-221861 (Annex "B"). A certified copy of the plan for registration purposes will be secured from the LRA and forwarded. We also enclose (LRC) Psd-12802, the 1950 subdivision plan of TCT No. 111 (Annex "C").

**2. Our concern is Lot 402.** PENRO Camarines Norte projects Lot 2-A over Cadastral Lots 401, 403 and 405, and shows **Lot 402 lying immediately east of it** (LMS-25-550, Annex "J"). The Department's records now treat Lot 402 as public land:
- Lot 402-B, 800 square meters, was granted to the Philippine National Police by Special Patent of 22 December 2017, registered as OCT No. 2018000090 (Annex "K").
- Lot 402-A, 973 square meters, was re-declared to the Municipality of Mercedes in 2022 "based on the certification issued by DENR" (Annex "I").

Lot 402 was not public land. It was held and declared by Mary Worrick Keesey, and it never left her by any instrument.

**3. Mary Worrick Keesey held the ground east of Lot 2-A.** The only instrument the Municipality of Mercedes has ever cited for its site is a copy of a 1953 deed of donation (Annex "D"). It is unsigned and was never registered or annotated on any title. That copy itself:
- describes TCT No. 111 as bounded on the east by "Rafael Carranceja (now Mary Worrick-Kessy)"; and
- describes the parcel it purports to donate as **8,951.22 square meters**, bounded **"on the East by property of Mary Worrick-Kessy."**

It describes no other land. The Assessor's records agree (Annex "E"):
- Mary Worrick Keesey declared land in the Poblacion from at least 1950 (Tax Declaration No. 1693).
- From 1954, her declaration runs from the "Mun. Govt. Site" on the west to the School Site on the east (Tax Declaration No. 1698).
- Her heirs hold declarations in that same ground to this day, and the Municipal Assessor acknowledges them (Annex "F").

In 1980 she donated to the Municipality, as "portion of TCT No. 4497", the street that bounds Lot 402 on the east: Doña Marciana Moreno Street, between the Municipal Government and the Elementary School Site (Annex "G").

**4. No instrument took Lot 402 from her.**
- **1954:** the Assessor transferred 3,675.72 square meters of her declaration to the Municipality "by virtue of Deed of Donation" (Tax Declaration No. 1699). The only deed of donation ever cited describes a different parcel, bounded on the east by her property.
- **17 March 1988:** Mary Worrick Keesey died. From that date nothing could pass from her name except through her estate or her heirs.
- **2017, 2018 and 2022:** the patent, the PNP's declaration (ARP No. GR-2014-HH-07-005-00220) and the Municipality's re-declaration (ARP No. GR-2014-HH-07-005-00236) all came long after her death.

None rests on an instrument from her, her estate or her heirs. In 1996 the Municipality's own Sangguniang Bayan described the land as "owned by Mary Worrick Keesey Estate" (Resolution No. 76-96, Annex "H").

**5. The source of the error is the cadastre.** Cad 1186-D labels the titled lot "Psd-229480", an error PENRO admitted in writing. It shows Doña Marciana Moreno Street as a "Barangay Road". It leaves Lot 402 unclaimed. Csd-05-019916-D, the Special Patent and the 2022 certification were all built on that map. A cadastral survey is not an instrument of ownership, and it cannot make a declared and possessed holding into public land.

**6. We ask the Department to correct its own record. Within fifteen (15) days of receipt, please:**

  a. state in writing the land status of Lot 402 when Csd-05-019916-D was approved and the Special Patent issued, and the record on which that status was determined;
  b. state whether the land investigation for the Special Patent identified Mary Worrick Keesey's declarations and her heirs as the declared holders of Lot 402, and whether they were notified;
  c. review the Special Patent over Lot 402-B and state in writing the Department's finding on whether it issued over land that was not public land;
  d. withdraw the 2022 certification on Lot 402-A, or state the record on which it was issued;
  e. correct Cad 1186-D and Csd-05-019916-D so that Lot 402 is no longer shown as unclaimed land, and correct the admitted "Psd-229480" designation;
  f. furnish certified copies of the Special Patent and its technical description; the approved plan Csd-05-019916-D with its lot data computation; the land-status determination and investigation report; and the 2022 certification; and
  g. confirm that no survey approval, patent or certification will issue over Lot 402 or Lots 401, 403 and 405, Cad 1186-D, while this is pending.

**7.** The heirs do not recognize any claim to this land that rests on no instrument, whether a tax declaration, a cadastral designation or a patent. If the Department does not act within that period, we will elevate the matter to the Director of the Land Management Bureau and to the Office of the Secretary, and proceed in court against the patent and its title.
