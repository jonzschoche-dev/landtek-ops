**2. Lot 402 today.** Lot 402, east of the municipal site, is now declared to two other parties (Annex "D"):
- **Lot 402-A**, 973 square meters, Municipal Government: ARP No. GR-2014-HH-07-005-00054, revised on 17 May 2022 to -00236 "based on the certification issued by DENR"; and
- **Lot 402-B**, 800 square meters, Philippine National Police: ARP No. GR-2014-HH-07-005-00209, revised on 18 September 2018 to -00220, which you recommended for approval.

No instrument of conveyance from Mary Worrick Keesey or her heirs has been produced for either. The only deed the Municipality has ever cited is an unsigned copy of a 1953 deed. It describes a single parcel of **8,951.22 square meters**, bounded **"on the East by property of Mary Worrick-Kessy"**, and no other land. It cannot support Tax Declaration No. 1699, and it cannot support the declarations of Lot 402 east of the municipal site. In 1996 the Sangguniang Bayan itself described the land as "owned by Mary Worrick Keesey Estate" (Resolution No. 76-96).

**3. No one has had authority to change the declared owner since 17 March 1988.** Mary Worrick Keesey died on 17 March 1988. No authority she had given survived her. Since then, no power of attorney has authorized anyone to donate, convey or consent to the transfer of any part of her Barangay 5 declaration:
- The heirs' 1991 Special Power of Attorney to Cesar M. de la Fuente covered supervision of the properties and payment of taxes.
- Their 1992 Special Power of Attorney to him covered only the sale of portions of Lot 2, (LRC) Psd-12802, under existing contracts to sell. It gave no power to donate, and it did not reach land outside that lot.
- Both were revoked on 15 August 2005.
- The 2005 Special Power of Attorney to Ida M. Buenaventura gave representation and supervision. It gave no power to sell, donate or convey.
- Every power of attorney the heirs had issued was revoked by published notice in September and October 2020.

The PNP declaration (2018) and the Municipality's re-declaration of Lot 402-A (2022) were made 13 and 17 years after Mr. de la Fuente's authority ended. No heir and no attorney-in-fact consented to either.

**4. Your duty is ministerial.** The Local Government Code (Section 472(b)) charges the Municipal Assessor to keep the real property identification and accounting system and the assessment records. That is a duty to record, not a discretion to decide ownership. A declaration standing in Mary Worrick Keesey's name, or her heirs', cannot be cancelled and re-issued to another declarant on the Assessor's own judgment. It can be done only on an instrument of transfer executed by the owner or someone with authority to act for the owner. A DENR certification of area is not such an instrument. Neither is a request from the Municipality or the PNP. The heirs therefore do not recognize the cancellation of their predecessor's declaration as to Lot 402, or the declarations issued in its place.
