# Levers beyond "supervision" — how the presidential apparatus actually reaches inside the Municipality of Mercedes
**Matter:** MWK-OP-PETITION (+ MWK-ARTA-1321 / 1210 / 0690-0792 / 0747 / 1212, MWK-LGU-RECOVERY) · 14 Sep 2026 · **HELD — analysis only, nothing sent.** *[verify]* = quote verbatim from the official text before pleading.

## 0. The reframing
"Can the OP direct the LGU?" is the wrong unit. The Municipality is not one thing. Three of the four offices that hold the records answer, by statute, to a national department the President **controls**, and every one of them is bound by national clocks that the President's own apparatus runs. The OP does not need to command the Mayor; it commands the Secretaries of Finance, Public Works and Interior, and the ARTA Director General, and each of those already has a lawful handle on a specific Mercedes officer. That is control, not supervision, and it is the answer to the "pedestrian" objection.

## B. National clocks the President's own apparatus runs
1. **8888 Citizens' Complaint Center (E.O. 6 s. 2016):** "concrete and specific action within 72 hours from receipt of the concern by the proper government agency or instrumentality"; non-action is a ground for administrative sanction *[verify coverage of LGUs in the EO text]*. Turn the PACE consignee problem into a lever: lodge each undelivered chartered service (assessor records, receipts, engineer certifications) as an 8888 ticket → 72-hour clock on the office, tracked by the OP itself.
2. **ARTA compliance track (§17(c); 2020 Rules Rule VIII, carried into 2023 Rules *[verify]*):** "upon receipt of a complaint … **shall issue a Compliance Order**" → 3 working days to explain → Notice of Warning → repeat = IELO case. Never used on any of the five dockets. The OP can order ARTA to run it now, on ARTA's *own* finding that the 1321 delay "would substantially exceed" the charter.
3. **ARTA automatic-approval / extension order (§10; Rules Rule VI):** the Order "shall contain a directive for the government official concerned to submit his Compliance within three (3) working days"; failure → IELO. Fits any chartered *certification* request (assessor certification of history; engineer certification of no record).
4. **ARTA Rule IX "Request for Comment" (empowerment function):** ARTA can put a red-tape *practice* (the SPA/"personality"/citizenship pre-condition; the charter omissions) to the LGU for comment and issue a policy recommendation "furnished to the Office of the President and the Congressional Oversight Committee on Ease of Doing Business." That converts a one-off grievance into a standing finding against the practice.
5. **ARTA CMEO Citizen's-Charter audit (§17(c) monitoring):** charter omissions are the head of office's violation (§8). An audit finding → compliance order to the **Mayor** as head of office — the one route by which a national order lawfully lands on an elective official for a records-service failure.

## D. Statutes with sharper teeth than R.A. 11032 §21 — same facts
- **RA 3019 §3(f)**: "Neglecting or refusing, after due demand or request, without sufficient justification, to act within a reasonable time on any matter pending before him … for the purpose of … **discriminating against any other interested party**." The Mayor's own answer in 0747 ("a foreign national … necessitates a more stringent exercise of due diligence", quoted in ARTA's resolution doc 967) is documentary evidence of the discriminatory purpose. §3(e) (undue injury / manifest partiality) alongside. Ombudsman jurisdiction; criminal, not just administrative.
- **RA 6713 §5(a)**: reply within 15 working days, "the reply must contain the action taken"; **§5(e)**: "all public documents must be made accessible to, and readily available for inspection by, **the public**" *[verify verbatim]* — citizenship-neutral by its terms, and it is the assessor's and engineer's records duty stated without any "government service" threshold. CSC/Ombudsman.
- **Constitution art. II §28 / art. III §7** for the Filipino co-owner (E).

## E. Change the requester — kill the citizenship defense at the root
The May petition itself pleads "the rights of the **Filipino co-owner** of the subject property whose interests were independently before ARTA" (doc 703 p. 28). Every citizenship-based refusal collapses if the request and the complaint are made by a Filipino co-owner (or by the estate/guardian once Spec. Proc. 2680 issues letters). Identify that co-owner in the title record and have the next demand and the CSC/Ombudsman complaints run in that name alongside Patricia's SPA. This is the cheapest lever on the list.

## F. Adjudicators reachable without ARTA and without an O.P. Case No.
- **CSC anti-red-tape unit (§16)**: "receive, review, hear, and decide" complaints — direct complaint vs Abla, Macale, Balane (appointive).
- **Ombudsman §15(2)** directive to "perform and expedite any act or duty required by law" + a Request for Assistance (fast track) — the single fastest records lever.
- **Mandamus (Rule 65, RTC Daet)** vs the assessor/engineer for ministerial certified copies.
- **LGC §252** — protest not decided in 60 days → deemed denied → LBAA (money lane, already tracked as BLGF_252_LAPSE_NOTICE).
- **Sangguniang Panlalawigan (LGC §61(b))** vs Mayor/VM/SB for dereliction (§60(c)); governor may preventively suspend (§63(a)(2)).

## G. The provincial tier (the President supervises the Governor directly)
Provincial Assessor Magana already produced the record classes (the 1321 comparator) and can **certify** them; provincial treasurer has technical supervision over Macale (§470(d)(6)); the Governor reviews municipal executive orders (§30) and chairs the tier that disciplines the Mayor. A single letter from the Governor's office moves three of the four Mercedes offices.

## H. Political oversight
Congressional Oversight Committee on Ease of Doing Business (copy-recipient of ARTA policy recommendations); DILG Bureau of Local Government Supervision; the Ease of Doing Business Advisory Council (DTI chair, ARTA vice-chair, DILG/DOF members) — the forum where "LGU charter omissions + citizenship pre-conditions" becomes policy.
