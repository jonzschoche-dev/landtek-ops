## Cluster A — Sanggunian records duties (the live DILG matter; CSC-ready vs. Olaguera)

### A1. §469(c)(5) — failure to furnish certified copies of public records
> *"(5) Furnish, upon request of any interested party, certified copies of records of
> public character in his custody, upon payment to the treasurer of such fees as may be
> prescribed by ordinance;"*
- **Duty-holder:** Secretary to the Sangguniang Bayan (Olaguera).
- **Conduct:** Requests of 11 Feb 2026 (sessions of 21/22 Jan, 4 Feb, 11 Feb 2026);
  reiteration 5 Mar; tracer 8 Jul; DILG escalation 22 Jul. Fees tendered. **Zero records,
  zero fee assessment, zero record-specific disposition** as of 5 Aug 2026.
- **Evidence:** doc 765 (SB Secretary letter 5 Mar 2026 — the refusal instrument), doc 817
  (Annex I chain), Gmail 107382/107385/107386 (22 Jul letters).
- **Note:** her "certified only after adoption" excuse is *colorable* for certified minutes
  ONLY. It does not reach the non-certifiable existing records (journal entries, agendas,
  attendance, motions, vote results, unadopted copies, recordings) that were expressly
  offered to be accepted. Build the charge there.
- **Lane:** DILG supervision now → CSC-RO V (RACCS Refusal to Perform Official Duty,
  grave; R.A. 6713 §5(a) lead; Simple Neglect fallback) per CSC_CASE_GUERRERO_OLAGUERA.md.

### A2. §469(c)(7) — office and records not open to the public
> *"(7) Keep his office and all non-confidential records therein open to the public during
> the usual business hours;"*
- **Conduct:** No inspection ever arranged or offered across the entire request chain —
  the offer to *inspect* (not just receive copies) removes every certification excuse.
- **Evidence:** same chain as A1.
- **Lane:** DILG supervision; CSC aggravator.

### A3. §469(c)(1) + §52(e) — the journal duty (the either-horn trap)
> *§469(c)(1): "Attend meetings of the sanggunian and keep a journal of its proceedings;"*
> *§52(e): "Each sanggunian shall keep a journal and record of its proceedings which may be
> published upon resolution of the sanggunian concerned."*
- **The trap:** If journals/records of the three named sessions **exist**, withholding them
  violates A1/A2. If they **do not exist**, that is itself the §469(c)(1)/§52(e) violation.
  There is no compliant answer except production — which is why the request was framed to
  accept "any available form."
- **Lane:** DILG supervision (verification ask #2 in the Tracer-Manifestation is built to
  spring exactly this).

### A4. §469(c)(6) — ordinance/resolution record book
> *"(6) Record in a book kept for the purpose, all ordinances and resolutions enacted or
> adopted by the sanggunian, with the dates of passage and publication thereof;"*
- **Conduct:** The pending street-naming ordinance's procedural status has never been
  stated despite requests; the record book (or its absence) answers it mechanically.
- **Status:** **PENDING** — violation ripens only if the book is not produced/maintained.
- **Lane:** DILG verification ask.

## Cluster B — The Mayor and the executive

### B1. §444(b)(1)(x) — failure to correct, and ratification of, subordinates' breaches
> *"(x) Ensure that all executive officials and employees of the municipality faithfully
> discharge their duties and functions as provided by law and this Code, and cause to be
> instituted administrative or judicial proceedings against any official or employee of the
> municipality who may have committed an offense in the performance of his official
> duties;"*
- **Duty-holder:** Mayor Pajarillo.
- **Conduct (two limbs):**
  1. **Failure to ensure faithful discharge:** with the SB Secretary's records default
     documented to his office since at least the 22 May 2025 letter chain and squarely
     since Feb–Mar 2026, his 24 Jul 2026 comment (operator copy; INGEST-GAP below)
     *defends* the withholding pattern rather than correcting it.
  2. **Failure to institute proceedings:** ARTA's 1212 Resolution referred his own
     Assistant, Tony Teope, to the Ombudsman for R.A. 3019 §3(i) (docs 1614, 3754); the
     0792 Resolution referred Engr. Balane to the CSC (doc 719). No record of any
     administrative action by the Mayor against either. §444(b)(1)(x) makes acting on
     such offenses his affirmative duty, not his option.
- **Lane:** Ombudsman (elective official; feeds the held 1891/expenditure theories) +
  DILG supervisory directive. NOT CSC (elective).

### B2. The all-heirs-SPA condition as a standing unlawful directive
- **LGC hooks:** §444(b)(1) (general supervision and control — the condition is his
  administration's policy, restated over his own signature 24 Jul 2026 and by Councilor
  Ong's 26 Jan 2026 letter, doc 836 p.9); cross-statute: R.A. 11032 (additional
  requirement) and Civil Code Art. 487 (any co-owner may act for the co-ownership).
- **Conduct:** Conditioning records/services of the estate on an SPA "signed by all the
  heirs of Mary Worrick" — Jan 2026 (doc 836), restated 24 Jul 2026 (Mayor comment).
- **Lane:** DILG supervisory directive (the Tracer-Manifestation ask); RA 11032 count is
  the live ARTA/OP material.

### B3. §19 + §22(a)(4) — occupation of the Municipal Hall parcel outside the only lawful
### acquisition paths
> *§19: "…the power of eminent domain may not be exercised unless a valid and definite
> offer has been previously made to the owner, and such offer was not accepted … upon
> payment of just compensation…"*
> *§22(a)(4): every LGU has the corporate power "(4) To acquire and convey real or
> personal property;"*
- **Theory (framed carefully):** The Code gives an LGU exactly two roads to land it wants:
  acquire by instrument (§22(a)(4)) or expropriate with offer + just compensation (§19).
  The record contains **neither** for the Municipal Hall Compound parcel of T-4497: no
  title, no conveyance, no expropriation, no offer, no compensation. The only instrument
  of record is the 14 Apr 1979 Deed of Donation — eight road lots, "only for the purpose
  of constructing roads" (doc 201). Occupation without either path is a taking outside
  the statute — the LGC frame for the T4497_TAKING_RECOVERY_SPINE.
- **Status:** ownership itself = RTC matter (reserved). The *LGC point* is narrower and
  supervisory: the Municipality asserts "legal title" (24 Jul) that its own record lacks.
- **Lane:** RTC recovery track primarily; DILG only as the substantiate-or-correct ask.

### B4. §305(a) + §335 — public funds applied to land the Municipality does not own
> *§305(a): "No money shall be paid out of the local treasury except in pursuance of an
> appropriations ordinance or law;"*
> *§335: "No public money or property shall be appropriated or applied for religious or
> private purposes."*
- **Conduct:** ₱2.88M expenditure (Jimmy P. Lo; Res. 76-96 = the LGU's own 1996 admission
  the heirs' land was never acquired) and the ₱2.6M Sports Complex spend flagged in doc
  724 — public works erected on private (estate) land without acquisition.
- **Status:** **PENDING-COUNSEL** — the §335 "private purposes" fit on improvements-on-
  private-land needs counsel's sign-off; the Ombudsman lane (R.A. 3019 §3(e)) is the held
  primary per the Mercedes-Ombudsman case-state. Pivotal to-obtain: the 2024 DV signatory.
- **Lane:** Ombudsman (held); COA audit request already drafted (docs 1247, 2431).
