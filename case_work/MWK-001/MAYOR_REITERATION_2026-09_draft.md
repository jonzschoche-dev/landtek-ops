5. Resolution of the **Anti-Red Tape Authority, 9 September 2026**, CTN SL-2026-0218-1378, received 15 September 2026.

**1. The matter, in one paragraph.** Structures stand and are occupied on parcels registered to the Heirs of Mary Worrick Keesey and declared for taxation by the Municipality **in the heirs' own names** — twenty-nine parcels, 72,384 square metres, on which the real property taxes have been paid (Annex "B"). Your own Building Official has stated in writing that no building permit was issued for at least one of them. **No lease, deed, written consent, or instrument of any kind has ever been executed by the heirs in favour of any occupant**, and no previous administrator of the estate held authority to consent to construction: the agency instruments of record confer a power *"to negotiate for the sale"* only, and under Articles 1877–1878 of the Civil Code a power touching an immovable is confined to its special object.

**2. What is respectfully requested.** Attention is respectfully invited to Section 444(b)(3)(vi) of the Local Government Code, which empowers the Municipal Mayor to —

> *"Require owners of illegally constructed houses, buildings or other structures to obtain the necessary permit, subject to such fines and penalties as may be imposed by law or ordinance, or to make necessary changes in the construction of the same when said construction violates any law or ordinance, or to order the demolition or removal of said house, building or structure within the period prescribed by law or ordinance."*

Accordingly, and in the exercise of that power:

  **(a)** That your Office **require each owner or occupant** of a structure standing on the parcels at Annex "B" to obtain the necessary permit, beginning with the Barangay 1 structure that your own Building Official documented on 21 October 2025.

  **(b)** That your Office cause the **Office of the Building Official to certify, from its own register**, for each such structure: whether a building permit issued (number and date); whether a Certificate of Occupancy issued under Section 309 of P.D. No. 1096; and what document satisfied Section 302(2) — which requires a certified true copy of the title and, where the applicant is not the registered owner, a contract of lease.

  **(c)** That, where neither a permit nor any instrument of right exists, your Office **order the removal or demolition** of the structure in the manner and within the period the law prescribes, with due process to every occupant.

  **(d)** That, under **Section 444(b)(1)(x)**, your Office require the officials concerned to discharge these duties, or state in writing why the foregoing is not acted upon.

**3. What is not asked.** The undersigned does not ask your Office to determine the ownership of any parcel, and does not raise here the matter of the municipal compound or of compensation — both are reserved and are the subject of separate representation. No demolition by any private hand is sought or proposed. Each item above is an act of your Office, taken on its own record.

**4. Period.** Section 5(a) of R.A. No. 6713 provides that *"[a]ll public officials and employees shall, within fifteen (15) working days from receipt thereof, respond to letters, telegrams or other means of communications sent by the public,"* and that *"[t]he reply must contain the action taken on the request."* A written reply stating the action taken on each of items (a) to (d) is respectfully requested **within fifteen (15) working days of receipt**. A chronology is at **Annex "A"**, so that the age of each item is plain on the record. Should a longer period be required for any item, kindly say so in writing and state the period.

Your favorable action on this request will be highly appreciated.
