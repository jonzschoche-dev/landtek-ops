# ANNEX "R" — SCHEDULE OF RECORDS REQUIRED TO MANAGE THE ESTATE (DRAFT — HELD)

> **What this is.** The finite list of records the estate needs released to manage its own
> registered land in Mercedes — organized by the management job each record serves, then by
> the office that holds it. Each row states the record, the holder, the legal basis for
> release, when it was asked for, what (if anything) came back, and what is still dark. This
> is Annex "R" to the Petition for the Exercise of Provincial Supervision and defines what
> **full compliance** looks like: when every row reads RELEASED, the supervision asked for is
> complete. It is also the standing checklist for every future records ask, so no office can
> say the estate's request was vague.
>
> **Discipline.** Every "requested" date is a corpus document. Every "released" entry is a
> corpus document. "Dark" means no production of record. Nothing here decides ownership; a
> record is asked for because an owner of registered land needs it to administer the land.
> Client separation: T-4497 estate (Mercedes Poblacion) only; T-30683 (Manguisoc) and
> T-4494 (Cabanbanan) are separate matters and are not in this schedule.
>
> **Legal bases used (short cites; verbatim text in the Petition, Part IV, and in
> LGC_VIOLATIONS_INVENTORY.md):** LGC §469(c)(5) and (7) (SB records); LGC §472(b)(5) (tax
> mapping) and §200 (province's RPT responsibility); R.A. 6713 §5(a) (15 working days);
> R.A. 11032 (Citizen's Charter periods; §21(b): additional requirements "other than those
> listed in the Citizen's Charter" are a violation); LGC §472(b)(9) (certified assessment
> records issue "upon request of any interested party" on payment of the fee); LGC §472(b)(12)
> (Provincial Assessor's technical supervision and visitorial functions over municipal
> assessors, incl. tax mapping); LGC §470(d)(6) (Provincial Treasurer's technical supervision
> over municipal treasury offices); Provincial Assessor Memorandum No. 2024-01 (item 2 excepts
> tax maps; §5 exempts a party to a pending case on presentation of a certified copy of the
> complaint — as reproduced in ARTA's Resolution, doc 6908); Constitution Art. III §7 and
> R.A. 6713 §5(e) (access to public records; PCOO-DILG JMC 2018-01 — Mercedes admitted on
> 24 Apr 2026 it had "yet to fully operationalize" LGU FOI, doc 700 p.2); P.D. 1096 §§205, 207,
> 301, 304 (the Building Official issues and keeps building permits). Currency: R.A. 12001 (2024)

## H. How the schedule is used

1. **In the Petition (Annex "R"):** Prayer VI-E asks each office to direct release of the rows
   under its supervision, row by row, with fees assessed for immediate payment; "full
   compliance" = every row RELEASED or a written, record-specific ground for withholding.
2. **In every future records letter:** cite the row number. No office gets to call the
   request vague again.
3. **In the recovery/regularization work:** Classes B and D are the gating inputs for the
   Schedule of Reasonable Compensation and the occupant-by-occupant regularization; Class F
   is the gating input for the client map. Class A-1 (2023 GR series) is the gating input for
   the tax-standing reconciliation.
4. **In the CSC / Ombudsman files:** each DARK row with a dated request and a paid fee is a
   count. Rows A-1/A-2/B-1 (Abla), C-1/C-3 (Macale), E-1/E-3 (Olaguera), D-1/D-5 (Mayor).
