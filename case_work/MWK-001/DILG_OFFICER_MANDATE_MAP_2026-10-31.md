### 0.1 All velvet glove

Every later authorized communication should be calm, respectful, factual and readily answerable.
Acknowledge assistance, identify the relevant record, and make one precise request appropriate to the
recipient's own responsibility. Ask for a record, confirmation, explanation or date. Leave room for
the office to supply information or correct the record.

Keep routine correspondence brief and free of accusations, threats, pressure language or announcements
of prospective escalation. Legal grounds can be cited plainly where needed. Keep candid procedural
analysis and possible next steps in the internal record. Formal filings retain their required facts,
legal grounds, disclosures and relief, expressed neutrally. Courtesy does not dilute deadlines,
evidence qualifications or the specific requested outcome.

For later drafting only: "We would appreciate a written update on the action taken under the
6 May memorandum, with a copy of any report or disposition. If the matter remains under review,
kindly identify the office handling it and the anticipated completion date."
