**MWK-001. Built 31 October 2026** on Jonathan's instruction: *"we will have a tailored approach to each
officer."*

**The principle this replaces.** One evidence pack to every officer would hand each recipient a document
that is mostly outside his own authority, which is the condition under which an officer refers instead of
acting. **Breadth is for proof of notice. Depth is for the forum that must rule. They are never mixed.**

**The rule for every row below.** One officer. One act he is actually permitted to perform. One document
he needs in order to perform it. One ask, answerable with a yes, a no or a date. **Our copy stamped
RECEIVED**, because a clock runs from proof of receipt and not from posting.

**Tags:** [V] verified from the primary source, [O] operator-stated, [J] my judgment, ARGUMENT a
constructed argument not a provision in terms, UNKNOWN not established.

(`DRIP_LADDERS_2026-10/DILG-CART-SECRETARIAT/step1b_*`). **I have not drafted into those rows and must

## 3. WHERE THE EVIDENCE PACK ACTUALLY GOES

The 102-page pack at `PRINT_SERVICE_2026-10-19/` was built as an **evidence bundle** and that is what it
is good at. Its home is a forum that **must rule**:

- as annexes to a **verified complaint before the Sangguniang Panlalawigan** under LGC Sec. 61(b), which
  is the only body with original administrative jurisdiction over an elective municipal official [V];
  ceiling suspension only, six months per offence under Sec. 66(b), because *Pablico v. Villapando* holds
  removal is lodged exclusively with the courts [V]; or
- to the **Office of the Ombudsman**, whose authority *Hagad v. Gozo-Dadole* holds survived the Local
  Government Code and runs concurrently, with a six-month suspension power [V]. CSC OAC-L Letter No. 270 of
  23 Jun 2026 **forwarded** this matter there [V]. **A forwarding letter is not a docketed case.** Whether a
  case was opened, and if so its docket, its respondents and the allegations it actually covers, is
  **UNKNOWN**. Whether the Ombudsman may itself dismiss an elective local official is open item I3 with the
  Chief of Staff. **The words "already seized" are not to be written anywhere.**
