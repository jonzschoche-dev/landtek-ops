# The basket-of-services path: correcting the record and fixing accountability

> Strategy memo, 16 September 2026. LandTek work product for Jonathan; not a legal opinion, and LandTek is not a law firm.
> Scope: the Mercedes LGU cluster (MEO/OBO, Assessor, Treasurer, SB Secretary) plus the record-custodian offices (RD, PENRO/LMB, BLGF).
> `[VERIFY]` = not confirmed from the corpus or the law library.

## The diagnosis

Every ARTA closure in this cluster has turned on one move: the request is characterized as a **request for information about records**, and information requests are held outside §4(f). We keep feeding that characterization by asking for records. Meanwhile the thing actually happening on the ground is broader and more actionable: **the Municipality has adopted a standing policy of not acting on anything Jonathan files**, across every office, until he "sufficiently establishes … proof of his legal personality" (CART Resolution No. 05, s. 2026, 6 April 2026, doc 716).

That policy is the case. It is not a records case. It is a refusal-to-serve case that spans a **basket of chartered services**, and it is documented in a resolution the LGU wrote itself, adopted unanimously, and transmitted to a national agency.

Two consequences:
1. **Stop litigating the records request.** Litigate the chartered services the LGU is refusing to render, where a posted processing time exists and the §4(f) wall cannot be built.
2. **Stop treating ARTA as the forum of accountability.** ARTA screens for prima facie red tape and closes. **RA 11032 §16** puts the power to "receive, review, hear, and decide on complaints on erring government employees and officials and noncompliance with the provisions of this Act" in the **Civil Service Commission**. Discipline lives there and at the Ombudsman, not at ARTA.

**The point of the table:** ARTA's §4(f) wall stands only in the second column's *absence*. Where a service is listed with a period, delay is a §21(e) violation on its face, and an unlisted requirement imposed on it is a §21(b) violation on its face. We have never put the LGU in that position. The LGU's own blanket policy guarantees that if we do, it will refuse — in writing, on a chartered service.

### Phase 1 — engineer clean violations across the basket (next 2–3 weeks)
File, on the same day, a set of **applications for listed services**, each in the LGU's own form, each with complete Charter requirements attached, each with the apostilled SPA and the TCT copies attached (so no deficiency can be claimed), and each demanding what §9(a) already requires:

- an **acknowledgment receipt** with the receiving officer's name, unit and date/time (§9(a)(4));
- a **unique identification number** for the transaction (§9(a)(3));
- if anything is deficient, a **written deficiency notice limited to Charter-listed requirements** (§9(a)(2));
- if disapproved, a **written notice stating the ground** (§9(b)(2), §9(c)).

Candidate applications (pick 3–4; each must be something the heirs genuinely want):
- **Fencing Permit** on an identified heirs' parcel (listed service, owner-applicant, cheap, and it tests the wall inside a chartered service).
- **Written request for inspection and enforcement** on the unpermitted structure, addressed to the Building Official under PD 1096 §205 — the duty version, not the records version.
- **Assessor:** a fresh, narrow application for certified copies of the ARPs for identified parcels, paying the fee and getting the OR.
- **Treasurer:** statement of account / tax clearance for identified ARPs, with payment tendered.
- **SB Secretary:** certified copy of an identified resolution or minutes, fee paid.

**Why this works.** If they serve them, the record gets corrected, which is the client's actual goal. If they refuse — which CART Resolution No. 05 commits them to — each refusal is:
- a §21(a) refusal to accept a complete application; and/or
- a §21(b) unlisted requirement on a **listed** service (no charter-omission defense available); and/or
- a §21(d) failure to give written notice of disapproval; and/or
- a §21(e) failure to render a service with a **posted period**; and
- an RA 6713 §5(a) failure to act on a letter within 15 working days, and §5(e) denial of access to public documents; and
- evidence that the policy is general, deliberate, and collegially adopted.

Deadlines mature in 3, 7 or 20 working days (§9(b)(1)). By late October there is a complete, clean, multi-office record built on the LGU's own charter.

### Phase 3 — compel the records where ARTA never could
ARTA cannot order production. **Mandamus can.** For ministerial duties — certified copies of public records, issuance of a certification from an office's own register, action on a complete application — the remedy is Rule 65 mandamus in the RTC. The record after Phase 1 is exactly what a mandamus petition needs: a demand, a duty, a refusal, and no other plain, speedy remedy. Cite the classic access cases `[VERIFY citations before use: Legaspi v. Civil Service Commission; Subido v. Ozaeta]`.

**Citizenship, and how to remove the objection entirely.** ARTA's 7 April 2026 resolution rested on Art. III §7 and EO 2 being limited to citizens. Do not litigate that if it can be mooted: have the request or the mandamus petition made by a **Filipino** requester with standing — a Filipino co-heir if one exists `[VERIFY the heirs' citizenships]`, or Philippine counsel of record in his own right as a citizen, or the estate's Filipino administrator once appointed. RA 6713 §5(e) ("the public") and RA 11032 ("applicant or requesting party") carry no citizenship limit, but the constitutional hook does, so remove the target.

2. **PENRO / LMB** — the cadastral mislabel PENRO already admitted.
3. **Provincial Assessor / BLGF** — the RPT record, which the Province has already shown it will produce.

That is what the ARTA manifestation does for 1378, and the same device works at BLGF, DILG and the Ombudsman.

## III. Discipline rules for this campaign
1. **One evidence spine, many forums.** Same exhibit numbering, same facts, same dates everywhere. A contradiction across filings is the only thing that can really hurt us (see the 21 Oct 2025 letter, described as both "phantom" and "admission").
2. **Charge what the paper proves.** The threat letter, the unlisted SPA condition, the fee paid for an unrendered service, and the CART resolution are documentary. Political favoritism is a hypothesis; treat it as something to investigate.
3. **Every transaction gets a receiving copy.** No acknowledgment, no case. Personal service or courier with tracking, plus email.
4. **Calendar every clock.** 3/7/20 working days under §9(b)(1); 15 working days under RA 6713 §5(a); 15 days for OP appeals; 20 working days for CART referrals.
5. **Never re-frame a chartered service as an FOI request.** That single word is what lost 0690/0792, 1210, 1319, 1321 and 1378.
