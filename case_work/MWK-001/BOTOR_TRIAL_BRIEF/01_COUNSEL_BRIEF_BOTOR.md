## 4. Theory of the case — and the crack lead counsel must fix

### 4a. The winning frame (lead with this)

**The 1992 SPA is a special power of limited object.** Its operative clause (doc 246, read clean;
quoted verbatim in `04_CAMPAIGN_PATTERN_MEMO.md` §2) authorizes de la Fuente to sell **only** "in
favor of third persons in possession of CONTRACTS TO SELL executed by the previous administrator,
MR. BEN LLAMANZARES," and/or prior down-payment holders, **after prior consultation with** the
principals. Three limits sit on the face of the instrument: **object** (the closed buyer class),
**purpose** (proceeds to pay the estate's unpaid realty taxes), **procedure** (prior consultation).

**Balane is outside the class — proven by the defense's own papers:**
- Her JA: she entered by buying "rights and possession" from Kenneth Asistin (₱16,500) and paid
  **₱50/month rent** to the owners' side; she holds **no Llamanzares Contract-to-Sell** (doc 1089
  T13, T18–19, T21–22).
- Their own receipts exhibit (doc 414): **earliest receipt is 20 Oct 2003** — a decade after the
  1992 SPA; none names Llamanzares; none predates the SPA. So she was not a "down-payment holder
  within the SPA's meaning" either. (This also converts SJ ¶10 from assertion to record.)
- She concedes she **never communicated with the owners** before the sale (doc 1089 T43).

A sale outside a special agent's mandate is **void as to that buyer**; good faith cannot cure it
(*nemo dat quod non habet*; Civil Code Arts. 1874, 1877–1878; special powers strictly construed —
*Cosmic Lumber*, G.R. 114311; *Bautista-Spille v. Nicorp*, G.R. 214057). **This bypasses the
good-faith fight and moots the revocation-notice dispute.**

**Backup prong (argue second, with honest limits):** the SPA was revoked 15 Aug 2005 (docs 76, 79)
— 11 years before the sale — and a buyer dealing with an agent bears the duty of inquiry
(*Yoshizaki*, G.R. 174978); Balane admits she only "inspected the Torrens title" and never asked
the owners. Honest limits (do not overstate): the 2005 disowning clause does not name Cesar; no
document proves 2005 notice *to him* (Salvador denies it, doc 407 S32); publication came 2020,
annotation 2021. Under the lead frame none of that matters.

### 4b. THE CRACK — our filed papers still lead with a retired theory

**The fix is argument-framing, not new evidence: at trial, concede the SPA authorized *a* sale and
stand on the class limitation + revocation.** Jonathan's testimony pack is already built on the

**This is the concrete substance behind Jonathan's "not proactive enough" concern — raise it

## 6. The greater picture — the documented de la Fuente campaign

This is the context Jonathan fears will be lost at trial. **It is documentary, not theory** — but
it must be used with discipline (full treatment: `04_CAMPAIGN_PATTERN_MEMO.md`).

**Seven de la Fuente sales are read and quotable in corpus**, each signed by him "as
Attorney-in-Fact of the Heirs of Mary W. Keesey": Balane (docs 233/239/366/415/432 — five versions
of one deed), **Rosalina Hansol — Gloria Balane's sister** (doc 413), Delia Nacasa, Alberto Victa,
Dolores Vela, Jose Pascual Jr., and a probable Cesar Ramirez (all in the RD-certified multi-deed
bundle **doc 590**). Balane's own JA corroborates the campaign by naming Ramirez and her sister as
fellow buyers (doc 1089 T39–T42).

**The two-instrument kill-shot (upgraded 2026-08-30 — Jonathan's flag, confirmed against
`T4497_AUTHORITY_OCR_FINDINGS.md`):** de la Fuente cited two different authority instruments across
the campaign, and **neither authorized the sale made under it**:
- **1991 Agcaoili SPA** (Doc 260/Pg 53/Ser 1991, 22 Aug 1991) — **administration only: no clause
  selling, conveying, or mortgaging anything** (vision-OCR verified from the instrument itself,
  docs 72 [cleanest, with notarial page] / 82 / 35; CTC in doc 369 pp 1–4). **The Hansol deed
  (doc 413) rests on this instrument** — a sale of land by an agent with no written authority to
  sell is **void under Art. 1874**, full stop; no class analysis even needed. *(Court-use gate:
  the reading is OCR-grade — have counsel eyeball the instrument image before offering; then it is
  exhibit-ready.)*
- **1992 Munda SPA** (doc 246) — a sale power, but class-limited to Llamanzares CTS /
  down-payment holders (§4a). The Balane and doc-590 deeds rest on this one.

And the deed proving the 1991-SPA void sale — to Gloria's own sister — was **procured from the RD
by defendant Erwin Balane himself** (doc 413, Ref. 2026002049). Their own exhibit documents a void
family purchase; handled correctly at trial, it backfires on them entirely.

**The verified fraud signals across the campaign:**
- **Below-value pricing:** Balane paid ₱96.6/sqm against the estate's own assessor market value of
  ~₱2.5M for the same parcel (~10% of MV; ledger docs 238/411); the other proven sales run
  ₱43–₱150/sqm.
- **Purpose betrayed:** the SPA's stated purpose was to raise money for the estate's unpaid realty
  taxes — the estate still owed **₱775,202** in RPT in 2023 (doc 290). Where the ~₱458,400 of
  known proceeds went is unaccounted (Art. 1891 accounting claim against Cesar's estate).
- **Serial re-execution:** the same Balane sale was papered **five times across 2016** with
  varying dates/notaries — evidence of a defective deed being patched, not a clean transaction.
- **Void sub-delegation:** Cesar purported to delegate his agency to Salvador O. dela Fuente in
  2012 (doc 38); absent principal authorization, sub-agency is void (Art. 1892). Cesar **died
  21 Jun 2017** (doc 673, RTC record in CV 6839) — so the 2021 registration cascade necessarily
  ran through others, four years after the agent's death.

**What "syndicate" is and is not, on the record:** a coordinated *campaign by one fiduciary*, with
family links among buyers and an unauthorized successor, is VERIFIED. A criminal syndicate
involving officials is **INFERENCE — PENDING VERIFICATION** and must never be asserted as fact in
court. The strongest verified thread in that direction: **the name "Romel C. Jacinto" appears as
instrumental witness on the Balane deed and the 2017 Deed of Confirmation AND as the OIC Register
of Deeds who issued Balane's title on 23 Nov 2021** (document faces read: doc 369 pp 10/16/22, per
`T4497_AUTHORITY_OCR_FINDINGS.md`). The *names on the documents* are verified; that it is the
*same person*, and any inference of complicity, is not — counsel decides whether to probe it on
cross, never to assert it. The ARTA dockets against Mercedes officials and the NBI referral remain
separate, human-gated tracks.

**Strategic sequencing (the estate-wide stakes of this trial):**
- The estate-wide consolidated RTC nullity/reconveyance action
  (`investigations/MWK-DLF/RTC_COMPLAINT_OUTLINE.md`) is deliberately **staged behind 26-360** —
