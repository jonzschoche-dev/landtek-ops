1992 de la Fuente Special Power of Attorney will either anchor or obstruct the estate-wide
recovery action we have discussed. I want your eyes on how the trial is framed before September 9,
and I would like the two of you and me aligned on one strategy — not two.

I have attached a briefing package prepared from our case database. Every factual statement in it
is keyed to a specific source document. In short, it covers:

1. **The case and its posture** — including two open items that concern me: the court has never
   issued a written resolution of our Summary Judgment motion (filed April 24), and the August 28
   Order is silent on it and on the defense's motion to admit Gloria Balane's excluded judicial
   affidavit. The package proposes a records check at the MTC docket as the immediate first step.
2. **The theory of the case, layered** — the SPA contains no words of disposition at all
   (negotiate is not sell — Arts. 1874, 1878(5), 1879; Cosmic Lumber; Bautista-Spille); in the
   alternative, any sale power it granted was expressly limited to holders of Ben Llamanzares
   Contracts-to-Sell, a class Mrs. Balane admits under oath she was never in; and in any event the
   SPA was revoked in 2005, eleven years before the sale.
3. **The greater picture** — certified Registry documents in our hands showing this was not one
   sale but a serial campaign: at least seven de la Fuente conveyances, including one to Mrs.
   Balane's own sister under a 1991 SPA that grants no power to sell anything, at roughly ten
   percent of assessed value, with the proceeds never applied to the estate taxes the SPA existed
   to pay. The package sets out how this pattern can properly reach the judge inside this trial —
   and what must stay out of my mouth on the stand.
4. **My testimony preparation** — the direct spine and the anticipated cross, which I would like
   to rehearse with you and Atty. Barandon before the 9th.
