## My concerns, plainly

1. **The trial may be fought too small.** This is not one disputed sale. Cesar de la Fuente sold
   off at least **seven pieces of the estate** the same way — one of them to **Gloria Balane's own
   sister** — under powers of attorney that never authorized any of it, at a fraction of the
   land's value, and the money never reached us or the estate's taxes. We hold the registry
   documents proving it. My fear is that the trial gets executed as a routine one-parcel dispute
   and the judge never sees the pattern — and this case is the door to recovering everything else,
   because it is the case that rules on the SPA every other bad title depends on.

2. **Our own filed papers have a soft spot the defense has already aimed at.** Our Summary
   Judgment motion and my own affidavit argue the SPA "only allowed negotiating, not selling."
   The defense answers by reading aloud the SPA's clause about "executing documents and receiving
   proceeds." I don't want to lose on our own wording when we don't have to: the stronger ground
   is that whatever selling the SPA allowed was **only to people holding contracts from the prior
   administrator, Ben Llamanzares — and Mrs. Balane swears in her own affidavit she was a
   ₱50-a-month renter with no such contract who never once contacted us.** And the SPA was revoked
   in 2005, eleven years before the sale. I need the trial argued on all of these together, not on
   the one argument they've already answered.

3. **The court has gone silent on two motions that decide the shape of this trial.** No written
   ruling ever issued on our Summary Judgment motion (filed 24 April), and none on the defense's
   motion to bring back Gloria's judicial affidavit, which the court **excluded** at pre-trial.
   The 28-Aug Order says nothing about either. If her affidavit stays out, the defense has no main
   witness statement; either way, I should not be walking into court on the 9th not knowing.

4. **I don't feel the case is being pushed proactively**, and with nine days left I can't fix that
   by worrying. I can fix it by getting you and Atty. Barandon in one conversation, with the
   record in front of you.
