## 2. The doctrinal doors — how pattern evidence gets in at THIS trial

26-360 is a Summary-Procedure case about **one parcel**. The court will not adjudicate the estate-
wide fraud, and counsel should not ask it to. But the campaign evidence is admissible and useful
inside this case through four legitimate doors:

**Door 1 — Relevance to the SPA's meaning (our Exhibit A context).** The same 1992-Munda-SPA
recital appears verbatim across the doc 590 deeds. That the agent used one limited-object
instrument as an at-large sale machine is probative of *how the instrument was abused*, supporting
the strict construction we advocate (Arts. 1877–1878; *Cosmic Lumber*; *Bautista-Spille*).

**Door 2 — Defeating good faith (the defense's own theory).** Balane's good-faith claim opens the
door to what a reasonably diligent buyer would have found: a series of sales by the same agent,
her own sister among the buyers (doc 413), at a fraction of assessed value, with no owner contact.
Her own affidavit (if admitted) names the fellow buyers — T39–T42 — making the pattern partly
**their evidence**. Rule on habit/pattern: similar acts are inadmissible to prove conduct-by-
propensity but admissible for knowledge, intent, plan, scheme, or system — a *scheme or system* is
precisely what the identical recitals show. Counsel frames it as scheme/system, never propensity.

**Door 3 — Impeachment and cross.** If Gloria's JA comes in and she testifies remotely or through
it, the sister-sale (doc 413), the ₱50 rent, and the no-contact admission are cross material that
paints the picture without our side asserting anything.

**Door 4 — Damages/bad-faith equities.** Exemplary damages and bad-faith possession turn on the
character of the acquisition; the below-value pricing and the tenancy origin are directly relevant
there even if the court trims pattern evidence from the title question.

**What counsel must actually DO for the judge to "catch wind":**
1. **Formally offer** doc 590 (certified bundle) and doc 413 — pattern evidence never reaches the
   judge if it is never marked and offered. If pre-trial marking has closed, move for leave on the
   ground the bundle is RD-certified and doc 413 was *procured by the defendants themselves*.
2. Weave the pattern into the **trial memorandum / formal offer commentary** — two paragraphs:
   "the same instrument, the same recital, the same agent, serially" — with the doc-cites.
3. Elicit it on **cross of the defense witnesses** (Salvador especially: the receipts he signed,
   the delegation he took, the sales he knew of).
4. Keep Jonathan's direct clean of it unless a question opens the door (his card §"campaign
   discipline" governs).

**What NOT to do:** do not plead new parties, new parcels, or conspiracy counts into 26-360; do
not argue officials' complicity (that is the ARTA/Ombudsman lane, human-gated); do not let the
trial become a mini-trial of six other sales — the pattern is seasoning, the class limitation is
the meal.

## 4. One-paragraph summary for the two counsel

The record proves — with certified registry documents already in our hands — that one fiduciary,
holding one limited-object SPA, serially conveyed the Keesey estate to tenants, relatives, and
strangers at ~10% of assessed value, never applied the proceeds to the taxes the SPA existed to
pay, papered the flagship sale five times, purported to hand his agency to his brother without
authority, and died four years before the titles issued. That pattern belongs inside 26-360
through formal offer of docs 590/413, the good-faith rebuttal, and cross — argued as scheme and
system, never as "syndicate." The syndicate question, if it ripens, ripens in the RTC end-vehicle
and the criminal/administrative lanes, on the strength of a 26-360 win, not at its expense.
