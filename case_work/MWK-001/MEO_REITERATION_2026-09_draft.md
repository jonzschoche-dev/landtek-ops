**1. What this letter changes, and why.** The letter at item 2 was captioned as a request for information under Executive Order No. 2, s. 2016. **That characterisation is withdrawn.** The Authority has since held that a request so framed falls outside Section 4(f) of R.A. No. 11032 — while stating expressly that its finding *"should not … be construed as a determination by ARTA that Respondent's refusal or failure to release the requested documents was proper."* Nothing is therefore being re-argued. The same subject matter is placed before your Office on the footing it should have carried from the outset: **not a request for a service, but a request that a statutory duty be performed.**

The distinction matters and is not merely formal. The Citizen's Charter of the Municipality lists seven external services for your Office — Program of Works, Project Inspection Report, Certificate of Final Inspection, Building & Electrical Permit, Demolition Permit, Fencing Permit, and Occupancy Permit. Enforcement of the National Building Code is **not** among them, and it is not a service at all. It is the duty Section 207 places on the Building Official personally, and it is owed whether or not anyone applies for anything.

**2. The reiterated matter.** Attention is respectfully invited to the following, each a duty of your Office:

  **(a) Inspection — Sec. 207.** That your Office *"enter any building or its premises at all reasonable times to inspect and determine compliance with the requirements of this Code,"* beginning with the Barangay 1 structure that is the subject of your own letter at item 1, and render a written inspection report.

  **(b) Building permit — Sec. 301.** That your Office determine and state in writing, from its own register, whether a building permit was issued for each structure standing on the parcels at **Annex "B"**, with number and date.

  **(c) Certificate of Occupancy — Secs. 308 and 309.** That your Office state whether a Certificate of Occupancy was issued for each such structure. Section 309 bars use or occupancy until one is issued; Section 308 makes a Certificate of Completion its precondition. Each structure remains in daily use.

  **(d) Proof of right to build — Sec. 302(2).** Where a permit issued, what document was submitted in satisfaction of the requirement of a *"[c]ertified true copy of the TCT covering the lot on which the proposed work is to be done"* and, where the applicant is not the registered owner, *"a copy of the contract of lease."* The titles are in the heirs' names, and **no lease, deed, or written consent has ever been executed by the heirs in favour of any occupant.**

  **(e) Transmittal to the Assessor — R.A. 7160, Sec. 210.** Whether copies of any permits issued were transmitted to the Municipal Assessor within the thirty (30) days that Section 210 requires.

  **(f) Enforcement — Secs. 207, 301, 215.** Where no permit exists, the issuance of a notice of violation and an order to stop work; where a structure is used or occupied without a Certificate of Occupancy, an order discontinuing that occupancy; and action under Section 215 as to any structure found dangerous or ruinous.

**3. On the authority question at item 3.** The undersigned acts under a broad, apostilled Special Power of Attorney from Patricia Keesey Zschoche, registered co-owner and heir, **enclosed**. It is also of record in the proceedings in which your Office was respondent. It is respectfully submitted, without argument, that the duties at paragraph 2 are owed to the public at large and are not conditioned on the personality of the person who draws attention to the violation; but the instrument is enclosed so that the question does not arise.

**4. No demolition is sought by any private hand**, and none is proposed. Every item above is an act of the proper officer, taken after his own inspection and with due process to every occupant.

**5. Period.** Section 5(a) of R.A. No. 6713 provides that *"[a]ll public officials and employees shall, within fifteen (15) working days from receipt thereof, respond to letters, telegrams or other means of communications sent by the public,"* and that *"[t]he reply must contain the action taken on the request."* A written reply stating the action taken on each of items (a) to (f) is accordingly requested **within fifteen (15) working days of receipt**. A reply stating that a particular item cannot be acted upon, with the reason, is a reply; silence is not.

A chronology of this matter is at **Annex "A"** so that the periods are plain on the face of the record. Should your Office require a longer period for any item, kindly say so in writing and state the period required.
