## Golden rules
- **Facts over adjectives.** "He built a structure and admitted it on 19 March" beats "the land is in peril."
- **Documents over memory.** Areas, values, dates → "it's in Exhibit ___." Don't guess. "I'd have to check the title."
- **Two sentences, then stop.** Silence is not your enemy. Don't fill it.
- **Concede small truths cheerfully** (Gerry didn't consent; the affidavit says "age"; you're the plaintiff's son). Be immovable on: **they are absent, they refuse, the land is being taken, and I'm asking to be supervised.**
- **Law and pleadings → "that's for counsel."** Rule 92, Rule 95, ancillary probate, the 1993 settlement, Letters of Administration — never from your mouth.
- **Don't characterize Gerry.** Not "greedy," not "difficult," not "she abandoned it." Quote her: *"We don't care to get involved."* Let the judge draw the conclusion.
- **Never mention the revenue engine.** In this courtroom the guardianship exists to *preserve*. Every true thing about monetization waits for a Rule 95 motion.
