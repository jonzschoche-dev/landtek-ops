Thank you for your letter of 24 August 2026 and for the two 2nd Indorsements of 25 August 2026. I acknowledge, and place on record my appreciation for, the concrete supervisory actions your Office has taken: the transmittal of my 18 August letter in full to the Municipal Mayor with a directive for a written action-taken report; the separate indorsement of the records matters to the Sangguniang Bayan through its Secretary; the transmittal of the complete record to the Provincial Director and the Regional Director; and your written commitment to advise me of the LGU's submission. This is the supervision the law contemplates, and I thank you for exercising it.

I write on a single point of clarification, so that one item is not lost from the LGU's pending response on a mistaken premise.

**Your letter sets aside Item 3(g)/4(f) — the Municipal Hall parcel — as *sub judice* by reference to Civil Case No. 26-360. Respectfully, that case does not touch this parcel.** Civil Case No. 26-360 is an *accion reinvindicatoria* against **private defendants** (the Balane parties) concerning a **different derivative parcel** of the estate. Neither the municipal compound, nor TCT No. T-32911, nor the Municipality of Mercedes is a party to, or the subject of, that case — or of any pending case. **There is no judicial proceeding, anywhere, adjudicating the ground on which the municipal compound stands.**

Nor does my request ask any DILG office to adjudicate anything. The situation is documentary: the heirs hold a **registered Torrens title** to that ground (TCT No. T-32911; the DENR's own records projection, letter LMS-25-550 of 26 November 2025, places the compound's lots within it), while the Municipality — across two years of requests — has produced **no title, no deed, no expropriation judgment, no instrument of any kind, and no proof of lawful occupancy**; yet it asserted its "legal title" in writing to your Office on 24 July 2026. My request 4(f) asks only that the LGU **identify the document** on which that written assertion rests — **or correct the assertion**. Producing a document, or stating that none exists, adjudicates no right and intrudes on no court. It is a records-and-compliance matter of exactly the kind your 2nd Indorsements already cover.

For completeness, I should state what I have actually seen. In two years of inquiry, the only paper I have ever encountered purporting to support the Municipality's occupation of this ground is a **photocopy of an unsigned instrument — unregistered, never annotated on any certificate of title, and never produced in any original.** If that is what the 24 July letter means by "legal title," the clarification sought here becomes all the more necessary: under the Torrens system, a title is a **certificate of title registered with the Register of Deeds**. An unsigned, unregistered photocopy is not a title; it conveys nothing, and it cannot stand against the heirs' registered certificate. If the Municipality holds something more than that photocopy, Item 4(f) asks only that it be **identified**. If it does not, the written assertion should be **corrected** — that is the whole of the request.

**I therefore respectfully request** that Item 3(g)/4(f), so understood, be included in the LGU's pending written response — whether by a brief supplemental indorsement or within the response your Office already awaits — so that the record your Office compiles for the Provincial and Regional Directors is complete on all eight items.

Nothing further is requested at this time. I await your advice of the LGU's submission, per your letter.

Thank you again for your Office's action, and for your service.
