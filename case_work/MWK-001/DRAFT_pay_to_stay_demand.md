**Counsel work product for Atty. Botor / Atty. Barandon.** The instrument that turns the 7-ha occupants into
payers **now, on Patricia's 1/3 alone** — without a formal lease (which would need a majority) and without
waiting on the guardianship. **LandTek is not a law firm; nothing served without operator + counsel.**
Worked for **T-52537** (Lot 2-X-4-E, 152 sqm, PIN 025-07-003-01-057, ARP GR-2014-HH-07-003-00169, Barangay 3,
Mercedes; parent T-32916), likely occupant **Sps. Delfin & Luisa Gaulit** `[HUMAN VERIFY]`. Reusable across
every T-32917 occupant.

## Why this works on 1/3 (the legal footing — do NOT drift from it)
- It is **NOT a lease.** A lease of the whole is administration needing a majority of interest (Art. 492);
  Patricia has 1/3. So this instrument is deliberately **compensation for the occupant's use of the common
  property** — the flip side of a co-owner's Art. 487 right to recover possession and reasonable compensation,
  which **one co-owner may enforce alone, for all**. The occupant paying voluntarily is discharging what they
  would owe anyway; no new tenancy is created.
- Keep it **month-to-month, terminable at will, without prejudice, and expressly "not a lease / creates no
  tenancy / no recognition of any right in the occupant."** That wording is what keeps it (a) inside Patricia's
  solo authority and (b) from ripening into security-of-tenure or a lease that binds the estate.
- **Do NOT frame it as agricultural share/tenancy** (RA 3844/6657 trap). This is occupation of titled land;
  the payment is compensation for wrongful occupation, not an agri-tenancy.
- **Does NOT waive past back-rent** — the pay-to-stay runs forward; the accrued compensation stays reserved.

## Guardrails (do not skip)
- Never call it a lease; keep the terminable/without-prejudice/not-a-tenancy wording verbatim.
- **UDHA screen** (RA 7279): confirm the occupant isn't underprivileged/homeless entitled to relocation before
  any *ejectment*; a competing-tax-dec/means-having occupant (Gaulit) is not shielded. Voluntary pay-to-stay
  is unaffected either way.
- Titled adverse holders (Balane + the 7 transferees) get the **nullity/reconveyance** track, **not** a
  pay-to-stay (offering it could imply recognizing their possession) — pay-to-stay is for **informal** occupants.
- Confirm occupant identity, the rate, and RPT status before service. Nothing served without Jonathan's go.
