### SWORN STATEMENT

**I, BEN BUMATAY BILINAN,** Filipino, of legal age, with residence at ______________________________________________, after having been duly sworn to in accordance with law, hereby depose and state:

1. I work as a handyman for **Mr. Allan V. Inocalla** in connection with the residential/apartment building at **2531 G. Del Pilar Street, Barangay 759, Santa Ana, City of Manila** (the "Property").

2. On **14 August 2026, at about 4:00 in the afternoon**, together with Mr. Allan V. Inocalla, we purchased cleaning and repair tools, equipment, and materials worth approximately **FIFTY THOUSAND PESOS (₱50,000.00)**, and we started cleaning and repairing the Property, beginning with **Apartment No. 3**. When we finished working that day, **we locked the door of Apartment No. 3**, with our tools, equipment, and materials secured inside.

3. Mr. Inocalla thereafter left for Bicol and authorized me to continue the cleaning and repair work at the Property in his absence.

4. On the morning of **15 August 2026**, I returned to the Property to continue the work.

5. I found that the **locked Apartment No. 3 had been broken into and opened without our consent**, and that **our cleaning and repair tools, equipment, and materials had been thrown away and were gone** from where we had secured them.

6. Upon the instruction of Mr. Inocalla, I **reported the incident in the blotter of Barangay 759** that same morning, and I likewise went to the **PNP station** to report it.

7. When I thereafter went back to the Property to resume work, I was **stopped and confronted by ACE VICENTE INOCALLA III** (also known as Vicente de Leon Inocalla III), who **threatened that I would be harmed if I entered the Property**.

8. Because of said threat, I was no longer able to continue my work at the Property, and I feared for my personal safety.

9. I am executing this Sworn Statement freely and voluntarily to attest to the truth of the foregoing based on my own personal knowledge, and to corroborate the Affidavit-Complaint of Mr. Allan V. Inocalla against Ace Vicente Inocalla III and Elena de Leon Inocalla Bombita.

**IN WITNESS WHEREOF,** I have hereunto set my hand this ____ day of September 2026 in the City of Manila, Philippines.

## PART III — COUNSEL-REVIEW FLAGS (must clear before Ben swears)

1. **¶¶5–7 are drafted from Allan's relayed account, not from Ben's own mouth.** Read the entire statement to Ben in Filipino and correct it to his actual recollection before swearing, in particular:
   - **¶4–5 (the discovery):** Did Ben personally see the broken-into unit and the missing/discarded tools, and when exactly? If he only *learned* of the break-in (e.g., from Punong Barangay Chua or a tenant) before going, say that instead — his statement must not overreach his personal knowledge any more than Allan's does. Note Allan's account has the barangay/PNP report (¶6) *before* Ben was stopped at the property (¶7); if Ben's memory sequences it differently (saw the break-in first → reported → returned → was threatened), fix the order to match HIS memory.
   - **¶5 (what happened to the tools):** "thrown away and gone" — get specifics from Ben: what did he see (tools scattered outside? in the trash? simply missing?), which items, condition of the door/lock/padlock. Concrete detail here decides robbery (force upon things) vs. theft vs. malicious mischief.
   - **¶7 (the threat):** If Ben remembers Ace's actual words (Tagalog), quote them verbatim in quotation marks and keep the paraphrase as fallback. Verbatim words materially strengthen a grave-threats charge. Also: was anyone else present who heard it?
2. **One language, one execution.** The .docx has the English version and the Filipino version on separate pages. Ben signs ONLY the version in the language he is comfortable in — an affiant swearing to an English text he can't read is impeachment fodder. If English is executed, consider adding a standard interpretation clause ("the contents were translated and explained to me in Filipino, which I speak and understand").
3. **Fill in Ben's personal circumstances** — complete residence address, competent evidence of identity. If Ben resides at or near the Property, note the security concern (Part IV #7 of Allan's file — the Bombita family caution) applies to him with more force; he is the one who was directly threatened.
4. **Attach Ben's paper trail:** the 15 Aug 2026 Barangay 759 blotter entry (certified copy) and any PNP record of his same-day report are *his* corroboration — annex them to this statement (or cross-reference Annex "I" of Allan's affidavit).
5. **Consistency check against Allan's affidavit before filing both:** dates (14–15 Aug), the ₱50,000 figure, "Apartment No. 3," and the sequence of reports must match across the two affidavits or be honestly explained — small unexplained contradictions between complainant and witness are the first thing a defense counsel will use.
