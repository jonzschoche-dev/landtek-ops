1. **Item 32 reads Residence, not Place of Business.** The 2026-09-18 key had "Place of Business"
   ticked against a Capacuan residential address, which cannot both be true. Capacuan is written, so
   **Residence** is ticked here. If Ramon R. Ramos does not in fact reside at Purok 4, Capacuan,
   item 33 needs his own address and this tick may change.
2. **Civil status.** **Single** is written, on Allan's own word of 2026-09-18. The file elsewhere
   carries "separated" — his 2 May 2026 statement, the DANB loan form, and the opening line of the
   Bombita complaint-affidavit. A tax registration and a sworn affidavit that disagree is a small
   thread worth not leaving loose.
3. **The ID address.** The driver's licence prints a Manila address; the form declares Capacuan.
   Bring the Barangay Certificate of Residency from Barangay Capacuan — the Bureau's own note on
   page 4 asks for IDs "consistent with the documents submitted."
