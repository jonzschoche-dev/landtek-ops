Title: CONCEPT PAPER — Upgrading a Minahang Bayan Small-Scale Mining Operation to a Financial or Technical Assistance Agreement (FTAA) Project, utilizing the planetGOLD approach at the Casalugan Mercury-Free Processing System, Brgy. Casalugan, Paracale, Camarines Norte.

Summary of contents (verbatim structure; full text held by Jonathan):
1. Executive summary — upgrade the Casalugan Minahang Bayan operations (centred on the planetGOLD MFPS turned over to SMBC) into an FTAA project; 100% foreign technical/financial participation; community ownership and benefit-sharing retained; priority interventions = leach/recovery upgrade, world-class tailings, wastewater, decontamination.
2. Background — ASGM ≈70% of PH gold output; MFPS built under planetGOLD (GEF-funded; UNIDO/UNEP; Artisanal Gold Council with DENR-MGB) on land allocated by SMBC within its Minahang Bayan; gravity + controlled leaching; recovery reportedly ~40–50% → ~80%+ in trials; plant and training facility turned over to SMBC in 2025; capacity limited relative to district potential; scaling needs capital + expertise + a legal vehicle permitting full foreign investment = FTAA.
3. Rationale/objectives — MB restricted by design; FTAA = Const. Art. XII §2 + RA 7942; six objectives incl. transition Casalugan (and contiguous community areas) to FTAA scale under one project, expand the existing plant, best-in-class TSF/detox/wastewater, maximise local benefit, replicable model.
4. Project concept — FTAA contractor (foreign/majority-foreign) works with or incorporates SMBC (or successor SPV) via equity, offtake, royalty; technical pillars: plant expansion (crushing/grinding, multi-stage gravity, optimised cyanidation or alternative lixiviants, CIL/CIP, electrowinning), environmental controls (engineered TSF or dry-stack, cyanide destruction to ICMC, wastewater treatment, legacy mercury decontamination, monitoring), mine development (PMRC resource, mechanised UG/open pit).
5. Legal framework — Art. XII §2; RA 7942; DAO 2010-21; FTAA to a Qualified Person incl. foreign-owned corp; max 81,000 ha onshore (1,000 meridional blocks); 25 + 25 years; "on the order of PHP 500 million paid-up prior to registration"; ≥US$50M for infrastructure/development; financial guarantee/bond; integration with existing MB rights (tenure continuity, SSM rights, FPIC, LGU endorsements, benefit sharing, SDMP); mandatory documents list.
6. Step-by-step pathway — 8 stages: pre-application; partnership & tenure alignment (MOA/SHA; SSM contract continuity or surrender/conversion with MGB); FTAA filing; area clearance & publication; evaluation & negotiation (government share); presidential approval, notification to Congress, registration; exploration/feasibility (DMPF, FS, ECC, EPEP, SDMP, FMR/DP); development & operations.
7. Technical upgrade priorities — leach/recovery list; TSF/wastewater/decontamination list.
8. Expected benefits — economic, environmental, social, demonstration.
9. Roadmap — Phase 1 Foundation (m1–6), Phase 2 Resource definition & feasibility (m6–24), Phase 3 Approval & permitting (m18–30), Phase 4 Construction (m24–42), Phase 5 Ramp-up.
10. Conclusion/next steps — multi-stakeholder consultation convened by SMBC and LGU; legal/technical advisors; baseline studies; preliminary discussions with foreign partners. Disclaimer: not an application, FS, or offer of securities.
