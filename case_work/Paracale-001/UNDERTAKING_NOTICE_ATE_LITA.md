> Jonathan/Allan want to send her a copy of **Casper's signed Undertaking of August 25, 1985**

> **Companion:** `LETTER_ATE_LITA_2026-08.md` (the goodwill letter, drafted 2026-08-24).

## 1. What sending the Undertaking accomplishes — and what it can't

**Why it is the right move now:**
- It **fixes Lita's knowledge** of the matured obligation, in a way she cannot later deny. The
  Heirs of Casper already acknowledged the Undertaking in the Final SPA (§5); a delivered copy +
  proof of receipt closes the "I never knew" door and supports bad faith if she sells anyway.
- It gives her the **face-saving family path**: honor Casper's own signed word — the exact
  "preserve his legacy" frame she herself offered on 2026-08-23. The goodwill letter set this up
  perfectly; this letter cashes it in.
- Anyone she is negotiating with who learns of the Undertaking has a harder time claiming
  **good-faith-purchaser** status — especially on parcels with no Torrens title.

**What a letter can NOT do — do not rely on it alone:**
- A letter binds *her conscience and the record*, not the land. Only registration-level acts
  (adverse claim / lis pendens through counsel) and direct notice to buyers stop a Torrens
  transfer. If sales are real and imminent, **§4 matters more than the letter.**

**Scope caution:** the Undertaking covers **only the four DBP lots** (T-20754/55/56/57, old
TCTs 4781/5941/4251/4695, ≈63.99 ha). If Lita is selling *other* Casper parcels, the lever is
different but still real: **no EJS was ever executed**, so estate property is undivided — she can
validly sell only her own undivided share (conjugal half + heir share), and her buyers step into a
co-ownership mess. The letter below covers both with one sentence (¶4's "or any other property
that is part of the family estate").

## 2. Pre-send checklist (do BEFORE the letter goes out)

1. **Check the back/next page of the original for a notarial block** (the faint "…conjugal
   partnership…" footer suggests one). Photograph every page, front and back, high-res.
2. **Send a PHOTOCOPY only. The signed original never leaves Allan's custody** — it is the
   keystone exhibit. Do not concede, mention, or invite discussion of the document's form in any
   conversation; if she or a lawyer raises notarization, the answer is "the family will address
   that with counsel."
3. **Provable delivery:** hand it over with a witness + photo, or registered mail with return
   card, or both. The notice value depends on proving she received it and when.
4. Ingest the Undertaking photos into the Paracale-001 corpus (retrieval-list item #1 — still open).

## 3. Draft letter (Allan → Ate Lita, transmitting the Undertaking)

> Send a few days after the goodwill letter if time allows; if a sale looks imminent, send both
> together — the frame ("Kuya Casper's legacy") is the same, so they do not clash.

Dear Ate Lita,

Following our conversation, I am sharing with you a copy of a document that is very close to my
heart: the Undertaking that Kuya Casper himself signed on August 25, 1985. I am keeping the
original safe for the family; this copy is yours.

In it, Kuya Casper put into his own written word what was to happen with the four titled lands he
repurchased from DBP — TCT 4781, 5941, 4251, and 4695, about 64 hectares in San Rafael, Jose
Panganiban and in Capacuan, Paracale. He undertook that once the lands were fully paid, they would
be transferred: TCT 4781 to Marilou, TCT 5941 to Herbert, and TCT 4251 and 4695 to Senen — and
Senen having passed away, her portion now belongs to her estate. The payments to DBP were
completed, so Kuya Casper's promise now stands ready to be fulfilled.

You told me that you and your children are committed to preserving Kuya Casper's legacy. This
document is his legacy — his signed word to his own brothers and sisters. Honoring it is the
truest way to preserve it, and the whole family will always remember that it was his wife and
children who kept it.

Because of this, I ask you with respect: please do not sell, mortgage, or promise to anyone these
four properties, or any other property that is part of our parents' estate, until we have sat down
together as a family and given effect to what Kuya Casper wrote. If any arrangement is already in
progress, please tell me now, so we can fix it together quietly — before it creates problems for
you, for your children, or for any buyer.

Let us settle this within the family, with love and respect, the way Kuya Casper would have wanted.

Your brother,

Allan

**Drafting guards (why the letter reads the way it does):**
- It **never accuses her of selling** — the report is [O]/unverified; the letter asks her to
  disclose and pause, which works whether or not the report is exact.
- It **asserts the obligation as ripe** ("payments were completed") on Allan's authority — fine
  for a family letter; the DBP Certificate of Full Payment is still needed before any court use.
- It says **nothing about notarization, lawyers, or cases** — one soft line ("before it creates
  problems… for any buyer") carries the warning without a threat.
- "Or any other property that is part of our parents' estate" extends the pause-request beyond
  the four lots without a legal lecture on co-ownership.

## 4. Protection track (runs in parallel — letters don't stop deeds)

| # | Action | Who | Why now |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Identify what she is selling**: parcels, buyers, brokers, stage (listing? earnest money? deed signed?) — barangay-level intel, tax-map/ARP pulls | Allan (field) | everything else keys off this |
| 2 | **RD Cam-Norte CTCs for T-20754/55/56/57** (search old nos. 4781/5941/4251/4695) — current registered owner + fresh annotations (a recent deed would show here) | Allan (field) | was already the "decisive next step"; now URGENT |
