**I, MARIO BANGASAN,** Filipino, of legal age, President of the **Philippine Inventors, Scientists and Innovators, Inc.**, with residence at ______________________________________________, after having been duly sworn to in accordance with law, hereby depose and state:

1. On **5 September 2026, at about 5:30 in the afternoon**, upon the invitation of **Mr. Allan V. Inocalla**, I was at his family's property at **2531 G. All of you, get out of here!"*).

3. Mr. Inocalla tried to calm the man down, but the man continued shouting threats and abusive words at him. I observed that the man was **holding his phone and appeared to be calling someone** while he was doing this.

4. Mr. Inocalla told us it was better to leave, and our group walked away toward **Pablo Ocampo Street**.

5. The man **followed and came toward Mr. Inocalla**, who stopped and faced him. The man continued yelling threats at Mr. Inocalla, shouting words to the effect of: **"Anong gusto mo? Patitirahin kita!"** (in English: *"What do you want? I will strike you!"*).

7. Because of the incident, we were **not able to complete the assessment and evaluation** of the Property that we came for.

8. I am executing this Sworn Statement freely and voluntarily to attest to the truth of the foregoing, which I personally saw and heard, and in support of the complaint of Mr. Allan V. Inocalla against Ace Vicente Inocalla III.

## PART III — COUNSEL-REVIEW FLAGS (both witnesses)

1. **Interview each witness separately before he signs, and let him correct the draft to his own memory.** The two drafts above are intentionally NOT word-identical, but they are both built from Allan's account [O]. The quoted utterances especially: each witness should state the words **he** actually heard — if one witness clearly heard "barilin kita" but the other only heard shouting and curses without making out the words, their statements should say exactly that. Honest differences between witnesses corroborate; scripted uniformity impeaches. Keep "words to the effect of" unless a witness is certain of verbatim words (then quote verbatim).
1a. **"Barilin kita" (I will shoot you) is the gravest utterance in the drafts — it must come from each witness's own ears.** Allan corrected his own account the same day from "susuntukin" (punch) to "barilin" (shoot), with the added epithets "manloloko, magnanakaw" (see the supplemental affidavit's Part IV #1). Do NOT tell the witnesses what Allan heard before asking them what they heard — take each man's version cold, then reconcile. A witness who independently confirms "barilin" makes the grave-threats charge near-unassailable; a witness fed the word is worthless and dangerous. Neither witness statement mentions Allan's knowledge of Ace's firearm — that stays exclusively in Allan's affidavit unless a witness independently knew or saw it.
2. **Language check.** Both statements are in English on the assumption that a corporate president and a licensed architect are comfortable in it; the Filipino quotes are preserved verbatim with translations, which is the correct form. If either prefers Filipino, mirror the format of Ben Bilinan's Sinumpaang Salaysay (`BLOTTER_AFFIDAVIT_BEN_BILINAN_2026-09-02.md` Part II).
3. **Complete their personal circumstances**: full legal names (confirm "Mario Bangasan" and "Mike/Michael Guevara" against their IDs), residence addresses, Guevara's PRC license number (professional standing adds weight — include it if he consents), and the **exact registered name of Bangasan's organization** — "Philippine Inventors, Scientists and Innovators, Inc." is reconstructed from Allan's message [O]; check the SEC registration or the org's letterhead.
4. **Identification of Ace (¶2 in both):** neither witness knew Ace beforehand — both drafts therefore say "whom Mr. Inocalla identified to us as his nephew," which is the honest basis. If either witness can identify him from a photo lineup or at the investigation, that direct identification supersedes and strengthens this. Do not draft them as personally knowing him.
5. **Their photographs are evidence twice over:** whichever of the group took the building photos, collect originals with EXIF/timestamps from every phone — they establish presence, time, and the lawful purpose of the visit. If either witness caught any part of the confrontation on video/audio, that is the single most valuable exhibit in the entire matter — preserve the original file, not a compressed re-send.
6. **Logistics:** each affidavit is executed individually before the administering officer (prosecutor's office, or notary) — the witnesses need not appear together. If the supplemental complaint goes to the Office of the City Prosecutor, these two statements attach to it as supporting affidavits; give each witness a copy of what he signed.
