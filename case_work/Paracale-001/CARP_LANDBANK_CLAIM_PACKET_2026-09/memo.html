3. Where the real value may be (questions for the reviewer)

  1. The 56.76 ha ordered into CARP in 2008 has no claim on file. DAR Secretary Order EXC-0804-156 (14 Apr 2008), on Casper Inocalla's petition for the heirs, excluded 202.10 ha as mineral land. It also directed DAR to place the remaining 56.7596 ha under CARP: T-3897 (Vicente L. Inocalla, 23.0935 ha), T-3424 (Beatriz Villafria, 22.5175 ha) and T-20755 (in DBP's name, 11.1486 ha) [Ex B-3]. That is more than twice the area of the two approved claims. We found no Land Bank claim folder, notice of valuation or CLOA for any of these three titles. Ask DAR PARO Camarines Norte and Land Bank AOC-V directly whether these were acquired and valued. If they were, a third, larger claim is unpaid. If they were not, the heirs still own the land. T-20755 also carries the 1985 Casper repurchase undertaking in favour of Senen's estate, so who is entitled there needs separate analysis.
  2. Is the 1999 valuation challengeable? The valuation works out to about ₱13,400/ha (T-1722) and ₱10,100/ha (T-1827). A petition to fix just compensation before the Special Agrarian Court (RTC) could raise it. Counsel to confirm whether that right has lapsed: current jurisprudence (e.g., LBP v. Dalauta, 2017) applies a 10-year period counted from notice of coverage. It also matters that the valuation seems never to have been accepted.
  3. Interest. Legal interest for late payment is awarded when the government delays. Land Bank will argue the delay here is the heirs'. Counsel to assess.
  4. Which route to authority? Getting an all-heir extrajudicial settlement means reaching every branch. That includes Vicente Jr.'s branch (Ace's side, which is hostile), Cipriana's seven children (no SPA), Francisco's ten children (Canada), Melvin's heirs and Casper's heirs. The court route, letters of administration, is already planned for the Inocalla estate (the intestate / special-administrator petition). It would let one appointed administrator collect these claims without every heir signing. Recommendation: fold these claims into that petition's estate inventory rather than chase signatures.
  5. Land Bank's contact person has died. Both letters go to Ms. Senen V. Inocalla (shot 2021). The heirs should write to Land Bank AOC-V to name a new contact, and ask for the current claim status and any expiry of the approval.
