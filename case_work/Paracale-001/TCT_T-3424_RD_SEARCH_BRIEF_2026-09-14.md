## 4. ⚠ The big risk: this land was ordered placed under CARP

**DAR Secretary Order dated 14 April 2008** (Nasser C. Pangandaman), docket A-9999-05-EXE-096-03, *Heirs of Vicente Inocalla Sr., represented by Casper Inocalla*. On appeal of the heirs' petition to exclude the family lands from CARP as mineral land, DAR partly granted it. It excluded 202.1026 ha as mineral land, but **directed the MARO/PARO to place under CARP coverage the remaining 56.7596 ha covered by TCT Nos. T-3897, T-3424, and T-20755.**
- Source: DAR Order, doc 1611 (4043064952650545.pdf pp. 9–14) and certified-copy photos 951452160728962 / 1320658876868976 / 1878975469446309.
- **Why:** the MGB report found lots #1, #2 and #10 are **not traversed** by the four gold-bearing quartz vein systems (Vallejo, Manila, Virginia, Pittsburgh). Lots #1 and #2 are transected by fractures inferred from air-photos that *may* host veins. MGB recommended exploration on those three lots.

**What this means for the RD trip:**
- T-3424 may already be **partly or wholly cancelled** by CLOA titles issued to farmer-beneficiaries after 2008. The same thing happened on T-1722 in Calaburnay: seven CLOAs were registered in 1998 while the tax dec stayed in Vicente's name.
- **Also check at the DAR Municipal Office, Jose Panganiban (MARO):** was a Notice of Coverage issued on T-3424? Were CLOAs generated? Was just compensation valued or paid by Land Bank? If the land was acquired, **compensation money may be owed to the heirs.** That is a separate lane.
- ⚠ **Contradiction to fix:** the "Short-Term Go Areas" chart that circulated (876392672111355.png) lists T-3424 as **"Confirmed mineralized (listed in cert)."** The DAR Order and the MGB findings it quotes say the opposite: **not traversed by the veins.** Do not repeat the "confirmed mineralized" claim in anything outward.
