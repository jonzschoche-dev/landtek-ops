## 2. Design decisions baked into the drafts (counsel to confirm or change)

1. **One proceeding for both spouses.** Vicente Sr. († 26 Nov 1984) and Beatriz († 12 Aug 1985) [V doc 509] were married before the Family Code, so the property regime is the **conjugal partnership of gains under the Civil Code**; Rule 73 §2 lets the conjugal partnership be liquidated in the intestate proceeding of either spouse, and where both have died a single joint settlement is the practice. The Manila building (TCT 44055, reinstated as (44055) 002-2026001670) is titled to **Beatriz** "married to Vicente Inocalla" [V RD Manila e-CTC], so Beatriz's estate must be in the caption or that asset is left out.
2. **Vehicle = petition for letters of administration (Rule 79) with a Rule 80 special-administrator prayer**, not a stand-alone special-administrator motion. A special administrator exists only *pending* regular letters, so the main petition must be on file. The urgent motion (02) rides on the petition.
3. **Venue = RTC of Camarines Norte** (Rule 73 §1 — province where the decedents resided at death). **Residence at death is not yet proven by any paper we hold.** The only indication is the registered-owner address "Paracale, Camarines Norte, Phils." carried on the reinstated Manila title [V RD Manila e-CTC p.1] — an address of record, not proof of 1984–85 residence — plus the operator's statement [O]. (The 1985 Undertaking's "Batobalani, Paracale" is **Casper's** residence, not the decedents'.) ⟦Counsel: the PSA death certificates (Annexes A/A-1) settle this; also confirm which RTC station (Daet Branches 38–41 vs Labo Br. 64 — from public SC station listings, verify) has territorial jurisdiction over Paracale.⟧
4. **Allan is the nominee** (operator decision 2026-07-04). The 2006 fraud finding in CC 98-88750 is **disclosed in the petition itself** (¶ 7.4) and answered with his heir-authorized service since 2024 (the documented SPAs run from July 2024; his part in the 13-131220 execution is operator-attested until the AIF-signed pleading is pulled); a bond is offered. Jesus is held in reserve as co-administrator if opposition at hearing makes it necessary.
5. **Ace and Elena are named as persons claiming an interest through Vicente Jr.**, served with notice, *without admitting* their filiation/adoption. They are not omitted (Rule 79 §2 requires all heirs and interested persons) and they are not conceded.
6. **The 1992 judicial partition (CC B-5625, also cited as 5626, RTC Br. 41 Daet; approved 28 Jan 1992; Certificate of Finality 26 Mar 2014) is pleaded, not hidden.** Its awards were never carried into the titles; the petition asks the settlement court to take the estate as it stands *subject to* that judgment and to complete/implement it, and to administer everything the partition did not reach. ⟦Counsel: the docket number must be fixed from the court record before filing; and confirm no Rule 73 §1 exclusivity problem — the 1992 case was a partition among heirs, not a settlement proceeding, and it is terminated.⟧
7. **Estate tax is flagged, not solved.** Both deaths are 1984–85. RA 11213 (Estate Tax Amnesty) as amended by RA 11569 and RA 11956 covered deaths on or before 31 May 2022, with the availment window last extended to **14 June 2025** (external research, not from the corpus — verify against the BIR's current issuance). ⟦Counsel + tax adviser: verify whether a further extension was enacted after 14 June 2025. If not, the regular estate-tax regime with surcharges/interest applies and must be budgeted before any distribution; it does not block filing the petition.⟧
