### AFFIDAVIT-COMPLAINT

**I, ALLAN V. INOCALLA,** Filipino, of legal age, with residence at Purok 4, Capacuan, Paracale, Camarines Norte, and at 2531 G. Del Pilar Street, Barangay 759, Santa Ana, City of Manila, after having been duly sworn to in accordance with law, hereby depose and state:

**THE PARTIES**

1. I am the complainant. I file this Affidavit-Complaint against **ACE VICENTE INOCALLA III** (also known as **Vicente de Leon Inocalla III**, alias "Ace") and **ELENA DE LEON INOCALLA BOMBITA**, both of whom may be served with notice at 2531 G. Del Pilar Street, Barangay 759, Santa Ana, City of Manila (the "Respondents").

**OWNERSHIP AND AUTHORITY**

2. The property involved is the residential/apartment building at **2531 G. Del Pilar Street, Barangay 759, Santa Ana, City of Manila** (the "Property"), covered by **Transfer Certificate of Title No. 44055** in the name of my late mother, **Beatriz V. Villafria, married to Vicente Inocalla, Sr.**, my late father.

3. By final judgment in **Civil Case No. 13-131220** (Regional Trial Court, Branch 15, Manila), affirmed by the Court of Appeals in **CA-G.R. SP No. 161072** and by the Supreme Court in **G.R. No. 256997** (final and executory on 31 August 2022), the title formerly invoked by the late Vicente Inocalla, Jr. — Respondents' predecessor — was declared **null and void and cancelled**, and by Order dated 25 November 2025 the court directed the **reinstatement of TCT No. 44055 in the name of Beatriz V. Villafria married to Vicente Inocalla, Sr.** Upon the advice of our counsel, I traveled to Manila and personally received the reinstated title. A copy of the Order and of the title are attached as **Annexes "A" and "B."**

4. Under our judicial partition, the **top-floor apartment** of the Property pertains to me, and I likewise act for my siblings **Jesus V. Inocalla, Herbert V. Inocalla, and Marilou V. Inocalla** as their attorney-in-fact under their respective **Special Powers of Attorney**, copies attached as **Annexes "C," "D," "E," and "F."**

**THE ANTECEDENTS**

5. On **4 August 2026**, I personally delivered written **letters of turn-over of the Property** to Respondents Ace Vicente Inocalla III and Elena de Leon Inocalla Bombita, and furnished a copy to **Punong Barangay Rowena T. Chua of Barangay 759**. I also went to every tenant of the building, personally informed them of the change of ownership, and left each of them a letter to that effect. Copies of the letters are attached as **Annex "G"** (series).

6. On **14 August 2026, at about 4:00 in the afternoon**, together with my handyman **Ben Bumatay Bilinan**, I purchased cleaning and repair tools, equipment, and materials with an aggregate value of approximately **FIFTY THOUSAND PESOS (₱50,000.00)**, and we began cleaning and repairing the Property, starting with **Apartment No. 3**. When we finished for the day, **we locked the door of Apartment No. 3**, with our tools, equipment, and materials secured inside.

7. I thereafter left for Bicol and gave Mr. Bilinan authority to continue the cleaning and repair work in my absence.

**THE INCIDENTS COMPLAINED OF**

8. Shortly after I left, I received a message from **Punong Barangay Rowena T. Chua** informing me that Respondent **Ace Vicente Inocalla III had broken into the locked Apartment No. 3** that we had been cleaning, **threw away and disposed of all our cleaning and repair tools, equipment, and materials**, and **threatened that "there will be trouble" if we go back** to the Property.

9. On the morning of **15 August 2026**, upon my instruction, Mr. Bilinan **reported the incident in the blotter of Barangay 759** and then proceeded to the PNP station to report the same. When Mr. Bilinan thereafter returned to the Property to resume work, he was **stopped by Respondent Ace Vicente Inocalla III, who threatened that Mr. Bilinan would be harmed if he entered the Property**.

10. The PNP advised that I, as the owner's representative and the principal complainant, should personally make the report. Hence, on **2 September 2026**, upon my return to Manila from Bicol, I appeared and caused this incident to be entered in the blotter, and I execute this Affidavit-Complaint.

**THE OFFENSES**

11. The foregoing acts of Respondent Ace Vicente Inocalla III — breaking into the locked apartment against the will of its lawful possessor, taking and disposing of our tools, equipment, and materials worth approximately ₱50,000.00, and threatening me and my handyman with harm should we return — constitute, among others, **Qualified Trespass to Dwelling (Article 280, Revised Penal Code), Robbery (Articles 293 and 299, Revised Penal Code) or, in the alternative, Theft (Article 308) and/or Malicious Mischief (Article 327), and Grave Threats (Article 282) and/or Grave Coercion (Article 286)**, or such other offenses as the evidence may warrant upon investigation.

12. Respondent Elena de Leon Inocalla Bombita is named herein as a co-occupant of the Property who was duly served the letter of turn-over and who, despite the final judgment and turn-over, continues to withhold the Property together with Respondent Ace Vicente Inocalla III; her participation in the acts complained of shall be established in the course of the investigation.

13. I am executing this Affidavit-Complaint to attest to the truth of the foregoing, to have the incidents placed on official record, and to formally request that an investigation be conducted and that the appropriate criminal charges be filed against the Respondents.

**IN WITNESS WHEREOF,** I have hereunto set my hand this **2nd day of September 2026** in the City of Manila, Philippines.

## PART IV — COUNSEL-REVIEW FLAGS (do not file past these blind)

1. **Hearsay structure.** Allan was in Bicol during the break-in. His personal knowledge covers ¶¶2–7 and ¶10; ¶¶8–9 rest on reports from Punong Barangay Chua and Ben Bilinan. For prosecutor-level filing, obtain **corroborating sworn statements from (a) Ben Bumatay Bilinan** (eyewitness to the 15 Aug threat; first discoverer of the break-in aftermath) **and (b) Punong Barangay Rowena T. Chua** (or at least the certified barangay blotter). The affidavit is drafted so Allan attests to *receiving* those reports, not to witnessing the acts — keep it that way.
2. **Offense designation is the prosecutor's call.** "Break and enter + robbery" (Allan's words) is pleaded as qualified trespass + robbery/theft in the alternative; whether it is robbery (force upon things, Art. 299) vs. theft vs. malicious mischief depends on whether items were *taken* or *destroyed/thrown away* — the evidence on what happened to the tools decides it. The draft deliberately pleads alternatives.
3. **"Judicial partition" (¶4) — identify the exact document.** The corpus verifies the 1992 deed of partition (doc 671) for the Paracale estate; whether it, or another instrument, allocates the *Manila building's top floor* to Allan is **[O] unverified**. If no instrument yet allocates the top floor, soften ¶4 to co-ownership + SPA authority (which is fully documented) before signing.
4. **The ₱50,000 figure is Allan's estimate [O].** Attach receipts (Annex H) or state "estimated" — prosecutors discount unreceipted round numbers. Robbery/theft penalties are value-dependent (RA 10951), so the receipts matter.
5. **Elena's overt acts.** No act by Elena is reported in the incidents themselves; ¶12 names her without attributing the break-in to her. Do not let a desk officer "strengthen" the entry by adding her to the break-in — that is fabrication risk.
6. **Barangay conciliation does not bar this.** Offenses punishable by more than one year / ₱5,000 fine are outside the Lupon's authority, and the parties' residences differ (Allan: Paracale) — but let counsel confirm before the prosecutor's office raises it. Note the standing administrative complaint vs. Punong Barangay Chua (1 Dec 2025) — she is now also a *source witness for Allan* (¶8); handle that relationship deliberately.
7. **Security note.** The Bombita family members are suspects in Vicente Jr.'s assassination per the special-admin dossier (§6). Advise Allan and Bilinan against returning to the Property alone; coordinate any re-entry with PNP presence.
8. **Consistency with the ejectment track.** This criminal blotter runs parallel to the planned Rule 70 unlawful detainer (`MANILA_EJECTMENT_BRIEF_OF_FACTS.md`). The 14–15 Aug break-in and threats are also **evidence of dispossession by force/strategy** — share this affidavit with ejectment counsel; it may support forcible-entry framing or damages.
