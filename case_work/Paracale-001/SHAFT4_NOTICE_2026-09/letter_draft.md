**Re: Notice of Prior Rights and Invitation to Settle Terms — Shaft No. 4, its Private Access Road and Electrical Line**

Dear Mr. Fournacier:

1. We write in good faith, and in a spirit of cooperation, to place on record certain rights that bear directly on your present operations at Shaft No. 4, and to invite you to settle them with us amicably before any misunderstanding arises.

2. **Your present access.** We understand that you have commenced work at Shaft No. 4, situated on property titled to my brother, and that my brother has permitted you access to the shaft. We do not question that permission as between you and him. It does not, however, extend to the rights described below, which are not his to grant.

3. **Our prior investment.** Shaft No. 4 was operated and developed by us for many years. The private road that provides the only practical access to the shaft, and the electrical line that powers it, were constructed and paid for by us, and they remain in service to the shaft today. Your operation depends on both.

4. **Mineral rights.** The mineral rights over the area are held by Northern Islands Builders and Development Corporation (NIBDC). I hold NIBDC's Special Power of Attorney. You will appreciate that under Republic Act No. 7942 (the Philippine Mining Act of 1995) and its implementing rules, mining within an area subject to mineral rights requires the proper authority of the holder. We raise this not as a threat but so that your considerable investment rests on a secure footing, and I am prepared to assist you in regularizing your position with NIBDC.

5. **Basis of our position.** The use of a private road and utility line built at another's expense, without agreement and without compensation, is not free. The Civil Code of the Philippines requires that a right of way over another's property be granted only upon payment of proper indemnity (Articles 649 to 657), and provides that no one shall enrich himself at the expense of another without just or legal ground (Article 22).

6. **What we propose.** We have no wish to disrupt your work. We welcome your investment and hope your operation prospers. We ask only that any arrangement for the continued use of the shaft, the road and the electrical line include fair compensation for the contribution that makes your operation possible. We are flexible as to how that compensation is structured.

7. **Why clarity matters to us.** Our family has already paid a grievous price for unclear rights. Two members of my family were killed in circumstances that we believe are connected to ambiguous rights and informal arrangements over our family estate, and both killings remain unsolved. We are determined that nothing of the kind should ever happen again, to anyone. That is why we insist that every operation on and around the estate rest on clear, written terms, properly regularized with the authorities under the law. We are presently pursuing that regularization with the proper government offices, and we ask your cooperation in it.

8. **Pending agreement.** Until terms are agreed, we respectfully request that you suspend further operations at Shaft No. 4, so that further capital is not committed while these matters remain open. For the avoidance of doubt, no consent has been given by us or by NIBDC for the use of the access road, the electrical line, or the mineral rights in the area.

9. **Request for response.** We would be grateful to receive your written response, or to meet with you, within three (3) days of your receipt of this letter. I may be reached at [phone / email].

10. **Reservation of rights.** We would much prefer to resolve this matter directly and amicably. We are, however, pursuing all legal remedies available to us and to NIBDC to regularize operations in the area, and should we be unable to reach agreement with you, we will be constrained to pursue those remedies as against your operation, including proceedings before the appropriate Lupong Tagapamayapa, the Mines and Geosciences Bureau, and the courts. Nothing in this letter waives any right, claim or remedy of the undersigned or of NIBDC, all of which are expressly reserved.

We thank you for your attention and look forward to a productive discussion.

Very truly yours,
