## COUNSEL / SENDER NOTES (delete before sending)
- **Why this letter exists:** it (1) puts the Bombitas on formal notice of the obligations they claimed, (2) creates dated proof of refusal or non-compliance for each ground under RA 3844 §36, and (3) is served with the estate as a **non-party** to the lost case. It is **not** a demand to vacate the adjudicated area. That is barred while the prior rulings stand.
- **⚠ Consent trap:** it contains no demand to "pay rent to us" and no acceptance of produce, because either could later be used as estate **consent** to a tenancy. Keep §5 as option (i) unless counsel chooses otherwise.
- **Area:** paragraph 4(d) must use the area and boundaries **actually adjudicated**. Fill it only from the PARAD/DARAB/CA decisions (build plan A2–A3). Leave 4(d) out if unknown.
- **Represented parties:** the Bombitas have counsel of record (Atty. Parale), so serve through or copy him.
- **Proof of receipt starts every clock.** Keep the registry return card or acknowledgment and log it in the drip ledger.
- **Security:** service by counsel, process server or registered mail only. **No family member delivers this.**
- **Check first:** if G.R. 274321 is still pending, counsel decides whether the letter or any later filing risks litis pendentia / forum shopping.
