## 1. ⚠ BARANGAY — Lot 4 is in SAN RAFAEL, not Santa Rosa Sur. This stops a filing.
**Finding.** Every document in the corpus that locates Lot 4 / OCT P-1616 places it in **Barangay San Rafael, Jose Panganiban**: the title itself (docs 633, 639 — "barrio of San Rafael"), the owner's duplicate (633/634), the certified Assessor's tax declaration ARP GR-2023-II-05-021-00306 (doc 4445, verified 13 Sep 2026), and the family land inventory (docs 520, 647, 1161). **No document places Lot 4 in Santa Rosa Sur.**

**Why the confusion arose.** "Sta. Rosa Sur" appears in 34 Paracale-001 documents, but in the six that mention both it and P-1616 it belongs to *other* lots in the same family inventory table — not to Lot 4. It is also a barangay on the EXPA-000250-V barangay list and in the Gracesen-era papers, so it is well represented in the corpus for reasons unconnected to this lot.

**Consequence.** The drafted `BRGY_ENDORSEMENT_CUSTOM_MILL_2026-09-14/` request is addressed to **Brgy. Santa Rosa Sur**. On this record it should go to **Brgy. San Rafael**. The barangay flows downstream into the SB endorsement, the ECC, the MPP, the CEMCRR, the Mayor's permit, the MB map amendment and both memos — all of which currently say Santa Rosa Sur.

**Before changing anything:** ask the Municipal Assessor of Jose Panganiban to state the barangay of record for ARP GR-2023-II-05-021-00306, and ask whether the barangay boundary has been altered since 1963 (a barangay split would explain both). One question, and it is the same office that must answer the unexplained "Lot 4 and 906" entry on the same declaration. **Hold the barangay and SB endorsement requests until it is answered.**
