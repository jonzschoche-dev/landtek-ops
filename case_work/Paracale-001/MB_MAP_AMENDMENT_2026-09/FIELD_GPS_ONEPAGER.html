Inocalla Titles: Find the Boundaries on Your Phone

Capacuan, Paracale, Camarines Norte · Works with no cell signal, because GPS needs only open sky · App: Organic Maps (free, iPhone & Android)

PART 1 · AT HOME, ON WI-FI (do this first)

  1. Install Organic Maps from the App Store or Google Play.
  2. Open it, search “Paracale”, and tap Download for the offline map. Wait until it finishes.
  3. Open the file INOCALLA_TITLES.gpx (sent by Messenger, email or Drive).
    iPhone: tap the file → Share → Organic Maps.
    Android: tap the file → Open with → Organic Maps.
  4. Tap the ★ star icon and check that “Inocalla titles” is listed and turned on. You should see two boundary lines with a label pin in the middle of each lot.
  5. Test: turn on Airplane Mode, keep Location ON, and open the app. The two lots must still show.

PART 2 · ONSITE

  1. Location ON (Airplane Mode is fine and saves battery). Tap the ➤ arrow to show your blue dot.
  2. Stand in the open 1–2 minutes so the GPS can lock on.
  3. Tap a boundary line or label pin to see which title it is (see map at right). Inside the lines = inside that title.
  4. Mark spots: long-press where you stand → Save → give it a name.
  5. Optional: record a track while walking a boundary.

HOW ACCURATE?
Phone GPS: about 5–10 m.
Title lines: approximate, ±20–30 m.
Within ~30 m of a line, you can't tell from the phone which side you're on.
Do NOT use the phone to set fences or corners. That needs a geodetic engineer.
BATTERY: bring a power bank · dim the screen · Airplane Mode ON, Location ON.
