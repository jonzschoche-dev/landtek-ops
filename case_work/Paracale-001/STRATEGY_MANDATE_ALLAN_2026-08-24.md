# STRATEGY MANDATE — ALLAN V. INOCALLA
### Manila Building (TCT 44055) & Estate of Vicente Inocalla Sr.

*Prepared by LandTek Assisted, 2026-08-24. Operator-grade directive for Allan and his legal counsel. Not legal advice; legal steps to be confirmed by engaged counsel. Fact basis: 1992 judicial partition (doc 671), CC 13-131220 / CA SP 161072 / SC G.R. 256997 judgments, alias writ 25 Nov 2025.*

**Objective:** Full control of the building, rents flowing to the family, Ace ejected and made to pay. Three tracks, run in parallel, never mixed.

**Why this matters:** The family cannot outsource its security to public systems that are themselves in decline — the environment, the drinking water, the hospitals, the schools, the grip of poverty all tell the same story across the country. Whatever the family will have, the family must build and hold itself. A recovered, well-run estate is that foundation: housing, steady rental income, funds for health and education, and land restored instead of stripped. That is what this fight is for — not just beating Ace, but ending the years in which the family's own inheritance was another degraded asset. *(This context is for the family and counsel's understanding of the mandate; it does not go into court filings, which stay strictly on the legal elements.)*

## TRACK 1 — POSSESSION (file within weeks, not months)

Direct counsel to:

1. **Obtain RD Manila certified copy of reinstated TCT 44055** + entry of judgment on Ace's MR. This is the anchor exhibit for everything that follows.
2. **File unlawful detainer, MeTC Manila**, vs. Ace (Vicente de Leon Inocalla III), Elena de Leon Inocalla, and all occupants, the moment the 15-day demand period lapses. Deadline discipline: must be filed **within 1 year of the last written demand**.
3. **Name Jesus as lead plaintiff** (non-Manila resident → exempt from barangay conciliation under RA 7160 §408; bypasses the obstructed barangay). Allan joins as attorney-in-fact. Do NOT make Allan lead plaintiff — his address of record is the building itself.
4. **Pre-empt Ace's one defense:** the 1992 court-approved partition gave his late father **one ground-floor door only** (ground floor, left, facing east). Plead that the award passed to Vicente Jr.'s five heirs collectively, Ace's filiation is contested, and it confers no right to the other nine units or to collect any rent.
5. **Claim in the ejectment:** possession + **fair rental value** + attorney's fees. Nothing more — the bigger money goes in Track 2.

## TRACK 2 — MONEY (build now, file after administrator appointed)

6. **Evidence capture starts today, no exceptions:**
   - Same-day police blotter for every incident.
   - Timestamped photos/video.
   - Damaged-equipment inventory: item, owner, purchase receipt, written repair/replacement quote. Courts award actual damages on receipts and competent proof, not estimates.
   - Sworn worker affidavits while memories are fresh.
   - Tenant list per door: what each paid Ace, and when.
7. **File criminal complaints** with the prosecutor: malicious mischief (equipment destruction), grave threats / grave coercion (intimidation). These anchor the civil damages.
8. **RTC damages suit later:** rents Ace actually collected (Art. 549, bad-faith possessor), equipment losses, moral + exemplary damages. **Never claim the same rents in both cases** — claim-splitting kills both. Recovery backstop: set off everything Ace owes against his branch's share in settlement.

## TRACK 3 — ESTATE (the blocker — engage counsel this month)

9. **Engage estate counsel on a narrow scope:** intestate settlement petition + Allan as **Special Administrator (Rule 80)**. Petition discloses the 1998 case (98-88750) up front, paired with Allan's record of recovering the building for the family, heir consents, and a surety bond.
10. **No new EJS is needed for the building** — the 1992 judicial partition (CC B-5625, RTC Br. 41 Daet; finality certified 2014) already allocated the land equally and the 10 doors by name. Counsel's jobs: estate-tax amnesty check (RA 11213 as amended), register the partition, sweep in the un-partitioned ~74.6 ha still titled to Vicente Sr.
11. **Income now:** willing tenants sign new written leases and pay into **one dedicated account with clean books**. Tenants who keep paying Ace get no credit for it. Those books are also Allan's fitness evidence for the appointment.

## RULES

- **No self-help retaking of units.** No confrontation with Ace's group — everything through blotter, counsel, courts.
- **Allan signs nothing** selling or encumbering estate property before appointment.
- Keep Inocalla matters strictly separate from all other client matters.
