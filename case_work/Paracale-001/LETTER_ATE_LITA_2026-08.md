## Polished letter (ready to send)

Dear Ate Lita,

Thank you for our conversation yesterday. It made me happy to hear you say that you and your
children are committed to preserving Kuya Casper's legacy, and that you are working to care for
and improve the properties that came from our parents.

That is the very same vision we carry at Inocallafarm.com — to protect our family's land, make it
productive, and pass it on stronger to the next generation.

It is also why we formed the Capacuan Small-Scale Mining Association: to upgrade, protect, and
legalize the mining on our Inocalla land, so that it is done safely, lawfully, and for the benefit
of the whole family.

Our doors are open to you and your children. If you wish to join us in this mission, you will be
most welcome. Kuya Casper's legacy is part of our parents' legacy — and it will be strongest if we
preserve it together.

With respect and love,

Allan

## Drafting notes (for Jonathan/Allan — NOT part of the letter)

1. **One deliberate wording change.** Allan's draft said *"the properties he inherited from our
   parents."* The polished version says *"the properties that came from our parents."* Reason: the
   four DBP lots (T-20754/55/56/57, ~64 ha) associated with Casper are earmarked to
   Marilou/Herbert/Senen's estate under the 1985 Undertaking. A signed letter from Allan
   affirming that Casper *inherited* the properties could later be quoted against the estate's
   position that the Casper branch holds them subject to a conveyance obligation. The neutral
   phrasing keeps the warmth without conceding ownership. If Allan prefers his original wording,
   that is his call — but he should know the trade-off.

2. **What this letter deliberately does NOT raise** (first contact is goodwill only):
   - the 1985 Undertaking / DBP conveyance obligation;
   - the Special-Administrator petition or any consent/SPA ask;
   - anything about the Minahang Bayan application or mining permits.
   Those come later, after the relationship is warm. Same discipline as the Cambronero branch
   (keep contentious issues out of consent-building contact).

3. **Follow-up worth capturing if the door opens:** full names, ages, and addresses of Lita's
   four children — the Rule 79 roster still has all four as blanks, and this branch's heirs must
   be named and served in the petition regardless of consent.
