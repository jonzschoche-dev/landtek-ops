### SUPPLEMENTAL AFFIDAVIT

**I, ALLAN V. INOCALLA,** Filipino, of legal age, with residence at Purok 4, Capacuan, Paracale, Camarines Norte, and at 2531 G. Del Pilar Street, Barangay 759, Santa Ana, City of Manila, after having been duly sworn to in accordance with law, hereby depose and state:

1. I executed an **Affidavit-Complaint dated 2 September 2026** against **ACE VICENTE INOCALLA III** (also known as Vicente de Leon Inocalla III, alias "Ace") and Elena de Leon Inocalla Bombita concerning the break-in, taking of tools worth approximately ₱50,000.00, and threats at our family property at **2531 G. Del Pilar Street, Barangay 759, Santa Ana, City of Manila** (the "Property"). I execute this Supplemental Affidavit to place on record a **new incident that occurred only three (3) days later**.

2. On **5 September 2026, at about 5:30 in the afternoon**, I visited the Property together with my guests, **Mr. Mario Bangasan**, President of the **Philippine Inventors, Scientists and Innovators, Inc.**, and **Architect Mike Guevara**, whom I had invited to assess and evaluate the family Property.

3. After a few minutes of viewing and taking photographs of the building, Respondent **Ace Vicente Inocalla III came out of his apartment and began yelling at me**, threatening and cursing me in front of my guests, shouting words to the effect of: **"Umalis ka dito at barilin kita! Putang ina mo, hayop ka! Manloloko, magnanakaw! Alis kayo dito!"** (in English: *"Leave this place or I will shoot you! You son of a whore, you animal! Swindler, thief! All of you, get out of here!"*).

4. I tried to calm him down, but he continued to threaten and verbally abuse me. While doing so, **he was holding his phone and calling someone**.

5. To avoid any violence, I told my guests that it was better for us to leave, and we walked away toward **Pablo Ocampo Street (formerly Vito Cruz)**.

6. I then noticed Respondent **coming after me**, so I stopped and faced him. He continued yelling threats at me, looking at me and shouting words to the effect of: **"Anong gusto mo? Patitirahin kita!"** (in English: *"What do you want? I will have you hit!"* / *"I will strike you!"*).

7. I was stunned, intimidated, and genuinely feared for my life. **I personally know that Respondent keeps a firearm and has openly shown off shooting it**, so his threat to shoot me was real to me. Moreover, my brother **Vicente Inocalla, Jr.** and my sister **Senen "Betty" Inocalla** were both **murdered, and both killings remain unsolved to this day**. Threats of violence from within this conflict are not empty words to me.

8. My companion Mr. Mario Bangasan, worried for my safety, **pulled me away**, and we left as Respondent grew visibly more aggressive. As we walked away, **he kept on yelling threats and curses at me and my guests**, still holding his phone.

9. Both **Mr. Mario Bangasan** and **Architect Mike Guevara** personally witnessed the entire incident and can attest to the foregoing.

10. The foregoing acts of Respondent — publicly threatening to **shoot** me and to strike me, and cursing me and publicly branding me a swindler and a thief in front of my professional guests — constitute, among others, **Grave Threats (Article 282, Revised Penal Code)** — the threat being to commit a crime against my person with a firearm — **and Grave Oral Defamation (Article 358)** for the public imputation of crimes, **and/or Unjust Vexation (Article 287)**, or such other offenses as the evidence may warrant, and form part of a **continuing pattern of intimidation** following the acts already complained of in my Affidavit-Complaint of 2 September 2026, intended to prevent me and my family from exercising our rights over the Property despite the final judgment in Civil Case No. 13-131220 and the reinstatement of TCT No. 44055 in the name of my late mother.

11. In view of Respondent's threat to shoot me and my knowledge that he keeps and shows off a firearm, I likewise respectfully request that the authorities **verify whether Respondent lawfully possesses any firearm** and take the appropriate action.

12. I am executing this Supplemental Affidavit to attest to the truth of the foregoing based on my personal knowledge, to have this incident entered in the blotter and added to the record of my pending complaint, and to request that it be considered in the investigation and in the filing of the appropriate criminal charges against Respondent Ace Vicente Inocalla III.

## PART IV — COUNSEL-REVIEW FLAGS

1. **The quoted utterances (¶¶3, 6) must be confirmed verbatim with Allan before swearing — and the correction trail matters.** Allan's first same-day relay (2026-09-05) rendered the threat as "susuntukin kita" (punch); **Allan corrected it the same day to "barilin kita" (shoot)** and added the epithets "manloloko, magnanakaw" — the garbled "Man lolo ko" fragment in the first relay is thereby resolved as "manloloko." The affidavit and blotter narrative now carry the corrected version. **Everything sworn from here on must consistently say "barilin"** — the blotter entry, this affidavit, and the witness statements — because a defense counsel who obtains an early "susuntukin" version will exploit the discrepancy; if asked, the honest answer is that the first relay was a hurried secondhand paraphrase corrected by Allan within hours. "Patira kita" is normalized to "Patitirahin kita" — confirm; note that in context "tira" can also carry a shooting connotation — ask Allan which he understood. All quotes remain hedged as "words to the effect of."
1a. **The firearm (¶¶7, 11) — pin down Allan's basis of knowledge before swearing:** has he personally seen Ace with the gun and seen him show off shooting it (when, where), or does he know it secondhand? The paragraph as drafted says "I personally know" — it must be literally true or softened to the actual basis. A threat to shoot from a man known to carry elevates Art. 282 gravity and justifies asking PNP to run a firearms-license (LTOPF/FEO) check on Ace — the request is now in ¶11. If Ace has no license, that is a separate criminal exposure (RA 10591) the investigators can pursue.
2. **Witness statements are the force multiplier.** Get short sworn statements from **Mario Bangasan** and **Arch. Mike Guevara** within days, while memory is fresh (skeleton in Part III). Confirm the exact legal name of Bangasan's organization ("Philippine Inventors, Scientists and Innovators, Inc." is reconstructed from Allan's message [O]) and both witnesses' full names and addresses.
3. **Preserve the evidence made today:** the photographs of the building taken during the visit (timestamps/EXIF intact — they prove presence, time, and purpose), any video or audio anyone captured of the confrontation, and Allan's own phone records. Check for CCTV along G. Del Pilar / Pablo Ocampo and at neighboring buildings — request preservation quickly; most systems overwrite in days.
4. **Ace on the phone (¶¶4, 8):** Allan doesn't know who was called — the affidavit deliberately states only the observable fact (holding phone, calling). Do not speculate about summoning reinforcements in the sworn text; investigators can pursue it.
5. **The murdered-siblings paragraph (¶7)** grounds the reasonableness of Allan's fear — an element that matters for grave threats. Corpus verifies both deaths: Vicente Jr. assassinated 2017, Senen shot Feb 2021, both unsolved [V — special-admin dossier §6]. Keep it factual as drafted; do not allege in this affidavit who killed them.
6. **Blotter this incident promptly** (ideally today/tomorrow at the same PNP station, cross-referencing the 2 Sept entry) — a same-day or next-day blotter has strong probative weight; a delayed one invites doubt. If the 2 Sept Affidavit-Complaint has not yet been sworn/filed, counsel may fold this into it as additional paragraphs instead of a separate supplemental — either works; do not file contradictory versions.
7. **Security posture — escalate further.** A threat to SHOOT from a man Allan knows to keep and show off a firearm, three weeks after a break-in and harm threats, against the backdrop of two unsolved family murders: Allan should not go near the Property again without PNP presence — not tanods alone — and should vary his routines while in Manila. Raise the firearm explicitly when blottering so it is on record before any future incident, and ask the prosecutor/PNP about protective measures once docketed.
8. **Strategic use:** this incident is also evidence for the ejectment/damages track (`MANILA_EJECTMENT_BRIEF_OF_FACTS.md`) — respondent's violent exclusion of the owners' representatives — and for opposing any claim by Ace of peaceful possession. It also documents interference with the property-development effort itself (the assessment visit by an architect and a professional-organization president was aborted), which is relevant to actual damages.
