## 1. Coverage screening (EMB MC 2014-005, Annex A item 2.1.5)

| Row | Threshold (annual **input** to the plant) | Category → document | Office |
|---|---|---|---|
| a) Metallic mineral or ore processing | ≥ 70,000 MT/yr | A (ECP) → EIS | EMB Central |
| | ≥ 10,000 but < 70,000 MT/yr | B → EIS | EMB RO |
| | **> 200 but < 10,000 MT/yr** | **B → IEE Checklist** | **EMB RO-V** |
| | ≤ 200 MT/yr | not covered → CNC | — |
| b) Precious-metal refining | uses ≥ 1.0 MT/yr of PCL/CCO chemicals → B; no such chemicals → not covered | | |

**AVI sizing.** LAB X target 10 tpd × 300 operating days = **3,000 MT/yr** → Category B, IEE Checklist. **Design rule for the file: declare and stay below 10,000 MT/yr input (≈ 33 tpd at 300 days).** Crossing it moves the project to a full EIS with a public-scoping process. The Gracesen ECC carried a "≤ 50 tons per batch per day" restriction (doc 1284, Restriction 21) for the same reason.

**Circuit rule.** Gravity-only (crush/mill → classify → [centrifugal rougher] → LAB X tables → doré). No cyanide, no mercury (mercury is banned outright in small-scale processing, DAO 2022-03 §14). This removes: EMB chemical certification under RA 6969 (MPP item A11), CCO registration, Hazardous Waste Generator ID, and the "precious-metal refining with PCL/CCO chemicals" row above.

## 4. Draft Project Description (Step 3 text; ≤ 4,000 characters — this draft is ~2,300)

> **AVI Gold Processing Plant** is a mercury-free, cyanide-free gold gravity-concentration plant proposed by Allan V. Inocalla (sole proprietor, DTI-registered business name AVI Gold Processing) on his own titled land, Lot 4, Psu-143364, Original Certificate of Title No. P-1616 (15.2069 ha), Purok ___, Barangay Santa Rosa Sur, Jose Panganiban, Camarines Norte. The plant occupies approximately ______ m² of the lot; the remainder stays in its present use.
>
> The plant will receive run-of-mine ore and old tailings from permitted small-scale mining contractors and mining-rights holders in the Paracale–Jose Panganiban gold district under written supply agreements, at a design throughput of 10 tonnes per day (about 3,000 tonnes per year, below 10,000 tonnes per year). Ore is crushed and milled, classified, passed through a centrifugal gravity rougher, and cleaned on shaking (concentrating) tables of the Wilfley/6-S type fabricated locally; the concentrate is smelted to doré. **No mercury and no cyanide or other chemicals listed in the Priority Chemical List or under a Chemical Control Order are used at any stage.** All gold produced will be sold to the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas or its accredited traders.
>
> Components: covered crushing–milling shed; classification and gravity circuit (rougher + ___ tables) on a rigid concrete pad; concentrate/smelting room; process-water tank and recirculation sump; lined tailings storage facility with settling pond and decant; silt traps and perimeter drainage; materials recovery facility; septic tank; rainwater cistern; oil-water separator at the generator/fuel bay; office/store; workers' quarters. Power from CANORECO with a standby generator; water from ___ (rain catchment / own well / surface source, with recirculation of process water).
>
> Process water is recirculated from the tailings decant; tailings (gravity, chemical-free) are contained in the lined TSF and are not discharged to any watercourse. Dust is controlled by wet processing and enclosure; noise by enclosure and the rigid foundation. Domestic waste goes to the MRF and the municipal collection; used oil is stored in the bunded bay and turned over to an accredited hauler.
>
> Construction: about ___ months, ___ workers. Operation: ___ workers on two shifts, local residents preferred. Project cost: ₱________ (estimate). The project is proposed as a formal, Minahang Bayan-aligned processing facility supporting the district's small-scale miners in place of mercury-based backyard processing.
