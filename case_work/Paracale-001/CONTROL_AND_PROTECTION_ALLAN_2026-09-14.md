# CONTROL AND PROTECTION — how the partnership stays in control and Allan is protected at every step
*LandTek internal note, 2026-09-14. INTERNAL-HELD. Operator rule (Jonathan): "We don't know that Stephen can execute this. We need to stay in control and protect Allan through every step." This note turns that into the design of every paper in the cluster. Not legal advice; the instruments below are for Philippine and Canadian counsel to draft.*

**The principle in one line:** control lives in the vehicles Jonathan and Allan own (PGC and Allan's Philippine company), never in a person; Stephen — or anyone — earns each next step by executing the last one; nothing Allan holds is ever assigned, pledged, optioned or exclusively committed to a party that has not yet performed.

## 2. Stephen: valued, not load-bearing, and proven in stages
What we actually hold from Stephen today: a board resolution *authorising* an agreement we have not seen; a use-of-funds picture he has not sent; a claimed profit share, a claimed shares offer, a claimed power of attorney over 40% of NIBDC, and a claimed 30-hectare partnership site — all unwritten; and a concept paper that would put a foreign contractor at the centre of the ground Allan and NIBDC hold. None of that is bad faith; all of it is unproven. So:

**Gate 0 — before any money moves:** the executed SMBC Operational Partnership Agreement; documentary proof of plant title and SMBC's authority to host equipment; the MFPS turnover deed and its conditions; the power of attorney over NIBDC (scope, term, principal); the name of the "prospective FTAA applicant." No gate-0 document, no drawdown.
**Gate 1 — Wave 1 execution:** plant activation milestones met on the staged schedule; LAB X table commissioned; toll station operating; first BSP sale through the trader. Only then does Stephen's role widen to the AVI plant design contract.
**Gate 2 — AVI design and build:** a written engineering-services agreement with deliverables, milestones, IP assignment and a termination-for-convenience clause; paid per milestone; our project manager on site; second-source fabricators identified so no single engineer is a single point of failure.
**Gate 3 — Wave 3:** the NIBDC approach is PGC's; Stephen's power of attorney is used *for* PGC's proposal on written instructions and disclosed as a related-party matter, or not used at all; any FTAA is PGC's or nobody's.

**Stephen's economics track his execution:** his shares offer, his profit share, his services fees and any equity in PGC are earned by delivery against these gates, documented at each gate, and never granted in advance. The "partners, not lenders" posture governs tone, not diligence.

## 3. Allan's protection, step by step
1. **Incorporation.** Allan's company is formed with Allan holding the majority and the presidency, with our counsel's articles and a shareholders' agreement that gives PGC protective rights only. No nominee arrangements, no undisclosed side letters, no foreign officer — the Anti-Dummy line is Allan's protection as much as the law's.
2. **His land.** OCT P-1616 stays his or goes only into his own company by property-for-shares on an SEC-accepted valuation (tax-free exchange, counsel to confirm). It is never leased or optioned to an outside applicant. The estate's land waits for the administrator; no one signs for the heirs.
3. **His cooperative standing.** Papered by resolution before any petition or MOA; the pre-1987 evidence pack and the MB petition are filed in the cooperative's name by Allan, not by any adviser.
4. **His NIBDC position.** Negotiated by Allan (with Jonathan), documented in Maribel's sign-off, held personally or through his company — never through a power of attorney held by someone else. Any earn-in is PGC's, on related-party terms that recuse Allan where he acts for NIBDC and pay him fairly where he doesn't.
5. **His PGC position.** Verified against the register now; his shareholding and presidency documented; his position protected in any round with Juliet's group by pre-emption and anti-dilution.
6. **His money.** The ₱5M and everything after it flows through AVI's books, VAT-compliant, to verified corporate accounts, with Allan and Jonathan both signing releases.
7. **His name and record.** Nothing filed with EMB, MGB, the PMRB, the BSP, TESDA or an LGU in Allan's name without his signature and a copy in the corpus; the Gracesen close-out done cleanly so the new applications start unencumbered.
8. **His exit.** If any partner fails a gate, Allan's company keeps the plants, the equipment, the agreements, the cooperative and the land, and PGC keeps its stake — the structure is built so that losing one person costs a schedule, not the business.
