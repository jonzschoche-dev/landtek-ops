## 2. Philippine constraints that shape the structure (≤15 tagged facts)

1. [V] **Natural-resource EDU is 40% foreign max** (Const. Art. XII §2; 13th Foreign Investment Negative List, EO 113 s.2026, effective 2 May 2026). Exception: FTAA with the President (100% foreign, large-scale).
2. [V] **Small-scale mining is 0% foreign** (13th FINL List A; RA 7076 §3(c) "Filipino citizens… cooperative"). Contracts are awarded only to registered individual miners or cooperatives (§9), run 2 years renewable (§13), and **cannot be subcontracted, assigned or transferred** (§12).
3. [V] **A foreign-owned corporation is a "qualified person" for an Exploration Permit, an FTAA and a Mineral Processing Permit** (RA 7942 §3(aq)); MPP term 5 years renewable to 25 (§56). This is the door for the listco's plant and exploration layers.
4. [V] **Masbate is the proven template**: B2Gold holds 40% of Filminera (tenement holder, 60% Zoom Mineral Holdings) and **100% of Philippine Gold Processing & Refining Corp**, which buys all ore under an ore sales and purchase agreement and runs the plant. Same shape as this vehicle's end state.
5. [V] **Anti-Dummy Law (CA 108)** criminalizes nominee/financed Filipino fronts; Narra Nickel (SC 2014/2015) applied the grandfather rule to a Canadian-listed layering that faked 60%; SEC MC 8 s.2013 tests 60% against both voting shares and total shares. **Allan's 60% must be his own capital or contributed assets, documented, dividends to him — the listco may not fund it.**
6. [V] **All small-scale gold must be sold to the BSP** (RA 7076 §17); sales to BSP by registered small-scale miners and **BSP-accredited traders** are exempt from income and excise tax (RA 11256, RR 4-2020). An accredited-trader entity is the clean revenue point for doré.
7. [V] **Custom mills / processing plants inside a Minahang Bayan are licensed by the P/CMRB** (RA 7076 §18); DAO 2022-03 (eff. 29 Apr 2022) now speaks of "Mineral Processing Zones" and a "Mineral Processing License". [PV] Whether a PMRB-licensed mill inside an MB falls within the FINL "small-scale mining" reservation (0% foreign) or is ordinary processing (foreign-eligible) — **counsel question #1**. Conservative design: a mill inside the MB processing zone is Allan-owned/60-40; a stand-alone MPP plant outside the zone is the 100% foreign-eligible asset. **AVI Gold Processing (JP) must be sited with this choice made first.** (Gracesen, Allan's earlier Capacuan plant — ECC 2012, amended to Allan 2021 [V docs 1284, 1304] — is OBSOLETE per Jonathan 2026-09-12; its 2017 EMB Notice of Adverse Findings [V doc 1289] is closed out with EMB before the AVI ECC is filed, nothing else carries forward.)
8. [PV] **The repo's NIBDC notes cite DAO 2015-03 §§8, 9, 14.** DAO 2022-03 revised the RA 7076 IRR; whether it repeals 2015-03 wholesale, and whether the reported "6-year maximum Minahang Bayan term including renewal" is in it, changes the investment horizon of Phases 1–2. Re-verify every 2015-03 citation against the 2022-03 text before it reaches counsel or a prospectus.
9. [V] **RA 12253 (Enhanced Fiscal Regime for Large-Scale Metallic Mining)**, signed 5 Sep 2025, operative 17 Feb 2026: margin-based royalty 1–5% outside mineral reservations (5% fixed inside), windfall profits tax 1–10% above a 30% margin (10% at ≥75%), project ring-fencing. Large-scale only; Phases 1–2 are outside it. [PV] The raw-ore export ban debated in the Senate version does not appear in the sources reviewed — confirm against the enrolled text.
10. [V] **Foreign investors may lease private land up to 99 years** for processing plants (RA 7652 as amended by RA 12252, 3 Sep 2025). The listco's PH subsidiaries never need to own land.
11. [V] **A >40%-foreign domestic-market enterprise needs ≥US$200,000 paid-in capital** (FIA as amended by RA 11647; US$100k if ≥15 Filipino employees/advanced tech). Budget this per foreign-owned PH subsidiary.
12. [V] **Canada–Philippines tax convention (1976):** dividends 15% where the Canadian company controls ≥10% voting power (else 25%); interest 15%; royalties 25% or the lowest rate the Philippines grants a third state. Dividends from a PH active-business affiliate are exempt-surplus in Canada (treaty country) — effectively tax-free at the listco.
13. [V] **RA 7942 §19(e):** areas covered by small-scale miners are closed to mining applications absent the small-scale miners' consent + community-trust-fund royalty; conversely once NIBDC holds an EP, an MB carve-out needs NIBDC's consent, ≤25% of area above 500 ha, ≥1.5% royalty to NIBDC (RA 7076 §14; DAO 2015-03 §8(c) [PV vs 2022-03]). Allan sitting on both sides is the control premise [V NIBDC_BOARD_PROPOSAL, MB_CONSENT note].
14. [V] **NIBDC is 100% Filipino, 5 shareholders**: Villareal bloc 58.9%, Marcelo bloc 41.1% (blocking minority for 2/3 acts); ₱28.37M paid-up; cash ₱451,850 (2024 AFS). Any ≥50% listco earn-in or EP assignment to a project SPV needs at least one Marcelo and MGB approval of the transfer.
15. [V] **The Inocalla estate is unsettled (no administrator)** — nobody can sign a surface-use agreement over the ~250 ha today; Phase 2 and any Phase-3 work on that ground wait on the Rule 80 petition [V NOTE_LARGE_SCALE_SCENARIO; project-inocalla-special-admin].

## 3. Canadian listing constraints (what the exchange actually tests)

- [V] **TSXV Tier 2 Mining (Policy 2.1, as at 31 Mar 2026):** Issuer has a **Significant Interest (= at least 50%) in a Qualifying Property, or the right to earn one**; ≥C$100,000 Approved Expenditures on the property in the prior 36 months (waivable if the Phase 1 or Phase 1+2 non-contingent program exceeds C$400,000, or the property is a Tier 1 Property); a Geological Report under **NI 43-101** recommending an initial-phase program of **≥C$200,000**; working capital for 12 months + C$100,000 unallocated; public float 500,000 shares and 200 public board-lot holders. No NTA requirement for Mining. Tier 1 Mining: C$2M NTA, material interest in a Tier 1 Property, ≥C$500,000 initial phase, 18 months WC + C$200,000.
- [V] The Exchange expects the issuer to hold ≥50% **and have control and direction over operations** on the property. **A 40% MPSA stake fails this on its face.** Three compliant routes: (a) list at **EP stage** with ≥50% of the EP holder / project SPV (foreign-eligible), restructuring to 40% + processing + ore-purchase at MPSA conversion, or to an FTAA if the project reaches US$50M scale; (b) an **earn-in/option agreement** with NIBDC to earn ≥50% of a project SPV by funding the exploration program (the "right to earn" language); (c) **CSE** listing, which has no Significant-Interest test — the fallback if TSXV balks.
- [V] **CPC route (Policy 2.4, 2021 rewrite):** a Capital Pool Company may now raise up to C$10M and the 24-month Qualifying-Transaction deadline is gone; the QT is evaluated against Policy 2.1 above. Direct IPO by prospectus (NI 41-101) or an RTO into an existing shell are the alternatives. TSXV has eliminated the sponsor requirement (2026 guidance) — a cost saver.
- [V] **Policy 3.1:** ≥3 directors, ≥2 independent; every director, officer, promoter and insider files a Personal Information Form and passes a security screening check. **Disclosure landmine:** Civil Case 98-88750 (RTC Manila Br. 16, decision 3 Aug 2006, final and executory 2013) found Allan used a forged 1978 deed and ruled fraud [V docs 506/509]. This must be disclosed on his PIF; Canadian counsel to assess whether Allan can serve as a director/officer/promoter or should hold only shares and the Philippine operator role. Design the listco board around this now, not at filing.
- [V] Standard prospectus package: audited financial statements (IFRS; 2–3 years or since inception — **start audit-grade books on day one of the PH subsidiaries**), NI 43-101 technical report by an independent QP (the PGC report by Manny Acena, doc 4929, is a *draft* and must not carry resource/ore/recoverable-oz language — Benguet historical estimates handled under NI 43-101 s.2.4), a **Philippine title opinion** on the EP, escrow of principals' shares (Policy 5.4, 36 months for Tier 2).
- [PV] US-side: Jonathan and any US investors in a Canadian junior face PFIC treatment and Reg S/Rule 144A placement limits — US tax counsel item, not a structural blocker.
