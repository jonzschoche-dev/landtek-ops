## ⚠ Read this first — the legal frame (and a live risk)

1. **A Torrens title never proves mineral rights.** Under the Regalian doctrine (1987 Const. Art. XII;
   RA 7942) minerals belong to the State regardless of surface ownership. "This is a mineral
   property" is proven by the **mineral-tenure layer** (claims, APSA/EXPA, MGB records, geology),
   never by the OCT itself.
2. **Double edge — do NOT argue "this land is classified mineral land."** OCT P-1616 is a **1963
   FREE PATENT — an agricultural public-land grant**. Free patents over land classified as *mineral*
   at the time of grant are void and revertible (Republic v. CA line). The safe and accurate frame is:
   **"titled agricultural surface land that HOSTS gold mineralization and is OVERLAPPED by mineral
   tenements"** — surface rights + mineral endowment, not mineral land classification. This also
   protects the parcel's use as clean loan collateral (the 2026-07-02 loan packet offers P-1616 as
   the anchor collateral: "clean, ~15.2 ha, borrower's own name, no subsisting lien").
3. **Dual-role wall (§6-B of SPECIAL_ADMIN_SUPERVISION.md):** the APSA/EXPA case-file material and
   the MGB document request run under Jonathan's **NIBDC mandate + NDA** (doc 803). Fine to use for
   the allied PGC⇄NIBDC integration story; must NOT be repurposed adversarially against NIBDC.
