## 2. Route decision — MPP (DENR/MGB-V) vs MPL (PMRB)

| | **Route A — Mineral Processing Permit** (recommended) | Route B — Mineral Processor's License |
|---|---|---|
| Law | RA 7942 §55; DAO 2010-21 §§109–115 | RA 7076; DAO 2022-03 §§14–18; DAO 2026-08 |
| Who issues | MGB Regional Director V (project ≤ ₱200M) | PMRB Camarines Norte (RD Molina chairs) through MGB RO-V |
| Where the plant may sit | anywhere lawful — Allan's own OCT P-1616 | only inside an LGU-designated **Mineral Processing Zone** |
| Whose ore | any lawful mining-rights holder (supply contracts "if applicable") | **only** SSMC holders / other MPL holders |
| Capex / capacity limits | none in the rule | ₱2M–₱25M; ≤50,000 t/yr |
| Extra prerequisites | ECC, CEMCRR, EPEP/FMRDP, SDMP, Sanggunian endorsement, NCIP or OCT | Small-Scale Miner's License, MPZ designation, ECC, PEIMP/CDMP/ASHP, SSMC supply contract |
| Precedent next door | Elnar's Gold Processing-B (31 Mar 2026) | none found in JP |
| Foreign equity later | company applicant may be foreign-owned (RA 7942 §3) — the listco-eligible asset | Filipino-only posture (counsel question #1 in the vehicle memo) |

**Why A.** It is the licence MGB-V actually issues to stand-alone plants in Jose Panganiban, it does not wait on an MPZ designation or on Allan's own MB (which has no declaration yet), and it lets Allan buy ore from any permitted source, not only SSMC holders. **Why B stays on the table.** If Allan's MB is declared over the same lot and the LGU designates the plant site as its MPZ, an MPL is the cheaper, lighter small-scale route and the one DAO 2026-08 expects for interim contractors' processing. **Counsel question (before filing):** DAO 2010-21 §109 ¶2 keeps *small-scale miners'* custom mills under RA 7076. Allan is CSSMA's president. Confirm that a plant owned by Allan personally/under his DTI name, buying ore under supply contracts, is an MPP applicant and not a "small-scale miners' custom mill" that MGB-V would bounce to the PMRB. Elnar's suggests MGB-V does issue MPPs to individuals here.

**Circuit.** Gravity-only, mercury-free (LAB X tables; planetGOLD-aligned) keeps the file light: no cyanide → no RA 6969 chemical certification (item 10 below), no CCO, no hazwaste generator ID. Add a leach tank and all three come in. Decide before the ECC application, because the ECC fixes the process.
