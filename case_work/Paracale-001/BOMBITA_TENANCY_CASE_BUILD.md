## 2. Legal frame [L]. Counsel must confirm each point.

- **Res judicata.** It needs identity of parties, subject matter and cause of action. The estate, the co-heirs and a special administrator were not parties; Senen and then a non-heir substitute were. That points to room for a new estate action. The Bombitas will argue the estate is Senen's successor-in-interest.
- **Defective substitution after Senen's death** (Rule 3 §16). A non-heir was substituted instead of the legal heirs or estate representative. Counsel to assess whether the CA and SC proceedings after 12 Feb 2021 bind the estate.
- **Grounds that arose after the last decision** cannot be barred by it. Each is a ground for dispossession under RA 3844 §36:
  - non-payment of rental (§36(6));
  - use contrary to the lease, e.g. mining or tunneling (§36(3));
  - substantial damage, e.g. tree-cutting (§36(5));
  - employing sub-lessees or letting others work the land (§36(7));
  - conversion to non-agricultural use with DAR approval and disturbance compensation (§36(1)).
  The lessor carries the burden (§37).
- **Prescription.** RA 3844 §38 sets three years from accrual. Non-payment accrues per harvest or season, so build the record from **Sep 2023 forward** at minimum.
- **Pre-filing requirement.** Check whether current DARAB rules require BARC mediation or a certification before filing.
- **⚠ Rent trap.** Accepting rent or harvest shares from someone claiming tenancy is classic proof of **consent**, which is element 3. **No one on the estate side accepts money or produce from the Bombitas** except through counsel under a written reservation, if at all.
- **Separate lever: Lot 905 exclusion.** A petition to exclude Lot 905 from CARP as mineral land, on an MGB certification (the Track 1 route), would attack element 2 (agricultural land) for the main ~5 ha.

### 2A. If their tenancy stands: what it gives them, and what it doesn't [L]

**What it gives them (RA 3844 unless noted):**
- **Security of tenure** over the *adjudicated* landholding. They can be removed only by DARAB order on a §36 ground (§7).
- **The lease survives the owner.** It survives sale, transfer, the death of the owner, and partition, and binds heirs and buyers (§10).
- **It passes down.** On the lessee's death it goes to one surviving spouse or descendant who cultivates (§9). In practice it can last for generations.
- **A home lot.** They keep exclusive possession of the home lot occupied, as part of the leasehold (§24).
- **A rent ceiling.** Rent is capped at 25% of the average normal harvest after production costs (§34). The estate's share is at most ~25% net. They can ask DAR/MARO to fix the rental.
- **A right to buy first, and to undo a sale.** Pre-emption if the estate sells the landholding, and redemption if it is sold without notice (§11–12). **This encumbers any sale or JV of those hectares.**
- **Disturbance compensation.** If removed for conversion (e.g. mining), they are owed **5× the average gross harvest of the last 5 years** (§36(1)).
- **Enforcement.** They can move for a writ of execution to be placed in possession as adjudicated. Whether that can be enforced against the estate as a non-party is a counsel question.
- **⚠ CARP exposure on Lot 905.** It is not excluded, and if it is agricultural land above the retention limits it could be placed under CARP. **Tenants are first-priority beneficiaries** (RA 6657 §22) and could end up with a CLOA. T-2194 is protected by the 2008 mineral exclusion. **This is the single biggest downside. The Lot 905 exclusion (E1) is the defense.**

**What it does NOT give them:**
- **No ownership.** They cannot sell, mortgage or title the land, short of a CARP award.
- **No mineral rights.** Minerals belong to the State (RA 7942). Mining by them is a lease violation (§36(3)) and illegal without a permit.
- **No sub-leasing or bringing in other workers** (§27, §36(7)).
- **No cutting trees or damaging the land** (§36(5)).
- **No expanding past the adjudicated area.** Nothing beyond it, e.g. the Lot 904 or 2 ha of T-2194 claimed in the Answer, unless those areas were also adjudicated.
- **No keeping it without paying rent.** Non-payment is a ground for removal (§36(6)).
- **No keeping it after abandonment or voluntary surrender** (§8).
