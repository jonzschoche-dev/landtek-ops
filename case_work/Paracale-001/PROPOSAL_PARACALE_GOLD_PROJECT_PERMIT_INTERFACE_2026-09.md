## 1. Executive summary

The Inocalla estate in Camarines Norte carries roughly 250 hectares of family land across five named vein systems — Manila, Vallejo, Virginia, San Rafael and Tumbaga — in one of the oldest gold districts in the Philippines. Around it sit three permit positions that, taken together, make a project: **Allan's own** (a titled lot, a registered processing business, a Minahang Bayan petition and a plant permit stack in preparation), **Stephen Lloyd's** (the operating partnership over the planetGOLD mercury-free plant at Casalugan), and **NIBDC's** (a 2,360-hectare exploration permit application endorsed by the regional mines bureau for issuance).

What we propose is not a land sale. The 2023 plan offered to sell 81 hectares of the estate for US$6 million and a 2% royalty. This proposal keeps the land in the family and builds the operating business on top of it: a Filipino mining corporation majority-owned by Allan, with **Paracale Gold Company** — the Canadian company already formed — holding a minority stake and carrying the capital and, in time, a public listing. Revenue starts from processing and ore trading inside ground that is already permitted, not from a drill program.

**The permit interface is the substance of this document.** Each party's position is set out in §4 with its status, and §5 shows how they connect: Allan's Minahang Bayan and plant permits are the operating base; Stephen's plant is Wave 1's cash flow; NIBDC's tenement is the exploration upside and the listing asset. No party's permits are absorbed by another's — they are connected by contract, which is what the law requires and what protects each holder.

## 2. The asset, stated honestly

**The ground.** Approximately 250 hectares of Inocalla family land in Barangays Capacuan (Paracale) and San Rafael / Santa Rosa Sur (Jose Panganiban), rolling to mountainous, metasedimentary and metavolcanic, about 200 km east-southeast of Manila. Within it, five vein systems are named in the family's own records.

**The vein systems, per the 2022 geologist's report.** A report titled *NI 43-101 Report on Mineral Resource EXPLORATION TARGET on the Bicol Gold Project*, by J. Zafra, PGeo FAusIMM, dated 3 January 2022, records traceable strike lengths and vein widths for Manila (800 m × 1.0 m), Vallejo (800 m × 0.40 m), San Rafael (1,200 m × 0.30 m) and Tumbaga (1,000 m × 1.80 m), with Virginia unexplored.

**What that does and does not mean — and why we state it this way.** An exploration target is a conceptual statement. Under the disclosure standard the report is named for, a target must be expressed as ranges, must carry the statement that there has been insufficient exploration to define a mineral resource, and must say it is uncertain whether further exploration will result in one. The single-figure tonnage, grade, ounce count and property value carried in the 2023 plan cannot be repeated to an investor or in any listing document, and are not repeated here. **The 2022 report itself is the document to obtain, and a Qualified Person's verification is the work to commission.** We would rather under-claim now than be unable to raise later.

**What is verifiable today** is the operating base: a titled lot in Allan's own name, a registered processing business, a permit stack in preparation, a built and permitted plant nearby with an operator in place, and a district with a documented production history. That is what this proposal is built on.

## 3. What we propose to build

**Wave 1 — Casalugan, now.** Finance the activation of the planetGOLD Mercury-Free Processing System that Stephen operates; add the LAB X gravity circuit; run a toll station and ore-supply agreements with the cooperative's contract holders; lend to the shafts against ore; rent equipment; and sell through a Bangko Sentral-accredited channel, which is tax-exempt by statute.

**Wave 2 — Jose Panganiban.** Allan's Minahang Bayan declared over his own titled Lot 4, and **AVI Gold Processing** built on it: a mercury-free plant designed from the LAB X programme, with its own permit stack.

**Wave 3 — the tenement.** When Waves 1 and 2 are running and audited, Paracale Gold Company proposes an earn-in over NIBDC's exploration permit through a project company, with a coexistence agreement between the large-scale permit and the small-scale cooperative. That project company is what a Canadian exchange would list.

## 5. How the permits interface

**The rule that shapes everything.** Ground held by registered small-scale miners is closed to large-scale mining applications unless those miners consent, and any royalty forms a community trust fund. A Minahang Bayan may be declared over an area under a pending exploration permit application, with the applicant merely notified. **So a declared Minahang Bayan is the strongest position on this ground, and it is Allan's to hold.**

**The interfaces, in order:**

1. **Allan's Minahang Bayan ↔ NIBDC's tenement.** Allan's lot sits inside the barangay list of the tenement application. Once his Minahang Bayan is declared, that ground cannot be taken into any exploration permit, production agreement or large-scale contract without his cooperative's consent. This is not a conflict to be smoothed over: it is the seat at the table that makes Allan a principal in any large-scale scenario rather than a surface owner watching one. **Declare first; consent later, on terms, or not at all.**
2. **Allan's plant ↔ his Minahang Bayan.** Under the small-scale rules, processing sits in a designated zone inside a Minahang Bayan under a processing licence; a stand-alone plant outside a zone holds a DENR Mineral Processing Permit instead. The siting decision therefore drives the entire permit list and the ownership question in §6, and it must be made before the environmental application fixes the location.
3. **Allan's plant ↔ Stephen's plant.** Two plants, not one: Casalugan is Wave 1's revenue and the training floor; AVI is the growth plant Allan owns. Ore, tolling and equipment move between them by contract. Neither absorbs the other's permits.
4. **Stephen's plant ↔ the cooperative's contracts.** Small-scale mining contracts are personal to registered Filipino miners and cannot be assigned or transferred. Outside money participates through ore supply, tolling, production advances and equipment — never by holding a contract. Every Wave-1 line is built that way.
5. **The tenement ↔ the listing.** A foreign company may hold an exploration permit outright; a production agreement caps foreign ownership at 40%; a Financial or Technical Assistance Agreement permits full foreign ownership but requires a firm commitment of at least US$50 million and presidential approval. The listing is therefore built at exploration-permit stage, with the later route chosen when there is a resource to justify it.

## 6. The structure

**Two layers, each holding what it lawfully can.**

- **A Philippine mining corporation, majority-owned by Allan** — newly incorporated, with mining *and* mineral processing in its purpose clause, trading as AVI Gold Processing. It holds the plant permits, the processing licence or permit, the ore-supply and tolling agreements, the lending book, the equipment fleet and the trader accreditation. Allan is President; foreign shareholders may sit on the board in proportion to their equity but hold no management office.
- **Paracale Gold Company (Canada), holding up to 40%** and lending beyond it. At or below 40% the Philippine company remains a national and can hold everything the law reserves to Filipinos — including, later, a production agreement over the tenement itself. This is the same shape the country's largest gold mine runs on: a Filipino tenement company with a foreign minority partner that owns the processing outright.

**Why a new corporation rather than converting the DTI name.** A business name has no legal personality and cannot take a shareholder; incorporating is the same filing either way, and a new company starts with a clean record, a cap table designed on day one, and a purpose clause that reads correctly to the mines bureau, the regulatory board, the central bank and a Canadian exchange.

## 7. What we propose to each party

**To Allan:** majority ownership and the presidency of the operating company, capitalised from day one; his Minahang Bayan declared and held as his own position; his plant built on his own land; the lending and rental businesses into the shafts he already knows; his land contributed only to a company he controls, if at all; and his estate's interests carried separately through the administrator so family land and operations never collide.

**To Stephen:** Wave 1's capital into the plant he operates, released in stages as it comes up; the LAB X programme built to his design; an engineering-services agreement for the AVI plant with milestones, payment per milestone and the drawings to the company; and a widening role as each stage is delivered.

**To Paracale Gold Company and its investors:** entry before the assets have been priced by a market, into an operation already built and permitted; returns through dividends, interest, the lending book, equipment rental, the company's own shares on listing, a processing company it may own outright, the exploration upside, and services fees.
