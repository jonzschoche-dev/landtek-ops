## 2. Rules for the visit (brief Dodie & Manumbas)
1. **This is a records request, not a confrontation.** Polite, member asking for his own file. Do not accuse anyone of anything.
2. **Everything in writing.** Hand over the letter, get the second copy stamped RECEIVED with date and the staff member's name/position. If they refuse to stamp, write the name of the person who refused, date, and time.
3. **Do not sign anything** except the receiving logbook. No waivers, no quitclaims, no "settlement" papers.
4. **Do not accept a verbal explanation as the answer.** If staff explains verbally, note the name and say "please put that in the written reply."
5. If they release documents on the spot, get them **certified true copies** if possible; plain photocopies are fine as a start.
6. Get the **name of the General Manager and of the person handling the reply**, and ask for a timeline for the written response.
7. Report back what happened the same day (photos of every stamped/received page into the CG).
