## 2. Where the Mayor's Permit sits in the sequence

The LGU cannot lawfully demand the ECC or the MGB permit **as a condition of accepting** the application (JMC 01-2021 item 7). But it can, and in mining municipalities does, issue the permit conditionally or hold the "Nature of business: mineral processing plant" line until Zoning and the national permits are shown — and the permit itself says it is revocable for non-compliance with zoning, health, fire, building and "other regulatory requirements". Practical order:

| Stage | What to have | Why |
|---|---|---|
| A — file now (office/yard stage) | DTI cert; OCT P-1616 CTC + tax declaration; barangay clearance (Santa Rosa Sur); CTC (cedula); capitalization declaration | gets AVI Gold Processing Plant on the LGU's books (business tax, RPT link, SSS/PhilHealth/Pag-IBIG registration flows from it); lets Allan open the business bank account and sign supply contracts |
| B — before the plant is built | Locational/Zoning Clearance (MPDO); Building Permit → Occupancy Permit (OBO); BFP Fire Safety Evaluation Clearance then FSIC; Sanitary Permit (MHO) | the "Occupancy Permit if required by national law" item bites for new plant structures |
| C — before operating as a plant | ECC (EMB-V), MGB Mineral Processing Permit; PTO for genset; PCO accreditation | the LGU will inspect against these on renewal and on complaint |

**Recommendation:** file Stage A as **"AVI GOLD PROCESSING PLANT — office and equipment yard, pre-operational"** with declared capitalization at the pre-operating amount, then **amend** the permit (change in nature/capacity) once ECC + MPP are issued. This is cheaper than a full mineral-processing assessment now and avoids paying business tax on a plant that is not running. Confirm with the BPLO that JP treats "pre-operational" this way (many LGUs do).

## 7. Steps (iBPLS route; fall back to the BOSS counter at the Municipal Hall)

1. Ask the JP BPLO for the **iBPLS portal link** (or file at the BOSS); create the account under Allan's name and DTI BN.
2. File the UAF (§4) with uploads: DTI cert + OR, OCT CTC, tax declaration, CTC, IDs, photos, capitalization declaration. Barangay clearance is generated in-system if JP has switched the module on; otherwise attach the barangay's paper clearance.
3. Receive the **Tax Order of Payment**; pay at the Treasurer (or e-payment); e-OR.
4. Permit released (JMC target 1–2 days; assume a week). Sanitary permit issued together; BFP inspects and issues the FSIC after.
5. Post the permit and ORs "in plain view" at the site; calendar **January renewal** (20 Jan deadline under the LGC) and the amendment once ECC + MPP issue.
6. BIR: with the Mayor's Permit + DTI, file Form 1901 at the RDO for Camarines Norte; register books and invoices.
